★ About this venue

Cross Pattaya Pratamnak — Cloud Club

Cross Pattaya Pratamnak's Cloud Club is best understood as a hotel wellness amenity, not a normal public gym. The hotel's own pages publish useful facility and hour details, but they do not currently publish a non-resident access product, so this record is valuable mainly as an honest hotel-facility reference rather than a guaranteed day-pass recommendation.

What training is on offer

The operator presents Cloud Club as the property's fitness and wellness centre. The current public hotel copy says the space is suitable for activities "from treadmill and bicycle workouts to a quiet space for yoga." It also says private yoga lessons or a personal trainer can be arranged with 24 hours' notice.

That language supports a real fitness room and wellness area, but it does not establish a commercial class timetable or a public membership model. This matters when comparing it with an independent gym. A hotel may honestly publish treadmills, bicycles and yoga space while still offering them only as guest amenities or by private arrangement.

The hotel's broader facility pages also support a saltwater pool and a children's pool. Those are useful leisure or recovery features, but they should not be turned into a lap-training claim or a public family-pool ticket unless the hotel publishes that separate access product.

If your actual goal is a hotel pool day rather than a room or stay package, compare named public offers in the swimming category and the swimming pools guide. If you need a normal independent weights session, use the fitness category instead.

What it costs

No current public gym, pool or combined day-pass tariff was published in the checked first-hand sources as of 2026-07-27. The earlier day-pass assumption has therefore been removed rather than repeated without support.

The only honest way to price this record is to contact the hotel and ask whether non-guests can access Cloud Club, whether pool use is bundled with that access, and whether private yoga or personal training has a separate rate. A blank price here means "contact first," not "complimentary."

Who it suits — and who it does not

Cross suits a hotel guest or traveller already considering this Pratamnak property who wants to know whether the published wellness facilities are real and what questions to ask before booking. It may also suit a non-guest willing to request a dated written offer directly from the hotel.

It is a poor fit for someone who needs a confirmed public gym pass today, a published monthly membership or a normal open-gym comparison against a commercial fitness club. The current site simply does not sell it that way in public.

Getting there

The hotel contact page publishes 286/4 Moo 12 Kasetsin Road, Nongprue, Banglamung Chonburi 20150 with phone +66 33 266 155. The property describes itself as being in a high-end residential district a short distance from Pattaya Beach and Walking Street, but that is still hotel context rather than proof of walkable sport access from every nearby stay.

Use the exact hotel pin and contact the property before travelling if the purpose is facility access rather than accommodation.

Before you go

The current public hotel information says Cloud Club is open 06:00-22:00 and the pools 07:00-20:00. It also says towels, refreshments and snacks are provided in Cloud Club. Those are hotel-published amenity details, not a guarantee that a non-resident can buy access on arrival.

Before turning up, ask whether the date is guest-only, whether outside visitors are accepted, whether advance reservation is required for the gym or yoga, and whether children may use the pool without an overnight booking.

What we could not verify

  • No current public non-resident day pass, gym pass or pool pass was published.
  • No current public group-class timetable was found.
  • The checked sources do not state a measured lap length, lane reservation or coached-swimming product.
  • The public pages do not explain non-guest parking, changing or locker rules.
