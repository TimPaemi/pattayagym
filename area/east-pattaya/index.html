03 About this neighborhood

What East Pattaya / Darkside is for.

If you only read one thing: the East Pattaya filter is broad. It currently returns 61 directory records across 14 categories, but that is an index result rather than a claim that 61 bookable venues sit within one compact neighbourhood. The location rule matches East Pattaya, Nong Prue, Pong, Mabprachan, Huai Yai and addresses containing Sukhumvit, so it also catches some records whose practical location may be described as Central, South or North Pattaya. Sixty records have no closure or verification warning in the data; one, Pattaya Floating Market, is explicitly marked non-sport. Open the live map and read the individual status before choosing accommodation around a result.

What the index actually contains

The largest groups are ten Muay Thai records, ten kids-and-youth records and nine racquet records. The remainder comprises six club records, five swimming records, four fitness records, four golf records, four adventure records, two yoga records, two equestrian records, two watersports records and one each for MMA, CrossFit and climbing. Those counts mix ordinary memberships, coached combat sessions, school or academy programmes, court hire, pools, golf courses and book-ahead attractions. There is no honest single day-pass price for the area.

Use dated prices to compare the same product

For coached Muay Thai, Sudsakorn Muay Thai published a ฿400 single session, ฿600 for two sessions in one day, ฿1,800 for one week at one session per day and ฿6,000 for one month at one session per day, checked 25 July 2026. Castra's fight-club page listed ฿300 for one group class, ฿2,500 for ten group classes valid for two months and ฿600 for a private class on the same check date; its general gym membership price was not published.

Court hire is a different comparison. Pattaya Padel Club listed ฿600 per hour from 10:00–16:00 and ฿800 per hour from 07:00–10:00 and 16:00–22:00, checked 25 July 2026. Its one-hour coaching ranged from ฿1,600 for one player to ฿2,600 for four. Silk Muay Thai's residential packages started at ฿16,000 for one week on the same date, but that product bundles a private room, training and specified inclusions. Compare session with session, court with court and residential package with residential package.

The current batch adds two useful East Pattaya contrasts. Fitness 7 Pattaya is central rather than inland, but its newly checked branch-wide rate card — ฿350 day, ฿1,600 month, ฿4,500 three months, ฿8,400 six months and ฿14,400 twelve months on 27 July 2026 — shows how cheap a plain gym-access product can look beside a camp package. Kombat Group and Silk Muay Thai are the opposite case: their published East-side numbers are residential bundles, not simple class-only or gym-only equivalents. Compare access with access before calling one side more expensive.

Who East Pattaya suits

The inland area works best for a longer stay when the chosen venue is near the accommodation and the booking model is already clear. It offers two-a-day Muay Thai schedules, residential camp products, racquet courts, golf and book-ahead outdoor activities. It is a weaker choice for a short visitor expecting to walk between several venues or rely on a universal public-transport route. Sukhumvit Road, the railway-road corridor, local sois and the Mabprachan/Pong belt are not one walkable cluster.

Before paying for accommodation, check the exact pin, road entrance, session time, parking or pickup policy and return plan. For a location-based shortlist, continue to the East Pattaya gym guide.

04 Common questions

East Pattaya / Darkside — FAQ.

Is East Pattaya (the Darkside) a good base for training?

East Pattaya, known locally as the Darkside, is the inland residential side around Mabprachan Lake. It is the value end of a long Pattaya stay: lower rents, quieter roads and a community feel, suited to a serious one to three month training stint on a budget.

What sports is East Pattaya best for?

East Pattaya has value-tier gyms and Muay Thai, several golf courses, equestrian facilities, and the Mabprachan Lake loop that runners and cyclists use. It is less about beachfront sport and more about training, golf and outdoor cardio.

How do I get around East Pattaya?

A car or motorbike is effectively essential here - the Darkside is spread out and baht-bus coverage is thin compared with central Pattaya. The Mabprachan area connects to the city via Sukhumvit and the Highway 7 corridor.

Who is East Pattaya best for?

Budget-conscious long-stay trainees and golfers who want lower costs, more space and a quieter pace, and do not mind needing their own transport.
