# Pattana Sports Resort

Thai Maps calls the 27-hole property **พัฒนา สปอร์ต รีสอร์ท**.

Pattana is a Khaokhunsong sports resort combining a 27-hole golf course, a 50-metre pool, other sports facilities and several accommodation types. One July golf promotion is current on the verification date, but its residency restriction matters: it is not a universal visitor rate.

## What training is on offer

The operator describes three nine-hole golf courses, making 27 holes in total, and says the layout serves players across different skill levels. Its sports and accommodation pages also document a 50-metre pool, the Arena, EnergyLab, a leisure pool near the hotel and longer-stay rooms near the sports facilities.

Those facilities make Pattana more than a single tee-time product, but they do not prove that a hotel booking automatically includes golf, pool lanes or EnergyLab access. Compare the resort with other choices in the [golf directory](/category/golf/) and verify each component of the intended package.

## What it costs

The operator’s **Early Bird Golf Deal**, checked 27 July 2026, is **THB 1,650 per golfer for 18 holes** on weekdays with a tee time before **07:30**. It includes the green fee, caddie fee and golf cart and runs from **1-31 July 2026**.

The restriction is decisive: the operator says the deal is exclusively for **Thai nationals and foreign residents of Thailand**. A tourist who does not satisfy that condition needs a separate quote. Ask whether a caddie tip, rental clubs, balls, tax, insurance or other charges remain outside the package. No general visitor green-fee table was published on the checked page.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Pattana suits a golfer or sports group that values course, pool, training and accommodation at one inland site. The July offer may suit an eligible resident who can accept an early weekday tee time. It is also relevant to longer stays because the operator publishes hotel, mansion, villa and apartment categories.

It is a weaker choice for a beach-based visitor seeking a walkable golf option, or for anyone assuming the promotional THB 1,650 applies without residency evidence. Golfers comparing course-first options can use the [Pattaya golf guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/); swimmers should verify access rather than treating the resort pool as a public lane session.

## Getting there

The operator and Maps place the resort at **99/89 Moo 9, Khaokhunsong, Si Racha, Chonburi 20110**. Maps publishes phone **+66 38 318 999**. This is an inland Si Racha resort, not central Pattaya. No first-hand source checked published a baht-bus route, public shuttle fare or fixed travel time.

Use the exact resort pin and state the booked facility at the gate or reception. Ask the golf desk where to check in if arriving before the hotel’s listed 15:00 check-in time.

## Before you go

Contact the golf desk with the date, player count, intended tee time and eligibility for the offer. Obtain written confirmation that the quoted total includes the green fee, caddie and cart. Ask about the caddie tip, club rental, dress requirements, cancellation, weather policy and the last arrival time.

For swimming or broader training, ask whether access is public, hotel-guest-only, member-only or separately bookable; request the lane timetable and equipment rules. The [swimming-pool guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/) explains why a documented pool is not automatically a public day pass.
