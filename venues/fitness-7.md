# Fitness 7 Pattaya

## Facilities

The official Pattaya branch page describes a gym of more than 2,000 square metres with cardio machines, weight machines, free weights, a boxing ring and a functional-training area. It also says classes are included.

## Hours and location

The branch is open 24 hours daily on the first floor of The Avenue Pattaya, room A100, on Pattaya Second Road. Its official branch page publishes +66 99 287 5677 and +66 87 908 7664; the live Maps listing confirms the first number.

The previous page added unsupported claims about free parking, access technology, staffing and languages. Those details have been removed.

## Price and first visit

The current official branch page does not publish a day pass, monthly fee or joining fee. Ask the branch for the full current price and whether a walk-in visitor needs identification, a deposit or advance registration.
