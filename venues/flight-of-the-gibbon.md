# Flight of the Gibbon Pattaya

## Closed

Flight of the Gibbon Pattaya formerly ran a zipline course at Khao Kheow. The Chonburi Attractions Association still lists the business as closed, while a local guide updated in April 2025 says the operation appears to have ended around 2020. The 25 July 2026 live-listing check also found no reopening signal.

The former phone, timetable, course statistics and package price have been removed because they are not current. Do not use this record to plan a visit.
