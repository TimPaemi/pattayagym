★ About this venue

Elite Gym & Fitness Pattaya

Elite Gym is on Sukhumvit Road between Soi 42 and Soi 44. The operator lists a 1,600-square-metre air-conditioned facility, general gym access, personal training and scheduled group classes including Bodycombat, Bodyfit, Elite Balance, Elite Dance, yoga and Zumba.

Prices checked 25 July 2026

  • day access: ฿400
  • ten visits: ฿2,500, valid for less than three months
  • one month: ฿3,000
  • personal training, one hour: ฿2,000; ten sessions: ฿18,000
  • group class: ฿300; ten classes: ฿1,800

Ask whether a class needs advance booking and whether the ten-visit validity wording applies from purchase or first use. General opening hours are daily 06:30-22:00, but individual class times differ.
