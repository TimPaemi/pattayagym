# Balance Yoga Studio Pattaya

Balance is a small studio on the 14th floor of Cosy Beach View Condominium in Pratamnak. Its programme includes Power Vinyasa, kundalini, yin yoga, stretching, body balance, sound healing and a children's class.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The studio's live price page lists:

- group yoga class: ฿500
- sadhu nail-board session: ฿1,500
- handmade nail-board session: ฿1,000
- group sound-healing session: ฿1,000

The public timetable page still displayed a February 2026 schedule when checked. Treat it as an example rather than a current weekly calendar and book directly. The first phone supports Thai and English; the second supports Russian and English.

## Finding the studio

This is inside a condominium rather than a street-front gym. Confirm building access and the session room before arrival, and allow time to check in with reception.
