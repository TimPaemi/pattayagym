# Planet Football Pattaya

The Thai directory entry names the main ground **สนามแพลนเน็ทฟุตบอล**.

Planet Football is a youth football operator with two East Pattaya grounds and a pathway from open beginner sessions to development training and invite-only academy squads. The main directory pin is Planet Football Stadium on Siam Country Club Road; families must confirm which ground hosts the selected age group before travelling.

## What training is on offer

Three Saturday open programmes use the Planet Football sports hall from 09:00 to 10:30. **Mini Kicks** is for boys and girls aged 4-6 and introduces movement, coordination and confidence; the operator says parents may be encouraged to join. **Kiddy Kickers** is for ages 7-9 and adds ball mastery, game knowledge, communication and teamwork. **Soccer School** is for ages 10-13 and covers technical work, positions, finishing, phases of play and tactics over a stated 12-15-week cycle.

The Development Programme separates ages 7-10 and 11-15. Its page lists Saturday and Sunday at Planet Football Stadium: ages 11-15 from 13:30 to 15:00 and ages 7-10 from 15:30 to 17:00. A combined ages 7-15 session is listed at FC Planet Stadium on Monday from 17:30 to 19:00.

FC Planet Academy is invite-only, normally reached through Soccer School or Development. The operator describes three weekday training sessions between 17:00 and 19:00 plus weekly competition. The [Pattaya kids-and-youth directory](/category/kids-youth/) contains other sports and access models.

## What it costs

Each of the three Saturday open-session pages lists **฿400 per player** (operator pages, checked 29 July 2026). The Development Programme page lists **฿2,500 per calendar month** and a separate **฿2,500 yearly membership** that includes three programme kits and a training ball (operator page, checked 29 July 2026).

Confirm whether both Development payments are due at joining, the first billing date, absence or make-up rules and whether matches, tournaments or travel cost extra. The public Academy page does not publish an invite-only squad fee.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Planet Football suits families seeking age-banded coaching rather than unsupervised pitch hire. The three open Saturday groups give new players a clear entry point, while the Development and Academy structure offers a route for children seeking more training and competition.

It is not a casual public field where any child can arrive at any hour, and Academy places are not walk-in products. Families who need a different sport or a wider activity shortlist can use the [kids-and-youth sport guide](/guides/kids-youth-sport-pattaya/). The [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) gives location context for the two inland grounds.

## Getting there

Planet Football Stadium, the headquarters pin in this record, is at 55/15 Moo 2 on Siam Country Club Road. FC Planet Stadium is a separate venue on Soi 10 off Phon Prapha Nimit 34 Road. The current Maps results distinguish the two pins.

Ask the coach to send the exact ground for the selected programme. Do not use the headquarters address for an FC Planet Stadium session. No operator-published journey time or public-transport fare is recorded.

## Before you go

The operator says children should register as full members after their first open session. Registered Soccer School players must wear the Planet Football kit with long socks and shin pads. Mini Kicks specifies trainers or futsal shoes for the indoor hall; the older open-group pages say football boots or similar. The facility page says metal studs are not allowed.

Planet Football Stadium lists a 7-a-side pitch, an enclosed 5-a-side pitch and a multi-sports hall marked for futsal and basketball, plus scoreboards, lighting, changing rooms, showers, spectator areas and an air-conditioned clubhouse bar. FC Planet Stadium has a full-size artificial-turf pitch configurable for 7-, 9- or 11-a-side play.

Use +66 38 248 212 for the main venue and +66 95 995 7693 for facility bookings. Provide the child's age, experience and date; confirm the ground, session, first-visit process, footwear, shin pads, kit and total cost.
