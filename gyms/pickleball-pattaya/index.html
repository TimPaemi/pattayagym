★ About this venue

PR Pattaya Pickleball

PR Pattaya Pickleball is the current public identity for the former Pickleball Pattaya venue on Pratamnak Soi 6. The official site still uses "Pattaya Pickleball Club" in places, while the exact live Maps identity uses the PR name, so this record treats them as the same club rather than two separate venues.

What training is on offer

The official site presents a dedicated pickleball product rather than a spare-court add-on inside a larger sports club. It says the venue has two full-sized courts with permanent nets, full surrounding fences and lighting strong enough for play until 21:00. The same page says paddles, balls and a ball machine are available at the courts.

The site also advertises beginner lessons from an English-speaking, licensed coach, plus private coaching and youth programmes for ages 5 and up. That gives the club a broader product mix than a simple court-rental-only venue. What it does not prove is that all of those options run at every hour the club is open, so players should state whether they want open play, coaching or junior instruction when booking.

For a wider racquet comparison, move from the racquet category to the padel and pickleball guide. If the group may switch sports, Pattaya Badminton & Tennis Inter Club is the more mixed racquet record.

What it costs

No current public rate card was visible on the checked official pages as of 2026-07-27. The homepage shows a "View Pricing" prompt, but the visible public crawl did not expose the actual amounts. Because of that, the old price assumptions have been removed instead of being recycled without a dated source.

The useful evidence is that the operator now runs a live booking form for Court 1 and Court 2. Ask for the exact open-play price, private-coaching fee, racket or paddle charge, ball policy, youth-programme pricing and any member versus non-member difference before confirming a slot.

Also clarify whether the quote is per court, per player or per coaching hour. A dedicated pickleball venue can sell those products in different ways, and the public pages checked for this run did not settle that question.

Who it suits — and who it does not

PR Pattaya Pickleball suits a player or small group that specifically wants pickleball rather than tennis, badminton or padel. The dedicated two-court setup, ball machine and beginner-coach language make it useful for first-timers who want a sport-specific introduction instead of borrowing a multi-sport court.

It is a weaker fit for someone who needs a fully published tariff before making contact or a player who wants a large club with many courts and several fallback sports under one roof. It is also not the right record if the main goal is tennis or badminton coaching; those decisions belong in the tennis and badminton guide.

Getting there

The exact Maps identity is PR Pattaya Pickleball at 358/1 Khao Road, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, with phone +66 61 116 9033. The official site describes the venue more loosely as being on Pratamnak Soi 6, so use the precise map pin rather than only the neighbourhood label.

Pratamnak is useful if the player is already based between Cosy Beach and South Pattaya. It is less convenient for someone staying far inland and hoping for a quick walk-up replacement for a central gym session.

Before you go

The official site currently says the club is open daily from 07:00 to 21:00. The separate booking form publicly exposes court reservations, which is stronger evidence of a real operating club than a static "contact us" page alone. Still, confirm the actual playing format before travelling because the homepage, booking form and legacy naming do not explain everything.

State whether the booking is for open play, a private lesson, a junior session or an enquiry about membership. Ask which hours are used for social or open play, whether equipment is included, and whether the venue can help a solo player find opponents.

What we could not verify

  • No current public price table was visible on the official pages.
  • The checked pages did not show a clear public membership breakdown or cancellation policy.
  • The visible booking form shows court reservations, but not the full payment rules.
  • The public pages do not confirm partner matching, tournament play or a fixed weekly open-play timetable.
