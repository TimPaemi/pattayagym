★ About this venue

BOXING CLUB Mixfight Pattaya

What training is on offer

Mixfight has a live Maps identity as a martial-arts school at Khao Makok 9/1. A March 2026 owner update on that listing names Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA, jiu-jitsu, yoga, fitness, children's groups and fight preparation. That is evidence of the school's advertised scope, not proof that every discipline runs every day or that every session accepts visitors.

Independent Sherdog and Tapology event records also place New Generation Championship activity at Mixfight, including MMA bouts. Those records support a combat-sport venue connection; an event card is not a recurring class timetable. Compare the school with the other contact-first record in the MMA category, and use the BJJ and MMA guide for the directory's discipline distinctions.

What it costs

No current operator-published price for a group session, private lesson, open gym, weekly plan or monthly plan was found during the 26 July 2026 check. The empty price field means price unknown, not free entry and not a reason to remove a confirmed operating venue.

Message the operator profile linked from Maps and request a written amount for the exact discipline, format and date. Ask whether registration, equipment use, a private trainer or event preparation creates a separate charge.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Mixfight may suit a visitor who wants a multi-discipline school in the Khao Makok area and is willing to confirm the session before travelling. The advertised children's groups may also make it relevant to a household with different training goals, but age bands and current enrolment remain direct questions.

It does not suit someone who needs a published drop-in tariff, a guaranteed English-language class or a confirmed visitor timetable before making contact. A long operating window should not be read as an all-day MMA class. Readers seeking only Muay Thai should compare dedicated records in the Muay Thai category.

Getting there

The current listing gives Khao Makok 9/1, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. It shows 08:00–21:00 Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 08:00–19:00 Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, and Sunday closed. Holiday hours may differ.

Use the exact Maps pin rather than navigating from the broad “South Pattaya” label. Ask which entrance to use and whether parking is available, especially if attending an announced event rather than an ordinary class.

Before you go

Contact @coach.pattaya on Instagram with the desired discipline, experience level, age where relevant, preferred date and whether group or private training is required. Ask for the exact start and finish time, coaching language, visitor eligibility, current fee, payment method, equipment and clothing requirements, and whether sparring or rolling is optional.

For jiu-jitsu, confirm gi or no-gi format. For striking, ask about gloves, hand wraps and protective equipment. For children, request the age band, supervision arrangement and trial policy.

What we could not verify

No current first-hand class-by-class timetable, public tariff, telephone number, trainer roster, language guarantee, equipment policy or child age bands were available. The live listing confirms the school; it does not resolve those product details. The independent event records do not establish ordinary walk-in access.
