★ About this venue

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is the current name of the Na Jomtien water park formerly called Cartoon Network Amazone. The old brand, slide count and zone descriptions have been removed; the live operator site now presents eight Columbia Pictures-themed water and land zones.

The park includes water slides, a wave pool, children's water play and optional land attractions. Hotel-style lap swimming is not the focus: this is a paid theme-park day out.

Tickets and first visit

The park is open daily from 10:00 to 18:00. Admission is sold through a dynamic booking flow, and no dependable static general-admission price was visible during verification, so check the operator's current ticket page for your date. Optional attractions can cost extra.

The operator advertises return transport from Pattaya. Check the current pickup points, fare and ticket conditions in the booking flow before paying.
