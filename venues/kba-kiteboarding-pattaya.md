# KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya

KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya is a Blue Lagoon watersports school in Na Jomtien focused on board sports rather than a generic beach-rental stall. Its current Pattaya page clearly ties the branch to kitesurf, kitefoil, efoil, wingfoil and Wing SUP, plus gear rental and shop support. The limitation is availability: wind and branch scheduling matter, so a named lesson or rental should be confirmed before you travel.

## What training is on offer

The current Pattaya branch page identifies KBA's Blue Lagoon base and lists kitesurf, kitefoil, efoil, wingfoil and Wing SUP as live activities, alongside gear rental and a shop. That makes this a specialist [watersports](/category/watersports/) record rather than a one-sport school. The operator copy also describes Pattaya as a beginner-friendly location, but that should be read as a branch-level positioning statement, not as proof that every wind day or foil session suits a first-timer.

## What it costs

KBA's current kitesurf page listed a one-hour trial at ฿2,200, a six-hour beginner course at ฿11,000 and a twelve-hour full course at ฿20,000 (operator kitesurf page, checked 2026-07-27). The same operator family also exposed matching wingfoil lesson points through the live gift-card catalogue, and the gift-card page listed an efoil introduction from ฿2,000 for 30 minutes (operator gift-card page, checked 2026-07-27).

Those are the published operator prices currently tied to this record, but they are not enough to promise that every product is running at the Blue Lagoon branch on your date. Before paying, ask the Pattaya branch to confirm the exact activity, lesson length, whether equipment is included, whether a rescue boat or supervision fee applies, the wind requirement and the cancellation or rebooking rule if conditions are poor.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

KBA suits a traveller who wants a specialist lesson product in Na Jomtien and is happy to book around conditions. It also suits someone comparing several sea-sport options and wanting a clearer published lesson ladder than many contact-first beach operators provide. If your priority is scuba trips and a central shop base rather than wind sport, [Adventure Divers Pattaya](/gyms/adventure-divers-pattaya/) is a more relevant starting point.

It is less suitable for someone who expects a fixed daily timetable like a city gym, or who wants a guaranteed same-hour session regardless of weather. It is also the wrong fit if the real goal is a low-cost uncoached beach rental rather than a structured board-sport lesson.

## Before you go

Message the branch with the exact sport you want and the date you want it. Ask whether the published lesson ladder on the operator-wide page is available at Pattaya, what the meeting time is, whether equipment and safety gear are included and whether the lesson can proceed in the expected conditions. That matters more here than at a fixed-indoor venue because the operator itself frames the activity around wind and water conditions.

If you are new to the sport, ask whether the first product should be the one-hour trial or a longer beginner lesson, and whether there are age, swimming-ability or weight requirements.
