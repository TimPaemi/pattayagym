# Kombat Group Thailand

Kombat Group is a residential combat-sports camp in Huai Yai. Its current course pages cover Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, while the contact page lists weekday and Saturday office hours.

## Price checked 25 July 2026

The operator lists a one-week standard Muay Thai or western-boxing package at **฿20,900**. This is a residential package rather than a simple gym pass and includes specified accommodation, training and camp services. The pricing page says 7% VAT is excluded and card payments add 3%.
