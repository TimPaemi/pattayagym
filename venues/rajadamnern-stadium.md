# Rajadamnern Stadium

Rajadamnern Stadium is operating, but it is in **Bangkok**, not Pattaya. This legacy page is retained only as an explicitly labelled day-trip reference and must not be counted as a local venue.

The official calendar currently advertises live Muay Thai seven nights a week. Gates open at 17:00 for many traditional cards and at 18:00 for other events; use the date-specific calendar rather than a fixed weekly timetable. The stadium ticket office opens daily at 15:00.

The current Maps official-ticket panel showed admission from **฿1,800** on 25 July 2026. The operator says prices vary by event and seating tier, so check the live booking flow for the selected date.

The former travel-time promises, fixed ticket table, free-beer claim and promotional “canonical weekend” language have been removed.
