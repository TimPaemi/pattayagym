What it costs

No current operator-published day-pass, weekly, monthly or personal-training tariff was accessible. Ask the venue to quote the product that matches the length of your stay and to state the total charge, start date and expiry.

Do not rely on an old review, photo or third-party listing as a current price card. A written reply from the owner is the useful evidence until a dated tariff is published.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Fatburn may suit someone staying near Jomtien Beach Road who wants an independent gym and values late weekday opening. The listed 23:00 weekday close provides more evening flexibility than the 21:00 weekend close.

It is a weaker choice for a visitor who needs a transparent online drop-in price, a verified class at a particular hour or a guaranteed specialist lifting setup. None of those details was published. Longer-stay visitors can also use the digital-nomad fitness guide to compare location, opening hours and membership questions rather than judging on a monthly price alone.

Getting there

The exact listing places the gym at 427 Moo 12 on Jomtien Beach Road. Use the live pin to identify the entrance; the long beachfront road and repeated Moo 12 addresses make the venue name and pin more useful than the street name alone.

The Jomtien area page gives broader context on nearby directory records. No fixed walking time, songthaew route, fare, parking provision or building-access instruction was published by the operator, so confirm those details if they affect the visit.

Before you go

The current listing gives +66 63 542 5444 and hours of 08:00–23:00 Monday through Friday, then 09:30–21:00 on Saturday and Sunday. Contact the gym before a public-holiday or late-evening visit because listing hours can change.

Ask whether a first visit needs identification or registration, whether indoor shoes or a towel are required, and whether the desk accepts the payment method you plan to use. Request confirmation of changing and shower facilities rather than assuming they are included in entry.
