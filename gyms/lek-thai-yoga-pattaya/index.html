★ About this venue

Lek Thai Yoga

Lek Thai Yoga is an appointment-led practice at Laguna Beach Resort 3 Maldives near Thepprasit Road. Its current site focuses on Thai yoga massage, Yin yoga, meditation, private work and teacher-training courses. It is more useful to someone seeking an arranged programme than to a visitor expecting a conventional studio timetable.

What training is on offer

The operator’s current catalogue includes yoga teacher training, Thai yoga massage and stretching courses, Yin yoga, meditation and private beginner work. The exact 300-hour course page describes a multi-style programme covering Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Yin, yoga philosophy and Thai yoga stretching. It states that the group is limited to six participants.

That course is a substantial training commitment, not evidence of a weekly drop-in class. The homepage also advertises shorter workshops and private products, but it does not present one stable local class calendar. Readers seeking a timetable-led studio can compare the wider Pattaya yoga directory.

What it costs

The operator’s exact 300-hour multi-style teacher-training page displayed a sale price of USD 3,100, reduced from USD 3,200, when checked on 26 July 2026. The quoted currency is US dollars. Reconfirm the dates, deposit, cancellation terms, accommodation, meals, manuals, assessment and certification inclusions before paying; the public page does not make every one of those terms clear.

No dependable local drop-in, one-to-one therapeutic-session or recurring-class tariff was found. Ask for the exact service name, duration, currency and complete amount rather than converting the teacher-training price into an assumed hourly rate.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Lek Thai Yoga suits a learner who wants direct contact with a small practice, particularly for a private session, Thai yoga stretching, meditation or a multi-day training product. The six-person cap published for the 300-hour course may matter to someone choosing between a small cohort and a larger school.

It is a weaker fit for a traveller who needs a guaranteed walk-in class, a live weekly booking grid or a baht-denominated drop-in price before making contact. The Jomtien area index gives nearby alternatives, while the Pattaya yoga guide helps separate ordinary classes from retreats and certifications.

Getting there

The exact Maps identity places the practice at Laguna Beach Resort 3 Maldives, 164 Moo 12 on Soi Thepprasit 17. This is a condominium complex rather than a street-front studio. Use the linked pin and arrange the meeting point with the operator before entering the property.

The public sources do not publish a baht-bus route, visitor parking procedure or reception check-in instructions. Do not plan around a generic “Jomtien” pin alone.

Before you go

The current Maps listing shows 08:00-18:00 Monday to Saturday and Sunday closed. Treat those as business hours, not a promise that a class starts at 08:00 or that an instructor accepts an unannounced arrival. Holiday hours may differ.

Contact +66 98 136 4264 or the email published on the operator site, lekspa@gmail.com. Send the desired service, experience level, preferred date and whether the booking is for one person or a group. Ask what clothing or equipment to bring and whether the session is taught in the language you need.

What we could not verify

  • No current weekly drop-in class timetable was published.
  • No local private-session, therapeutic-session or standard class price was clear enough to record.
  • Accommodation and meal inclusions for the priced 300-hour course were not established.
  • Visitor parking, condominium access, equipment provision, refund terms and teaching language require direct confirmation.
