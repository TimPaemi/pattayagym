# Jungle Gym Pattaya

## Affiliate status

The gym's own site still publishes a page headed CrossFit Pattaya and lists group classes. However, CrossFit's official affiliate directory now labels **CrossFit Pattaya (departed)**. This directory therefore does not describe Jungle Gym as a current CrossFit affiliate.

## Published training times

The owner page lists group functional-fitness sessions:

- Monday to Friday: 09:00–10:00 and 19:00–20:00
- Saturday: 09:00–10:00
- Sunday: closed

Open-gym and private-training slots are offered between group classes on weekdays. No current price was published on the pages checked, so this record omits membership and drop-in prices.

## Before attending

Contact the gym to confirm that the published timetable is current and ask what programming is running under the Jungle Gym name. Visiting CrossFit members should not assume affiliate privileges.
