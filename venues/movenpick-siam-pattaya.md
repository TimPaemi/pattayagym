# Mövenpick Siam Hotel Na Jomtien Pattaya

The hotel publishes a 24-hour fitness centre alongside its seafront swimming pool, spa and Little Bird Kid's Club. This is a hotel facility, not a confirmed public gym: none of the current operator pages checked on 25 July 2026 published a non-resident fitness day pass.

The resort is at kilometre 156.5 on Sukhumvit Road in Na Jomtien, well south of central Pattaya. Hotel guests can use the linked resort page for current facility notices; non-guests should ask the hotel directly about access before travelling.
