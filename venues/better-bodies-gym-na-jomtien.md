# Better Bodies Gym

Better Bodies is a weight-training gym at Somphong Condotel in Ban Amphur, Na Jomtien. The operator describes a practical training floor for younger and older members, with help available for general fitness, weight loss or gain, injury prevention and contest preparation. It is a neighbourhood gym rather than a resort package or class-only studio.

## Prices checked 26 July 2026

The operator's services page publishes:

- one day: **THB 250**
- one week: **THB 400**
- one month: **THB 1,000**
- three months: **THB 2,500**
- six months: **THB 4,500**

These are access periods, not coached-class packages. The same page says nutritional advice, a body-fat check and progress checks are free for members, while personal training and contest preparation are available on request. Ask what is included in the particular term before assuming that a trainer-led session is part of the access price.

## Guidance and member support

Better Bodies says a trainer is available to show newcomers how to use the equipment and that personal training is free during the first month. The operator also describes weekly tracking that can include weight, body-fat percentage, the programme and diet, heart rate and blood pressure. Those are operator-described services rather than medical care; a visitor with an injury or clinical concern should state the limitation before training and seek appropriate professional advice where needed.

Training help is available in Thai, English and Dutch according to the services page. Free drinking water and Wi-Fi are listed. This combination can suit a longer-stay member who wants a small gym with direct staff contact, but it does not establish a timetable of group classes, a rehabilitation service or round-the-clock access.

## Who it fits

The published menu makes Better Bodies easiest to compare for a one-day trial or a one-to-six-month local routine. It is a weaker fit for an early-morning workout, Sunday session or visitor seeking a scheduled group class: opening begins at 10:00, Sunday is closed and no class timetable was published. Someone choosing between this gym and a resort facility should compare the exact training floor and staff support, not hotel amenities that are not part of this membership.

## Hours, address and first visit

Opening is listed Monday to Saturday from **10:00 to 20:00**, with Sunday closed. The exact Maps identity remains active and links back to the operator site. The operator and Maps formats do not agree on every postcode and phone detail: the website publishes Somphong Condotel unit 99/15 on Sukhumvit Soi 26 and lists **0910 428 879** for Thai or English and **0848 832 967** for English, while Maps displays **084 883 2967**. Use the exact pin and confirm the entrance rather than relying on the postcode alone.

For a first visit, state whether you want a one-day trial, unsupervised weight training, a personal-training appointment or contest preparation. Ask when a trainer will be present, whether a first-month personal-training offer applies to the chosen membership, what changing and locker arrangements are available, and which payment methods are accepted. The published THB 250 day price makes a trial possible without inventing a commitment, but it does not guarantee a trainer at every point in the ten-hour opening window.
