# Manta Kids Pattaya

Manta Kids Pattaya operates at IG Center on Chaiyaphruek 2. The host venue describes an indoor saltwater pool with UV treatment, a water temperature of 31-33°C and age-based programmes for children from four months to twelve years. Listed class lengths are 30 minutes for the younger Splash and Swirl groups and 50 minutes for Swim.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The current Manta Kids operator site displays Splash at ฿8,500, Swirl at ฿8,500, Swim Beginner at ฿8,500 and Swim at ฿10,900. The IG Center branch page still carries older figures, so parents should confirm the Pattaya total, number of sessions, make-up policy and term dates before paying.

The current operator contact for Pattaya is +66 98 191 9954. The host page also lists +66 63 985 5567; use the first number for the programme and the second if access to the IG Center facility needs clarification.
