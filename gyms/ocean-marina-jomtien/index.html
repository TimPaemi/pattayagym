★ About this venue

Ocean Marina Jomtien

Ocean Marina Jomtien is a working marina at 167/5 Moo 4 in Na Jomtien. The operator publishes marina, charter, berth and sailing contacts from this address, while the live Maps results distinguish the marina from the adjoining resort.

Sailing activity and current fee

The operator's 2026 Platu calendar lists championship rounds at the marina, including 4-6 September, 26-28 October and 30 October-1 November 2026. It publishes an entry fee of ฿7,500 per regatta, checked on 25 July 2026. This is a crew-entry fee for the Platu series, not general public admission or a berth rate.

The operator describes coaching and charter packages as enquiry-based. No current public berth, lesson or visitor tariff was available, so none is quoted here. Contact the marina or its on-site sailing businesses for access relevant to your boat or course.

The previous address, phone, berth-capacity table and maximum-yacht claims were replaced because the current contact page publishes different contact details and those statistics were not needed to describe the verified sporting use.
