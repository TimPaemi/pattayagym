# Pattaya Panthers Rugby Football Club

Pattaya Panthers is a social rugby club rather than a bookable pitch or spectator attraction. Its current Facebook introduction says **all levels are welcome**, names both **contact and touch rugby**, and publishes training on **Thursday from 19:15 to 20:45**. The page locates current club activity at Horseshoe Point Resort.

Use the Facebook page or email **PattayaPanthersRFC@gmail.com** before attending. The club also publishes the **pattaya_panthers_rfc** Instagram identity. Ask for the exact pitch and whether the advertised Thursday session is running that week; a resort map pin does not identify the field entrance or prove that every group follows the same schedule.

## Contact, touch and the women's branch

The club's public description covers two formats. Contact rugby involves tackling and should not be assumed suitable without the organiser's placement and safety guidance. Touch rugby removes tackles but still needs a confirmed group, footwear and session plan.

The official site describes **Pattaya Panties** as the women's touch branch of the Panthers. It says players of all ages, abilities and experience levels are welcome, with a focus on teamwork, fitness, skill development and social participation. That invitation does not establish a separate weekly timetable on the public page, so women interested in the touch group should contact the club rather than relying only on the main Thursday listing.

## Current tournament activity

The Panthers' official site advertised the 2026 Chris Kays Memorial as a one-day event at Horseshoe Point on **Saturday 2 May 2026**. A club post on 5 May thanked participants in the Pullman Pattaya G Rugby 10s and showed current event activity at Horseshoe Point. Because the tournament date has passed, it is evidence of recent operation rather than a future fixture.

The site still links to the 2025 programme while inviting enquiries about the 2026 event. Do not use an old programme to infer the next tournament date, team list, entry fee or weekly practice venue.

## Joining and first session

No current first-hand membership fee, match fee, insurance charge or visitor drop-in price was published on the pages checked on 26 July 2026. Ask the club for the complete cost and whether registration or insurance is required before contact play.

Send the organiser your age, experience, preferred contact or touch format and whether you are visiting once or looking to join. Confirm:

- the exact Thursday meeting point and arrival time;
- whether the session is adult, youth, women's or mixed;
- suitable boots for the surface and any mouthguard requirement;
- whether a first-time player may join training immediately;
- changing, water and transport arrangements; and
- the current membership, match or tournament pathway.

The club's all-levels message is a welcome signal, not permission to enter a contact drill without an organiser's placement.
