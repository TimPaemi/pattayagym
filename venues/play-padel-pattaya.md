# Play Padel Pattaya

The operator site is the local source for **เพลย์ พาเดล พัทยา**; its court page specifies **6** air-conditioning units per court beneath an **11.5-metre** roof.

## What training is on offer

Play Padel is an indoor, climate-controlled arena in Pratamnak. The operator describes six air-conditioning units per court beneath an 11.5-metre roof and says licensed coaches are available. Its offer covers court booking, private coaching and equipment rental.

The operator presents padel as suitable for children, adults and seniors. That broad invitation does not reserve a court or place a new player into a group. State the player count and experience level when asking for a court, lesson or help finding other players.

## What it costs

The operator's live tariff checked on **26 July 2026** publishes:

- standard court: **THB 1,200 per court-hour**;
- private coaching: **from THB 600 per hour**;
- standard racket rental: **THB 100 per hour**;
- demo racket: **15 minutes free**, then **THB 200-300 per hour** depending on the model;
- balls: **THB 200-300 per pack** depending on the model; and
- new rackets: **THB 2,000-12,000** depending on the model.

The quoted court price is for the court, not each player. Divide it among the confirmed participants only after checking whether coaching, rackets or balls are separate. The operator warns that a late arrival does not move the reservation: if a booking begins at noon and a player arrives at 12:30, charging still starts from the reserved time.

The page also displays a personal membership at **THB 1,000 per month**, with a THB 100 hourly discount and additional coaching discounts. Its wording says the programme starts “from November” without a year, so confirm current availability and eligible products.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Play Padel suits groups wanting an indoor court with a published hourly rate and beginners prepared to book coaching. The whole-court price can be divided among a confirmed group, making player count important to the real per-person cost.

It is less suitable for a solo player who assumes immediate matching, a visitor who needs a free racket, or anyone unwilling to prepay a first advance booking. “Drop-in bookings” means an unplanned booking may be possible, not that a court or playing group will be waiting.

## Getting there

The address is **400/34 Soi Kasetsin 3, Pratamnak, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150**. Use the exact Maps pin. The office number shown by both the site and Maps is **+66 65 123 4105**; the site also lists English/Swedish contacts Mattias at +66 82 940 1875 and Erik at +66 80 621 0000.

Kasetsin Soi 3 is near the Cosy Beach area. The operator advises extra travel time during long holidays because road queues can delay arrivals while the reserved hour remains chargeable.

## Before you go

The operator publishes **10:00-22:00 daily**. Bookings use **Padelmates**. It says known players can reserve and pay at the arena, while new players booking ahead must pay in advance. Cash and Thai QR are preferred; credit-card payment through Padelmates adds **THB 100**.

Confirm the date, start time, player count, duration, equipment, coaching format and cancellation terms. Arrive early enough to change, collect rentals and warm up. Ask which shoes are suitable and whether balls are included.

## What we could not verify

The public pages do not state a guaranteed player-matching time, universal refund rule or year for the “from November” membership wording. The operator says security cameras are installed and may be used inside courts, and asks people who do not want to appear in photos or video to tell reception; ask how footage is handled if that matters.
