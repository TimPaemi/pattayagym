Pattaya Boxing World

Pattaya Boxing World is a legacy spectator-stadium listing, not a confirmed current fight-night option. The available tourism and local-press sources describe its earlier programme near Mini Siam, but an exact-name Maps search on 25 July 2026 did not establish an active venue, current schedule, ticket seller or contact. Do not plan a fight night around this record without finding a current operator announcement.

What training is on offer

The Thai native-name entry records the former 387 Moo 6 stadium as พัทยาบ็อกซิ่งเวิร์ล and lists 600 seats.

The historical sources describe a Muay Thai spectator stadium rather than a verified training gym. They are useful for identifying the former venue and its place in Pattaya’s fight-night history, but they do not prove that lessons, a camp, a gym floor, trainers or any present-day programme are available. No current operator channel or live timetable was found in the sources checked. This record therefore does not describe training sessions as if they can be booked today.

What it costs

No current ticket price, admission category, training fee, package, membership or payment method could be verified on 25 July 2026. Earlier pricing or promotion claims would be historical and are not presented as a current tariff. A traveller who sees a listing under this name should obtain a dated operator ticket page or direct confirmation before paying or making transport plans.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This record may help a researcher distinguish a former North Pattaya / Sukhumvit Road stadium listing from current venues with active programmes. It does not suit someone who needs a dependable place to watch Muay Thai this week, train Muay Thai, buy tickets online, or rely on a published class schedule. For a current activity, use a venue with an active operator and dated programme.

Getting there

The legacy address is recorded as formerly 387 Moo 6, Sukhumvit Road, Naklua, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, near Mini Siam. That is historical location context, not a claim that a visitor can enter a live stadium there. The exact-name Maps search did not resolve to a confirmed current place, so no route, transport fare, parking arrangement or entrance instruction is provided.

Before you go

Do not travel to the former address solely on the basis of this page. Check a current operator announcement, current ticketing page or live venue listing that clearly identifies the promoter, date, card and location. A social post without a current venue identity is not enough to establish that Pattaya Boxing World is operating.

What we could not verify

The searches completed on 25 July 2026 did not verify current operation, promoter, fight calendar, ticket source, phone, website, social channel, training offer, admission price, access arrangements or an active Maps venue. The official-tourism and Pattaya Mail sources available for this record concern the former programme, not a current reopening.
