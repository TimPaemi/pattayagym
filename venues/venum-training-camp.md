# Venum Training Camp Thailand

Venum Training Camp operates on Soi Khopai 12 and advertises Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and cross-training. The operator says its programmes are open to beginners as well as experienced athletes.

## Price and first visit

No usable current public tariff was available. Ask for the exact class, private-session or longer-stay price before booking. The official site and Google Maps currently disagree about opening hours: the site lists daily access from 07:00 to 19:00, while Maps lists 09:00 to 19:00 Monday-Saturday and Sunday closure. Confirm the session you want directly with the camp.
