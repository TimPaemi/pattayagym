# Battle & Conquer Gym

The Thai KKday listing labels the location ยิมต่อสู้และพิชิต and says personal gloves and hand wraps are excluded, with rental priced at 50 baht per session.

Battle & Conquer Gym is at 359/119 Moo 12 in Pratamnak. The operator publishes twice-daily group Muay Thai at 08:00-10:00 and 16:00-18:00, alongside a weights gym, sauna and cold plunge. Normal facility hours are stated as 07:00-22:00 daily.

## What training is on offer

The operator sources support group Muay Thai, private sessions and general gym access. Its facilities page supports the weights area, sauna and cold plunge. The sources do not establish a named trainer’s availability, sparring policy, children’s programme, equipment hire or a separate class timetable beyond the two group windows.

## What it costs

The current price list gives Muay Thai packages of ฿2,500 for a week, ฿7,000 for a month, ฿18,000 for three months and ฿35,000 for six months; these include group training and the listed facilities. Gym-only access is ฿200 daily, ฿600 weekly, ฿1,300 monthly or ฿12,000 yearly. A group session is ฿500, and private 90-minute training is ฿1,200 single, ฿4,300 for five or ฿7,500 for ten.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The documented group and gym-only options suit a visitor choosing either Muay Thai or general fitness access. No source confirms a beginner trial, a women-only format, injury screening, child policy or availability of gloves and wraps. Confirm the required session before arriving.
