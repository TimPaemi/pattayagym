# Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya Pool

Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya describes a 2,000-square-metre freeform leisure pool with a sand beach, jacuzzi, underwater speakers, guitar-shaped deck, children's zone, slides and a cocktail pool. The hotel's Thai pool page uses the name **สระว่ายน้ำฮาร์ดร็อค พัทยา** for the same 2,000-square-metre pool, which can help Thai-speaking visitors identify the product. The hotel publishes a specific daytime product for non-residents, making access easier to evaluate than at hotel pools that disclose amenities but not public eligibility.

## What training is on offer

This is a recreation setting rather than a documented lap-training pool. The operator's page emphasises leisure features, family play and poolside facilities; it does not publish lane lengths, reserved lap hours, a coached-swimming timetable or a fitness programme.

Choose it for a leisure swim or family pool day, not because the 2,000-square-metre figure implies a competition layout. Swimmers who need lane evidence can compare the [Pattaya swimming directory](/category/swimming/) and the [swimming-pool guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/) before paying for hotel access.

## What it costs

The official pool page, checked on 26 July 2026, lists non-resident access at **฿500 net for an adult**, including one selected cocktail, and **฿400 net for a child under 12**, including one fruit punch. These are operator-published inclusions, not a general food-and-drink allowance.

The same page lists tubes from **฿200 net per day**. It describes a cabana as suitable for a maximum of four people or one family but does not display a current cabana price. Ask whether the day-access fee, tube and cabana are available on the intended date and whether any other deposit or condition applies.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The pool suits a non-resident who wants a clearly priced central hotel-pool day and values leisure features over lane training. The child product and children's zone make it relevant to families, subject to the hotel's supervision and access rules.

It does not suit a swimmer who needs a documented lane, measured training distance or coached set. It is also a poor fit for anyone expecting a cabana within the standard ticket: the operator presents cabanas separately and does not publish their rate. The [hotel-gym day-pass guide](/guides/hotel-gym-pattaya/) explains why a hotel pool product should not be assumed to include gym or spa entry.

## Getting there

Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya is at 429 Moo 9 on Pattaya Beach Road in Central Pattaya. Use the exact hotel map pin and ask which entrance handles non-resident pool access; the public product does not establish that every hotel entrance sells it.

No universal transfer, parking validation, public-transport route, fare or walking time is published on the pool page. The [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) provides location context without implying a fixed trip from every hotel.

## Before you go

The pool operates daily from 07:00 to 21:00, while the published non-resident access window is 09:00 to 19:00. Call +66 38 428 755 before travelling, especially on an event date or busy weekend, and ask whether advance reservation is required.

Confirm the child's age rule, what identification or registration is needed, and whether towels, lockers, changing facilities or parking are included. Ask where to collect the included drink and whether the selected cocktail has alternatives.

- No lap-lane dimensions, lane timetable or coached-swimming programme is published.
- The official page does not show a current cabana price.
- Reservation, capacity, refund, locker, towel and parking terms were not stated.
