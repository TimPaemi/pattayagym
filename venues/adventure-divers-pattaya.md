# Adventure Divers Pattaya

A Thai government sports-tourism PDF lists the operator as **โรงเรียนสอนดำน้ำ Adventure Divers**, while the live centre listing gives **391/77-78** Moo 10 for this Pattaya base.

Adventure Divers Pattaya is a current Central Pattaya dive operator with both day trips and a PADI course ladder, not only a shopfront selling excursions. The strongest current evidence is the operator’s own price page plus PADI’s live centre listing, which identifies a training pool, classroom and dive boat. The main limitation is that published hours and contact detail differ across sources, so the exact booking time still needs reconfirmation.

## What training is on offer

Adventure Divers presents two clear product groups. First are Pattaya dive trips for certified divers, snorkellers and beginners. Second is the PADI training path from Discover Scuba through recreational and professional levels. PADI’s live centre page identifies the business as a PADI 5 Star Dive Center and lists a training pool, classroom and dive boat, which is stronger operational evidence than a generic “dive shop” label alone.

This record therefore suits a reader comparing current [watersports](/category/watersports/) training operators rather than casual beach activities. If the real goal is a wind-based lesson or Na Jomtien board sport, [KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya](/gyms/kba-kiteboarding-pattaya/) is a more relevant comparison than a PADI dive centre.

## What it costs

Adventure Divers’ current price page listed passenger or snorkeller places at ฿1,500, Discover Scuba Diving at ฿4,000, a two-dive day trip with rental equipment at ฿3,300, a two-dive day trip with your own gear at ฿2,800 and an additional dive at ฿1,000 (operator price page, checked 2026-07-27). The same page states that day trips include free local pickup and drop-off, lunch, tea, coffee and drinking water.

Those prices are useful because they distinguish certified-diver and beginner products, but they are not the whole booking answer. Before paying, confirm the number and type of dives, whether full rental gear is included, whether medical paperwork or certification proof is needed, the pickup area, the boat day length and the weather or cancellation rule.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Adventure Divers suits a traveller who wants a current, source-backed PADI operator in the central corridor and values a visible beginner and certified-diver price ladder.

It is less suitable for someone who wants a spontaneous beach activity without advance contact, or for a reader who needs one clearly unified public shop-hours source before they feel comfortable booking. It is also the wrong fit if the activity sought is a non-dive adventure product; in that case the [adventure guide](/guides/adventure-pattaya/) is the better next step.

## Getting there

The current address used in the record is 391/77-78 Moo 10, Thappraya Road, Pattaya, Chonburi 20150, placing the business in the central-south Pattaya corridor rather than at a beach launch point. That distinction matters because the shop location does not prove that the boat departs from the same pin. Save the exact map and ask where the meeting point is for your booked trip.

The record currently retains +66 81 578 1199 and +66 64 283 5665 because contact details vary across public sources.

## Before you go

Tell the operator whether you are a certified diver, a first-timer or a non-diving companion. Ask which product you are buying, what documents to bring, whether pickup reaches your accommodation, whether rental gear is included and what time the boat day starts and ends. Those questions matter more than at a fixed indoor venue because a Pattaya dive day includes transport, weather and qualification variables.

If you are comparing central dive operators, keep the written inclusion list beside the price. A lower amount is not always cheaper once transfers, equipment, food, insurance or extra dives differ between shops.
