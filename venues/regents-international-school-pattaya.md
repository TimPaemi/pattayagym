# Regents International School Pattaya Sports Programme

Regents International School Pattaya runs a school PE, athletics and competitive-sport programme at its Pong campus. The operator publishes substantial facilities and a wide activity list, but this record is not a public gym or municipal sports centre. It suits families evaluating the school or a specifically announced school programme, not visitors looking for walk-in court, pool or gym access.

## What training is on offer

The school's sports and PE page says the programme is taught by specialists, including three swimming coaches, a dance specialist and former professional athletes. It lists basketball, football, swimming, athletics, rugby, cricket, volleyball, badminton, tennis, short tennis, gymnastics, golf, Thai boxing and several outdoor activities among the options offered to pupils. It also describes regular fixtures with international schools, swimming galas, house events and an annual sports day.

The facilities page is more precise about the campus infrastructure. It lists a full-size 400-metre all-weather running track, a shaded Astroturf pitch, an air-conditioned sports hall, a fitness centre and two outdoor swimming pools with spectator seating. Those are school facilities supporting the pupil programme; their presence does not create a public day-pass product.

## What it costs

No public sports-facility tariff is published because the operator presents these activities as part of the school experience. School tuition is a different product and is not treated here as a sports membership price. The source list includes the school's 2026 summer-camp brochure as evidence that Regents can announce time-limited programmes, but a past or specific camp brochure should not be read as standing public access.

Families considering an announced camp should confirm the current dates, age range, registration deadline, fee, included meals or equipment, supervision and refund terms through the exact registration channel for that camp. For school enrolment, use the admissions team rather than attempting to book individual sports facilities.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record is useful for parents comparing pupil sport within an international-school setting. The published breadth includes both participation and competition, plus IGCSE PE and Sport, Exercise and Health Science in the IB Diploma Programme. It may also be relevant when the school publishes a camp with its own eligibility and booking process.

It does not suit a tourist seeking a pool lane, football pitch, track session or weight-room day pass. No general public access policy was found. For bookable youth activities outside a school enrolment context, compare the [kids and youth directory](/category/kids-youth/) and confirm the access terms on each operator's page.

## Getting there

The school publishes its address as 33/3 Moo 1, Pong, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150 and its general number as +66 38 418 777. This is the inland Pong campus, not a central Pattaya walk-in venue. Use the school's address and confirm the campus entrance when planning an admissions visit or attending a registered event. The [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) gives context for other inland sports records, but it is not an access guide to the school.

## Before you go

Contact Regents before arriving. Ask whether the activity is limited to enrolled pupils, whether a current camp accepts external children, which campus entrance applies and what registration or identification is required. Do not infer access from the presence of a facility or from an old camp announcement.
