★ About this venue

Golf Hub Pattaya

Golf Hub is a two-branch indoor golf practice, coaching, fitting and repair business. The Soi Buakhao and East Pattaya branches share the operator site but use different addresses and contacts. Its technology and service pages are detailed; its current public price information is not, so a bay, lesson or workshop job needs a direct quote.

What training is on offer

The operator lists indoor practice with GCQuad launch monitoring and Swing Catalyst analysis, plus instruction from PGA professionals. Its lesson page separates beginner-to-advanced coaching, short-game work, on-course teaching and online lessons. A complimentary 30-minute swing analysis is offered before a lesson package is recommended; that analysis is not treated here as a full free lesson.

The East Pattaya branch additionally lists an indoor putting green and sand bunker. The repair and fitting workshop publishes regripping, reshafting, frequency matching, swing weighting, FLOing, club building, lie-and-loft adjustment and stamping, with more than 450 head-and-shaft combinations stated for fitting. Browse the Pattaya golf category to distinguish this indoor service from booking a course.

What it costs

No current bay, lesson, membership, fitting or repair tariff was published on the pages checked on 27 July 2026. The membership page describes hourly, weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-year and annual options, daily pre-booking and a free trial, but it does not display the amounts. Ask which branch and service the trial covers.

For a useful quote, name the branch, date, bay duration, number of players and required technology. For workshop work, identify the club and request labour, parts, fitting and turnaround separately. A blank directory price does not mean that launch-monitor use, a lesson or repair labour is included.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Golf Hub may suit a golfer who wants measured indoor practice, a coached swing assessment, or club fitting and repair without booking a full round. The East branch may better match short-game practice because the operator specifically lists the putting green and bunker there. The best golf courses guide is the better starting point for someone choosing an outdoor round.

It is not a substitute for a course tee time, and the published technology does not establish that every device or instructor is available at both branches at all times. A visitor who needs a firm total before travelling must obtain a branch-specific quote.

Getting there

The central branch is 350 Moo 9, B201, Soi Buakhao, with phone +66 90 913 0552. The East Pattaya branch is 311/3 Moo 6, Soi Pornprapanimit / Siam Country Club Road, with phone +66 65 978 1622. Use the matching map and contact; “Golf Hub Pattaya” alone does not identify the intended branch. The Central Pattaya area page only describes the Buakhao context.

Before you go

Both branches publish daily hours of 10:00-17:00. Those hours do not reserve a simulator bay, professional or workshop slot. Contact the chosen branch, state whether the visit is practice, a lesson, fitting or repair, and obtain the date, time, equipment access and payment terms in writing. Ask whether clubs and balls are supplied for practice and how far ahead a member or visitor may book.

What we could not verify

  • No current public price list was found for bays, lessons, memberships, fittings or repairs.
  • Rental-club availability, payment methods, cancellation terms and workshop turnaround were not published.
  • The checked pages did not prove that every listed technology, instructor or service is present at both branches every day.
