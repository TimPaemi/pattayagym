# EasyKart Pattaya Thepprasit

EasyKart operates daily from 10:00 until 01:00 on Thepprasit Road. The operator separates kids, regular, fast and two-seat products; its beginner track is listed at 350 metres. The same page gives conflicting figures for the faster track, so no exact length is repeated here.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

- kids kart, one race: ฿499; operator age guidance is 7-13 with height above 125 cm
- regular kart, one race: ฿699; three races plus shirt: ฿1,999
- fast kart, two races: ฿1,499; three races: ฿1,999
- two-seat kart, one race: ฿699; three races: ฿1,999

Closed shoes are the practical default for karting. Confirm the driver's eligibility, current wait and weather conditions before making a special trip, particularly for a child close to the operator's height threshold.
