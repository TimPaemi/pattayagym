# Thai Polo & Equestrian Club

Thai Polo & Equestrian Club occupies more than 2,000 rai in Pong, east of Pattaya. The operator lists three full-sized polo grounds, two practice fields, cross-country and eventing courses, stables and an equine clinic. Current 2025/26 fixtures and a February 2026 FEI schedule confirm active competition use.

The live Maps listing gives Tuesday-Sunday 08:00-17:00 and Monday closed. Event access, lessons, riding and facility use are not implied by those hours; this is a competition and club complex, not a walk-in riding attraction.

No public riding or membership tariff was published. Contact the club about event admission, lessons, horse access, dress rules and advance booking.
