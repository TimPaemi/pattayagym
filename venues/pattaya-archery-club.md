# Pattaya Archery Club

SoiDB's Thai field names the club พัทยา อาร์เชรี่ คลับ; its operator page describes a 2006 founding and free coaching for beginners on 4 shooting days.

Pattaya Archery Club is a non-profit range-based club in the Huai Yai corridor, not a tourist attraction selling anonymous walk-in tickets. Its own pages are unusually clear about beginner coaching being free and about the annual club-fee structure, which makes it useful for a parent, hobbyist or long-stay resident deciding whether to try archery properly.

## What training is on offer

The club’s coaching page says Pattaya Archery Club has several multilingual coaches for beginners and novices and keeps training bows and arrows for anyone wanting to try the sport. The same page states that the use of the club’s equipment and the coaching are free while being coached, and that a beginner can “come along, shoot as many arrows as you want, and see if you like shooting a bow”.

The current record frontmatter also reflects the club’s published shooting schedule: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday from **10:00 to 12:00**, with beginner coaching specifically described on Tuesday and Saturday mornings or by prior arrangement. This is a club-and-coaching model rather than a one-price leisure-attraction format.

Because the club is range-based and volunteer-led, call first. The coaching page explicitly asks people to phone ahead so a coach can attend. That is a practical difference from a staffed gym or an always-open range.

## What it costs

The operator’s current membership page, checked 2026-07-27, lists **adult annual club membership at ฿2,000**, **Thai partners of fully paid annual members and children under 16 at ฿1,000 per year**, plus a **one-time joining fee of ฿1,000**.

The same page also says all members must join Pattaya Shooting Park, which the page lists at a **discounted club-member rate of ฿2,500 per year**. Those are annual membership costs, not a casual tourist ticket.

For beginners, the coaching page states that the club’s equipment and coaching are free while being coached. If you are testing the sport for the first time, ask what, if anything, you need to pay on day one before becoming a member, and whether the shooting-park membership is only required once you continue beyond the introductory coached stage.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Pattaya Archery Club suits a beginner who wants coached first exposure without immediately buying a bow, and a longer-stay resident who is happy with a club structure rather than a commercial entertainment format. It also suits multilingual members better than many specialist hobbies because the operator pages specifically mention multilingual coaching and an international member mix.

It is a weak fit for someone wanting a late-evening casual activity, a central Pattaya location or a simple online “book now” leisure product. For a wider sport-club comparison, use the [clubs category](/category/clubs/) or the [family-friendly guide](/guides/family-friendly-pattaya/) if the question is broader than archery alone.

## Getting there

The club places itself within the Pattaya Adventure and Shooting Park setting on Sukhumvit Highway in Chonburi / Huai Yai, and this record uses the fuller shooting-park address in frontmatter. The operator’s own pages give a simpler “Sukhumvit highway, Chonburi 20150” address, so use the exact map before travelling rather than relying on that short text alone.

This is not a beach-road venue. Plan it as a Huai Yai trip and bring the exact contact number you intend to use on the day. If you are combining it with another inland activity, compare it with other [Huai Yai and East Pattaya records](/area/east-pattaya/) rather than a central-city schedule.

## Before you go

Phone ahead. The coaching page explicitly asks for a call before attending so a coach can be arranged. That is especially important if you are a first-timer, bringing a child or want a coach who speaks a particular language.

Ask whether you need closed shoes, whether children need parental supervision throughout, whether your own equipment is allowed and at what point the annual club and shooting-park memberships become compulsory. If you are choosing between this and another beginner activity, the [best for beginners guide](/guides/best-for-beginners-pattaya/) gives wider Pattaya context even though this club itself is more membership-led than most entries there.
