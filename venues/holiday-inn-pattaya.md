# Holiday Inn Pattaya

Holiday Inn Pattaya's current amenities page lists an air-conditioned fitness centre with cardio equipment, free weights, strength machines and Pattaya Bay views. The hotel also has two infinity pools and Tea Tree Spa.

The pools are listed from 06:00 to 20:00 and the spa from 11:00 to 21:00. The operator does not publish a non-resident gym or pool day pass, so this record should be treated as hotel-guest information unless the property confirms outside access directly.
