# Anytime Fitness Pattaya

This record covers **two separate Anytime Fitness clubs**, not one gym with two entrances. The first is **Again Pattaya** in Naklua. The second is the South Pattaya branch whose official operator page uses **Bukit Point Pattaya**, while the social and map wording often appears as **Bukis Point South Pattaya**. The point is the same: the branches are separate and should not be merged casually when asking for price or access.

Thai-language company information writes the brand as **เอนี่ไทม์ ฟิตเนส** and lists the Pattaya branch at 85 Soi Naklua 33.

## What training is on offer

Both branches are current 24-hour chain-gym records, but the strongest facility evidence belongs to the official **Bukit Point Pattaya** page. That page lists cardio equipment including treadmills, rowing machines, cycles, stair climbers and ellipticals; functional equipment such as kettlebells, dumbbells, battle ropes, resistance bands and TRX; and strength equipment including barbells, free weights, plate-loaded and selectorized machines and squat racks.

The same Bukit Point page also lists **classes**, **personal training**, **small-group training**, **private showers**, **private restrooms**, **parking**, **24-hour security** and **worldwide club access**. Those are useful facts - but only for the branch documented by that official page.

Again Pattaya is still confirmed as a separate live branch through its exact map identity and phone, but no equally detailed current branch-owned facility page was found for that location. A visitor who specifically needs a certain rack, machine or class should ask Again directly rather than assuming Bukit Point's published inventory is duplicated.

## What it costs

No local branch tariff was published on the official Pattaya page as of 2026-07-27. The operator instead says that **rates are subject to change according to club location and current promotions or offers**. That makes branch-specific quoting mandatory.

The official Bukit Point page does publish two useful sales routes: **1 Day Free Trial** and **Membership Inquiry**. The Bukis Point South Pattaya Facebook presence also publicly identifies the club as a 24-hour branch. Those are contact routes, not guarantees of instant anonymous entry.

Ask for the exact amount for the intended branch, term and access type, including joining fee, key or fob fee, minimum contract length, freeze or cancellation terms, visitor entry rules and personal-training charges. A blank directory price here means unpublished current local pricing, not free use.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Anytime suits an existing Anytime member who values round-the-clock access and a familiar commercial-gym model, or a traveller who wants to compare a chain option against independent Pattaya gyms. It can also suit a long-stay visitor who prefers secure member access over a simple day-pass setup.

It is a weaker fit for someone who needs a guaranteed staffed walk-in at any hour, a clearly published short-stay tourist tariff or proof that the Naklua and South Pattaya branches share identical equipment and services. The checked public sources do not support those assumptions.

## Getting there

**Again Pattaya** is published at **Again Pattaya, 2nd Floor, 85 Soi Naklua 33, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150** with phone **+66 64 167 0270**. The official Pattaya page for **Bukit Point Pattaya** uses the map-style location **WV5W 9M7 Pattaya City, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri 20150, Thailand**. Use the exact branch pin instead of the combined directory area label.

The [Naklua area guide](/area/naklua/) is useful context for Again Pattaya. The [24-hour gyms guide](/guides/24-hour-gyms-pattaya/) is the better follow-on comparison if the real question is key-fob access versus staffed entry.

## Before you go

Both branches publicly support **24-hour member access**. Treat that as door-access evidence for members, not as proof that reception or visitor registration runs all night. First visits, free trials and membership activation often depend on staff availability even at a 24-hour gym.

Before travelling, ask which branch is intended, whether the first entry must happen while staff are present, what identification is required, whether overseas reciprocal access is immediate, and whether classes need separate booking. Also confirm locker, towel, shower and parking terms branch by branch.
