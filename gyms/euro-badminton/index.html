★ About this venue

Euro Badminton Club Pattaya

What training is on offer

Euro Badminton Club remains identifiable through an exact live Maps listing under the Thai name ยูโรแบดมินตันคลับพัทยา. Maps categorises it as a sports club and publishes a phone number and weekly hours. That is enough to confirm the venue exists, but not enough to promise coaching, social games, tournaments or a particular number or type of court.

Approach it as a local court enquiry. Ask whether the available product is a court booking, coached session or open play, and specify singles or doubles, player count and ability level. The current source does not say that casual visitors will automatically be paired with players.

What it costs

No current operator tariff was found on 27 July 2026. The absence of a public price is not a reason to treat the club as closed. Call +66 81 295 2285 and ask for the price per court-hour, whether the amount covers the whole court or each player, and whether lighting, coaching, racket hire, shuttlecocks or a deposit cost extra.

Obtain a date-specific total before travelling. Do not reuse the old hire price previously attached to this record because it was not supported by a current first-hand source.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Euro may suit players who already have a partner or group and are comfortable confirming a local booking by phone. The published late closing time makes it a possible evening option, subject to court availability. Beginners may also enquire, but should not infer that a coach or beginner group is present until the club says so.

It is a weak choice for someone who needs instant online booking, a guaranteed English-language coach, a verified wheelchair route or a published all-in budget. None of those details appears on the current source. It also should not be selected for a pool, restaurant or fitness centre: the old claims about those amenities remain unverified.

Getting there

Maps provides the Plus Code VWP6+3X and the locality Pattaya, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, but no street-number address. Use the exact Euro Badminton Club Pattaya result and send that pin to the driver. Confirm the entrance and a nearby landmark by phone rather than relying on the older Chaiyaphruek-area wording.

Because a Plus Code can identify the pin more precisely than a loose district description, check that the displayed Thai club name and phone ending 2285 match before setting off.

Before you go

The current Maps schedule is 09:00-22:00 Monday to Saturday and 09:00-21:00 Sunday. These are venue hours, not a guarantee that a court, coach or social game is available throughout the window. Holiday hours may change.

Confirm the date, start and finish time, court surface, indoor or covered status, shoe rule, equipment availability, payment method and cancellation policy. Bring water and ask whether changing facilities are available instead of assuming that they are.

What we could not verify

No current owner website or maintained owner social page was found. Court count, court surface, air conditioning, coaching timetable, casual-player matching, equipment hire, accessibility, changing facilities and all prices remain unverified. The live Maps identity, phone, Plus Code and weekly opening hours are the facts this record can currently support.
