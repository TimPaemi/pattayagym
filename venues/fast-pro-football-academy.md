# FAST PRO Football Academy Pattaya

## Programme

FAST PRO publishes structured football training for children aged 4–15. Its current Horseshoe Point page describes age-grouped technical training on natural grass in small groups. The academy also retains an IP Soccer Club location listing at 64/124 Soi Thepprasit 5/1.

## Current timetable

The current venue pages did not publish the fixed weekly times previously recorded, so those times have been removed. Contact the academy with the child's age and preferred venue for the current training days and start times.

## Price and first visit

No regular fee or current free-trial offer is published on the pages checked. Ask for the complete price, what equipment the child needs and which location matches their age and level.
