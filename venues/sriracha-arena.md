# Sriracha Arena Sport Club

Sriracha Arena is a large indoor football and badminton complex at 888 Moo 6 on Sukhumvit Road in Thung Sukhla. The operator website and current Maps identity agree on the venue and connect it to separate sport desks. Maps lists +66 64 563 5665 as the main phone and daily operation from 06:00 to midnight; those are venue hours rather than a guaranteed open slot.

## What training is on offer

The official site publishes four indoor artificial-turf football pitches described as FIFA-standard and six badminton courts described as international-standard. It also markets the site for sport days and larger events and says the venue can support events of up to 1,000 people. That is event capacity, not a promise that all courts or pitches are available together on an ordinary day.

The operator also advertises youth football and badminton academy activity and league enquiries. It describes coaches with AFC or former youth-national-athlete backgrounds, but the public page does not expose a current age-by-age academy timetable. For other regional team-sport records, use the [adventure and activity directory](/category/adventure/).

Request a complete quote for the exact date, start time, duration and sport. Ask whether the amount covers the whole court or pitch, a team or each person, and whether lighting, balls, shuttlecocks, rackets, bibs, referee, deposit, tax or overtime are extra.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Sriracha Arena suits a football or badminton group that can reserve in advance and values a late closing time, covered play, showers and on-site parking. The operator says parking is available for more than 100 cars. It may also suit an organiser planning a school or company sport event, provided capacity and exclusive-use terms are confirmed.

It is a weaker fit for a solo traveller expecting an automatic pickup game, a beginner assuming academy placement or anyone choosing only by the midnight closing time. Open hours do not establish partner matching, coaching, court availability or a public walk-in price.

## Before you go

Confirm the specific pitch or court, match format, surface, footwear rule, arrival time and changing arrangements. Ask whether showers are operating, whether towels or lockers exist and which equipment can be borrowed or rented. A youth enquiry should include the player's age, experience and preferred days.

For an event, ask for the usable capacity, setup and teardown windows, parking plan, food rules, sound restrictions, deposit, cancellation terms and the person responsible on the day. Use the sport-specific contact rather than sending one generic request.
