# Rajadamnern Stadium

Rajadamnern is an operating Bangkok stadium, not a Pattaya gym. It stays in the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/) only as an explicitly labelled day-trip reference for readers comparing live stadium cards with Pattaya training options.

## What training is on offer

The current operator evidence supports nightly fight events rather than a public training product. Rajadamnern's own pages describe live Muay Thai seven nights a week. The ticket and home-page material indicate several different event formats rather than one repeated session: Monday, Tuesday and Friday event nights with 18:00 gates, a Saturday card with 18:00 gates, Sunday with 17:00 gates, plus Wednesday New Power and Thursday Petchyindee cards opening at 17:00 according to the operator snippets available during this check.

That is useful for a spectator planning a Bangkok fight night, but it does not establish visitor training, a Muay Thai class, pad rounds or open gym. Readers who want to train should use a Pattaya gym record or the [best Muay Thai gyms guide](/guides/best-muay-thai-pattaya/) instead.

## What it costs

Rajadamnern's ticket page says prices vary by event and seating tier, and it exposes several seating classes rather than one flat rate: Superior Panoramic Balcony Seat, VIP Lounge, Ringside, Club Class, 2nd Class LEO and 3rd Class. The current Maps official-ticket panel showed admission from ฿1,800 when checked 2026-07-27, but the operator's own ticket flow still needs to be checked for the exact card and seat you want. Treat Rajadamnern as a variable-ticket venue, not as a stadium with one standard Pattaya-style drop-in price.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits a Pattaya-based traveller who wants a named Bangkok fight card and is willing to choose a seat class, ticket tier and gate time in advance. It also suits readers comparing stadium viewing with actual camp training.

It does not suit anyone trying to find a same-day Pattaya training session, a beginner class or a transparent Muay Thai day pass. For that use case, stay inside Pattaya records in the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/) or compare trip length in the [1 week versus 1 month guide](/guides/train-muay-thai-pattaya-1-week-1-month/).

## Getting there

The operator and Maps address is 8 Rajadamnern Nok Road, Wat Somanas, Pom Prap Sattru Phai, Bangkok 10100. The ticket office hours published on the ticket page begin daily at 15:00, but that is not the same thing as the event gate time. Use the exact [Maps pin](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rajadamnern+Stadium+Bangkok) and the date-specific ticket flow together.

## Before you go

Check the event date, gate time and seating section before leaving Pattaya. Rajadamnern uses several recurring promotions and event brands, so the useful planning question is not whether the stadium is open but which card you are buying. If your real goal is training rather than spectating, compare a current Pattaya gym first and keep Rajadamnern as a separate evening plan.
