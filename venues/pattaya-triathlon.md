# Pattaya Triathlon

**Current status: no current Pattaya edition verified.**

The official Pattaya Triathlon site remains online, but its published event material refers to the 2019 Toyota Thailand Pattaya Triathlon Super Series. The Thailand Tri-League site did not provide a current Pattaya registration page or 2026 race date on 25 July 2026.

The former record's annual-series wording, entry price, phone, route, distances and participation figures have been removed. A separate 2026 triathlon at Pattana Sports Resort is not evidence that the Pattaya Triathlon has returned.

Do not book travel for this event until an official organiser publishes a new Pattaya race date, rules and registration link.
