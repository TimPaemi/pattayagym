# Jetts Fitness Central Si Racha

## What training is on offer

Jetts Central Si Racha is a commercial fitness club on the second floor of Central Si Racha. The operator's membership page advertises gym equipment, classes and 24/7 all-club access for members. The branch page does not publish a machine-by-machine inventory or a fixed class timetable, so check the intended training need against the live club schedule.

The visitor-pass flow allows Central Si Racha to be selected and says the pass includes the purchased home club and its classes. Class inclusion does not guarantee space in a particular session. This is a separate branch from Jetts Little Walk in Pattaya Klang; use the [fitness category](/category/fitness/) to compare Pattaya-area products.

## What it costs

After selecting **Central Si Racha (Chonburi)** on Jetts Thailand's official visitor page, prices checked on 26 July 2026 were:

- **1 day: ฿500**
- **3 days: ฿1,000**
- **7 days: ฿1,500**
- **30 days: ฿2,500**

The operator says visitor access is limited to staffed hours, with no entry fee or long-term contract, and asks for an ID card or passport. These are selected-branch visitor products, not a general monthly-membership tariff. Confirm when a pass starts and whether its days are consecutive.

Jetts describes membership as monthly and no-lock-in, with all-club access and 24/7 entry, but no stable Central Si Racha monthly total was published on the pages checked. Ask for the full first payment, access-card charge, billing and cancellation terms.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

A visitor pass may suit a trip of up to 30 days when training can happen during reception hours. Membership may better fit a longer stay or someone needing member-only round-the-clock access, but the full membership terms need a branch quote.

It does not suit a Pattaya visitor who assumes “Central” means Central Pattaya: this branch is in **Si Racha**. It also does not suit a tourist-pass holder expecting overnight access. The [24-hour gym guide](/guides/24-hour-gyms-pattaya/) explains the difference between a 24-hour facility and a product that permits entry at that time.

## Getting there

The branch is **Room 226, second floor, Central Si Racha, 8/1 Sukhumvit Road, Si Racha, Chonburi 20110**. Members have 24-hour club access. Staffed hours are **06:00–22:00 Monday–Friday** and **08:00–20:00 Saturday, Sunday and public holidays**.

Visitor-pass entry and first registration must be planned inside that staffed window. Confirm which mall entrance remains available outside retail hours, how to reach Room 226 and whether parking validation applies.

## Before you go

Use **+66 80 281 3660** for the branch or **+66 2 255 8042** as the second number published on the official club page. Arrive while staff are present with the same identity document used for registration. Before paying, check the class timetable for the intended dates and ask whether booking is required, because “all classes” does not guarantee a place in a full session.

Also confirm locker and towel arrangements, footwear rules and any induction requirement. For very early or late training, establish that the product is a 24-hour membership rather than a visitor pass.

## What we could not verify

The checked pages did not publish a stable branch membership amount, detailed equipment inventory, dated class timetable, locker or towel terms, pass start rule or mall after-hours route. The current visitor prices are verified only for Central Si Racha as selected on the official flow; they must not be copied to another branch.
