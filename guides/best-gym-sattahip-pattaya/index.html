Na Jomtien & Sattahip.

By TimPaemi · 9 min read · Updated 2026-07-30 · How we rank →

Compare the southern corridor from Na Jomtien and Ban Amphur through Bang Saray to Sattahip. The useful distinction is access: public gym, hotel-guest amenity, coached studio or book-ahead activity.

Pattaya · 218 venues source-checked · Compare gyms

If you only read one thing: “Sattahip” is not one compact gym district. This guide covers the southern corridor from Na Jomtien and Ban Amphur through Bang Saray to Sattahip. The live Sattahip area page currently contains four first-match records: Tarzan Adventure, The Best Fitness Sattahip, WeSquare and Chan Pilates. Many geographically relevant Na Jomtien records appear instead on the Jomtien area page because the directory assigns each record to the first matching area. This guide brings those two views together without pretending that every result is nearby or publicly accessible.

The corridor offers three different kinds of decision. An ordinary trainee can compare local weight rooms and studios. A hotel guest can choose a resort with documented fitness facilities. A visitor can book a destination activity such as golf, watersports, sailing or a water park. Those products should not be ranked by one headline price.

A blank public price is a reason to contact the venue, not evidence of free access. A broad Maps opening window is not a class timetable. A hotel amenity is not a public day pass unless the operator says so. Keeping those distinctions intact produces a shorter but more useful shortlist.

Ordinary gyms and studios

Better Bodies: the transparent long-stay option

Better Bodies Gym is at Somphong Condotel in Ban Amphur. Its operator describes an air-conditioned weight-training gym with staff guidance for general fitness, contest preparation and injury prevention. Prices checked on 25 July 2026 were ฿2,500 for three months or ฿4,500 for six months, paid in advance. The operator lists 10:00-20:00 Monday-Saturday and Sunday closure.

Those are multi-month memberships, not day-pass prices. Better Bodies is the strongest fit for a longer stay near Ban Amphur when weight training is the main activity and the 10:00 opening suits the schedule. A short visitor should ask whether a single visit, week or month can be purchased and obtain the complete amount before travelling.

The Best Fitness: current, but contact first

The Best Fitness Sattahip is the most direct conventional-gym record in Sattahip proper. Current owner channels and the exact Maps record support the location, +66 65 823 7903 phone and a programme spanning fitness equipment, personal training, yoga and dance. The public evidence does not provide a complete dependable weekly timetable or a stable current tariff. Maps exposed Saturday 07:00-21:00 during the last check, but one day's hours should not be expanded into a seven-day schedule.

Send the operator the intended date and product: gym access, personal training, yoga or dance. Ask for the start time, total price, membership validity, registration charge and what areas or classes the quote includes. This is a practical option only after that reply closes the schedule and price gaps.

WeSquare and Chan Pilates in Bang Saray

WeSquare is a neighbourhood gym at 88/259 Moo 9. Its exact Maps record remained active on 26 July 2026 and lists +66 96 531 9363, 07:00-21:00 Monday-Friday and 07:00-20:00 on weekends. No operator website, stable tariff or complete equipment list was available. Call for the entry product and ask whether the advertised hours are also staffed hours.

Chan Pilates is a studio at the same broad Bang Saray address area with a separate +66 96 997 4552 contact. Its live listing gives broad daily hours of 08:00-20:00 and the operator has current social activity. Treat those hours as an enquiry window, not proof that a class starts on demand. Ask whether the session is private or small-group, its duration, experience level, package conditions and exact appointment.

Resort fitness: useful for guests, not a presumed day pass

The Na Jomtien coast contains several hotels with documented fitness facilities. Their records describe hotel amenities unless an operator page publishes outside access.

  • Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach publishes a 24-hour Technogym fitness centre for registered guests, staffed 07:00-21:00. Its onsen, sauna and steam facilities have their own 07:00-21:00 schedule.
  • Pattaya Marriott Resort and Spa documents a 24-hour fitness centre and three outdoor pools for resort guests.
  • Mövenpick Siam Hotel Na Jomtien Pattaya documents a 24-hour fitness centre together with its hotel pool, spa and children's-club context.
  • Renaissance Pattaya Resort & Spa documents a fitness centre and three pools for registered guests but requires confirmation of current facility hours.

None of those records currently establishes a stable public gym day pass. A non-resident should contact the hotel with the exact date and ask whether a fitness, pool or wellness product exists, which facilities it covers, and whether advance reservation is required. A resort room can make sense when the included facilities are part of the accommodation decision; it is not a fair price comparison with a local gym membership.

Watersports and marina bookings

Clubloongchat Watersports publishes lessons and rentals from Na Jomtien. Prices checked on 25 July 2026 included ฿1,700 for a one-hour lesson, ฿300 for one hour of SUP or surf-board use, ฿500 for a one-hour beginner windsurf set and ฿1,200 for one hour of kite plus twin-tip rental. The operator requires an instructor for the first hour of a first kitesurf or sailboat rental at an additional ฿400. Skill and weather determine whether the planned product can run.

KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya operates from Blue Lagoon. The operator-wide products checked on 25 July were ฿2,200 for a one-hour kitesurf trial, ฿11,000 for a six-hour beginner course and ฿20,000 for a twelve-hour course, with equipment included. Those prices are not automatically a guaranteed Blue Lagoon booking. Confirm branch availability, wind window, lesson allocation, rescheduling and the exact Pattaya total.

Ocean Marina Jomtien is marina infrastructure and a base for sailing, not a conventional gym or walk-in beach rental. Its operator published a ฿7,500 crew entry fee per Platu regatta in the 2026 series. That does not establish public admission, berth cost, a lesson or a private charter. SailBreeze Yacht Charter is an enquiry-led operator at the marina; state whether the request is for a charter, training or a crewed trip.

Golf and family activity prices

Chee Chan Golf Resort publishes a dated public rate card valid from 1 April to 30 September 2026. Eighteen holes cost ฿4,500 on weekdays or ฿5,500 on weekends and public holidays. The compulsory 18-hole caddie-and-cart charge is another ฿1,300, producing a required ฿5,800 weekday or ฿6,800 weekend total before optional rental and any tip. Reserve the tee time and confirm the current card, dress code and rental needs.

Ramayana Water Park is a leisure day rather than fitness-centre access. The tourist page checked on 26 July 2026 listed ฿1,099 online or ฿1,199 at the entrance for guests at least 106 centimetres tall, with shorter children free under the displayed promotion. Lockers, towels and cabanas were separate products. The park publishes 11:00-18:00 opening except Wednesday closed, and height affects attraction eligibility as well as ticket treatment.

Tarzan Adventure Pattaya is one of the four direct Sattahip-filter records. Its live information supports a Na Chom Thian zipline location and daily 09:00-17:30 hours, but no current operator-published tariff. Confirm the course, participant age or size rules, equipment, supervision, weather policy and total before booking. It should not be treated as a substitute for an ordinary workout.

Choosing where to base

Ban Amphur or Na Jomtien

This northern part of the corridor offers Better Bodies, resort fitness, children's swimming, watersports and Ocean Marina records. It suits a trip whose primary booking is already in that cluster. The directory does not publish a universal transfer time between Na Jomtien and central Pattaya; route the exact venue and accommodation pins.

Bang Saray

Bang Saray provides the WeSquare and Chan Pilates choices plus access to farther-south activities. It works when a neighbourhood gym or appointment-led studio is sufficient. It is weaker for someone who wants several transparent walk-in gyms within a short walk, because both current records require direct price confirmation.

Sattahip proper

The Best Fitness is the clearest general-gym identity in the direct Sattahip set. Choose this base when its confirmed timetable and tariff fit the stay, not merely because the directory label says Sattahip. U-Tapao may be relevant to an arrival route, but the airport's presence in the district does not establish a fixed transfer time or make every southern venue close to the terminal.

A reliable booking sequence

  1. Choose the product: ordinary gym, coached class, hotel amenity, watersports lesson, golf tee time or leisure attraction.
  2. Open the exact venue map and compare it with the actual accommodation, not a broad “Pattaya” label.
  3. Send the date, participant count, ages, experience and equipment needs to the operator.
  4. Request the complete price and inclusions in writing, including compulsory charges, deposits, registration or rental.
  5. Reconfirm the schedule, weather or holiday status close to the visit and plan the return journey.

For an ordinary gym search farther north, use the Jomtien gym guide or Central Pattaya gym guide. For product-led research, continue to the diving and watersports guide, golf-course guide or family-friendly sport guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which Sattahip-area gym publishes current membership prices?

Better Bodies Gym published ฿2,500 for three months and ฿4,500 for six months, checked on 25 July 2026. It did not publish a day-pass price, so short access still requires confirmation.

Can non-guests use Na Jomtien resort gyms?

Do not assume so. The current Andaz, Pattaya Marriott, Mövenpick and Renaissance records document hotel fitness amenities but no stable public gym day pass. Ask the named resort for a dated outside-guest product before travelling.

Why do some Na Jomtien venues appear under Jomtien instead of Sattahip?

The directory assigns each venue to the first area pattern its location matches. Na Jomtien therefore matches the Jomtien page before the later Sattahip rule. This guide intentionally combines the four direct Sattahip-filter records with relevant southern records from the Jomtien filter.
