# Anytime Fitness Pattaya

This record covers **two separate Anytime Fitness clubs**, not one facility with two entrances. Anytime Fitness Again Pattaya is on the second floor of Again Pattaya at 85 Soi Naklua 33. Anytime Fitness Bukis Point South Pattaya is in the Bukis Point development farther south. The operator spells the latter “Bukit Point Pattaya,” while the exact Maps identity uses “Bukis Point south Pattaya”; the operator address and map point refer to the South Pattaya club.

The phone in this record, **+66 64 167 0270**, belongs to the Again Pattaya listing. The public Bukis Point listing did not publish a phone number during the check, so a visitor planning to use that club should start with the operator's membership or trial form rather than assuming the Again branch can resolve every local question.

## What is verified at each branch

Both exact Maps listings show **open 24 hours**. Treat that as member-access information, not proof that reception, sales staff or visitor registration operate all night. Neither listing publishes a dependable staffed-hours table.

The official Pattaya club page is specifically for **Bukit Point Pattaya**. It lists treadmills, cycles, rowing machines, elliptical trainers, stair climbers and spin bikes; functional equipment including dumbbells, kettlebells, battle ropes, a multifunctional rack, medicine balls, resistance bands and TRX; and strength equipment including barbells, free weights, plate-loaded and selectorized machines and squat racks. It also lists classes, personal training, small-group training, private showers and restrooms, parking, security and worldwide club access.

That inventory should not be copied automatically to Again Pattaya. The Again listing confirms an active 24-hour gym at the second-floor Naklua address, but no current branch-owned facility page was found. Ask the Again team whether the specific rack, machine, class or amenity you need is present before buying access.

## Trial, membership and price

The Bukit Point operator page links to a **one-day free-trial request** and a club-specific membership enquiry. A form link is an application route, not a guarantee of immediate entry or eligibility. Confirm the date, staffed arrival time, identification requirements and what the trial includes before travelling.

No location-specific membership tariff is published on the club page. The operator says rates can vary by club and current promotion. Request the complete amount for the intended branch, term and access type, including joining or activation charges, access-key or fob costs, deposit, minimum term, cancellation terms and any personal-training fee. A blank directory price does not mean free membership.

## Choosing the right Pattaya branch

Use Again Pattaya when the Naklua location is the reason for choosing Anytime Fitness, and contact its published phone directly. Use the Bukis Point map and TH-0028 operator page for the South Pattaya club. Do not navigate from this record's combined area label alone: the two pins are in different parts of Pattaya.

Existing Anytime Fitness members should ask when reciprocal or overseas club access becomes available and whether first entry must occur while staff are present. New visitors should also confirm class booking, towel and locker terms, outside-shoe rules, parking and holiday staffing. The useful verified constant is round-the-clock member access; the price, staffing and branch-specific equipment still require a current reply.
