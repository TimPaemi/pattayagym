# ATV & Buggy Adventures Pattaya

ATV & Buggy Adventures Pattaya runs guided off-road routes from a base in Pong near Lake Mabprachan.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

- Explorer route, 27 km and about two hours' riding: ฿2,990
- Adventure route, 34 km and about two hours' riding: ฿3,500
- private VIP departure: add ฿2,000 per group

The operator describes each standard option as about three hours in total. Confirm hotel-transfer coverage, passenger rules, age or licence requirements, insurance, safety equipment and the vehicle assigned before paying.

Maps lists the venue itself as 09:00-18:00 daily, while the operator's contact page lists office hours of 08:00-20:00.
