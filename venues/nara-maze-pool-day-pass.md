# Nara Maze Pool Day Pass at Terra Nara

Terra Nara explicitly sells access to its Nara Maze pool to people who are not staying at the hotel.

## Current price

The operator's offer, checked on 24 July 2026, is **฿500 net per person**. It includes:

- Pool access from 09:00 to 18:00
- Free-flow drinking water
- One smoothie
- 20% off food and non-alcoholic drinks

The hotel states that commercial photography is not allowed. Reserve by phone, Line or Facebook inbox, and confirm that the offer remains available for your date.

This is a leisure pool day pass, not a lap-swimming or coached-swim facility.
