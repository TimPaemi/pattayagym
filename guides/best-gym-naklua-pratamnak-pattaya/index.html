Naklua & Pratamnak.

By TimPaemi · 11 min read · Updated 2026-07-26 · How we rank →

Choose the side and access product first. The current filters contain 24 north-side records and 13 hill records, mixing public gyms, coached sessions, courts, clubs, hotel amenities and status warnings.

Pattaya · 215 venues hand-checked · Compare gyms

Naklua and Pratamnak are separate training bases, not one compact neighbourhood. Naklua is the north-Pattaya and Wong Amat side; Pratamnak is the hill between central Pattaya and Jomtien. The directory combines them in this guide because both suit readers choosing accommodation outside the central nightlife corridor, not because a gym on one side is automatically convenient from the other.

As checked on 26 July 2026, the Naklua filter contains 24 records across ten sport categories. The Pratamnak filter contains 13 records across six categories. Those totals are records, not recommendations or public day passes. Naklua includes three unverified identities and one non-sport attraction; Pratamnak has no closure or verification warning in the current dataset, but several of its cleanly identified records are hotel or member facilities rather than public gyms.

Start with the side, then the access model

Naklua is the stronger first look for Fairtex Muay Thai, Wong Amat-area yoga, a north-side dive shop and resort fitness tied to a hotel stay. Pratamnak offers the denser mix of commercial strength training, a priced Muay Thai gym, indoor padel, pickleball, yoga, a public exercise park and a member yacht club. The exact pin matters on both sides. A record described as North Pattaya can be near a large road rather than the beach, while Pratamnak addresses spread across the hill, Kasetsin sois, Thappraya and the Royal Cliff side.

Do not choose accommodation from an area label alone. Open the venue map, identify the building entrance and check the live route at the times you expect to train. This guide does not publish fixed drive times, taxi fares or a universal public-transport route because traffic, hill gradients and pickup location materially change the journey.

Naklua: coached training and north-side access

Fairtex Training Center Pattaya has the strongest public training evidence in the Naklua filter. The operator publishes Muay Thai and BJJ products for different experience levels. Its centre hours are Monday to Saturday from 07:30 to 20:00, while the relevant class time depends on the programme. In addition to the Muay Thai prices in the table, Fairtex listed BJJ at ฿300 for one session, ฿2,600 for ten sessions or ฿4,000 for one month, and private Muay Thai at ฿1,500.

Fairtex suits a visitor who wants a named combat product with a dated tariff and is prepared to reserve the correct session. It does not justify old claims about ring counts, a guaranteed trainer language at every class or accommodation inclusions that are not attached to the selected package. Ask whether the quote covers training only or a stay-and-train plan, which equipment is required and whether a beginner can join the chosen time.

The filter contains other Muay Thai identities, but they do not support the same price-led comparison. Pattaya Boxing World is an event and visitor product rather than an ordinary open training gym. EAGLE GYM has a current location identity but no stable public price in the record. A reader whose priority is an exact drop-in total should compare the broader Muay Thai category rather than assuming every north-side result sells the same class format.

Naklua fitness: chain membership versus hotel amenity

Anytime Fitness Pattaya is one record covering two branches. The Again Pattaya branch is in Naklua, while Bukis Point is in South Pattaya. Both publish 24-hour member access, but prices, staffed hours and first-visit arrangements vary. Ask about the exact branch, joining or key fees, minimum term, trial access and whether a membership from another country activates immediately. Round-the-clock member access does not mean a visitor can register at every hour.

SUN Fitness Pattaya likewise spans multiple locations. Its Naklua listing closes at 22:00, while the Buakhao and Pratamnak records publish later hours. No current owner tariff or cross-branch rule was accessible, so do not assume that a pass bought at one branch works at the others. The filter also contains independent and hotel fitness records whose equipment and eligibility differ.

Cape Dara Resort publishes a fitness centre with cardio and strength machines, free weights and yoga equipment, open daily from 06:00 to 22:00. Its current facility policy reserves the pools, fitness centre and playroom for registered in-house guests. Manhattan Pattaya Fitness Centre, Dusit Thani, Centara Grand Mirage and Holiday Inn are also accommodation-led records. A hotel page confirming a gym is not evidence that a non-resident can buy a pass.

Naklua yoga, water and court options

ONE-D YOGA PATTAYA is a current Wong Amat studio with a 09:00-10:30 morning window and evening periods on several days. Those Maps hours are not a class-by-class timetable, and no current operator tariff was found. Message the studio with the date, experience level and preferred style rather than arriving at the start of a general operating period.

Dive Station Pattaya is the north-side SSI dive record. On 25 July it listed Try Scuba at ฿3,000, an SSI Basic Diver day at a ฿4,000 sale price, a certified-diver day at a ฿3,000 sale price and snorkelling at ฿1,350. These are full activity products with different eligibility and inclusions; shop hours were not clearly published, so arrange the meeting point and arrival time directly.

For swimming, Nara Maze is the clearest published non-resident product. Its ฿500 net pass includes pool access from 09:00 to 18:00, drinking water, one smoothie and a food-and-drink discount. Wong Amat Beach is a public beach with no verified supervised hours or permanent safe-swim zone. The generic Naklua Public Swimming Pool entry remains unverified and should not be used as directions.

Naklua 16 Badminton provides a current named court alternative off Soi Photisan. Its Maps listing was checked on 26 July and showed 12:00-23:00 Monday to Friday and Sunday, with Saturday closed. No current court price, phone, court count or equipment policy was published; ask through the operator page whether the quote is per court or player.

Pratamnak: priced Muay Thai and public strength options

Battle & Conquer is the most transparent Pratamnak combat-and-fitness record. It publishes group Muay Thai daily from 08:00 to 10:00 and 16:00 to 18:00. The ฿500 single group session and weekly or monthly Muay Thai packages include access to the boxing room, weights gym, sauna and cold plunge. Gym-only access is separately priced at ฿200 for one day, ฿600 for one week and ฿1,300 for one month. Compare the named product rather than assuming the lowest amount covers coaching and recovery facilities.

Muscle Factory Pattaya is a current independent fitness centre on Pratamnak Road, open daily from 07:00 to 22:00. Its live public evidence supports strength equipment and group exercise, but not the former claims about building size, specialist platforms, air conditioning or old membership amounts. Human Strong Gym Pattaya publishes free weights, resistance machines, cardio, Muay Thai and boxing, with weekday hours of 07:00-22:00 and weekend hours of 08:00-22:00. Neither has a stable current public tariff in the dataset. Contact the gym when a particular machine, coached format or short-term pass is essential.

Pratumnak Fitness Park is a different product again. Pattaya City material records a walking and running surface, multipurpose court and 12 outdoor exercise stations at Chaloem Phrakiat Park. No current municipal tariff or formal daily schedule is published. The site suits self-directed outdoor training; it does not promise air conditioning, lockers, showers, a verified barbell setup or staffed supervision.

Pratamnak racquets and yoga

Fitz Club is the most complete outside-guest sports-club product. The ฿800 adult day pass covers the gym, pool, sauna and steam rooms but excludes tennis and squash. The club separately publishes seven tennis courts, two squash courts, a multipurpose court, table tennis and coaching. Gym, class, sauna and steam hours are 07:00-21:00; courts and pool close at 20:00. Reserve court hire or coaching rather than assuming the day pass covers it.

Play Padel is the clear price-led indoor-court option. Its ฿1,200 tariff is per court-hour, not per person, and private coaching begins at ฿600 per hour. New players booking in advance may need to prepay through the Padelmates flow; card payment in the app adds ฿100. PR Pattaya Pickleball is the current identity for the former Pickleball Pattaya location. Maps shows morning and late-afternoon playing windows, but no stable current tariff. Ask about open play, partner matching, equipment and whether a slot must be reserved.

For yoga, Ashtanga Yoga Pattaya offers the clearest dated tariff and publishes practice windows rather than all-day reception. Balance Yoga Studio listed a group yoga class at ฿500 and a group sound-healing session at ฿1,000 on 25 July. Its public timetable still showed February 2026 when checked, so book the specific class and confirm access to the condominium building before arrival.

Sailing and resort records on Pratamnak

Royal Varuna Yacht Club is a member sailing club and RYA-accredited training centre. Its 2026 programme supports youth and adult sailing, private tuition, powerboat and instructor training, but the public pages checked did not publish current membership or course amounts. Training can be enquired about without treating the club as a walk-in beach rental. Confirm whether a course includes use of the pool, lawn, beachfront, restaurant or other member facilities.

Cross Pattaya Pratamnak publishes a Cloud Club with treadmills, exercise bicycles and a yoga area from 06:00 to 22:00, plus saltwater pools from 07:00 to 20:00. The current site presents these as hotel activities and does not publish a non-resident day pass. InterContinental Pattaya likewise confirms a hotel fitness centre, three leisure pools and a spa but no public gym ticket or current fitness-centre hours. Treat both as accommodation amenities unless the hotel gives a written non-resident offer for the chosen date.

Decision paths that avoid false comparisons

  • One coached Muay Thai session: compare Fairtex at ฿800 with Battle & Conquer at ฿500, then check class time, inclusions, location and equipment.
  • One ordinary workout: Battle & Conquer has a published ฿200 gym day. Fitz is a broader ฿800 facilities pass. Other commercial gyms require a current quote.
  • Hotel-based training: choose the hotel first and verify guest eligibility, fitness hours and essential equipment. Do not import a public-pass assumption from another resort.
  • Racquet session: Play Padel publishes a court price; Fitz separates its facilities pass from court charges; Naklua 16 and PR Pickleball require contact.
  • Swimming: Nara Maze is a verified leisure pass, Wong Amat is an uncontrolled public beach and the generic Naklua pool record is unverified.
  • Long stay: request a written monthly total, registration or key fees, cancellation terms, branch access and staffed arrival hours before choosing accommodation.

Use the comparison tool to keep access model, hours and dated price attached to each record. For alternatives outside these two districts, compare the Central Pattaya guide and the Jomtien guide. The right result is the product you can actually access at the exact pin, not the venue with the longest feature list.

FAQ

Which side is better for Muay Thai?

Naklua has Fairtex, with a broad published Muay Thai and BJJ menu and a north-Pattaya location. Pratamnak has Battle & Conquer, with twice-daily group Muay Thai plus separately priced gym and recovery access. Compare the exact class, total, inclusions and route rather than choosing on area reputation.

Can non-hotel guests use the resort gyms?

Only when the operator publishes outside access or confirms it for the chosen date. Fitz explicitly sells an outside-guest facilities pass. Cape Dara reserves its listed facilities for registered in-house guests, while Cross and InterContinental do not publish a current non-resident gym pass.

What should I confirm before paying?

Confirm the exact product, date, start time, full price, eligibility, registration or key fee, equipment, booking and cancellation terms, staffed arrival window and exact entrance. For hotel records, ask whether access is included in the room. For court or water activities, add weather, equipment hire and meeting-point questions.
