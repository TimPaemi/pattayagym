★ About this venue

Pattaya Cricket Club

A Thai sports report writes the team name as พัทยา คริกเก็ต คลับ and describes its participation in the Koh Chang event; the current record remains anchored to the club's own 2026 fixtures.

Pattaya Cricket Club is an active playing club whose current league record names Horseshoe Point as its home ground. It welcomes playing and social enquiries, but it does not publish a walk-in session or current membership price. Contact the club before travelling because fixtures can use other grounds and training is not a daily attraction.

What training is on offer

The club says it was formed in January 2010 and currently describes a first XI called the Stallions, an over-45 social side called the Thoroughbreds and a junior programme open across age groups. It says women are welcome depending on ability and invites both players and social participants.

The club's batting-nets page describes fully enclosed wire-mesh nets with floodlights, seating, an equipment storeroom and a changing room. That page says other teams can book the nets for a fee through Outback, but it does not display a current booking price and may not describe every present-day arrangement. Reconfirm before relying on it.

What it costs

No current membership fee, junior fee, practice price, match contribution or equipment charge was published on the club and league pages checked on 26 July 2026. The batting-nets page mentions fee-based outside-team use without naming the amount.

Ask the club for the correct playing or social membership product, registration period, match fees, training or net fees, equipment expectations and payment terms. A blank public tariff does not imply free participation.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The published team structure makes the club relevant to an adult player seeking league or over-45 social cricket, a junior whose family can confirm the current programme, or a supporter wanting club contact. Current Bangkok Cricket League records support active competition in the 2025/26 season.

It is a poor fit for a traveller expecting an on-demand tourist lesson, guaranteed equipment rental or a match at Horseshoe Point on any chosen day. Use the Pattaya clubs category for other organised activities or the East Pattaya area for nearby venue records.

Getting there

The league identifies Horseshoe Point as Pattaya Cricket Club's home ground. Maps confirms a current cricket-ground identity at Horseshoe Point in Pong, using Plus Code WX4F+CJ4, but the listing is unclaimed and publishes no phone or official website.

Use the exact Maps pin and the fixture's named ground. The current league list includes away fixtures, so the club's home-ground identity does not establish where every match is played. No fixed fare, journey time or club transport was published.

Before you go

Consult both the club and the exact league fixture before attending. Ask whether the enquiry concerns senior league cricket, over-45 social cricket, juniors, nets or social membership; then confirm the date, ground, arrival time, eligibility, clothing and equipment.

The club contact page provides a form and pattayacricketclub@gmail.com but no current public phone. Do not use the Maps ground listing as a substitute for club confirmation.

What we could not verify

  • No current membership, training, junior or net tariff was published.
  • A recurring training timetable and current junior age bands were not available.
  • Equipment loan, coaching provision, changing-room access and instruction language were not established.
  • The live Maps ground profile is unclaimed, and fixture locations can differ from the home ground.
