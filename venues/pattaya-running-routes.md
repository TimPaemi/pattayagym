# Pattaya Public Running Locations

This is a route-planning entry, not an organised club or a claim that every path forms a measured running course.

Useful starting points confirmed on current Maps listings include **Pattaya Beach**, **Jomtien Beach**, **Mabprachan Reservoir**, **Nong Prue Public Park** and **Pratamnak Park**. They cover beachfront, lakeside and park settings, but surfaces, lighting, construction and access conditions can change.

## Before running

Inspect the route in daylight, follow local signs and keep clear of traffic, cyclists and busy promenade areas. Heat and humidity are material risks in Pattaya; carry water and avoid assuming that a mapped path has lighting, toilets, drinking water or uninterrupted public access. There is no organiser to contact and no fixed timetable.
