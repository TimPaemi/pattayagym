# Siam Country Club Pattaya

Siam Country Club's current site lists four Pattaya courses: the Old Course, Plantation, Waterside and Rolling Hills. The official course pages give opening years of 1971, 2008, 2014 and 2020 respectively.

## Booking

The official booking page is a tee-time enquiry rather than a stable public tariff. No current green fee or caddie fee is recorded here because the club did not publish one on the first-hand pages checked on 25 July 2026.

Choose the course before submitting an enquiry and confirm the complete player price, caddie and cart requirements, club rental, dress code and arrival time directly with the club.
