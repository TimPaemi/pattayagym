# Clubloongchat Watersports

Thai Maps expands the 23/137 Soi Na Jomtien 14 identity as **คลับลุงชัช Clubloongchat: Wing Foiling / Windsurfing Club / Kitesurfing School Pattaya**.

## What is offered

Clubloongchat operates from Na Jomtien and publishes lessons and equipment rental for kitesurfing, wingfoiling, windsurfing, sailing and stand-up paddleboarding. Conditions govern whether a session can run, so contact the club before travelling.

## Current prices

Prices below were published by the operator and checked on 25 July 2026:

- One-hour lesson: ฿1,700
- Wing plus SUP: ฿700 for one hour
- Wing plus foil: ฿1,000 for one hour
- Beginner windsurf set: ฿500 for one hour or ฿1,100 for three hours
- SUP or surf board: ฿300 for one hour
- Kite plus twin-tip board: ฿1,200 for one hour or ฿3,000 for three hours
- Kite plus foil: ฿1,500 for one hour
- Sailboat: ฿1,200 for one hour or ฿3,000 for three hours

For a first kitesurf or sailboat rental, the operator requires an instructor for the first hour at an additional ฿400. The lesson and rental pages are the price sources.

## First visit

The club publishes daily opening hours of 10:00–18:00. Book ahead and tell the club your experience level. Independent kitesurf rental requires suitable experience or certification. Bring sun protection and allow for a weather-related change or cancellation.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The published lesson and rental menu gives beginners a clear route through an instructor-led lesson. Independent kitesurf rental is explicitly experience-dependent. The sources do not establish a children’s programme, a guaranteed wind window, safety-boat cover, disability provision or a fixed group lesson size.
