Jomtien's clubs result mixes one genuine informal playing group, one public multi-sport complex, a self-directed running reference and two Na Jomtien attractions. It is not evidence of a weekend cycling club calendar. Pattaya Jomtien Beach Volleyball Courts is the clearest social-sport choice; Eastern National Sports Center is the contact-first public facility. Pattaya Public Running Locations is planning information, while Khao Chi Chan and Nong Nooch Thai Cultural Show are not participatory clubs. The beach-volleyball courts near the Sea Rescue area at Jomtien and Dongtan offer the most direct join-in model. Beach Volleyball Thailand identifies three floodlit courts, mixed-level doubles and fast sixes generally starting around 16:00, and free play with voluntary equipment donations. Those details were checked 27 July 2026, but they are not a reservation or attendance guarantee. Weather, turnout and court use can change the format. Bring water, use the exact court pin and ask the players how rotation and donations work; no organiser phone, coaching menu, equipment inventory, locker or shower provision is published. Eastern National Sports Center on Chaiyapruek 2 is the structured public alternative. DASTA describes athlete camps, regional competition and general recreation, lists daily 09:00-20:00 hours and states that general public use is available without an ordinary admission charge. Free general access does not make every hall, court or field unreserved or free for a team. Call +66 84 932 9997 about the named sport, facility, event restrictions, booking, equipment and any separate charge before crossing Sukhumvit and travelling inland. The running-locations record does not supply a session. It identifies Jomtien Beach among several possible public starting points, but publishes no measured route, pace group, lighting guarantee, bag storage, water station or leader. Use it only for self-directed planning after inspecting the chosen segment. Anyone seeking a hosted run or ride should start with the running and cycling guide; this page has no verified Jomtien cycling meetup to promise. Khao Chi Chan and Nong Nooch need explicit separation from sport. Khao Chi Chan is a respectful cultural and light-walking stop open 06:00-18:00, with no verified training, club, admission amount or fitness service. Nong Nooch sells garden and show packages. For a visit selected on 26 July 2026, its operator displayed foreign-tourist packages from THB 600 adult / THB 400 child for admission to THB 1,000 / THB 750 for the admission, cultural show, elephant show and sightseeing-bus combination. The Thai cultural show runs at 10:30, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30 and contains staged martial-arts sequences; it is spectator entertainment, not martial-arts instruction. For a Jomtien stay, choose the beach courts for informal social volleyball or contact the sports center for a specific public-facility request. Plan a run independently only after checking the route. Treat the two southern attractions as cultural visits, not substitutes for a club session. This is a varied five-record page with only two participatory sport leads, and that coverage limit should govern any comparison.
