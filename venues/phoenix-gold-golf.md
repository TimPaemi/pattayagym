# Phoenix Gold Golf & Country Club

Phoenix Gold operates three nine-hole loops in Huai Yai: Mountain, Lakes and Ocean. The club's current site publishes daily 06:00-18:00 opening, its full address and two booking numbers. TPR Golf Academy also continues to list instruction at the Phoenix Gold driving range.

## Price and first visit

The club did not expose a stable public playing tariff on the pages checked. Ask reservations for the green fee, mandatory caddie and cart charges, rental-club price, dress code and the nine combination in play on your date. Do not rely on older reseller prices.
