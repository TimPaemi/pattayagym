# Sityodtong Muay Thai Camp

Thai Maps signposts the camp as **ค่ายมวยศิษย์ยอดธง Sityodtong Muaythai Camp**, matching the record whose published training periods begin at 08:00 and 15:00.

Sityodtong is the current Bang Lamung home of the Muay Thai camp established by Kru Yodtong Senanan in 1960. The operator explicitly welcomes beginner, intermediate and advanced students. Its timetable and one pad-work price are public, but the full visitor and residential tariff still requires direct contact.

## What training is on offer

The official site describes Muay Thai training for adults and children, including private lessons, trainer-led pad work and small-group work. It also presents a four-week fight-camp route with housing. That range can serve a first session, a technique-focused private booking or a longer training block, but the public pages do not map every product to a daily class.

The camp publishes two training periods from **08:00 to 10:00** and **15:00 to 17:30**, Monday to Saturday, with Sunday closed. Those periods establish when training is scheduled; they do not prove that a particular trainer, children’s group or private slot is available without booking.

## What it costs

The operator’s exact training-price page displayed **THB 400 for one individual pad-work session with a trainer** when checked on **26 July 2026**. That is one named session product. It should not be presented as the price for a full group class, both daily periods, private technical coaching or a fight-camp package.

No complete current tariff for weekly training, monthly training, children’s sessions, private lessons or the four-week residential option was readable in the checked public content. Ask for the exact product, duration, training frequency, housing category, taxes and payment method in writing before sending money.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Sityodtong suits someone who values a long-established camp identity and wants to train at a place whose operator explicitly accepts different experience levels. The THB 400 pad-work product also gives a visitor one concrete question to start a booking conversation. For broader product comparison, use the [best Muay Thai guide](/guides/best-muay-thai-pattaya/).

It is a weaker fit for a traveller who will only book from a complete online package grid or who needs guaranteed English explanation from a named trainer. The [Muay Thai directory](/category/muay-thai/) includes camps with more extensive published tariffs and residential inclusions.

## Getting there

The operator site and current Maps identity place the camp at 36 Pattaya City in Bang Lamung and publish the same +66 86 333 9693 telephone. It is an inland Nong Prue record, not a Beach Road gym. Use the exact pin and confirm the entrance before travelling.

The [East Pattaya index](/area/east-pattaya/) gives area context. No fixed journey time, fare, parking instruction or public-transport route was published.

## Before you go

Contact the camp with the intended date, experience level, preferred morning or afternoon period and whether the request is for pad work, group training, private coaching, a child or a residential block. Ask which session to attend and when a first-time visitor should arrive.

Confirm glove and hand-wrap arrangements, sparring expectations, trainer language, drinking water, changing facilities and accepted payment. Do not assume the THB 400 pad-work product includes general training or equipment.
