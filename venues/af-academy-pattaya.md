# AF Academy Pattaya

AF Academy offers football training for ages 3-17 and says it accepts beginners as well as experienced children. It suits a family willing to confirm the child's age group, exact pitch and product directly. The academy publishes short-pack prices, but its Naklua company address is not presented as the training ground.

## What training is on offer

The operator divides students into Preparatory ages 3-6, Junior ages 7-9, Intermediate ages 10-14 and Senior ages 14 and older. Preparatory work covers ball control, physical ability and team interaction; Junior introduces kicking and positions; Intermediate develops positioning and club-programme progression; Senior gives more attention to playing time and tactics.

The operator describes sessions moving through warm-up and stretching, technical drills such as mechanics, shooting and passing, then game time. Its current IP Soccer Club timetable lists Preparatory and Junior on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 17:00-18:00, followed by Intermediate and Senior from 18:00-19:00. Confirm the selected date because this is one named ground, not a city-wide timetable.

## What it costs

The exact English operator page, checked 2026-07-26, lists a free first trial. A single 60-minute session is ฿600 with no rescheduling. Eight 60-minute sessions cost ฿3,000 and are presented as two sessions per week; the page says this product includes friendly matches and tournaments and allows a maximum of two reschedules. Twelve 60-minute sessions cost ฿3,600 and are presented as three sessions per week.

Ask which terms apply to each product. The eight-session inclusions and reschedule limit should not be copied automatically to the single or twelve-session purchase. Request validity, missed-session, holiday, refund, tournament, registration and uniform costs in writing.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

AF Academy suits a family seeking age-banded coaching, a free-trial route and published short packs. It is less suitable for someone who needs one fixed ground, a complete season calendar or a Maps-verified entrance before contacting the operator. Casual pitch hire is a different product.

Compare other age-specific programmes in the [kids and youth directory](/category/kids-youth/) and use the [youth-sport guide](/guides/kids-youth-sport-pattaya/) for registration and guardian questions.

## Before you go

Send the child's age, football experience, preferred language, chosen product and available days. Ask for the assigned group, coach, start and finish, surface, footwear, entrance, guardian policy and drop-off and collection point.

The operator names Alex Khairulin and Apichai Boonkomon and publishes biographies, but that does not establish who leads every session. It also says students can use changing rooms and showers; confirm which ground has those facilities. Contact the academy on +66 96 078 8308, info@afacademy.pro, WhatsApp or Telegram before the first visit.
