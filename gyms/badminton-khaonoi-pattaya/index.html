★ About this venue

Badminton Kho-Noi Pattaya

The Thai directory's linked local identity is สนามแบดมินตันเขาน้อยพัทยา, while its contact listing gives 212/114 Moo 5.

Badminton Kho-Noi is a dedicated 12-court badminton venue on Soi Boonsampan in East Pattaya. It suits groups that need a court outside normal commercial-gym hours: the owner page lists 24-hour opening. The limitation is price transparency, because no current court tariff is published online.

What training is on offer

The venue's 12 April 2026 owner post divides the site into seven courts in the older building and five in the newer building. The same post describes 8 mm rubber flooring across all 12 courts. This is a badminton facility rather than a general racquet club; the owner page does not advertise tennis, padel or squash.

No current coaching timetable, organised beginner session or open-play schedule is published. Players looking specifically for coaching or a social group should compare the wider Pattaya racquet directory rather than assuming a coach or playing partner will be available on arrival.

What it costs

No current public court rate was found on the owner page or live Maps listing when checked on 26 July 2026. That means there is no dependable online figure for hourly court hire, a per-player charge or a late-night surcharge.

When calling, ask for the price for the exact start time and duration, whether payment is per court or per person, and whether racket hire, shuttlecocks or a deposit cost extra. A price quoted without those details is not enough to compare this venue with the options in the Pattaya badminton guide.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The published 24-hour schedule and 12-court capacity make this a practical fit for an existing group that wants early-morning, daytime or overnight play in East Pattaya. The owner also publishes toilets with showers and parking for up to 70 cars, which helps groups arriving together.

It is a weaker fit for a solo visitor who needs a guaranteed partner, an English-speaking coach or confirmed equipment rental: none of those services is published. Travellers staying on the beach side should also compare venues nearer their accommodation before committing to the inland trip; the East Pattaya area page explains the wider location trade-off.

Getting there

The owner page and Maps listing place the courts at 212/114 Moo 5, Soi Boonsampan 1-3, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung. The owner page uses the Khao Noi name and publishes +66 83 871 8780 for enquiries and court reservations.

Parking for up to 70 cars is an operator claim from April 2026. No current source says that the venue sits on a baht-bus route, so do not plan around public transport without checking separately.

Before you go

Phone to reserve a court even though the venue lists 24-hour opening; round-the-clock access does not prove that a suitable court is free at the time you want. Confirm the building and court number, because the 12 courts are split between old and new buildings.

Bring playing shoes and your preferred racket and shuttlecocks unless the venue confirms rental or sales. Showers are published, but towels, lockers and changing-room supplies are not. The owner page does not state the language used for bookings or coaching.

What we could not verify

  • No current court-hire or per-player rate is published as of 2026-07-26.
  • Racket hire, shuttlecock sales and locker or towel provision are not stated.
  • No coaching, beginner-session or open-play timetable is published.
  • No public-transport or baht-bus access information is published.
