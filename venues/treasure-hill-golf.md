## What training is on offer

The operator describes an 18-hole course with a total par of 72. Individual hole pages publish several tee distances, but this record does not combine those numbers into an unverified total yardage. Thailand LPGA event material for the 2025 SINGHA-NSDF Chonburi Ladies Championship names Treasure Hill as the competition venue, which supports current course use beyond the directory listing.

The operator also publishes separate pages for a golf cart, pro shop, restaurant, ofuro and locker room. The pro-shop page says it sells golf balls, gloves, towels, branded polos and other golf items. That confirms retail categories, not guaranteed stock or rental clubs. For a broader course comparison, use the [Pattaya golf guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/).

## What it costs

No current first-hand green-fee, caddie-fee or cart-fee table was available in readable operator content on 27 July 2026. Blank pricing here means unknown, not free or inexpensive. Ask for the date-specific total for 18 holes, itemised into green fee, caddie and cart; also ask whether a cart is compulsory, whether a caddie tip is separate and whether any weekday, resident or group condition applies.

Do not build a budget from an aggregator promotion without confirming it with the club. The [golf directory](/category/golf/) includes venues with different levels of published price evidence.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Treasure Hill suits a golfer willing to travel to Ban Bueng and confirm a conventional 18-hole booking by phone or Line. The current tournament evidence may matter to players who prefer a course used for organised competition. It is a weaker fit for a visitor who needs an instantly bookable, fully itemised public tariff before choosing transport.

Someone combining golf with a resort stay or a 50-metre pool should compare [Pattana Sports Resort](/gyms/pattana-sports-resort/), whose product mix is different. Neither record should be chosen from price symbols alone.

## Before you go

Maps lists **06:00-18:00 daily**, checked 27 July 2026. Those are venue hours, not proof of a vacant tee time or a final tee-off at 18:00. Contact the club with the playing date, number of golfers, preferred start window and handicap information if requested.

Confirm payment method, cancellation terms, club and shoe rental, locker access, dress rules, caddie and cart arrangements, and the latest time an 18-hole round may start. If buying supplies on site, confirm the needed item and size with the pro shop first.
