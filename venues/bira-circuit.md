# Bira Circuit

Bira Circuit is a 2.41-kilometre road-racing circuit in Pong with shared motorcycle and car sessions, private circuit hire and operator-arranged driving activities. It is not a public go-kart attraction or an unscheduled driving venue: access depends on the calendar, the selected product and an accepted reservation.

## What training is on offer

The operator describes the main circuit as a 2.41-kilometre layout combining hills with a compact street-circuit character. Its experience page advertises track days for car clubs and a driving academy whose courses can be designed to the customer's needs. The track-information page also presents the circuit for vehicle testing, product events and team activities.

Those are operator products, not proof that a public session runs every day. The reservation page carries a calendar and asks users to select the circuit and date. A visitor seeking ordinary recreational karting should compare the [Pattaya adventure directory](/category/adventure/) rather than assuming Bira's main circuit works like a rental-kart track.

## What it costs

The official track-hire page, checked **26 July 2026**, lists a shared motorcycle session from **09:00 to 12:00 for ฿1,800** and a shared car session from **13:00 to 17:00 for ฿3,500**. Whole-circuit hire is listed at **฿130,000 on a weekday** or **฿150,000 on a weekend or holiday**.

Those amounts identify the product and time shown by the operator; they do not disclose vehicle preparation, transport, fuel, tyres, insurance, damage liability, timing, medical cover or equipment costs. The same page lists property-rental items, but they are not circuit-admission prices and are not used as visitor recommendations.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Bira suits an experienced driver, rider, club or organiser who has an eligible vehicle and can complete the circuit's reservation and safety checks. The driving-academy enquiry may suit someone seeking structured instruction, but the operator does not publish a standard academy syllabus or tariff on the checked page.

It does not suit a traveller expecting to arrive, rent an ordinary road car and drive immediately. Families seeking a simple timed kart session should start with the [Pattaya adventure guide](/guides/adventure-pattaya/). People based near Pong can use the [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) for broader location context.

## Getting there

The operator contact page and current Maps identity agree on **111 Moo 5, Pong, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150** and phone **+66 85 110 1000**. Use the exact circuit pin; the venue is inland and should not be confused with a central Pattaya attraction.

No operator-published public-transport route, transfer fare or journey time is recorded. Ask whether the booked activity uses the main circuit, a kart area or another meeting point within the property, and request arrival and paddock instructions with the reservation.

## Before you go

Use the official reservation form or call before travelling. Send the date, vehicle type, licence or experience details, intended product and number of participants. Ask which protective equipment and clothing are mandatory, what technical inspection applies, when check-in closes and whether passengers or spectators are allowed.

Request written terms covering weather, cancellation, refunds, incident liability, insurance, towing and recovery. A time on the public rate page is a session window, not confirmation that the chosen date is running or that a particular vehicle is eligible.
