# Pattaya Archery Club

Pattaya Archery Club is a non-profit club founded in 2006 and based within the Pattaya Adventure and Shooting Park complex. Its operator site welcomes beginners through experienced archers and publishes shooting from 10:00 to noon on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday.

Beginners can try coaching and the club's training equipment without charge on Tuesday or Saturday mornings, or by prior arrangement. Call first so a coach can attend.

## Current membership cost

As checked on 25 July 2026, the operator publishes:

- Adult club membership: **฿2,000 per year**
- Thai partner of a paid annual member or child under 16: **฿1,000 per year**
- One-time club joining fee: **฿1,000**
- Required Pattaya Shooting Park membership at the discounted club rate: **฿2,500 per year**

These fees come directly from the club's membership page. The former accommodation, pool, tennis and fishing claims have been removed because the club does not present them as archery-club amenities.
