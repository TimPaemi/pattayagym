# Royal Varuna Yacht Club

Royal Varuna is an established member sailing club on the Pratamnak coast. Its 2026 training programme includes RYA youth and adult sailing courses, private tuition, Powerboat Level 2 and instructor training. The club's current calendar also confirms active 2026 racing and events.

## Access and facilities

Membership is open to all nationalities and carries an entrance fee plus a monthly subscription; the club does not publish the current amounts on the pages checked. Training enquiries can be made through the club even if you are not yet a member.

The member facilities page lists a swimming pool, lawn, private beachfront, restaurant and bar. Confirm whether a course booking includes access beyond the scheduled training period.
