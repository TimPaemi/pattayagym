Active training options outside this one-record category

Castra Gym is the clearest short-visit option with a current operator-published timetable and tariff. Its fight-club page lists BJJ on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 19:00 to 20:00. Prices checked on 25 July 2026 were ฿300 for one group class, ฿2,500 for ten group classes valid for two months and ฿600 for a private class; gym access is included. The operator says all levels, ages and genders are welcome, but a traveller should still confirm the chosen date and whether the session is gi or no-gi.

Rage Fight Academy identifies itself as a boxing, BJJ, MMA and Muay Thai gym and describes a dedicated BJJ/MMA mat zone within its South Pattaya camp. No stable current public BJJ fee table or dependable class-by-class schedule was available on the operator pages checked on 25 July 2026. Rage is therefore a contact-first alternative rather than a price comparison: send experience level, preferred date and gi or no-gi preference before travelling.

Kombat Group Thailand is a residential Huai Yai option, not a simple drop-in academy. Its live pricing page checked on 26 July 2026 listed a standard-room BJJ Academy package from ฿17,900 for one week. That package includes training, equipment use, accommodation, three meals a day, pool access and specified extras. A separate Bronze BJJ package started at ฿10,900 for one week with a fan room and Thai-style bathroom, three Thai meals daily and gym use, but excluded the pool and other resort facilities. The operator says its displayed prices exclude 7% VAT and a 3% credit-card fee.

How to choose without a false ranking

Choose Castra when a published evening group schedule and per-class price matter most. Consider Rage when a multi-discipline camp near Thappraya is more important than a public tariff. Consider Kombat only when accommodation and meals are part of the purchase. Rusich Club also publishes judo, sambo, jiu-jitsu and grappling sessions, but its programme is not presented as a standalone Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy; do not assume that every wrestling or grappling class follows BJJ rules.

Before paying anywhere, ask for the exact discipline, coach, start time, class duration, gi or no-gi format, belt and experience mix, rolling policy, equipment requirement, language and total price including registration or taxes. 03 Common questions

BJJ / Grappling in Pattaya — FAQ.

Is there a BJJ (Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu) gym in Pattaya?

Yes. Pattaya has a dedicated Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy, listed in this category, training both gi and no-gi grappling. It is the city main home for BJJ.

Can beginners train BJJ in Pattaya?

Yes. The academy welcomes complete beginners alongside experienced grapplers, with fundamentals classes to learn the basics safely. No experience is needed to start. Contact the academy about beginner sessions and whether a gi is provided or required.

Can visitors drop in to train BJJ?

Yes. Visiting and travelling grapplers are welcome to drop in for sessions or train over a longer stay. It is good practice to message the academy ahead to confirm class times and mat-fee or package options.
