# Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach

The 345 Moo 3 Na Jomtien resort appears in Thai Maps as **โรงแรมแอนดาซ พัทยา จอมเทียน บีช - อะ คอนเซ็ป บาย ไฮแอท**, alongside its documented 24-hour fitness centre.

Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach is a Na Jomtien resort with a 24-hour Technogym fitness centre, separate movement space, three pools and a changing weekly activity programme. Hyatt describes the experiences as exclusive to registered guests. No public gym or pool pass is currently documented.

## What training is on offer

The hotel’s experience page lists Technogym cardio and strength equipment and a separate room for yoga and stretching. It states that the fitness centre is open 24 hours and staffed from **07:00 to 21:00**. The operator does not publish a machine-by-machine inventory, free-weight range or floor area.

The current resort-experiences PDF includes a rotating mix of beginner yoga, mat Pilates, circuit training, bodyweight work, aqua boxing, beach boot camp, family stretching and basic Thai boxing. It also lists walking meditation, tai chi, foam-roller work and core sessions on selected days. The same programme identifies paddleboarding and kayaking from **08:00 to 18:00 daily**, subject to the resort’s arrangements.

## What it costs

No standalone public fitness day pass, pool ticket or outside-visitor wellness tariff was found on the Hyatt pages checked on **26 July 2026**. The hotel describes the activity programme as guest-exclusive.

The PDF marks basic Thai boxing as chargeable but does not display the amount. That means “hotel guest” should not be read as “every class included.” Ask the Recreation Team which activities are complimentary, which carry a fee, and whether personal training, equipment or cancellation has a separate charge.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Andaz suits a registered guest who wants several training formats without leaving the resort and who is comfortable checking a weekly programme. The separate yoga/stretching room and mix of low-impact and higher-effort sessions give a family or mixed-interest group more choices than a weights-only room. The [family-friendly guide](/guides/family-friendly-pattaya/) compares other family activity models.

It is not a documented public day-pass option. It is also a poor match for someone who needs a guaranteed combat class, a public monthly membership or reserved lap lanes. The [Pattaya fitness category](/category/fitness/) lists standalone gyms and other hotel facilities without assuming access.

## Getting there

The hotel is at 345 Moo 3 in Na Jomtien, Sattahip. Hyatt’s hotel-information page and the current Maps identity agree on the address and +66 38 221 234 telephone. Use the exact hotel pin and main entrance rather than assuming a central Jomtien Beach location.

The [Sattahip area page](/area/sattahip/) gives broader south-corridor context. No fixed transfer time, fare or public-transport route is stated here.

## Before you go

The programme can change, and the PDF tells guests to make reservations through the Recreation Team. Ask for the activity sheet covering the actual stay dates, then confirm the meeting point, capacity, charge, footwear and weather policy for the selected session.

The Water Court, beachfront infinity pool and family Lagoon Pool serve different uses; none is documented as a reserved training pool. The onsen, sauna and steam facilities are listed from **07:00 to 21:00**. Confirm age, supervision and swimwear rules before planning family use.
