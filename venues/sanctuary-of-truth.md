# Sanctuary of Truth Museum

The Sanctuary of Truth is a cultural museum, not a gym, sports club or training venue. It remains in the directory as a clearly labelled legacy record because earlier coverage treated optional visitor rides as sport-related activities.

## Tickets and visit

The official ticket page checked on 25 July 2026 lists the daytime entrance ticket at ฿500 for an adult and the night programme at ฿700. The museum's own site also lists optional visitor activities, but these are sightseeing add-ons rather than organised sport or instruction.

Use the official booking page for the current tour window. The corrected address is 206/2 Moo 5 on Pattaya-Naklua Road.
