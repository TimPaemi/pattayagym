## What it costs

The published Pattaya fee image currently shows a ten-week term at ฿8,000 for ages four months to 2.9 years, ฿9,500 for ages 2.9 to nine years and a ฿1,000 registration fee for first enrolment (branch fee image, checked 2026-07-27). Those are course prices, not a casual single-entry swim amount.

Before paying, ask which class your child fits by age and ability, whether the term has already started, what happens if a lesson is missed and what the total first-payment amount is for a new family. If you need a trial, ask whether one exists rather than assuming the term fee can be split.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Swimming Kids Pattaya suits parents looking for a structured baby or child swim-school environment near Na Jomtien. It is especially relevant for families who want a purpose-built indoor heated saltwater setting rather than an outdoor hotel pool. The age-banded course structure also helps a parent who wants a school-type programme rather than an improvised lesson arrangement.

It is not the right fit for an adult swimmer, a traveller wanting a one-off public pool session or someone who only needs general family splash time. Those readers should use a public-pool or hotel-pool guide instead of a children’s school record.

## Before you go

Contact the branch before the first visit and name your child’s age, experience and whether you need a beginner placement. Ask what swimwear, nappies, towels or parent participation are required for the exact class. That is more important here than at an ordinary pool because the branch is selling a coached age-specific programme.
