# MAX Muay Thai Stadium Pattaya

MAX Muay Thai Stadium is a spectator fight venue on Sukhumvit Road, not a walk-in training gym. Its official site carries an event calendar, ticket route and seating plan, while the live Maps listing confirms the stadium identity and address. A dated card must be checked before planning an evening.

The Thai activity listing identifies the 42/108 Sukhumvit Road venue as **สนามแม็กซ์มวยไทย**.

## What training is on offer

MAX offers scheduled Muay Thai fight cards for spectators. It does not publish a standing visitor-training product, pads session, sparring class or daily camp timetable. People seeking coached training should use the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/) or the [best Muay Thai guide](/guides/best-muay-thai-pattaya/) instead of treating a stadium listing as a gym.

The operator calendar is the useful current source because fight times are event-specific. When checked on 26 July 2026, it showed The Global Fight cards at **19:00 on Monday 27 July and Tuesday 28 July**, with five bouts listed on each card. Those dated entries demonstrate current programming; they do not establish a permanent nightly timetable.

## What it costs

The operator's current home page, checked 29 July 2026, says Pattaya tickets normally start at **about THB 1,500**, for either standard or VIP/ringside seating, and advises advance online booking for important dates. This is an operator-stated approximate starting point, not a guaranteed price for every card or seat section.

Before payment, confirm the seating section, all-in amount, service or delivery fee, collection method and refund or rescheduling terms. A seating plan explains location inside the stadium; it does not establish availability, accessibility arrangements or which section a seller will issue.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

MAX suits a visitor who wants to watch a confirmed Muay Thai event and is prepared to check the card and ticket terms for one date. It can also suit someone comparing the sport's spectator side with a training holiday, provided the two products are kept separate.

It does not suit a traveller looking for a drop-in workout, private coaching or a monthly camp. It is also a poor spontaneous choice when the calendar does not show a confirmed card. The bout names and weights on a dated listing are provisional event information, not a guarantee that every advertised matchup will proceed unchanged.

## Getting there

The stadium address is **42/108 Moo 9 on Sukhumvit Pattaya 42 Road**. Open the live map pin before leaving. The [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) describes a wider mapped corridor; it does not promise that the entrance is on Beach Road.

The operator contact page lists **+66 80 471 6008** for calls and WhatsApp. Maps also lists **+66 38 416 999**, retained as an alternate. Use the operator number first for event, ticket, entrance and arrival questions.

## Before you go

The operator lists office hours of **13:00–21:00 daily**; fight times remain event-specific. Send the intended date and party size, then ask:

- Is a fight card confirmed for that date, and what is the advertised start time?
- Which seating sections are still available, and what is the all-in price?
- Is the ticket issued immediately or collected at the venue?
- Are child admissions or age restrictions different?
- What happens if the card or start time changes?

Ask when doors open, whether a digital booking must be exchanged at the box office and which entrance serves the issued section. If a particular fighter or bout is the reason for attending, ask whether the card has been revised.

## What we could not verify

- The operator does not publish a date-by-date, section-by-section fixed ticket tariff in accessible text.
- A permanent weekly or nightly fight timetable is not published.
- Child rules, accessibility arrangements, door time, ticket delivery and refund terms require date-specific confirmation.
- The calendar cannot guarantee that every advertised fighter, weight or bout remains unchanged.
