# Greta Sport Club Pattaya

## What training is on offer

The event record is stronger than a generic “sports club” label. TATP's SINGHA Classic ran at Greta from 23–31 May 2026. The ITF lists the indoor-hard-court Greta Sport Club Master #1 from 13–18 June 2026. It also lists the BT10 3 Mermaid's Cup #4 beach-tennis event at Greta on 26 July 2026, with men's and women's doubles draws.

Those records establish tennis and beach-tennis use and show that tournament blocks can affect ordinary access. They do not prove that a visitor can book a court during every opening hour or that coaching is continuously available. ## What it costs

No current first-hand public court-hire, coaching, equipment-rental or membership tariff was available as of 2026-07-26. The TATP page lists a THB 600 tournament entry fee for its event, but that is a participant fee for that competition, not an ordinary court price and is not used as the venue tariff.

Ask for the complete price for the chosen sport, date and duration. Confirm whether the quote is per court or player and whether rackets, balls, lighting, coach, deposit, tax or tournament access are separate. A blank directory price means the normal transaction is unpublished, not free.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Greta suits a player or team seeking a current competition-linked tennis or beach-tennis venue in the Na Jomtien corridor and willing to reserve directly. The ITF records may be useful to players who care about documented hard-court or beach-tennis activity. The [Sattahip and Na Jomtien guide](/guides/best-gym-sattahip-pattaya/) provides broader location context.

It is a weaker fit for a casual walk-in who needs an instant tariff, equipment guarantee or automatic partner matching. A federation event at the venue is not evidence of public spectator access, permanent academy sessions or court availability outside the event.
