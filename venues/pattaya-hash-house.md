# Pattaya Hash House Harriers

The Thailand Hash House directory groups this chapter under **Pattaya Hash House Harriers** and records the local chapter's **1984** start; the Thai rendering here is a searchable transliteration.

Pattaya Hash House Harriers is a current Monday social run-and-walk club rather than a self-directed route listing. Its own site says the group has met every Monday since 1984, with gathering outside Buffalo Bar on Third Road from 14:30, the last bus leaving at 15:00 and the first circle starting at 16:00. It is a better fit for sociable runners and walkers than for anyone who wants a timed road race.

## What training is on offer

This is a hash, so the offer is organised trail movement and group ritual rather than formal coaching. The operator's current next-run page publishes the week's A-site, GPS, hare, bus note and circle timing. That makes it a real organised club entry in the [clubs category](/category/clubs/), not just an old expat rumour or a copied calendar listing.

The useful distinction is between Pattaya Hash and the surrounding alternatives. [Pattaya Public Running Locations](/gyms/pattaya-running-routes/) is a self-directed planning page with no organiser at all, while the [running and cycling clubs guide](/guides/running-cycling-clubs-pattaya/) compares this weekly mixed group with tougher or more self-directed options. Pattaya Hash provides a clearer recurring structure than either a generic beach run or a once-a-year event.

## What it costs

The operator's current public pricing checked 2026-07-27 is:

- **฿400** for men
- **฿150** for women
- **฿50** for children aged 16 or under

The group site treats that as the current run contribution rather than a month membership. Because the trail site changes weekly, treat the live next-run page as the active source instead of assuming every Monday will carry identical logistics or extras.

No separate published visitor insurance fee, annual membership fee, shoe-hire product or bag-drop tariff was found. If you want to know exactly what the contribution covers on a specific Monday, ask against the posted run rather than relying on a historical hash description.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Pattaya Hash suits runners, joggers and walkers who want company, a published weekly structure and a more social atmosphere than a solo dawn beach run. It can also work for new long-stay arrivals who want a low-barrier way to meet people without committing to a gym contract.

It does not suit someone who needs a measured course, chip timing, a sober family outing or a fixed weekly distance. If you want a more formal event target, compare the [Pattaya Marathon](/gyms/pattaya-marathon/) instead. If you want pure self-directed cardio, use [Pattaya Public Running Locations](/gyms/pattaya-running-routes/) and plan your own route.

## Getting there

The recurring central reference point is **Buffalo Bar on Third Road**, but that is only the pickup and meeting location. The actual trail site changes each week and is published on the operator's next-run page with an A-site note and GPS details.

That means a traveller should use two pins: Buffalo Bar for the bus assembly, and the live A-site if self-driving. The broad Central Pattaya context on the [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) is useful for deciding where to stay, but it is not the weekly trail location.

## Before you go

Read the next-run page before every visit, even if you have hashed before. The checked site publishes not only the bus and first-circle times, but also special notes and occasional off-pattern events. Bring cash, running shoes that can cope with mixed ground and a charged phone. A torch can be sensible if the light changes or the trail extends.

Because this is a social trail event rather than a marshalled city race, also ask what the current walking option looks like, whether children are genuinely suitable for that week's route, and when the return transport leaves. The Monday structure is stable; the terrain is not.
