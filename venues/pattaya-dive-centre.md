# Pattaya Dive Centre

Pattaya Dive Centre operates from Tipp Plaza on Beach Road. Its current PADI listing gives daily hours of 08:00-20:00, while the operator site publishes recreational, professional and technical training.

- Pattaya two-dive day trip with rental gear: ฿3,000
- PADI Discover Scuba Diving, two dives: ฿4,500
- three-day PADI Open Water course: ฿14,990, or ฿13,000 each for two or more people
- snorkelling trip: ฿1,500

The operator says the listed packages include the stated transfers, standard equipment where applicable, assistance, lunch and refreshments. Confirm the exact inclusions, medical form and departure time before booking.
