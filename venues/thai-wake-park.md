# Thai Wake Park Pattaya

Thai Wake Park Pattaya is a cable wakeboarding venue at 58 Soi 3 in Nong Pla Lai. The operator publishes Monday-Friday 10:30-18:30 and Saturday-Sunday 09:00-18:30, with low-speed beginner sessions from opening until noon on weekdays and 09:00-10:30 on weekends.

Riding rates include a life jacket and basic board. A helmet is compulsory and can be rented. Riders under 15 require a parent or guardian waiver, and all riders must register.
