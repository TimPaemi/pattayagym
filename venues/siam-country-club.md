# Siam Country Club Pattaya

Siam Country Club operates four separate Pattaya-area courses: Old Course, Plantation, Waterside and Rolling Hills. Current operator pages publish visitor green fees and the compulsory cart-and-caddie cost stack. The decision is not simply which club name to book; each course has its own layout, location and headline green fee.

## What training is on offer

The operator lists the Old Course as an 18-hole course opened in 1971 and renovated in 2007. Plantation opened in 2008 and has 27 holes arranged as three nine-hole loops. Waterside and Rolling Hills are separate 18-hole choices. Each has its own scorecard and tee-time selection.

The Old Course page also lists a natural-grass driving range, putting green, chipping area, lockers, showers, drying facilities, a pro shop and clubhouse dining. Do not transfer that exact facility inventory automatically to the other three courses; choose the course first and read its own operator page. The [Pattaya golf directory](/category/golf/) also includes independent courses, ranges and indoor practice records.

## What it costs

The operator pages checked on **26 July 2026** list Old Course visitor green fees at **฿6,500 on weekdays** and **฿8,500 on weekends and public holidays**. Plantation, Waterside and Rolling Hills each list **฿5,700 on weekdays** and **฿6,700 on weekends and public holidays**.

Every player must also take an individual **฿1,000 cart** and a **฿500 caddie**; carts cannot be shared, the caddie drives and the caddie tip is separate. The pages list club rental at **฿2,500**, shoe rental at **฿800** and umbrella rental at **฿500**. Before tip or optional rental, that makes the published minimum Old Course stack ฿8,000 weekday or ฿10,000 weekend/holiday, and the other three courses ฿7,200 or ฿8,200.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Siam Country Club suits a golfer who wants a full booked round and is comfortable with compulsory one-player cart and caddie charges. Plantation offers the only 27-hole complex in the group; the other course pages present distinct 18-hole layouts. The published cost stack is useful for travellers who need more than an unexplained green-fee headline.

It does not suit someone seeking an inexpensive walk-in bucket of balls or a city-centre gym. Compare [Pattaya Golf Driving Range](/gyms/pattaya-golf-driving-range/) for a practice-only record, or use the [Pattaya golf-course guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) to compare full-round products.

## Getting there

The address in this record is the Old Course at 50/10 Moo 9, Pong. The operator's tee-time page provides separate map choices for Old Course, Plantation, Waterside and Rolling Hills. Select the course-specific pin rather than navigating only to the Siam Country Club brand name.

The operator directs Pattaya traffic via Siam Country Club Road. These are East Pattaya golf locations, not Beach Road facilities; the [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) gives wider location context. No fixed fare should be inferred from the course pages.

## Before you go

The booking page is an enquiry form. It asks for player count, preferred date and time, selected course and whether clubs are required; it says single players and groups of five are allowed on weekdays and directs groups of 20 or more to sales. Submit the exact course and wait for availability confirmation rather than treating the form as an instant reservation.

Ask the reservations team to reconfirm the date-specific green fee, compulsory cart and caddie charges, tip guidance, rental equipment, dress code, payment and cancellation terms. Rain can restrict carts to the cart path according to the operator pages.
