# Thai Sky Adventures

Thai Sky Adventures operates a drop zone in Nong Kham, Si Racha. Its current site advertises tandem skydives from up to 13,000 feet, with about 60 seconds of freefall, plus AFF and A-licence training.

## Price checked 25 July 2026

The operator's April 2026 price list gives a tandem jump at ฿9,450. Optional media packages start at ฿3,800. The same price list says Pattaya round-trip transport is free for a tandem customer and one companion; confirm the pickup area and date when booking.

Jumps depend on weather and operational conditions. Ask the operator about medical or body-size restrictions, documentation, the expected full-day timing and refund or rescheduling terms before paying.
