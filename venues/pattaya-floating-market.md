# Pattaya Floating Market

Pattaya Floating Market remains open at 451/304 Moo 12 on Sukhumvit Road. Its current Maps listing links to the operator domain, gives the phone above and lists daily opening from 09:00 to 19:00.

This is a cultural and shopping attraction, not a dependable place to train or exercise. The previous record's Muay Talay schedule, rowboat, amphibious-boat and zipline claims were not supported by a current first-hand activity page and have been removed. No current admission tariff was available from the operator page during verification.

The record is retained to correct the legacy entry, but it should not be counted as a verified sports facility.
