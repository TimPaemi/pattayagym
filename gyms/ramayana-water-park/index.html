★ About this venue

Ramayana Water Park Pattaya

Ramayana is a full-day water park south of Pattaya. The operator currently publishes 11:00-18:00 opening, Thursday through Tuesday, with Wednesday closed.

Price and first visit

The tourist promotion displayed on 25 July 2026 was THB 1,099 online or THB 1,199 walk-in for visitors at least 106 cm tall; children below 106 cm were free. The live page also showed separate charges for lockers, towels and cabanas. Promotions change, so recheck the booking page and its terms for the chosen date.
