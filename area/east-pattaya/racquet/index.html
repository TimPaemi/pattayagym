East Pattaya's racquet list is broad but price transparency is narrow. It covers tennis, padel, pickleball and six badminton leads spread from Sukhumvit and Nong Prue to Mabprachan, Chaiyaporn Withi and Huai Yai. Pattaya Padel Club is the only listing with a current static tariff; Prime Padel prices through Playtomic, while the tennis and badminton venues require direct quotes. Pattaya Padel Club beside Mabprachan Reservoir lists whole-court hire at THB 600 per hour from 10:00-16:00 and THB 800 during 07:00-10:00 and 16:00-22:00, checked 27 July 2026. One-hour private coaching is THB 1,600 for one player, THB 2,000 for two, THB 2,200 for three and THB 2,600 for four. Confirm whether the lesson includes the court and whether rackets, balls or app fees cost extra. Prime on Sukhumvit 41 has covered courts, coaching, racket hire and daily 08:00-23:00 hours, but no dependable rate outside its live booking flow. Tara Tennis Pattaya publishes indoor tennis, pickleball and basketball with daily 06:00-22:00 access, but no court count, tariff or coaching timetable. Pattaya Badminton & Tennis Inter Club supports tennis, badminton and pickleball from 06:00-21:00. Its older operator posts do not meet the current dated-price bar. For either club, name the sport, court type, time, equipment and coaching need before comparing a quote. Badminton Kho-Noi is the clearest capacity and overnight option: an April 2026 owner post identifies 12 courts with 8 mm rubber flooring, split between seven older-building and five newer-building courts, plus 24-hour opening, showers and parking for up to 70 cars. No current court price is public. Round-the-clock hours do not guarantee a vacant court or staffed rental desk, so reserve the building and court number. The other halls are contact-first. Pattaya Monkey lists Monday-Saturday 10:00-23:00 and Sunday 17:00-23:00, with a June 2026 competition confirming active use. JP Badminton lists weekday 08:00-22:00 and weekend 08:00-21:00, plus a dated 10:00-12:00 walk-in-group notice that must be reconfirmed. 272 Estadio lists daily 08:00-22:00; SB Badminton Huai Yai lists 13:00-23:00. None publishes a dependable court fee, rental menu, court inventory or permanent social timetable. Choose Pattaya Padel Club for a known court or coaching price, Prime for live-booked covered padel, Tara or Inter Club for a multi-sport enquiry, Kho-Noi for a reserved late-hour badminton court, or the nearest contact-first hall after confirming its terms. Ask whether pricing is per court or player and whether rackets, shuttlecocks, balls, lighting, footwear, deposit and cancellation are separate. The tennis and badminton guide gives the wider city comparison.
