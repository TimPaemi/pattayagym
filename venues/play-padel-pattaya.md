# Play Padel Pattaya

Play Padel is an indoor, climate-controlled arena at **400/34 Soi Kasetsin 3** in Pratamnak. The operator publishes **10:00-22:00 daily** and describes six air-conditioning units per court beneath an 11.5-metre roof. The office number shown by both the website and current Maps listing is **+66 65 123 4105**; the site also lists English/Swedish contacts Mattias at +66 82 940 1875 and Erik at +66 80 621 0000.

The operator presents padel as suitable for children, adults and seniors and says licensed coaches are available. That broad invitation does not reserve a court or place a new player into a group. State the player count and experience level when asking for a slot or lesson.

## Court, coaching and equipment prices

The operator's live tariff checked on **26 July 2026** publishes:

- standard court: **THB 1,200 per court-hour**;
- private coaching: **from THB 600 per hour**;
- standard racket rental: **THB 100 per hour**;
- demo racket: **15 minutes free**, then **THB 200-300 per hour** depending on the model;
- balls: **THB 200-300 per pack** depending on the model; and
- new rackets: **THB 2,000-12,000** depending on the model.

The quoted court price is for the court, not each player. Divide it among the confirmed participants only after checking whether coaching, rackets or balls are separate. The operator warns that a late arrival does not move the reservation: if a booking begins at noon and a player arrives at 12:30, charging still starts from the reserved time.

The same live page displays a personal membership at **THB 1,000 per month**, with a THB 100 hourly discount and additional coaching discounts. Its wording says the programme starts "from November" without naming a year, so confirm that this membership is currently offered and ask which products receive the discount before paying.

## Booking and payment

Bookings use the **Padelmates** app. The operator says known players can reserve and pay at the arena, while new players who book ahead must pay in advance. Cash and Thai QR payment are preferred. A credit-card payment through Padelmates adds **THB 100**, checked on the operator's booking page on 26 July 2026.

The page also says drop-in bookings are available. That means an unplanned booking may be possible, not that a court will be free. For a fixed game, reserve ahead and confirm the cancellation or no-show rule. If one player is missing, ask whether the arena can help with player matching rather than assuming the booking will be rearranged.

## Location and first game

Kasetsin Soi 3 is on Pratamnak near the Cosy Beach area. The operator advises allowing extra travel time during long holidays because road queues can delay arrivals, while the full reserved hour still remains chargeable. Use the exact Maps pin and arrive early enough to change, collect rentals and warm up.

Before payment, confirm the date, start time, player count, court duration, total equipment cost, lesson format and refund or rescheduling terms. Ask which shoes are suitable, whether balls are included, whether a coach is licensed for the requested level and how photos or court-camera footage are handled. The operator's general-information page says cameras are installed for security and may be used inside courts, and asks anyone who does not want to appear in photos or video to tell reception.
