# Underwater World Pattaya

Underwater World Pattaya is an aquarium on Sukhumvit Road in South Pattaya. The operator publishes daily 09:00-18:00 hours, last admission at 17:30, and a clear acrylic tunnel more than 105 metres long.

Admission checked 25 July 2026 is **THB 550 for visitors over 131 cm** and **THB 320 for children 91-130 cm**. The operator also publishes feeding presentations in its coral reef, open ocean and Giant of Siam zones; schedules can change, so check the activity page on the day.

The former shark-dive programme, manta experience, animal count, jellyfish superlative and language list were removed because the current operator pages checked did not support them. This record covers the aquarium visit rather than a bookable scuba venue.
