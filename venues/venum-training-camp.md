# Venum Training Camp Thailand

Venum Training Camp is a multi-discipline combat-sports gym on Soi Khopai 12. Its current official site advertises Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and cross-training for beginners through experienced athletes. That breadth is the useful distinction here: it is a camp to investigate when more than one combat discipline matters, not evidence that every programme runs at every hour.

## What training is on offer

The operator names five activities: Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and cross-training. It also says beginners are welcome. No current class-by-class timetable was visible, however, so the discipline list should not be read as a promise that a BJJ, MMA or boxing session will coincide with a particular day visit.

For alternatives focused specifically on Thailand's striking art, compare the [Muay Thai directory](/category/muay-thai/). The [boxing and kickboxing guide](/guides/boxing-kickboxing-gym-pattaya/) is the better starting point if western boxing is the priority rather than a mixed camp.

## What it costs

No usable current public tariff was found on the operator's site or exact Maps listing. The booking interface displayed “PRICE 0$” during the check, but it did not identify a free class or a zero-cost package. We do not treat that placeholder as a price.

Ask for the exact rate for a single class, private session, day, week or longer stay, as applicable. Also ask what the quoted amount includes and whether gloves, wraps, accommodation, meals or equipment rental are separate. Obtain the total and cancellation terms in writing before paying.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Venum may suit a beginner who wants to ask one camp about several combat disciplines, or an experienced visitor trying to combine striking, grappling and conditioning. The operator's all-levels wording supports an enquiry from a first-timer, but it does not replace confirmation of the appropriate session.

It is a weaker fit for someone who needs a published drop-in price, a fixed online timetable or a guaranteed English-language class: none was verified. People interested mainly in grappling can compare the [Pattaya BJJ directory](/category/bjj/) before choosing a mixed programme.

## Getting there

The exact live listing places the camp at 399/8 Soi Khopai 12 in Nong Prue, south of the central beach corridor. Use the linked Maps pin rather than navigating only to “Soi Khopai,” because the numbered side street is part of the address.

No operator-published hotel transfer, public-transport route, journey time, parking rule or fare was found. Arrange the outward and return trip around the confirmed session rather than assuming transport will be included.

## Before you go

There is a material hours conflict. The official site lists 07:00–19:00 daily, while the exact Maps listing shows 09:00–19:00 Monday through Saturday and Sunday closed. Contact +66 87 285 7699 and ask for the start time of the specific discipline you want; general opening hours do not establish a class schedule.

Tell the camp your experience level, preferred discipline and dates. Ask what clothing and protective equipment to bring, whether rental gear is available, and whether advance booking or identification is required.
