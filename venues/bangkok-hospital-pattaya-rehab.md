# Bangkok Hospital Pattaya Rehabilitation Center

Bangkok Hospital Pattaya's Rehabilitation Center is a **clinical hospital department, not a gym, recovery lounge or self-directed exercise facility**. The hospital places it on the third floor of Building A and publishes centre hours of **08:00–20:00 every day**. The hospital's main Maps listing is open 24 hours, but that does not make the Rehabilitation Center a round-the-clock walk-in service.

The current official page says the centre handles post-orthopaedic, musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation. It describes physiotherapy assessment by physicians and treatment by physical therapists, with programmes adapted to the patient's individual needs. That wording supports a medically assessed pathway; it does not support choosing a machine or treatment from a menu without an evaluation.

## Rooms, services and clinical team

The centre lists an evaluation room, treatment room, traction room, chest-therapy room, child-development stimulation room, activities-of-daily-living stimulation room and an exercise zone. Its published facilities include deep and superficial heat therapy, cold compression, whirlpool, laser and electrical therapy, neck and back traction, partial-body-weight support, shock-wave therapy and occupational therapy for children.

Those are capabilities of the department, not promises that every technique is appropriate for every injury. The official page says the staff group includes physicians, physical therapists, occupational therapists, nurses and physical-therapist assistants. It also lists acupuncture as an available alternative-medicine service. A patient should ask the treating clinician which, if any, of these services belongs in the assessed plan.

## Appointments, records and cost

The direct centre number is **+66 38 259 986**, and the hospital lists **info.pattaya@bangkokhospital.com**. Contact the centre before travelling and describe whether the request concerns a recent injury, recovery after treatment, a neurological condition, paediatric occupational therapy or another rehabilitation need. Ask whether the first visit should be booked with a rehabilitation physician or therapist, and whether a referral, imaging report, operation note, medication list or previous rehabilitation plan should be brought.

No stable public tariff for an ordinary rehabilitation assessment or treatment course was found on the official page checked on 26 July 2026. The page displays separate time-limited performance-testing packages, but those are named testing products and should not be treated as the price of physiotherapy or rehabilitation. Request a written estimate for the proposed assessment and follow-up plan, including professional fees, equipment or procedure charges and any hospital fees.

If using insurance, confirm direct billing, pre-authorisation, exclusions and the documents the insurer requires before the appointment. International patients should also ask which languages are available for the booked consultation and whether an interpreter must be arranged.

## What this listing can and cannot answer

The directory can identify the department, location, hours, contact and services the hospital currently publishes. It cannot diagnose an injury, determine whether a treatment is suitable or predict how many sessions will be needed. For urgent symptoms or an emergency, use the hospital's appropriate emergency pathway rather than relying on the Rehabilitation Center's daytime listing.

Before arrival, reconfirm Building A, third floor, the appointment time and the correct hospital entrance. The campus address is **301 Moo 6, Sukhumvit Road Km 143 in Naklua**. Keep the centre's direct number available because the main hospital phone and 24-hour Maps hours describe the wider hospital, not the rehabilitation desk.
