# SailBreeze Yacht Charter

SailBreeze is based at Ocean Marina and currently presents private yacht charters, marine services and crewed shared sailing cruises. The shared programme advertises half-day and full-day outings with a qualified captain and crew.

The former record described SailBreeze as a sailing school and claimed certification courses. The current site instead recommends a separate established sailing school, so those claims have been removed.

## Price and first visit

No current public charter tariff was displayed. Ask for the vessel, duration, maximum passengers, route, weather policy and exact inclusions, including marina fees, island fees, insurance, food and water activities.
