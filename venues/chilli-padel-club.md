# Chilli Padel Club

Chilli Padel is a six-court club on Bun Kanchana 3 in Jomtien. Five courts are roofed, making it a practical rainy-season option. The club also offers coaching, beginner introductions, social formats, a pro shop and a bar.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The official site lists a whole court at ฿1,000 per hour for up to four players, with happy-hour courts from ฿600 per hour. Rackets and balls are available for hire, but their current rental price is not published.

## Booking and hours

The operator publishes daily hours of 07:00–24:00, while Google Maps currently shows a 23:00 close. Check the live Playtomic schedule for the final available slot. First-timers can book a 90-minute introduction; solo players can also use the club's social groups and rotating-partner events to find a game.
