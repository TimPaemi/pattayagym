# Thai Sky Adventures

## What training is on offer

Thai location listings call the 777 77 Sriracha drop zone **ไทยสกายแอดเวนเจอร์** and describe tandem flights up to 13,000 feet.

Thai Sky Adventures operates a drop zone at Nong Kham in Si Racha. The operator advertises tandem skydives from up to 13,000 feet with about 60 seconds of freefall. A tandem places a first-time participant with an instructor; the current booking flow also offers media add-ons.

For students seeking a qualification rather than a one-off jump, the live booking page lists AFF Level 1, a full AFF course and an A Licence programme. These are distinct products with different commitments. The site should be asked to place a prospective student at the correct starting point.

## What it costs

Prices checked on the operator's live booking page on **27 July 2026** were **THB 9,450** for a basic tandem, **THB 13,250** with video, **THB 14,250** with video and photos, and **THB 15,250** for the deluxe option. Training was listed at **THB 25,950** for AFF Level 1, **THB 90,950** for the full AFF course and **THB 149,550** for the A Licence programme.

An operator PDF labelled 2026 showed some different figures, so this record uses the live checkout prices. Confirm the selected product and final total in the booking flow before payment. The terms page says Pattaya pickup and drop-off for tandem customers is limited to Pattaya City, Naklua and Jomtien areas.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The basic tandem is the clearest route for a first jump because the instructor controls the skydive. AFF and licence products suit students prepared for ground instruction, assessment and a multi-stage training commitment.

This venue does not suit a traveller who needs a guaranteed jump time regardless of weather. It also should not be booked until the operator has accepted any medical, mobility or body-size circumstances and confirmed the required documents. This page does not make an eligibility decision.

## Getting there

The exact Maps listing is at **777/77 Nong Kham, Si Racha, Chonburi 20230**, well outside central Pattaya. Its main published phone is **+66 85 900 3412**. Use the drop-zone pin rather than searching for a Pattaya beachfront office.

If relying on included tandem transport, give the operator the exact hotel and wait for pickup acceptance. Being in greater Pattaya does not automatically place an address inside the three named pickup areas.

## Before you go

Maps publishes **08:00-16:00 daily**, while holiday hours and actual jump operations can vary. A confirmed booking matters more than the general opening window. Ask for pickup or self-arrival time, expected finish, clothing and footwear, identification, media package, weight or health checks, and the weather rescheduling process.

Read the operator's terms before paying. Training-course cancellation deductions and tandem transfer conditions are product-specific, so do not apply one product's rules to another.

## What we could not verify

The public pages checked do not provide a single universal completion time or guarantee that a booked date will fly. This record also does not independently verify aircraft availability, a particular exit altitude on every load, or individual medical eligibility. Weather and operational approval remain with the operator.
