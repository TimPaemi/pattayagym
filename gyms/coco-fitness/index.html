★ About this venue

Coco Fitness

Coco Fitness is a fourth-floor commercial gym inside Mike Shopping Mall, with weights, cardio, stretching and boxing areas in a space the operator describes as more than 1,000 square metres. It suits Central Pattaya visitors considering a monthly membership, but it is not a 24-hour gym and no current day-pass price is published.

What training is on offer

Owner posts from April and May 2026 describe more than 1,000 square metres of machines, weights, cardio equipment and stretching zones. A May post also lists boxing facilities. That supports a broad general-fitness offer, but not an exact machine count, equipment-brand list or guarantee that a particular rack, platform or specialist machine will be free.

The owner page remains active in July 2026. It does not publish a current group-class timetable, personal-training tariff or statement that coaching is included with membership. Compare the wider Pattaya fitness directory if scheduled classes or round-the-clock access matter more than the mall location.

What it costs

The operator's 13 July 2026 membership graphic, checked on 26 July 2026, lists:

  • ฿1,599 for one month
  • ฿4,299 for three months
  • ฿8,299 for six months
  • ฿12,999 for 12 months

A separate July promotion lists ฿2,998 for either two months for one person or one month each for two people. Confirm that promotion is still available before paying and ask when the membership period starts.

No current owner-published day pass, joining fee, deposit or personal-training price was found. The tariff also does not say whether towels, lockers, classes or parking validation are included, so those are questions for the desk rather than implied benefits.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Coco is a useful fit for someone staying in Central Pattaya who wants cardio and resistance training in one indoor venue and can train between 07:00 and 22:00. The published longer memberships give a concrete comparison point for a multi-week or multi-month stay.

It does not suit someone who needs guaranteed access after 22:00; use the 24-hour gym guide to separate true round-the-clock access from extended hours. Strength athletes who require a particular platform, bar or plate inventory should ask before buying, because the owner describes equipment categories rather than a complete itemised list.

Getting there

The venue is on the fourth floor of Mike Shopping Mall at 262 Moo 10. Its live listing uses the Pattaya Second Road address, while owner posts describe the mall location from the Beach Road side. Use the linked Maps pin for the entrance rather than relying on the road wording alone.

Owner posts in May 2026 say parking is available at the building. They do not publish a parking fee, validation rule or vehicle-height restriction. The Central Pattaya area page gives context on nearby fitness alternatives.

Before you go

The owner page lists daily opening from 07:00 to 22:00 and publishes +66 93 383 3817. Call before the first session if you want a day visit, because the current public tariff covers memberships rather than a single workout.

Ask whether identification or registration is required, which changing-room items are supplied, and whether the boxing area needs separate permission or equipment. Bring a towel, water bottle and any specialist lifting accessories unless the desk confirms provision.

What we could not verify

  • No current day-pass, joining-fee or personal-training tariff is published.
  • The current group-class schedule and booking rules are not available online.
  • Towel, locker, shower and parking-validation inclusions are not stated.
  • The operator does not publish an exact machine inventory or equipment brands.
