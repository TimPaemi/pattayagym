# Siam Bayshore Pattaya Tennis Courts

Siam Bayshore publishes six tennis courts inside its South Pattaya resort and explicitly opens them to the public as well as hotel guests. The current operator page gives useful court, rental and rain terms. It does not publish live availability, coaching or evening lighting, so this remains a daytime reservation.

## What training is on offer

The resort describes six championship-level courts available daily from **07:00 to 18:00**. Public eligibility is unusually clear for a hotel facility. The page does not publish a coaching timetable, social session, tournament calendar, court surface or floodlight policy.

This is therefore a documented court-hire product rather than a coached tennis programme. Players seeking other court sports can compare the [Pattaya racquet directory](/category/racquet/) before assuming one facility serves tennis, badminton, padel and pickleball interchangeably.

## What it costs

The exact resort page, checked on **26 July 2026**, lists **THB 350 per court-hour**. Racket hire is **THB 150 per racket-hour**; four used balls are **THB 100 per hour**; a basket of balls is **THB 300 per hour**; and a can of new balls is **THB 300**.

In-house guests receive one complimentary daytime hour. That hotel inclusion does not make public court hire free, and guests should still reserve a time. Published loss or damage charges are **THB 5,000 for a racket** and **THB 150 for each ball**. These are liabilities, not rental prices.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The courts suit hotel guests who want an on-property game and public players who value a clearly published daytime hourly rate near South Pattaya. Six courts may provide more scheduling capacity than a single hotel court, but no availability calendar is public.

They are a weaker fit for anyone needing guaranteed coaching, organised partner matching or late-evening floodlit play. The [tennis and badminton guide](/guides/tennis-badminton-pattaya/) separates court hire from clubs and coached products.

## Getting there

The courts are within Siam Bayshore Pattaya at 559 Beach Road near the Walking Street end of the city. Use the resort entrance and front desk rather than looking for a separate street-front tennis club. The current Maps identity supports the hotel address, telephone and operator website.

The [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) provides corridor context. The resort does not publish an outside-player parking rule, court check-in desk or fixed transfer time.

## Before you go

Contact +66 38 428 679 with the date, preferred start, number of players and equipment required. Ask whether 18:00 is the final playable hour or the equipment-return deadline, and confirm identity, deposit, footwear, towel, locker and changing-room rules.

The operator states that if rain interrupts play within 15 minutes, the court fee is waived. If play lasts up to 30 minutes, half the court fee applies. Ask how the rule applies after 30 minutes, whether a replacement slot is offered and what happens to rental charges.

## What we could not verify

- No live court availability, advance-payment rule or cancellation window was published.
- Court surface, lighting, coaching and social-session details were not stated.
- Outside-player parking, changing-room, towel, locker and drinking-water access remain unclear.
- The operator does not explain the rain outcome after 30 minutes or the treatment of equipment charges.
