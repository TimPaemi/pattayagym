# Laem Chabang International Country Club

The 27-hole club's Thai Maps label is **สนามกอล์ฟ แหลมฉบัง อินเตอร์เนชั่นแนล คันทรี คลับ**.

Laem Chabang International Country Club is a 27-hole Si Racha golf complex offering daytime and night-golf charges, compulsory caddie and buggy service, rental equipment and a current stay-and-play promotion. It suits golfers who want a booked full round and can confirm the available loop and tee window before travelling.

## What training is on offer

The operator presents three nine-hole loops and publishes both day and night golf. An 18-hole booking uses the course allocation offered for the date; the public charge page does not guarantee a particular loop combination. The club site also lists a driving range, but ordinary range access should be confirmed separately from the booked-round product.

The charge page states group sizes of three to five players on weekdays and three to four on weekends and holidays. Those limits do not guarantee that a single player will be paired or accepted at every time. The [Pattaya golf directory](/category/golf/) includes other courses and practice-only records.

## What it costs

The operator's public charge page, checked **26 July 2026**, lists an 18-hole daytime green fee of **฿3,500 on weekdays** and **฿4,000 on weekends**. The caddie fee is **฿450 per 18 holes** and the compulsory one-golfer buggy is **฿980**. The required published stack is therefore **฿4,930 weekday** or **฿5,430 weekend**, before any caddie tip.

Night golf is listed at a **฿3,500 green fee on weekdays and weekends**, plus the same caddie and buggy charges, for a required published stack of **฿4,930** before tip. Full-set club hire is **฿1,300**, shoe hire **฿400** and an 18-hole umbrella **฿200**. The page says VAT is included, rates can change and advance booking is required.

A separate operator promotion checked 26 July lists stay-and-play from **฿4,080 per person** for **1 April–30 September 2026**, including a room, 18-hole green fee, caddie and golf cart. Treat that as a dated accommodation package, not the ordinary walk-in round price.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Laem Chabang suits a visitor who wants a complete booked golf day and values a published cost stack rather than a green fee alone. Night golf may help someone planning around daytime commitments, but it must be confirmed for the chosen date.

It is a poor fit for someone seeking inexpensive city-centre practice, a cart-free walking round or guaranteed spontaneous access. Compare [Siam Country Club](/gyms/siam-country-club/) for a different four-course cost stack, or use the [best golf courses guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) for the broader trade-off.

## Getting there

The operator and current Maps identity agree on **106/8 Moo 4, Bueng, Si Racha, Chonburi 20230** and phone **+66 82 222 3031**. The course is in the Laem Chabang/Si Racha corridor, not central Pattaya. Use the exact club pin rather than the nearby lodge or similarly named properties.

No fixed transfer time, taxi fare or public-transport route is published in the checked first-hand sources. Ask whether the booking confirmation includes an arrival gate, bag-drop point and required check-in time.

## Before you go

Weekend and public-holiday flight windows on the charge page are **06:30–08:43** in the morning and **from 11:24** in the afternoon. These are published windows, not live availability. The operator permits only soft-spike or spike-less shoes and requires one buggy per golfer.

Send the date, preferred time, player count, handicap information if requested, rental needs and day-versus-night preference. Ask about deposit, cancellation, rain, dress code, caddie-tip guidance, course allocation and promotion eligibility. Wait for a confirmed tee time before travelling.
