Central Pattaya's MMA result is a single multi-discipline school, not a choice of dedicated cages, open mats or equivalent visitor packages. The listed venue is BOXING CLUB Mixfight Pattaya at Khao Makok 9/1 in Nong Prue. Its broad South Pattaya mapping can be useful to someone based around the centre, but the exact pin matters: Khao Makok is not the Beach Road or Pattaya Klang hotel strip. Treat this as a planned trip to one school rather than evidence that Central Pattaya has an MMA cluster within walking distance. Use the full MMA directory when location or a second operator matters more than this one record. The school's current public listing advertises Muay Thai, western boxing, MMA, jiu-jitsu, yoga, fitness, children's groups and fight preparation. That discipline list comes from a March 2026 owner update on the exact Maps listing. Independent Sherdog and Tapology records also connect New Generation Championship MMA bouts with Mixfight. Those event records are useful evidence that MMA is more than a loose search tag, but they do not turn an event card into a normal class timetable. A visitor should state whether the aim is MMA, striking, jiu-jitsu, general fitness or fight preparation and ask which ordinary session covers it. For jiu-jitsu, confirm gi or no-gi; for striking, ask what gloves, wraps and protective equipment are required. No current public price was found for a group class, private lesson, open gym, weekly stay or monthly plan during the source check. That leaves no honest local price spread to publish: one unpriced venue cannot support a cheap-versus-premium comparison. Contact the operator profile and request a written quote for the exact discipline, format and date. Ask whether registration, equipment use, private coaching or fight preparation is charged separately, and whether a trial or visitor session is available. A quoted total should also identify the payment method and cancellation terms before it is compared with a camp elsewhere. The published business window is 08:00-21:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 08:00-19:00 on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, with Sunday closed. Those hours do not establish continuous MMA instruction. Ask for the exact start and finish time, coaching language, experience level, visitor eligibility and class size; for a child, request the age band, supervision arrangement and trial policy. Also confirm the entrance and parking rather than navigating from the area label alone. For a Central Pattaya stay, the decision is therefore between a contact-first, multi-discipline school south-east of the tourist core and travelling farther for a venue with a published product that better matches the intended training. Choose Mixfight only after its operator confirms the discipline, session and price in writing. Choose another area when a dated tariff, guaranteed drop-in class, dedicated BJJ format or residential camp is essential. This page's one listing is an honest coverage limit, not proof that other Central Pattaya MMA options do not exist.
