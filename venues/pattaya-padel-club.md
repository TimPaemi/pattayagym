# Pattaya Padel Club

A Thai local report names the venue สโมสรเพเดิ้ลพัทยา and places its launch at 44 Soi Mabprachan Lang 2 in Pong, matching this record's address.

Pattaya Padel Club is a Mabprachan-side padel venue with current public court prices and current coaching prices on its own site. That makes it stronger than many contact-only racquet records for visitors who want to compare a real booking total before travelling. It still works best as a pre-booked destination rather than an impulsive central-Pattaya walk-in.

## What training is on offer

The official club site presents Pattaya Padel Club as a venue for players of all levels and pushes app-based court booking rather than phone-first reservation. The same site separates two products clearly: whole-court hire and private coaching. It also names coach Phils and describes him as a certified padel coach with English, French and Chinese spoken.

That matters because a beginner deciding between “just book a court” and “book a lesson” is making two different purchases. The public coaching grid is structured around one-hour private lessons for one to four players. The public court page is structured around the hour and time band instead. The operator's own copy also frames the club as a padel-specific venue rather than a broader tennis or multisport complex.

No extractable public group-class timetable was found on the checked pages. The useful current evidence is therefore the court-booking model, the named private coach and the published training prices, not a rolling class schedule.

## What it costs

On the official club page, checked 2026-07-27, public court hire was listed at **฿600 per hour per court** for the daytime slot **10:00-16:00** and **฿800 per hour per court** for the prime-time slots **07:00-10:00** and **16:00-22:00**.

The same official site listed one-hour private coaching at **฿1,600 for 1 person**, **฿2,000 for 2 people**, **฿2,200 for 3 people** and **฿2,600 for 4 people**, all checked 2026-07-27. Those figures make the per-person cost drop sharply if a pair or four-player group shares the lesson.

What the checked public pages did not state clearly is whether racket hire, ball use or any app booking fee sits outside those headline numbers. Ask for the whole total if you are new to the sport and need equipment as well as court time. If you are comparing this venue with [Play Padel Pattaya](/gyms/play-padel-pattaya/), compare whole-court cost, coaching cost, racket hire and booking method together.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Pattaya Padel Club suits players who want an exact current court price before they leave the hotel, and beginners who prefer a named private coach rather than an informal social game. It also suits a small group that can split one coaching hour and one court.

It is a weaker fit for someone who wants a walkable central location or an operator that publishes a broader public timetable of clinics, ladders or social sessions. ## Getting there

The operator lists the club at 44 Soi Mabprachan Lang 2, Pong, Bang Lamung District, Chon Buri 20150. The official page describes the setting as overlooking Lake Mabprachan, which is the useful landmark for route planning. This is inland East Pattaya rather than a beach-road court.

Use the exact map pin before setting off. A Mabprachan address can still mean a material drive from central Pattaya, Jomtien or Na Jomtien, and this record does not invent a fixed journey time or taxi fare. If you are travelling with four players, confirm parking and the exact court-booking name inside the app before departure.

## Before you go

The club's public booking call to action is to download the app and check court availability there. Do that before travelling because the operator is clearly set up around pre-booking rather than a guaranteed walk-in court. If you want coaching, ask whether the selected hour already includes the court or whether you need a separate court booking.

New players should also confirm racket availability, ball policy, cancellation terms and whether the lesson is better for one person, a pair or a four-player beginner group.
