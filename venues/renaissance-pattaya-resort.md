# Renaissance Pattaya Resort & Spa

Renaissance Pattaya Resort & Spa is a Na Jomtien hotel with fitness, pool and wellness facilities for registered guests. Marriott does not publish these amenities as a public gym or day-pass product. It can suit a hotel stay built around light training, but non-residents should choose a facility with explicit outside access.

## What training is on offer

Marriott's current experience page lists a Fitness Center, Main Pool, Sunset Pool, Kids Pool, Spa and Kids Club. The fitness summary describes a modern gym, yoga, bikes and seaside jogging, but it does not publish an item-by-item machine or free-weight inventory. The same page's frequently asked questions identifies the fitness centre and pools as hotel-guest facilities.

These amenities support general resort exercise and leisure swimming; they do not establish coached classes, lap-lane availability, bike-hire terms or a public training programme.

## What it costs

No current non-resident fitness-centre or pool tariff was published on the Marriott pages checked on 26 July 2026. Room offers are accommodation prices, not a comparable gym fee, and are therefore not reproduced here.

Registered guests should confirm whether the chosen room rate includes the desired facility and whether spa treatments, bike use, yoga or other scheduled activities cost extra. A non-guest should ask whether any written day-access product exists, what it includes and the complete price; until confirmed, do not travel expecting to buy entry.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The property suits a registered guest who wants a gym and several pools at the same resort, particularly when general cardio, mobility or a casual workout is enough. The Kids Pool and Kids Club may also matter to a family, subject to the hotel's current rules.

It does not suit a non-resident comparing guaranteed day passes, a swimmer who requires documented lanes or a lifter who must confirm a precise rack, barbell, plate or dumbbell setup. Compare explicitly public products in the [Pattaya fitness category](/category/fitness/) or the broader [Sattahip-area directory](/area/sattahip/).

## Getting there

Marriott and Maps place the resort at 9/9 Moo 3, Na Jomtien, Sattahip, south of central Pattaya. Maps confirms the current hotel identity and phone +66 38 259 099. Use the exact resort pin rather than treating “Pattaya” in the name as a central location.

The checked operator pages do not publish a fixed transfer fare or journey time. Complimentary parking is described for hotel guests, not as permission for a non-resident facility visit.

## Before you go

Marriott publishes hotel check-in at 15:00 and checkout at 12:00. Those times are not fitness-centre or pool hours. Ask the hotel for the current opening hours, maintenance schedule, guest eligibility, age rules, activity timetable and any reservation requirement.

If a particular exercise depends on equipment, obtain confirmation from the resort rather than relying on the general “modern gym” description.
