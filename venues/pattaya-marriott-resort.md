# Pattaya Marriott Resort and Spa

Thai Maps gives the 64 Moo 1 property the name **โรงแรม พัทยา แมรีออท รีสอร์ต แอนด์ สปา** and matches its guest-only 24-hour fitness offer.

## What training is on offer

Pattaya Marriott Resort and Spa is an operating Na Jomtien hotel with fitness and pool amenities. Marriott's official experiences page lists an included **24-hour fitness centre** and identifies access for hotel guests. Asset World Corporation, the property owner, describes the fitness room as having cardio and strength machines.

The same first-hand sources describe three outdoor pools: a children's pool, a family or water-slide pool and an adult infinity pool. These are hotel facilities, not evidence of a coached fitness programme, public lap-swim session or reservable sports class. The [hotel-gym guide](/guides/hotel-gym-pattaya/) explains why an amenity and a visitor day pass are different products.

## What it costs

The fitness centre is described by Marriott as included for hotel guests. No current operator-published **non-resident gym or pool pass** was found on the pages checked on 26 July 2026. Treat outside access as unconfirmed unless the resort supplies a written offer for the intended date.

Room prices are date-dependent accommodation rates and are not quoted here as a sports-facility tariff. A room booking may include amenities under its own terms, but that does not establish a cash gym price for someone staying elsewhere.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The facilities suit a registered hotel guest who wants on-property cardio or strength equipment and leisure pools. Round-the-clock gym access can be useful for a guest working around another activity, subject to the hotel's age and access rules.

This record does not suit a non-resident who needs guaranteed walk-in entry, a public barbell gym, a coached class or a reserved lap lane. Compare ordinary access products in the [fitness category](/category/fitness/) and public or contact-first pool records in the [swimming-pools guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/).

## Getting there

The resort's official address is **64 Moo 1, Na Chom Thian, Sattahip, Chonburi 20250**. That is in the Na Jomtien coastal corridor, not central Pattaya. Check the exact map pin before choosing transport from a beach-road hotel.

Marriott publishes the fitness centre as open 24 hours, represented here as 00:00-24:00. The pool, spa, reception and restaurant schedules are separate and may vary. Do not infer pool access at every hour from the fitness-centre listing.

## Before you go

Registered guests should ask reception which room key or registration is required, where the fitness centre is, and whether an induction, age restriction, footwear rule or adult supervision policy applies. Reconfirm the pool hours and which pool is intended for adults, families or children.

Non-residents should call **+66 33 168 542** or email **pattaya.reservations@marriott.com** and ask specifically whether a dated gym-only, pool-only or combined facility product exists. Request the full price, hours, inclusions, towel and locker terms, booking rule and access exclusions in writing.

## What we could not verify

The checked first-hand pages did not publish a non-resident tariff, detailed equipment inventory, class timetable, pool-by-pool hours, lap-lane provision, locker policy or day-pass terms. They also did not establish sauna or spa access as part of fitness-centre use. The hotel and guest amenities are confirmed; public sports access is not.
