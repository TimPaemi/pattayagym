# Adventure Divers Pattaya

Adventure Divers offers recreational dive trips and a PADI course path from Discover Scuba Diving through professional training. PADI's current listing identifies it as a PADI 5 Star Dive Center and lists a training pool, classroom and dive boat.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The operator's price page lists:

- passenger or snorkeller: ฿1,500
- Discover Scuba Diving: ฿4,000
- day trip with rental equipment: ฿3,300
- day trip with your own equipment: ฿2,800
- additional dive: ฿1,000

The operator says the day-trip price includes local pickup and drop-off, lunch, tea, coffee and water. Confirm the dive count, equipment, certification or medical requirements and pickup time before paying.

Published hours and phone numbers differ across the operator, PADI and Maps listings. Use the operator phone above and confirm the shop time when booking.
