# Pattaya Padel Club

Pattaya Padel Club is a dedicated padel venue on Soi Mabprachan Lang 2 in Pong. Its official site publishes court hire and private coaching, while the live Maps listing confirms the current address, phone and daily 07:00-22:00 hours.

## Prices

Checked 25 July 2026:

- court hire is **฿600 per hour from 10:00-16:00**;
- court hire is **฿800 per hour from 07:00-10:00 and 16:00-22:00**;
- one-hour coaching is **฿1,600 for one player**, **฿2,000 for two**, **฿2,200 for three** or **฿2,600 for four**.

The official site is the price source. Confirm availability before travelling because padel bookings depend on court and coach capacity.

## First visit

Ask whether racquets and balls are included or rented separately, and arrive early enough to change and warm up. Beginners should say that they are new when booking so the club can advise on a coaching slot or suitable game.
