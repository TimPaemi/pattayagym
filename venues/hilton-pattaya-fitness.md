# Hilton Pattaya Fitness Centre

Hilton's current hotel-information page confirms a fitness centre, spa and outdoor pool among the hotel's fitness and recreation amenities. The hotel listing places Hilton Pattaya at 333/101 Moo 9 above Central Pattaya on Beach Road and publishes +66 38 253 000 as the direct hotel number. Hilton's main property page identifies the outdoor infinity pool on the 16th floor.

## Access and first visit

Hilton does not publish a current public gym day pass, non-resident access policy, fitness-centre timetable or equipment inventory on the checked property pages. The former claims of 24-hour access and an arrangeable outside day pass therefore remain removed.

Registered hotel guests should confirm the fitness-centre entrance and hours at reception because hotel check-in and check-out times are not facility hours. A non-resident should call before travelling and ask whether any dated gym, pool or wellness product is offered to outside guests, what it includes, and whether advance reservation is required. Without that confirmation, this record describes a hotel amenity rather than a public walk-in gym.
