# Nara Maze Pool Day Pass at Terra Nara

Terra Nara explicitly sells its Nara Maze pool pass to people who are not hotel guests. The current operator offer is a leisure-pool product with drinks and a restaurant discount, not a coached-swimming or lap-lane session. It suits a daytime pool visit; swimmers seeking structured training should compare dedicated facilities.

## What training is on offer

The offer provides access to the hotel's outdoor Nara Maze pool from 09:00 to 18:00. Terra Nara presents it as a non-resident pool day rather than a lesson, lane reservation or fitness-centre entry. The operator does not advertise coaching, a measured lap length or a dedicated training lane with this pass.

Use the [Pattaya swimming directory](/category/swimming/) if the priority is independent lap training, children's instruction or a municipal pool rather than leisure access.

## What it costs

The operator's exact promotion page lists **฿500 net per person for non-hotel guests** (Terra Nara pool-pass page, checked 26 July 2026). The amount includes pool access during the published 09:00-18:00 window, free-flow drinking water, one smoothie and 20% off food and drinks excluding alcohol.

The page does not say that a towel, locker, changing-room item, pool toy, gym entry or alcoholic drink is included. Confirm those possible extras when reserving. For another dated non-guest leisure product, compare the [Hard Rock Hotel pool](/gyms/hard-rock-pool/) rather than assuming that two hotel passes with similar prices include the same things.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Nara Maze suits a non-resident who wants a defined daytime pool product and values the included water, smoothie and restaurant discount. The net price makes the core cost clear, while direct reservations are available by phone, Line and Facebook inbox.

It does not suit someone who needs guaranteed lap space, a 25- or 50-metre pool, coached swimming or gym access. The [Pattaya pool guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/) separates leisure passes from training facilities and children's courses.

## Getting there

The pool is at Terra Nara Hotel Pattaya, 239 Moo 9 Beach Road, Nong Prue, Chonburi 20150. The hotel publishes the same address and +66 38 362 500 on its contact information. Use the Terra Nara Maps pin and report to the hotel for pass access rather than treating the pool as a separate street-front facility.

The venue falls within the site's [Naklua and North Pattaya area](/area/naklua/) filter. No operator statement about day-visitor parking, validation or a public-transport route was found on the promotion page.

## Before you go

Reserve or reconfirm through +66 38 362 500, Line **@terranara** or the hotel's Facebook inbox. Give the date and number of people, and ask where non-guests check in and whether advance payment or identification is required. Confirm towel, changing-room, locker and child-access arrangements before arrival.

The promotion states that commercial photography is not allowed. Visitors planning any organised or commercial shoot should not assume that an ordinary pool pass grants permission.
