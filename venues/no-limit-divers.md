# No Limit Divers

## What training is on offer

No Limit Divers publishes products for complete beginners, certified divers and students progressing through PADI training. A certified diver can book local fun dives. A first-timer can ask about the two-dive try-diving product, while the Open Water course is the entry-level certification route.

The operator's Open Water page describes a three-day course with e-learning or paperwork, pool work and four ocean dives. It gives a minimum age of 10, a maximum training depth of 18 metres, and swimming and medical prerequisites. Those are course conditions, not a blanket statement that every applicant will be accepted.

## What it costs

Prices checked on **27 July 2026** were **THB 2,800** for a two-dive certified-diver fun trip including equipment, **THB 3,900** for try diving, and **THB 15,900** for PADI Open Water. The price page also listed six fun dives at **THB 8,500** and ten at **THB 12,000**; it says those packages can be shared with friends or family and remain valid for one year.

Confirm what equipment, boat costs, meals, transfers and learning materials are included in the selected product. Do not infer one course's inclusions from another. Get medical-review, cancellation and weather-rescheduling terms before paying.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This centre suits certified divers comparing local two-dive trips and beginners who want a structured try dive or certification path. The three-day Open Water outline helps travellers decide whether their itinerary can accommodate the classroom, pool and ocean components.

It does not suit anyone who cannot meet the relevant swimming, medical or age conditions, or who needs a dive guaranteed for a particular site regardless of sea conditions. A shop booking is not medical clearance. Tell the operator the certification level, last dive, equipment needs and health considerations truthfully.

## Before you go

Maps publishes shop hours of **08:00-20:00 daily**. The operator says typical trips depart around **08:30-09:00** and return around **14:00-15:00**, but the booking confirmation controls. Its contact page recommends booking one or two days ahead and bringing a towel, swimwear and sunscreen.

For the published Open Water outline, pool work occupies roughly three to five hours on day one, followed by two ocean dives on each of days two and three, shown as 08:00-15:30. Confirm the exact schedule, pickup, course language and required forms.
