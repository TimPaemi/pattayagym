# Play Padel Pattaya

Play Padel is an indoor arena on Kasetsin Soi 3. Courts are booked through Padelmates; the operator says new players booking ahead must prepay, while known players may pay at the arena. Credit-card payment in the app adds THB 100.

## Price and first visit

The operator's current page lists THB 1,200 per court-hour, private coaching from THB 600 per hour and standard racket rental at THB 100 per hour, checked 25 July 2026. Confirm the slot, player count, ball arrangement and cancellation terms before paying.
