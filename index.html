02 How we check

Independent, local, and boringly thorough.

Pattaya.Gym is published by TimPaemi Co., Ltd. — a two-person operation run from Pattaya by Tim and Paemi, alongside our other Pattaya publications.

Every venue is visited or verified against first-hand sources before it gets a page. Prices carry the date we saw them. When a venue closes, moves or changes owner, the page says so instead of quietly staying up.

  • No paid placements. No venue can buy a listing, a ranking or a badge.
  • Prices are dated. A rate with no as-of date is a rumour, so we do not publish one.
  • Thin categories stay thin. One BJJ academy is listed as one, not padded out.
  • Corrections are welcome and get made the same week — gym owners included.
