# Golf Hub Pattaya

Golf Hub operates two Pattaya branches. The Soi Buakhao location is at 350 Moo 9, B201; the East Pattaya branch is at 311/3 Moo 6 on Soi Pornprapanimit, also known as Siam Country Club Road.

The operator lists GCQuad launch-monitor practice, Swing Catalyst analysis, lessons, club fitting and repairs. The East Pattaya branch also lists an indoor putting green and sand bunker.

## Price and first visit

No current public practice, lesson or fitting tariff was found. Both current branch listings and the operator site publish daily hours of 10:00 to 17:00, but the branches use different phone numbers. Contact the branch you intend to visit and ask whether a simulator bay or lesson must be reserved.
