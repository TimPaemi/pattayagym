# Chanthong Badminton Court

The Chanthong Pattaya lists a badminton court among its hotel facilities, and its contact page gives the Pattaya Third Road address and phone. A separate live Maps listing confirms current court activity at the same location.

## Booking and first visit

The live court listing gives daily hours of 10:00–23:30. The hotel does not publish a court tariff, booking method, equipment-hire details or a clear non-resident access policy. Phone before travelling to ask whether outside players can book, whether hotel guests receive priority and what equipment is available.
