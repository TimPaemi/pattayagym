# Koh Larn (Coral Island)

Koh Larn is reached by public boat from Bali Hai Pier. The Tourism Authority of Thailand describes it as a Pattaya day trip and notes that minibus and motorcycle-taxi services are used on the island.

## Ferry information checked 25 July 2026

Pattaya City's published transport table gives a public-ferry fare of ฿30. It lists departures from Pattaya at 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:30, 17:00 and 18:30, with separate return tables for Naban and Tawaen piers.

The municipal PDF does not display a clear revision date, and boat operations can change with weather and local decisions. Confirm the destination pier, final return and fare on the day before boarding.
