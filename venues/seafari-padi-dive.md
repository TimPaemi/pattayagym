# Seafari Diving Center

PADI currently lists Seafari as a 5 Star IDC at Mike Shopping Mall, open daily from 08:00 to 19:00. Activities include recreational scuba, technical diving, snorkelling, conservation and first-aid training; the shop also offers equipment rental and servicing.
