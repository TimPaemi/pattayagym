East Pattaya's golf page is a course comparison, not an indoor-practice or walk-in range list. The two current records are Siam Country Club Pattaya, a four-course operation around Pong, and Phoenix Gold Golf & Country Club in Huai Yai. Both need a booked tee time and an inland journey, but their cost models and course choices differ. A golfer should choose the course and date first rather than treating “East Pattaya golf” as a single nearby product. Neither listing establishes a city-centre practice bay, an instant tee-time confirmation or a fixed transfer fare. Siam Country Club's operator pages distinguish Old Course, Plantation, Waterside and Rolling Hills. Old Course is an 18-hole layout; Plantation has three nine-hole loops; Waterside and Rolling Hills are separate 18-hole choices. As checked 29 July 2026, Old Course visitor green fees are THB 6,500 weekdays and THB 8,500 weekends/public holidays, while the other three courses list THB 5,700 and THB 6,700. Every player also requires an individual THB 1,000 cart and THB 500 caddie, before a separate caddie tip. That puts the published minimum at THB 8,000 weekday or THB 10,000 weekend/holiday for Old Course, and THB 7,200 or THB 8,200 for the other Siam courses before optional rental. Choose the exact Siam course and its map pin; the shared brand name does not guarantee identical facilities or fees. Phoenix Gold offers a different 27-hole structure: Mountain, Ocean and Lakes nines, with a documented driving range, chipping green and putting green. Its current price list gives an 18-hole green fee of THB 3,000 weekdays or THB 3,500 weekends, plus THB 400 for a caddie and THB 700 for a cart: a published THB 4,100 weekday or THB 4,600 weekend stack before tip. For 27 holes, the stated green fee is THB 4,100 weekdays or THB 4,900 weekends, with THB 600 caddie and THB 1,050 cart charges. These are date-specific published figures, not a promise that the desired nine combination, rental set or promotion will be available. Booking mechanics matter. Siam's form asks for player count, date, time, course and club requirement, then needs availability confirmation. Phoenix's daily 06:00–18:00 facility window is not a last tee time; its operator lists +66 84 873 5363 and +66 92 225 8449 for reservations. At both clubs, ask for a written total covering the correct green fee, compulsory or optional cart and caddie arrangement, rental clubs, shoes, payment, deposit, cancellation and weather terms. Phoenix documents rental sets and one-player-per-cart allocation, but stock and the assigned nines remain date-specific. For a golfer staying east of Pattaya, Siam suits a choice among four named course products with a higher published cost stack; Phoenix suits a player who values 18- or 27-hole options and its documented practice facilities. Travel elsewhere when the need is a short indoor session, a public lesson or a guaranteed instant booking. Use the Pattaya golf-course guide after deciding whether course choice, total cost or practice access is the priority.
