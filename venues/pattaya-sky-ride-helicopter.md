# Pattaya Sky Ride

Pattaya Sky Ride's official site is live and advertises Pattaya sightseeing plus Bangkok-Pattaya helicopter transport. The operator publishes a Huai Yai address and three contact numbers: +66 99 775 5573, +66 98 276 5341 and +66 89 099 9974.

The official package page displayed **“No Package Found”** on 25 July 2026. The old record's ฿129,200 charter price, aircraft models, capacities and Royal Cliff landing claim have therefore been removed rather than presented as current.

## Booking

Contact the operator for an itinerary and written quotation. Confirm the departure and landing points, passenger and baggage limits, weather and cancellation terms, identification requirements and the aircraft assigned before paying. Flight availability is weather- and operator-dependent.
