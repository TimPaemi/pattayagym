# Dive Station Pattaya

## Diving and courses

Dive Station publishes daily fun dives, first-dive experiences, SSI Open Water training, continuing education, freediving and professional courses. Technical-diving enquiries are handled directly.

## Current prices

Operator prices checked on 25 July 2026:

- Try Scuba: ฿3,000
- SSI Basic Diver: ฿4,000 sale price; full-day trip, lunch, one confined-water dive and one open-water dive
- Certified-diver day: ฿3,000 sale price; lunch, two dives, tanks and weights
- SSI Open Water course: ฿14,990 sale price; three days, equipment hire and SSI e-learning
- Snorkelling trip: ฿1,350 sale price; full day, lunch, two sites and a local guide
- Certified-diver packages: ฿8,500 for three days, ฿13,000 for five days or ฿25,000 for ten days

The prices are published on the operator's current price page. Standard equipment, boat transfer, an instructor or guide and lunch are included for the listed day trips; a dive computer and torch are extras. Confirm that any sale-labelled price is still available when booking.

## First visit

Send your certification level, recent dive history and equipment needs before booking. Beginners should choose the Basic Diver or Open Water option rather than a certified-diver trip. Shop hours were not clearly published, so arrange arrival time directly.
