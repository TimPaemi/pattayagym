★ About this venue

Chilli Padel Club

The Thai company listing records the operator as บริษัท ชิลลี่ พาเดล จำกัด; the club's official page lists 6 courts at Jomtien.

Chilli Padel Club is a dedicated Jomtien padel venue with six courts, five of them roofed, plus coaching, social events and a bar-led club setup. It suits visitors who want a full padel environment rather than a single rentable court. Its main limitation is that some operational details remain live-booking questions, including the final playable slot and exact rental pricing.

What training is on offer

The club's current official site describes Chilli as a six-court venue on Bun Kanchana 3, with five roofed courts and daily operation. That roof coverage matters in Pattaya because it changes the rainy-season risk. The same site also presents the club as more than simple court hire: coaching, intro sessions, events, a pro shop, a bar and community groups are all part of the current offer.

For beginners, the official site says first-timers can start with a 90-minute intro. For returning players, the site also outlines regular social formats such as Americano, Mexicano and King of the Court. That makes Chilli a strong candidate in the racquet category for players who need partner-matching and club atmosphere, not just a surface to rent.

What it costs

The club's official site says a court costs ฿1,000 per hour for the whole court, for up to four players, and that happy-hour court time starts from ฿600 per hour (official site checked 2026-07-27). Those are whole-court prices, not per-player prices, so groups should divide them accordingly rather than assume the listing is an individual session fee.

The site also says rackets and balls are available to rent, but it does not publish a visible current baht figure for that rental on the checked public pages. The same applies to intro-session pricing and coaching pricing.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Chilli suits four-player groups that want a clear whole-court price, solo visitors who need community events to find a game, and first-timers who want a formal intro instead of figuring out padel from scratch. The roof coverage also makes it one of the better wet-season racquet options in the Jomtien area.

It is less suitable for someone who wants the most stripped-down possible court rental with no club atmosphere, or for someone who insists on seeing every coaching and rental amount published before sending a message.

If your choice is really between social club culture and purely transactional court hire, start with the padel and pickleball guide and then decide whether Chilli’s community layer is what you actually want to pay for.

Getting there

The club lists its address as Bun Kanchana 3, Jomtien, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. The official site and the live Maps identity both point to the same Jomtien base. That places the venue in the south side of the Pattaya urban area rather than in Central Pattaya or the inland Darkside belt.

The operator site positions the club as minutes from the beach, but the useful navigation fact is the Bun Kanchana 3 address and exact pin. If you are combining a session with another Jomtien listing, save the exact map rather than relying on a generic padel search.

Before you go

Check Playtomic or message reception before travelling, especially for evening play. The site says evenings fill first, and the public pages show a mismatch between the official 24:00 closing time and a Maps listing that currently shows 23:00. Use the live booking flow to confirm the actual last slot on your date.

If you are new to padel, ask whether the 90-minute intro includes rackets and balls. If you are arriving solo, ask whether the community groups or scheduled social formats are the best fit for your level. If you are splitting the court with friends, confirm whether the quoted booking is for the whole court or for a named event slot.

What we could not verify

  • No public racket-rental fee or ball-rental fee was visible on the checked pages.
  • No full public coaching price ladder was visible on the checked pages.
  • The official site says daily 07:00-24:00 while Maps currently shows 23:00 close, so the final slot should be checked live.
  • No static public cancellation-policy wording was visible on the checked pages as of 2026-07-27.
