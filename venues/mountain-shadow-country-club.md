## What training is on offer

The Tourism Authority of Thailand's course page lists 18 holes, par 72, a course rating of 72.2 and a total length of 6,804 yards. Its description emphasises narrow, undulating fairways, numerous water hazards, fast greens and extensive out-of-bounds areas. Those are useful indicators of the test: this is not presented as a short beginner layout, and accuracy can matter more than simply hitting for distance.

The same official tourism source lists golf-cart availability. It does not establish whether a cart is optional, whether a caddie is compulsory, or what either service costs in 2026. Ask those questions with the tee-time enquiry rather than treating “golf carts available” as an all-inclusive rate.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Mountain Shadow is most relevant to golfers willing to travel beyond Pattaya for a full 18-hole round and who prefer a tighter course with water, boundary and approach-shot decisions. Higher-handicap players can still consider it, but should ask which tee is appropriate and whether the course has any temporary-green, maintenance or cart-path restrictions on the intended date.

## Before you go

The live listing showed daily 06:00–17:00 hours when checked on 26 July 2026. Confirm the first and last available tee times rather than assuming a full round can start at either edge of the opening window.
