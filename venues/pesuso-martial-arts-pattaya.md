# Pesuso Martial Arts Academy Pattaya

Pesuso teaches taekwondo from beginner fundamentals through belt testing and competition development. Its current official contact page places the Pattaya academy in the taekwondo room on the second floor of Lotus North Pattaya and says enrolment starts at age four.

The Wave 2 backlog used a Chaiyaphruek branch label. Current academy sources also mention a Chaiyapornwithi 15 branch, but they do not publish enough address detail to create a separate record safely. This page therefore covers only the clearly documented North Pattaya location.

## Price and first visit

No current fee or weekly timetable is published. Contact the academy with the student's age, experience and preferred days. Ask about a trial, uniform costs, belt-test fees and which Pattaya branch the class will use.
