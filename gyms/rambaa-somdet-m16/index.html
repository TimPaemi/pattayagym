Rambaa Somdet M16

Rambaa Somdet M16 is a confirmed East Pattaya fight gym on Soi Map Yai Lia 39/1, but it remains a contact-first booking rather than a neatly packaged website transaction. The live Maps identity supports the phone and split daily training periods, while the current Facebook and Instagram presence show active Muay Thai and MMA promotion. It is useful because the gym clearly exists, not because every commercial detail is settled.

What training is on offer

The Thai DASTA place record calls the Soi Map Yai Lia 39/1 venue แรมบ้า สมเดช เอ็ม 16 ยิม and describes it as a Muay Thai, MMA and BJJ gym.

The public identity supports a combat-sports gym rather than a general fitness room. The Facebook page brands the venue around Muay Thai, MMA and BJJ, and the current Instagram reels promote public training at the Pattaya gym. Maps currently lists two daily periods Monday to Saturday, 07:00-09:00 and 15:00-18:30, with Sunday closed.

That does not prove that every discipline runs as a separate class inside both periods. A traveller who wants a specific product such as MMA rounds, BJJ, private coaching or a children's session should ask against the exact date and time rather than translating general business hours into a detailed timetable. For broader comparisons, use the MMA category, the East Pattaya area page and the BJJ and MMA guide.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits someone who wants a smaller East Pattaya combat-gym identity and is willing to message the operator directly. It can also suit visitors who care more about coach access and gym culture than about a polished website.

It does not suit a buyer who needs a clean online rate card, a published weekly class grid or a clear public separation between Muay Thai, MMA and BJJ slots before committing. If you want the more documented side of Pattaya combat training, compare the Muay Thai beginners guide and the BJJ and MMA guide.

The checked public sources did not publish a landmark description, parking note or pickup service. Share the exact pin with the driver and the operator if arrival accuracy matters more than the broad East Pattaya label.

Before you go

Message the gym with the exact discipline you want, the date, your experience level and whether you are asking about one session or something longer. The public content confirms current activity, but not the detailed booking logic that matters on a first visit.

Also ask whether the afternoon session really ends at 18:30 or whether the coached part finishes earlier, whether gloves or shin guards are available, whether sparring is optional, and whether the gym currently offers accommodation or only training. Where public posts conflict, the direct operator reply should outrank the marketing reel.
