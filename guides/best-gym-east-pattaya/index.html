Best gym on the Darkside.

By TimPaemi · 10 min read · Updated 2026-07-30 · How we rank →

East Pattaya spans several inland corridors. Choose by current access product, exact pin and travel plan rather than treating the Darkside as one compact gym district.

Pattaya · 218 venues source-checked · Compare gyms

East Pattaya is a collection of inland corridors, not one walkable gym district. The directory filter currently returns 60 records across 14 categories: ten Muay Thai, ten kids-and-youth, nine racquet, six clubs, five swimming, four fitness, four adventure, three golf, two yoga, two equestrian, two watersports and one each for MMA, functional fitness and climbing. Fifty-nine carry no closure or verification warning in the main dataset. Pattaya Floating Market is the one non-sport result, caught because its address contains Sukhumvit Road; it is not a training choice.

The useful way to choose an east-side venue is by product and exact pin. A group Muay Thai session, a residential combat package, a gym membership, a booked court and a youth academy all require different questions. Start with the East Pattaya area directory for the full filter, then use this guide to match the strongest current evidence to the trip you are planning.

Inland location belts

Khao Talo and Nong Prue hold neighbourhood fitness, combat schools, municipal facilities and several youth programmes. Pong and the Mabprachan area add padel, equestrian sport and other book-ahead facilities. Huai Yai, Chak Nok and Nong Pla Lai include residential camps, badminton, cable wakeboarding and dispersed outdoor venues. Those labels overlap in ordinary usage, and the directory's area rule is intentionally broad.

Do not choose accommodation from the words “East Pattaya” alone. Open the exact Maps pin for the venue and the accommodation, check the road entrance and decide how the return journey works after an early or late session. The directory does not publish fixed taxi fares, guaranteed pickup coverage or universal travel times. If a camp offers a transfer, get the pickup location, date, flight or bus details and any surcharge in writing.

Short coached combat sessions

Sudsakorn Muay Thai Gym is the clearest East Pattaya choice for a visitor who wants a current, ordinary group-training tariff. The operator publishes Monday–Saturday training from 08:00–11:00 and 16:00–19:00, with Sunday closed. Prices checked on 25 July 2026 were ฿400 for one session, ฿600 for two sessions in one day, ฿1,800 for one week at one session per day, ฿3,000 for one week at two per day, ฿6,000 for a month at one per day and ฿9,000 for a month at two per day.

That menu lets a traveller compare training frequency without pretending every three-hour operating window is one continuous coached class. Contact the gym with your experience level and selected date. Ask about the session start, equipment, private coaching, sparring expectations and whether advance booking is required.

Castra Gym is a different product: a Khao Talo weights-and-cardio gym with a separate fight-club timetable. Its current operator page lists Muay Thai Monday–Friday at 09:00–10:00 and 17:30–18:30, and BJJ on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 19:00–20:00. Fight-club prices checked on 25 July were ฿300 for one group class, ฿2,500 for ten group classes valid for two months or ฿600 for a private class, with gym access included. A current general gym membership tariff was not published, so contact the gym if weights access rather than a coached class is the main need.

Residential combat programmes

Silk Muay Thai and Kombat Group Thailand are relevant when accommodation is part of the purchase. Silk's operator pages list group periods Monday–Saturday and packages from ฿16,000 for one week, ฿34,000 for one month with one daily Muay Thai session and ฿39,000 for a month with two daily sessions, checked on 25 July 2026. The one-week starting product includes a private room, one daily session, a fundamentals class and one meal; package inclusions vary. The site also describes pool, sauna, spa and restaurant access within the residential offer.

Kombat's live pricing page listed a one-week standard Muay Thai or western-boxing package at ฿20,900 on the same check date. The operator says its displayed prices exclude 7% VAT and that card payments add 3%. Kombat also sells MMA and BJJ programmes, but prices and inclusions vary by discipline, room and package tier. Neither starting figure is a normal drop-in price.

Compare the final invoice, not the headline. Request the exact room, bathroom and air-conditioning standard, training frequency, meal count, laundry, equipment, recovery access, transfer, arrival and departure dates, taxes, card charge and refund terms. Someone who already has accommodation should compare the training-only value separately rather than assuming a residential bundle will be cheaper.

MMA and functional training need direct confirmation

Rambaa Somdet M16 is an active Nong Prue Muay Thai and MMA gym. Its current listing publishes two periods Monday–Saturday, 07:00–09:00 and 15:00–18:30, with Sunday closed. Rambaa Somdet's fight history supports the MMA association, but it does not prove which discipline is taught in each period or establish a visitor tariff. Contact the gym specifically about MMA, grappling, private coaching and beginner eligibility.

Jungle Gym Pattaya publishes functional group sessions Monday–Friday at 09:00–10:00 and 19:00–20:00, plus Saturday 09:00–10:00. Open gym or private training can be arranged between weekday sessions. The important status detail is that the official CrossFit Games directory marks the former CrossFit Pattaya affiliate as departed. Treat Jungle Gym as a current functional-fitness venue, not an active licensed CrossFit affiliate. No stable current drop-in or membership tariff was found.

Racquet sport around Mabprachan and Huai Yai

Pattaya Padel Club beside Mabprachan publishes daily hours of 07:00–22:00 and time-band court pricing. Checked on 25 July 2026, a whole court was ฿600 per hour from 10:00–16:00 and ฿800 from 07:00–10:00 or 16:00–22:00. One-hour coaching ranged from ฿1,600 for one player to ฿2,600 for four. Ask whether rackets and balls are included and whether a solo visitor can book coaching or join a suitable social game.

SB Badminton Huai Yai publishes the simplest badminton comparison: ฿180 per court-hour and daily operation from 13:00–23:00, checked on 25 July. That fee is for the court; equipment charges were not published. The broader east filter contains nine racquet records, but most require direct contact for a current tariff. Select the sport first, then compare the whole court, equipment and travel plan.

Football and youth programmes

Planet Football Pattaya operates two separate East Pattaya stadiums. Its Siam Country Club Road headquarters includes 7-a-side, enclosed 5-a-side and multi-sports-hall spaces; FC Planet Stadium off Phon Prapha Nimit 34 Road has a full-size artificial-turf pitch configurable for 7-, 9- or 11-a-side use. The exact stadium matters because the programmes move between them.

The operator publishes Saturday 09:00–10:30 open sessions at ฿400 per child for ages 4–6, 7–9 and 10–13. Its Development Programme serves ages 7–15 over Saturday, Sunday and Monday sessions at the two venues. Tuition is ฿2,500 per calendar month, while a separate ฿2,500 annual membership includes three kits and a training ball. FC Planet Academy is invite-only, normally reached through the open or development pathway. Families should send the child's age and experience before paying and confirm the pin, footwear, shin pads, kit, first-session process and full cost.

K Football Stadium Pattaya is a different type of football record: an indoor artificial-turf pitch on Moo 9 with a current operator identity, phone and 24-hour booking label. No current owner tariff, pitch format or equipment inclusion was published. The 24-hour label means possible booking availability, not guaranteed walk-in space. Call with the team size, date and preferred duration, then confirm deposit, footwear, lighting and ball arrangements.

Book-ahead outdoor and specialist sport

Thai Wake Park Pattaya in Nong Pla Lai publishes cable-wakeboarding hours of 10:30–18:30 on weekdays and 09:00–18:30 at weekends. It schedules lower-speed beginner periods from opening until noon on weekdays and 09:00–10:30 at weekends. Basic board and life jacket are included in riding rates, while a helmet is mandatory and available to rent. The live booking widget did not expose a readable numeric tariff without starting a booking, so use it for the exact date, duration, lesson and equipment upgrade.

Horseshoe Point is the current equestrian reference in Pong. Its operator describes private and group lessons, trail rides and pony rides, and a March 2026 competition schedule confirms current equestrian use. No stable public lesson tariff or weekly lesson schedule was found. Contact the venue about non-resident eligibility, rider age and experience, clothing, helmet provision and event restrictions.

Decision checklist

  • One or two coached combat sessions: start with Sudsakorn's dated session menu or Castra's fight-club timetable.
  • Weights plus scheduled combat: ask Castra for the current general gym tariff and confirm the selected class.
  • Stay-and-train: compare Silk and Kombat only after obtaining itemised inclusions, taxes and room terms.
  • Padel or badminton: book the exact court, time band and equipment rather than using a per-person assumption.
  • Youth football: match age and level to Planet Football's open, development or academy pathway.
  • Team pitch hire: contact K Football with the team format and booking duration; no public rate is established.

Blank prices in this guide mean no stable current operator-published tariff was found, not free admission. Before choosing a long-stay base, verify the venue's current map, schedule and access product, then test the route at the time of day you will actually travel. For a beach-corridor alternative, compare the Central Pattaya guide or Jomtien guide.

FAQ

Is East Pattaya practical without a scooter?

It can be, when accommodation is close to one chosen venue or the camp provides a documented transfer. The area spans several inland corridors, and the directory does not guarantee taxi availability or a fixed fare. Check the exact route and return plan before booking accommodation; do not ride without the appropriate licence, insurance and experience.

Which East Pattaya option has the clearest drop-in price?

For coached Muay Thai, Sudsakorn publishes ฿400 for one session and ฿600 for two in one day, checked 25 July 2026. Castra publishes a ฿300 fight-club group class. For court sport, SB Badminton lists ฿180 per court-hour and Pattaya Padel Club lists ฿600 or ฿800 per court-hour by time band. Reconfirm the selected product and date.

Can a family book Planet Football as a casual public pitch?

The youth coaching pages publish age-banded sessions and development programmes, while the facilities page provides separate booking contacts. A coaching fee does not establish a whole-pitch hire price. Contact Planet Football with the child's age for a programme or with the team size and duration for facility hire, and confirm which of its two stadiums is used.
