# SailBreeze Yacht Charter

SailBreeze is based at Ocean Marina and presents private yacht charters, marine services and crewed shared sailing cruises. Its published shared products are outings led by a qualified captain and crew. They should not be confused with a formal learn-to-sail course: the same site recommends a separate established school for international certification.

## What training is on offer

The current shared-cruise pages describe full-day and half-day sailing experiences rather than lessons. The full-day product is listed as eight hours. The overview calls the half-day option 4.5 hours, while its detail page says four hours; confirm the actual boarding and return times before booking.

The operator also advertises private charters, but no universal vessel, route or passenger count applies across every enquiry. Readers seeking other sea activities can compare the [Pattaya watersports directory](/category/watersports/) and the [diving and watersports guide](/guides/diving-watersports-pattaya/).

## What it costs

The official full-day page, checked on 26 July 2026, lists **฿3,250 for an adult**, **฿1,625 for a child aged 3–12**, and no charge for a child under three. It lists hotel pickup and drop-off, freshwater shower, towel, snorkel gear and fins, buffet lunch, island entrance fees and Ocean Marina fees among the inclusions.

The half-day detail page lists **฿2,500 per person excluding VAT**. It lists shower, towel, snorkel equipment, snacks and fruit, island fees and marina fees. Optional jet ski, banana and sofa activities cost extra. Ask for the VAT-inclusive total and exact product name.

The general booking page requests a 50% deposit and says the balance is due 40 days before the trip, with full payment for bookings inside 40 days. Confirm in writing that those terms apply to the shared cruise selected.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

SailBreeze may suit someone who wants a crewed marina day with a published shared-cruise price and stated inclusions. The two durations create a choice between a longer outing and a shorter product, once the half-day timing conflict is resolved.

It does not suit someone seeking verified sailing certification or a scheduled instructional syllabus. It is also a weak fit for a traveller who cannot confirm weather, cancellation and accessibility arrangements before paying. The [Sattahip area page](/area/sattahip/) gives broader southern-coast context but does not imply a transfer from every Pattaya hotel.

## Getting there

The operator identifies Ocean Marina in Na Chom Thian as its base. Use the exact booking instructions and map pin because a marina complex has controlled meeting points; arriving at the general property does not establish the boat or boarding location.

The full-day page lists hotel pickup and drop-off as an inclusion, but the pickup area, timetable and applicability to every address were not stated in the retained evidence. Confirm the exact hotel and collection time.

## Before you go

Contact +66 82 320 8506 for English, +66 61 658 2707 for Thai, or +66 64 951 1122 for Thai or Chinese. Request the product name, vessel, date, departure and return time, passenger ages, route, pickup, VAT-inclusive total and payment schedule in one written confirmation.

Ask about weather cancellation, seasickness, swimming ability, lifejackets, insurance and accessibility. Do not assume optional water activities or a sailing lesson are included.

- The operator's half-day pages disagree between four and 4.5 hours.
- The booking page's terms need confirmation for the selected shared product.
- Vessel assignment, capacity, insurance detail and weather-refund terms were not published consistently.
