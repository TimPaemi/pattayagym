# Deep Climbing Gym

## Closed

Deep Climbing Gym is retained as a closure finding. The exact live Maps listing is marked permanently closed, and the current Harborland operator site does not present Deep as an active attraction.

Pattaya Mail's opening report confirms that this was the climbing venue on Harbor Pattaya's eighth floor. Its former hours, telephone, wall specifications and admission prices have been removed because they are no longer useful for planning a visit.
