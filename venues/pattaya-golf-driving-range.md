## Who it suits — and who it does not

The range suits a golfer seeking ball practice in North Pattaya and a PSC member who can use the documented tray benefit. The current **07:00-22:00** Maps schedule supports both early and evening planning, subject to bay availability.

It is less suitable for someone who needs a guaranteed English-speaking coach, a published rental-club set or a climate-controlled simulator. The [Pattaya course guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) is the better route for a full round, while [Golf Hub Pattaya](/gyms/golf-hub-pattaya/) is a different indoor product.
