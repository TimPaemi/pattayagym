Course availability, dive sites and the exact sequence of practical sessions are operator decisions; the page does not turn them into a fixed personal itinerary.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The Discover Scuba and Open Water listings show that beginners have a documented course route; certified divers have boat-diving and package options to discuss with the centre. The evidence does not confirm that every medical history, certification level, child age or equipment requirement is accepted. A prospective diver should describe certification and recent-dive history accurately rather than treating the directory as clearance to dive.

A boat itinerary can change with weather and conditions, and the operator determines the safe site choice. Confirm equipment needs, start time and inclusions for the selected course or package.
