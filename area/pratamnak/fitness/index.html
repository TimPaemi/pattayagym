Pratamnak's fitness results cover three different access models: public commercial gyms, a public outdoor exercise park and hotel or club facilities whose outside access varies. The strongest transparent visitor products are Fitz Club and Muscle Factory Pattaya. SUN Fitness and Human Strong Gym are active but publish no dependable current tariff. Pratumnak Fitness Park is self-directed outdoor exercise, while Cross Pattaya Pratamnak and InterContinental Pattaya are accommodation-led records rather than guaranteed public gyms. Fitz Club has the clearest baht-denominated day product. Royal Cliff's January 2026 outside-guest tariff lists THB 800 per adult and THB 400 for a child under 12, including gym, pool, sauna and steam but excluding tennis and squash. Personal training and private Thai boxing or sparring are separately listed at THB 1,100 per hour. Gym, classes, sauna and steam operate 07:00-21:00; courts and pool run 07:00-20:00. Children under 14 are not permitted in the fitness centre, so a child pool or court product must not be described as child gym access. Muscle Factory publishes current US-dollar products instead of a Pattaya baht tariff. Its store, checked 29 July 2026, lists US$20 for one drop-in, US$105 for seven drop-ins, a US$128 one-month sale price and a US$1,280 twelve-month sale price. Do not convert those figures or import terms from another branch. The Pattaya venue lists daily 07:00-22:00 hours, but no complete equipment inventory, class timetable, locker, towel or joining-fee policy is public. Call +66 83 695 9196 with the pass length and essential equipment before paying. SUN confirms one membership card across its Buakhao, Pratamnak and Naklua branches but publishes no stable day, month or annual price. The Buakhao and Pratamnak branches list midnight closes, yet the branch equipment and staffed activation periods remain unclear. Human Strong lists free weights, resistance machines, cardio equipment, personal training, Muay Thai and boxing, with weekday 07:00-22:00 and weekend 08:00-22:00 hours. It publishes neither a phone nor a tariff or combat timetable. For both, ask for the exact branch, product, total and coaching arrangement in writing. Pratumnak Fitness Park is the non-commercial alternative at Chaloem Phrakiat Park. Municipal evidence supports a walking and running surface, multipurpose court and 12 outdoor exercise stations, but no formal daily timetable, tariff, lighting schedule, shower, locker or supervised class. A public-park label is not proof that every apparatus or organised activity is always free and available. Cross publishes Cloud Club from 06:00-22:00 and pools from 07:00-20:00, with private yoga or personal training arranged on 24 hours' notice, but no non-resident gym or pool pass. InterContinental similarly documents a fitness centre, three lagoon-style pools and spa facilities for its resort context without publishing outside access or current fitness-centre hours. Choose either only as a registered guest or after written access confirmation. For a Pratamnak stay, Fitz is the clearest combined public day pass, Muscle Factory the clearest short commercial entry, and the Pratamnak gym guide the wider comparison.
