★ About this venue

The Gym Boxing & Fitness

The Gym Boxing & Fitness is a current boxing-led exercise gym on the second floor of Atara Mall in Sriracha. It suits travellers or residents who want a mixed boxing-and-general-fitness space rather than a pure weights room. Its limitation is pricing transparency: the active public channels confirm the venue identity, hours and family-friendly positioning, but they do not publish a stable rate card or fixed weekly timetable.

What training is on offer

The venue's current Facebook identity and recent social posts describe a boxing and fitness gym rather than a single-discipline Muay Thai camp. The public material also shows that the gym welcomes both adults and children, and Atara Mall's own post confirms it as a current second-floor tenant.

The strongest supported programme facts are broad rather than highly scheduled. Public posts clearly frame the venue around boxing exercise and general fitness, and one recent Instagram post specifically promotes a class format for children. That is enough to present the gym honestly in the fitness category and to flag it for parents comparing broader kids and youth sport options.

What it costs

No stable public price list was confirmed on the checked pages as of 2026-07-27. That includes day-pass, monthly-membership, children's-class and private-session pricing. The public profiles are useful for identity and opening hours, but they do not expose a reliable dated tariff that can be promoted into the directory without guessing.

What is public is the trial message. The venue's current Facebook profile text says one trial class is free and asks visitors to reserve it by message. If cost matters, ask the gym for the exact first-visit product, the full membership options and whether kids' sessions are priced separately from adult sessions.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The Gym Boxing & Fitness suits someone who wants coached striking exercise plus general conditioning in one room, especially if Atara Mall is convenient. It also suits parents who want a current gym that explicitly markets children's training rather than assuming all boxing rooms handle juniors.

It is less suitable for a traveller who needs a fully published public tariff before leaving the hotel, or for someone who wants a known open-gym-only strength product with no coaching element.

If the deciding factor is a child-friendly sport option, pair this record with the kids and youth guide. If the deciding factor is flexible solo fitness, compare it with the wider fitness listings before messaging.

Getting there

The gym is at 222/6 Srirachanakhon 3 Road inside Atara Mall, on the second floor. Both the gym's public pages and the mall's own post support that location. The current public opening window shown in recent profile text is daily from 10:00 to 22:00.

Because the venue sits inside a mall, use the mall entrance and then head upstairs rather than looking for a street-front standalone fight camp. The record keeps both public contact numbers because different current public posts expose different numbers.

Before you go

Message before travelling. That matters here because the venue promotes a free trial session and children’s training, but the public pages do not show a fixed class board. Ask whether the first session is coached, what age range the junior sessions cover, what gloves or wraps you need to bring and whether the trial must be booked for a specific time.

If you are going for a child, send the child’s age and experience level rather than just asking “can kids join?” If you are going as an adult, ask whether the session is boxing-focused, general fitness or a mixed class. The public channels make the venue look active; they just do not answer all the practical first-visit questions on their own.
