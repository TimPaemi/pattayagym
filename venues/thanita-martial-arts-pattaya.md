# Thanita Martial Arts Pattaya

Thanita Martial Arts is a children's academy at The Chill in Nong Prue. Its current Facebook identity advertises taekwondo, hapkido, pencak silat and kickboxing, and the 2026 FightingTKD system records the team in Thailand's 19th national pencak-silat championship.

The live Maps listing confirms the address, phone and weekly hours. Recent Thai-language Maps reviews refer to children training here over multiple years and focus on attentive teaching, discipline and a friendly class environment.
