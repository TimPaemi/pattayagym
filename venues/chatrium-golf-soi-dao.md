# Chatrium Golf Resort Soi Dao Chanthaburi

Chatrium Soi Dao is an 18-hole resort course in Chanthaburi, roughly three hours from Pattaya. The earlier directory record incorrectly called it a 27-hole course.

## 2026 renovation status

The full-course closure ended on 24 July. From **25 July through 30 October 2026**, the operator says holes 10–18 are open while holes 1–9 remain under renovation. All 18 holes are due to reopen on 1 November. Confirm the available loop and tee time before making the long journey.

## Current stay-and-play offers

The live nine-hole package starts at ฿2,850 per night and includes breakfast, nine-hole green fee, caddie and cart. The 18-hole package starts at ฿3,700 per night; during the works, this uses the open nine twice. Both are hotel packages, not standalone walk-in green fees.
