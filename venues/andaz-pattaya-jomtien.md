# Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach

Andaz Pattaya Jomtien Beach has a 24-hour fitness centre with Technogym cardio and strength equipment and a separate yoga and stretching room. The resort also lists three pools, an onsen, sauna and steam room.

Hyatt describes these as activities and facilities exclusively for registered hotel guests. No current public gym or pool day pass was found.

## Classes and first visit

The resort programme can include activities such as yoga, Pilates, circuit training, aqua boxing and basic Thai boxing. The timetable changes, so guests should use the current programme supplied by the hotel and ask which sessions require advance registration.
