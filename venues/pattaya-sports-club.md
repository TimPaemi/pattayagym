# Pattaya Sports Club Association

Pattaya Sports Club Association is a membership office and sports network on Pattaya 3rd Road, not a single all-in-one sports complex. Its current site ties the club to golf administration, social sport, member discounts and information across several activities. That makes it useful for someone who wants the PSC membership product, but not for a visitor expecting to walk into one building and find every sport named on the website.

## What training is on offer

The Thai directory labels the 3/197 Pattaya 3rd Road office **พัทยา สปอร์ต คลับ** in its building entry.

PSC does not present itself as a coached gym or court venue. The current website instead works as a club-and-membership hub covering golf services, discounts and activity information across sports such as badminton, cricket, lawn bowls, padel, pickleball, tennis and table tennis. That means this [clubs](/category/clubs/) record is about membership access and club administration rather than an on-site lesson timetable.

For a traveller, the practical distinction matters. PSC can be relevant if you want its network, discount structure or golf administration support. It is not a substitute for opening a venue record with its own court, pool or class schedule. For a central-area venue shortlist, compare it alongside the [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) rather than assuming the office itself supplies every sport in the club's network.

## What it costs

PSC's join page listed one year of membership at ฿600 plus a ฿400 signup fee, or six years at ฿3,000 plus the same ฿400 signup fee (operator join page, checked 2026-07-27). Those are membership charges for the association itself, not a general public day pass to a central sports centre. If you are joining for a specific benefit, ask whether the named discount or arrangement still applies on the date you plan to use it.

This is also where many readers can get misled by the low headline amount. A cheap association membership is not evidence that court hire, golf balls, green fees, lessons or outside-club venue access are included. Treat PSC as an admin-and-membership product first, and ask for the total cost of the exact activity you want to do.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

PSC suits a resident, long-stay visitor or repeat golfer who wants a local sports association membership and is prepared to use its network rather than one central facility.

It is not the right fit for a traveller who simply wants to book a court, lift weights or join a coached session today. Those readers should open the specific venue or guide for the sport itself. For example, a person looking for a current central combat or gym session will do better with the [best Central Pattaya gym guide](/guides/best-gym-central-pattaya/) than with an association office record.

## Getting there

The current public address is 3/197 Pattaya 3rd Road, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. The office sits in the central Pattaya belt rather than inside a beachside resort or sports campus. The operator site exposes 095 706 2646, and the record also retains 038 415 424 from club contact information. Maps currently shows Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 opening and weekend closure, which is consistent with an office-style access model.

Because this is an admin base rather than a sports hall, save the exact map and arrive expecting paperwork or information, not an all-day venue environment.

## Before you go

Ask PSC which exact benefit or sport service you want before you join. If the reason is golf administration, discount access or membership proof for another service, request the current process, accepted payment, membership issue timing and whether the benefit starts immediately. That is more useful than asking only whether the club is "open".

A first-time visitor should also ask whether the named sport activity is handled by PSC itself or by another operator that recognises PSC membership. The site's list of sports is broader than the office's on-site function.
