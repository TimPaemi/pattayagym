# KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya

KBA's Pattaya branch operates from Blue Lagoon in Na Jomtien. Its current location page lists kitesurf, kitefoil, efoil, wingfoil and Wing SUP, together with equipment rental and a shop. Training is weather-dependent, so arrange a time directly rather than arriving on the assumption that a session will run.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The operator's general kitesurf page lists a one-hour trial at ฿2,200, a six-hour beginner course at ฿11,000 and a twelve-hour full course at ฿20,000, with equipment included. Its current gift-card page lists an efoil introduction from ฿2,000 for 30 minutes and wingfoil at the same ฿2,200 / ฿11,000 / ฿20,000 course points.

These are operator-wide published products. Confirm availability and the exact Pattaya booking total, wind window and cancellation terms with the Blue Lagoon branch before paying.
