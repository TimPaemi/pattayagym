# MAX Muay Thai Stadium Pattaya

MAX Muay Thai Stadium is a spectator fight venue on Sukhumvit Road, not a walk-in Muay Thai training gym. Its official website carries a live event calendar, ticket route and seating plan, while the current Maps listing confirms the stadium identity and address. People looking for coached pads, sparring or a daily camp should use the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/) instead.

## Current event evidence

The operator calendar is the useful source because fight times are event-specific. When checked on 26 July 2026, it showed The Global Fight cards at 19:00 on Monday 27 July and Tuesday 28 July, with five bouts listed on each card. Those dated listings demonstrate current programming; they do not establish a permanent nightly timetable. Check the calendar again for your intended date.

The official ticket page provides a seating-plan image and routes buyers to the stadium's selected sales channels. It does not expose a dependable exact operator tariff in text, so this record does not repeat older ticket prices or amounts shown by resellers. Confirm the section, final price, fees, delivery method and refund terms before payment.

The bout names and weights shown on a dated card are also provisional event information, not a guarantee that every advertised matchup will proceed unchanged. If a particular fighter or bout is the reason for attending, ask the office whether the card has been revised. A seating plan explains location inside the stadium; it does not by itself establish availability, accessibility arrangements or which section a sales channel will issue.

## Contact and arrival

The operator contact page lists the office as open daily from 13:00 to 21:00 and publishes +66 80 471 6008 for calls and WhatsApp. Maps also lists +66 38 416 999. Use the operator number first for event and ticket questions; the second number is retained as an alternate listing contact.

The stadium address is 42/108 Moo 9 on Sukhumvit Pattaya 42 Road. Open the [Central Pattaya area page](/area/central-pattaya/) and the map pin before leaving: “Central Pattaya” in the directory describes the wider mapped corridor, not a promise that the entrance is on Beach Road. Ask when doors open, whether a digital booking needs exchange at the box office, and which entrance serves your seating section.

## Before booking

Send the stadium your intended date and party size, then ask these questions:

- Is a fight card confirmed for that date, and what is the advertised start time?
- Which seating sections are still available, and what is the all-in price?
- Is the ticket issued immediately or collected at the venue?
- Are child admissions or age restrictions different?
- What happens if the card or start time changes?

Fight cards can change. Treat the live event page and direct confirmation as the final check, rather than relying on an old “daily fights” description.
