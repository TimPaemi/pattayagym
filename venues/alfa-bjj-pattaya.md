# ALFA BJJ Pattaya

ALFA BJJ Pattaya is **closed as a standalone academy**. In an owner post dated 22 November 2024, the academy said the ALFA page would receive no further updates and directed followers to instructor Andrea Signorini’s BJJ page. The shared notice placed his Friday classes at Rage Fight Academy; it did not establish that every current class still runs there. The exact ALFA Maps identity remains marked permanently closed.

The UAEJJF club page is retained as historical identity evidence, not proof of a current venue. For present-day BJJ training, check the current operator schedule rather than travelling to the former ALFA address.
