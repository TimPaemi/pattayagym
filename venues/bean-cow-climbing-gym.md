# STICKY Climbing Gym

Thai Maps labels the Huai Yai indoor climbing venue **STICKY climbing gym(ยิมปีนเขา)** and lists Tuesday-Sunday 11:00-20:00 current access.

STICKY is the current operating identity for the Huai Yai climbing gym formerly known as Bean Cow. The current Maps place and July 2026 owner posts support the same address and a live STICKY rebrand, so this remains a valid [climbing record](/category/climbing/) even though the operator still uses older Bean Cow social URLs.

## What training is on offer

The strongest current evidence is the July 2026 STICKY launch post published through the former Bean Cow Facebook page. That post describes a 900-square-metre climbing gym with air conditioning, more than 100 routes and separate training and stretching space. The venue record and active social identity also support indoor bouldering plus roped climbing under the new STICKY name. That is enough to establish a real climbing venue rather than a stale rebrand notice.

What the public material does not yet do is split those routes into a stable bouldering count versus top-rope or lead count, or publish a beginner-course structure. If you need a coached first session rather than an independent wall visit, message the operator before travelling and ask whether the chosen time is suitable for a first-timer. The [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) is the right comparison page if the Huai Yai location matters more than the climbing format.

## What it costs

No dependable current rate card was published in the checked first-hand sources. A July 2026 owner post promoted a group discount, but the post snippet available during this check did not expose the standard walk-in amount or the exact terms of the offer. Because the old Bean Cow pricing belongs to the former identity and may no longer be current after the STICKY relaunch, this page leaves the price blank rather than carrying old numbers forward.

The useful call is to ask for the base entry price first, then confirm shoe hire, harness hire, belay requirements, lesson costs, children's access and whether the quoted amount is per person or per session. Missing published price is a contact step, not a reason to drop a confirmed operating venue.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

STICKY suits a climber who is willing to confirm the exact session and rental setup directly with the operator and wants a real indoor wall east of central Pattaya. It is also useful for a group comparing climbing with other non-gym sport options in the [East Pattaya area](/area/east-pattaya/).

It does not suit someone who needs an immediately bookable online checkout, a fully published beginner programme or a fixed tariff visible before contact. Readers who want a broader activity comparison should use the [climbing guide](/guides/climbing-pattaya/) once they have narrowed the district.

## Before you go

Current owner information lists Tuesday to Sunday opening from 11:00 to 20:00, with Monday closed and an extra closure on the first day of each month. That is unusual enough to reconfirm before travelling. The public pages checked did not publish a phone number, so contact is likely to start through social messaging. Include your date, group size, experience, equipment needs and whether anyone needs a first-time introduction.
