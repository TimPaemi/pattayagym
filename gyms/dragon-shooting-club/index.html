★ About this venue

Dragon Shooting Club

Dragon Shooting Club is an indoor shooting range at 105/2 Nong Mai Kaen 12, Moo 8, Bang Lamung. The operator site also uses Golden Dragon Shooting Club for the same location. The current Maps listing gives +66 64 794 2625, while the operator site gives +66 80 063 8222; both numbers remain visible because the live sources disagree.

What training is on offer

A Thai booking listing calls the 105/2 Moo 8 range พัทยาดราก้อนชูตติ้งคลับ and describes it as a live-fire facility.

The operator presents instructor-led shooting packages at the indoor range. The package page is evidence of a supervised range product, but does not establish a named coaching curriculum, competition programme, recurring class schedule or a particular firearm being available at every booking. Range instruction and safety procedures should be confirmed directly with the operator.

What it costs

The operator package page, checked on 2026-07-29, lists two firearms with 30 shots at ฿2,000; three firearms with 30 shots at ฿3,000; four firearms with 40 shots at ฿4,500; three firearms with 50 shots at ฿6,000; and four firearms with 85 shots at ฿7,500. It also lists non-package options from ฿1,000. The selected firearms and ammunition count are part of the product, so the headline price alone is not enough to compare packages.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The published packages are the clearest documented route for a visitor seeking an instructor-led range experience. They do not confirm eligibility for every visitor, a children’s programme, disability accommodation, competitive training, a membership option or an unsupervised session. Contact the range in advance if any of those conditions affects the planned visit.

Getting there

The stated address is 105/2 Nong Mai Kaen 12, Moo 8, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. Use the exact Maps link in the record. Because Maps and the operator page publish different phone numbers, start with +66 64 794 2625 and try +66 80 063 8222 if necessary; the difference is disclosed rather than silently choosing one unsupported contact.

Before you go

The range publishes daily hours of 09:00-18:00. Confirm the selected package, firearms, ammunition count, identification requested and the booking time before travelling. Follow the range officer’s safety directions throughout the session; this directory does not substitute for the operator’s range rules.

What we could not verify

The sources do not establish a detailed age policy, live firearm inventory, languages for every instructor, group-size limit, cancellation terms, accessibility, transport service or walk-in availability. The dated package prices and normal hours are published; the remaining operating terms need direct confirmation.
