## Before you go

Maps lists **06:00–22:00 daily**, checked 26 July 2026. The listing showed Thai public-holiday warnings for the coming week, so confirm the date if travelling during a holiday. Recent owner media supports current activity but does not replace a reservation or rate confirmation.

Tell the range whether the player needs only a bay and balls or also clubs and instruction. Ask about peak periods, advance booking, footwear, dress, children, left-handed equipment, weather closure, last basket sale and the policy if practice is interrupted.
