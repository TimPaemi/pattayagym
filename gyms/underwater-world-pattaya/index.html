★ About this venue

Underwater World Pattaya

Underwater World Pattaya is a South Pattaya aquarium on Sukhumvit Road, not a dive school or swim-training venue. The operator currently publishes daily 09:00-18:00 opening, a 17:30 last-admission cut-off and a clear acrylic tunnel more than 105 metres long.

What training is on offer

This is an observation and education product rather than a coached watersports session. The operator's activity page currently lists the aquarium tunnel, a touch pool, a magic tank, reptile and otter presentations, and feeding sessions in the coral reef, open ocean and Giant of Siam zones. Those are visitor features, not proof of a scuba lesson, snorkel class or hands-on animal programme.

That distinction matters inside the watersports category. If you want a real dive or boat-training transaction, use the diving and watersports guide or compare actual operators such as Pattaya Dive Centre. Underwater World is more useful as a family or rainy-day marine stop than as a substitute for in-water training.

The current operator pages also promote school and group visits, but that still does not turn the aquarium into a sporting venue in the usual gym-or-club sense.

What it costs

The operator's current public ticketing checked 2026-07-27 lists ฿550 for visitors over 131 cm and ฿320 for children 91-130 cm. Those are the ordinary walk-in admissions stored in frontmatter, taken from the operator's own site rather than an old tourism listing.

The separate schools-programme page lists a different product: ฿90 per nursery or primary student, ฿120 per secondary student and ฿150 per university student, with one teacher free per 15 students and one complimentary guide. Those are group-education rates, not a public day-pass shortcut for individual visitors.

No current annual pass, resident discount, family bundle or marine-experience add-on price was verified from the checked first-hand pages. If you are booking for a school, group or company, ask which programme version applies and whether food, guide time or transport changes the total.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits families, school groups and travellers who want a timed indoor attraction with a clear admissions policy.

It does not suit someone who wants lane swimming, freediving practice, a discover-scuba session or a certified course. For that, move to the Central Pattaya area page only as a location filter, then pick a genuine operator from the watersports category or the diving and watersports guide. The aquarium is a paid attraction, not a sports lesson.

Getting there

The operator publishes 22/22 Moo 11, Sukhumvit Road, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150 with phone +66 38 756 878. Use the exact live pin rather than a generic South Pattaya aquarium search, because this is a Sukhumvit Road stop rather than a Beach Road walk-in.

The contact page makes the location straightforward, but it does not publish a baht-bus route, parking rules or a guaranteed drop-off bay. Send the exact pin to the driver or booking desk instead of relying on a broad area label.

Before you go

Check the current activity page on the same day if you care about a particular feeding or presentation slot. The operator lists the aquarium as open daily from 09:00 to 18:00 with last admission at 17:30, so arriving too late can turn a full-price ticket into a shortened visit.

If you are booking a school or private group, ask which programme applies, whether the teacher and guide allowances still match the public page, and how early the group must check in.

What we could not verify

  • No current annual pass, resident discount or family-package page was found.
  • No current scuba, snorkel or other in-water training product was verified from the operator's checked pages.
  • Locker rules, stroller policy, accessibility details and parking terms were not published in the checked first-hand sources.
  • The checked pages did not confirm a current corporate-event tariff or any separately priced interactive animal session.
