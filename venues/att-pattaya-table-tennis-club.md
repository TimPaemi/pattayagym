# ATT Pattaya Table Tennis Club

Thai Maps carries the club's listing under the Thai label **ปิงปอง พัทยา** alongside its English name.

ATT Pattaya Table Tennis Club is a central Pattaya table-tennis venue on the third floor of Mike Shopping Mall, where the operator and Pattaya Sports Club both identify table rental, training and organised play.

## What training is on offer

The operator advertises private and group training, table rental, tournaments and FastPong practice technology. Pattaya Sports Club describes 10 international-standard tables, air conditioning, sports flooring and parking at the shopping-centre location. Those details distinguish ATT from a hotel recreation table or a bar game.

The public descriptions welcome beginners, visitors and families, but do not substitute for a coach-led session booking. Ask whether the preferred time is open play, private tuition, group tuition or a tournament slot, and whether an opponent can be arranged.

## What it costs

The official Thai price page establishes that the club sells membership and training packages, but the accessible current extract did not expose a stable general tariff for a visitor. Ask for the table-rental rate, coach fee, paddle hire, ball policy and any membership or package condition before confirming.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

ATT suits a traveller seeking indoor table tennis near central Pattaya, including a beginner who wants coaching rather than a casual table only. It is weaker for someone who needs a fully public walk-in price or a guaranteed opponent at a particular hour.

## Getting there

Pattaya Sports Club places the club on the third floor of Mike Shopping Mall, roughly in the central shopping corridor. The published club number is +66 95 410 8727 and the email is pattaya.att@gmail.com. Use the mall entrance and verify the floor after arrival.

## Before you go

Send the number of players, level, date and whether paddles are required. Ask about current session times, adult or child suitability, coach language and any court-sharing rule.
