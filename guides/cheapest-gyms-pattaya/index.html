Cheapest gyms.

By TimPaemi · 10 min read · Updated 2026-07-29 · How we rank →

The lowest price changes with the transaction. Compare ordinary gym entry, coached-combat facilities and resort access using exact operator tariffs checked 25-26 July 2026.

Pattaya · 215 venues hand-checked · Compare gyms

If you only read one thing: “cheapest” depends on the product and length of stay. The lowest current one-day general-gym price in this comparison is not attached to the lowest monthly membership, and a resort facilities pass is not the same purchase as a weights room. Compare day with day, month with month and included facilities before deciding.

This guide uses only amounts tied to exact operator rate pages in the venue records. Prices were checked on 25-26 July 2026. A blank amount means no stable current first-hand tariff was found; it does not mean free access or a negotiable rate.

The price table is deliberately short

The table below contains five records with comparable general-gym or facilities access. It excludes old review prices, city-wide estimates and automated guesses from a baht-tier symbol. It also excludes a venue when its only public price is for an unrelated coached class, hotel room or residential camp.

A “day” can still cover different facilities. Battle & Conquer's gym-only product is a weights and facilities purchase. ISS separates its general gym and facilities pass from its coached boxing and Muay Thai classes. Fitz Club's outside-guest day pass covers the gym, pool, sauna and steam rooms but excludes tennis and squash. Read the product column before comparing the number.

Lowest published gym-only day and month: Battle & Conquer

Battle & Conquer Gym on Pratamnak publishes a gym-only menu of THB 200 for one day, THB 600 for one week and THB 1,300 for one month, checked on 25 July 2026. A one-year gym product is THB 12,000 on the same dated operator page.

Those amounts are not Muay Thai prices. A single group Muay Thai session is THB 500, and the coached packages follow a separate menu. The gym-only product is the useful comparison for someone seeking ordinary training access. Ask whether the boxing room, weights area, sauna and cold plunge are included in the exact gym-only term, because the operator describes broader inclusions explicitly for some Muay Thai packages.

Battle is the cheapest known day and month in this six-row comparison, but it will not suit every trip. Someone based far from Pratamnak may spend more time reaching it than the difference saves. Someone wanting a central mall gym, a 24-hour member door or a hotel pool is comparing another access model.

Best transparent longer-stay value: Better Bodies

Better Bodies Gym in Ban Amphur publishes THB 250 for one day, THB 400 for one week, THB 1,000 for one month, THB 2,500 for three months and THB 4,500 for six months, checked on 26 July 2026. The one-month amount is lower than Battle's, while Battle has the lower one-day amount.

The operator says free water and Wi-Fi are included and describes staff guidance, a body-fat check and progress checks for members. It also says personal training is free during the first month. Confirm which membership activates that offer, when a trainer is present and whether changing or locker facilities meet the visit. The gym opens at 10:00 and closes Sunday, so its low long-stay rate does not solve early-morning or seven-day access.

Better Bodies is the strongest value here for someone living near Ban Amphur who wants one to six months of straightforward weight training and direct staff contact. It is a weaker choice for a central Pattaya visitor, a Sunday routine or someone seeking a published group-class timetable.

Central published gym access: ISS

ISS Boxing and Muay Thai on Pattaya Klang publishes a general gym and facilities product of THB 300 per day, THB 800 per week and THB 1,500 per month, checked on 25 July 2026. The operator lists general access Monday-Saturday from 09:00 to 21:00.

ISS also publishes coached boxing and Muay Thai at THB 1,000 per day, THB 4,000 per week or THB 8,000 per month on the same dated page. Those class prices must not be substituted for general-gym access or vice versa. A separate recovery and weight-cut room menu starts at THB 200 per day. Ask the desk to name the product and facilities included in the intended payment.

ISS is useful when a central location and a published weekly option matter. It is not the lowest month in this table, it closes Sunday, and the combined boxing/Muay Thai class label does not promise a hands-only session. Use the boxing and kickboxing guide if coached striking is the actual goal.

Elite and Fitz answer different facility questions

Elite Gym & Fitness Pattaya publishes THB 400 for one day and THB 3,000 for one month, checked on 25 July 2026. Ten visits cost THB 2,500 and remain valid for less than three months on that dated operator page. A group class is a separate THB 300, while one hour of personal training is THB 2,000. Do not treat the day-access amount as coached training.

Fitz Club publishes an outside-guest day pass of THB 800 per adult, checked on 25 July 2026. It covers the gym, pool, sauna and steam rooms while excluding tennis and squash. A child under 12 is THB 400 on the same 2026 tariff. Fitz costs more than the ordinary gym days above because it is a broader facilities purchase; courts, coaching and memberships are separate.

Choose Elite when the requirement is a conventional East Pattaya gym, visit pack or a separately priced class. Choose Fitz when pool and wet-area access belong in the day. Neither should be called expensive or cheap without defining the intended facilities.

Why SUN, Tony's and the 24-hour chains are not ranked by price

SUN Fitness operates Buakhao, Pratamnak and Naklua branches and says one membership card can be used across them, but its current operator pages expose no stable day, week or month tariff. The former THB 1,090 claim was removed because it was not on a current first-hand rate card.

Tony's Fitness Group has a current Third Road Maps identity and a 24-hour label, but no current operator-published tariff, staffed schedule or visitor terms. The newest visible operator posts checked for its venue record were from April 2022. Its old reputation cannot be converted into a 2026 “budget” price.

Jetts, Anytime Fitness and Fitness 7 also lack a stable current public Pattaya visitor tariff in their records. A 24-hour access label is a schedule signal, not a price. Use the 24-hour guide to separate member entry from staffed enrolment, then ask the exact branch for joining fees, access-card terms and short-stay eligibility.

Public parks are not automatically a zero-baht gym

Pratumnak Fitness Park is documented by city material and a current local update as a public park for running, jogging and walking with exercise apparatus along the route. No current municipal tariff was published in the checked material. This guide therefore does not label it “free” or claim a former inventory of free weights and machines.

A public outdoor route can still be the lowest-cost movement option, but it does not replace a staffed gym. Equipment condition, lighting, water, lockers, changing space and coaching are not established. Check the site and any temporary organised activity on the intended date rather than treating public-park status as a complete price and service promise.

Choose by expected attendance, not the smallest headline

For one visit, compare the day amount and location. For a week, Better Bodies, Battle and ISS publish distinct weekly products checked on 25-26 July 2026. For a month, Better Bodies has the lowest current figure in this table, but its Ban Amphur location and Sunday closure matter. Battle is close behind with a Pratamnak gym-only product, while ISS provides a priced central option.

Longer prepayment lowers the arithmetic only when the venue remains usable. Ask when the term begins, whether it can be paused or transferred and whether unused days or visits expire. Do not divide a six- or twelve-month headline into a daily “saving” unless the traveller genuinely intends to attend for the full term.

Costs that remain questions

  • Joining or registration fee and any access-card deposit.
  • Towel, locker, shower, drinking-water or parking terms.
  • Class booking, personal training and induction charges.
  • Equipment rental, including gloves or wraps at a combat facility.
  • VAT, card surcharge, promotion eligibility and refund rules.
  • Whether a day, week or month starts on purchase or first entry.

Those items are questions, not claims that every venue charges them. Ask for the complete payable total and named inclusions. Save the reply if a promotion or unusual access term determines the purchase.

A practical shortlist

  • Lowest known one-day gym-only amount: Battle & Conquer at THB 200, checked 25 July 2026.
  • Lowest known one-month amount: Better Bodies at THB 1,000, checked 26 July 2026.
  • Central gym and facilities access: ISS at THB 300 per day or THB 1,500 per month, checked 25 July 2026.
  • Gym plus pool and wet areas: Fitz outside-guest day pass at THB 800, checked 25 July 2026.

Use the comparison tool for category and area filtering, then open the individual venue record for the exact source and unresolved terms. “Cheapest” is a useful answer only after the transaction, location and attendance period match.

FAQ

What is the cheapest currently published gym day in this comparison?

Battle & Conquer publishes gym-only access at THB 200 for one day, checked 25 July 2026. Better Bodies is THB 250 and ISS general gym and facilities access is THB 300. These products have different locations and inclusions, so confirm the exact access before paying.

Which Pattaya gym has the lowest published monthly price here?

Better Bodies publishes one month at THB 1,000, checked 26 July 2026. Battle & Conquer lists gym-only access at THB 1,300 and ISS general gym and facilities at THB 1,500, both checked 25 July 2026. Better Bodies is in Ban Amphur and closes Sunday, which may outweigh the lower amount.

Are Pattaya's public fitness parks free?

This guide does not make that universal claim. Current city and local material supports Pratumnak Fitness Park as a public exercise setting, but no current municipal tariff was published in the checked sources. It also does not provide a staffed gym's equipment, coaching or changing facilities.
