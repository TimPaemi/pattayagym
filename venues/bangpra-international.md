# Bangpra Golf Club

Bangpra Golf Club is an operating 18-hole course and hotel in Bang Phra, Si Racha. The exact Maps record and active owner page use the same club identity and address at 45 Moo 6. Maps lists daily opening from 06:00 to 20:00 and phone **+66 81 295 6154**; the operator page also publishes **+66 62 719 0016** and Bangpragolfclub@gmail.com.

## Course format

Tourism Authority of Thailand material describes an 18-hole, par-72 course measuring 7,393 yards. Those figures establish the full-round format, but they do not tell a visitor which tees will be used, the current playing condition or the pace for a particular date. Ask the booking desk which tee set suits the group and whether any maintenance affects the intended round.

The operator describes the property as a golf club and hotel. This record does not turn that general description into an accommodation package: room eligibility, breakfast, golf inclusions and check-in terms require a separate quote.

## Dated 28–29 July package

An owner post published in July 2026 advertises **฿1,850 for 18 holes on 28 or 29 July 2026**. The graphic and post state that the amount includes:

This is a two-day special, not the club’s ordinary weekday rate and not evidence of the price after 29 July. It also does not state whether a caddie tip, rental clubs, shoes, food or other extras are included. The owner graphic says advance booking is first come, first served and gives +66 81 295 6154, +66 92 924 7288 and +66 62 719 0016 for reservations.

## Who it suits

Bangpra is relevant to a golfer looking for a full 18-hole round in the Si Racha/Bang Phra belt and willing to reserve with the club directly. The short-lived July offer gives a precise total for its two valid dates; outside that window, the page is a course-format and contact record rather than a price comparison.

It is not a city-centre practice facility or a walk-in simulator. Golfers who only want a ball session should compare the [golf directory](/category/golf/) for driving-range and indoor-practice records. ## Before booking

Give the desk the exact date, preferred tee time, number of golfers and whether anyone needs rental clubs. Ask for the current green fee, caddie fee, cart rule, caddie-tip guidance, equipment hire, dress code, payment method, cancellation terms and arrival time. For 28–29 July, ask the club to confirm the ฿1,850 package is still available before travelling; for every other date, request a fresh complete quote rather than extending the special.
