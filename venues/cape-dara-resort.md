# Cape Dara Resort

Cape Dara's fitness centre has cardio and strength equipment, free weights and yoga equipment, and opens daily from 06:00 to 22:00. The resort describes its normal pools, fitness room and playroom as facilities for in-house guests.

No current public gym or pool day pass was found. Non-residents should ask the hotel whether any separately bookable wellness programme is running before travelling. A previously listed Sunday promotion and its prices were removed because the offer could not be reconfirmed on the live operator page.
