# Pattaya Sky Ride

The Thai news report describes the service as **เฮลิคอปเตอร์ชมวิวพัทยา**.

## What training is on offer

Pattaya Sky Ride is a helicopter sightseeing and transport operator, not a training school or walk-in sports facility. Its live official site advertises aerial sightseeing over Pattaya and helicopter transport between Bangkok and Pattaya. The operator also names emergency medical and VIP transport among its services, but this directory does not interpret those statements as availability for a particular request.

The exact operator website, contact page and linked map all support a current identity. The site asks customers to contact the company for a tailored flight and price. Readers comparing other bookable outdoor activities can use the [Pattaya adventure directory](/category/adventure/) and [adventure guide](/guides/adventure-pattaya/).

## What it costs

The official package page displayed **“No Package Found”** on **27 July 2026**. No current public sightseeing, transfer or charter tariff is therefore quoted. The old record's THB 129,200 amount is not restored because the current operator does not present it as a live product.

Request a written quotation for the exact date, route and passenger group. It should identify all taxes, landing or handling charges, ground transfers, waiting time, baggage terms, payment schedule and refund conditions. Do not infer a package price from a review, reseller, aircraft photo or historical promotion.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The service may suit a customer seeking a pre-arranged sightseeing flight or a quoted point-to-point helicopter transfer. It does not suit someone expecting a scheduled public departure, a same-day walk-in attraction or a pilot-training product. “Book now” language on the site does not prove that a slot or aircraft is available.

Passengers should disclose mobility, medical, baggage and seating requirements directly to the operator. The directory cannot determine fitness to fly, aircraft suitability or regulatory eligibility for an individual.

## Getting there

The operator publishes **789 Moo 14, Charnok 19, Pattaya, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150** and links that address to Google Maps. This is the company contact location. It should not be assumed to be the departure pad for every sightseeing or transfer booking.

Ask the operator to name both departure and landing points in writing and send live pins. The [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) gives context for the published office address only; it does not establish a transfer route or flight origin.

## Before you go

Contact **+66 99 775 5573**, **+66 98 276 5341** or **+66 89 099 9974**, or email **info@pattayaskyride.com**. State the date, passenger count, approximate passenger and baggage needs, requested route and flexibility. Ask which operator entity takes payment and what confirmation document will be issued.

Before paying, obtain the aircraft type assigned, passenger and baggage limits, identification requirements, reporting time, transfer arrangements, weather decision process, cancellation terms and refund timing. Reconfirm shortly before travel because aviation operations are conditional.

No current public package, tariff, schedule, flight duration, fixed departure point, aircraft assignment, capacity, baggage allowance, accessibility procedure or weather-refund rule was available on the checked operator pages. The site confirms an operator and enquiry route, not a guaranteed flight on any requested date.
