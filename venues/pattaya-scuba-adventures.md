# Pattaya Scuba Adventures

Pattaya Scuba Adventures operates from Pattaya Sai Song 15. Its current contact page lists dive operations seven days a week, with the retail shop closed on Sunday; PADI's location-specific profile identifies it as a PADI 5 Star Dive Center.

## Prices

The live Open Water product page lists the PADI Open Water Diver course at **฿14,990 for one person** or **from ฿13,490 per person for two or more**, checked 25 July 2026. The operator's broader price table labels itself as updated June 2025, so older fun-dive figures have not been carried forward as current prices.

An official three-day package page also lists six- and nine-dive options, but divers should confirm inclusions directly because equipment, certification level, site selection and weather can change the final plan.

## First visit

Tell the centre your certification, recent-dive history and equipment needs. Beginners should ask which medical declaration applies before paying. Certified divers should bring their certification card and logbook details. Boat sites are weather-dependent, and the operator—not this directory—decides the safe itinerary.
