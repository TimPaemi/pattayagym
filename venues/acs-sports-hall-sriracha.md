# ACS Sports Hall

ACS Sports Hall is a badminton hall associated with Assumption College Sriracha in Surasak. The exact current Maps place confirms the hall identity, phone and weekly business hours. ## What training is on offer

The live listing classifies ACS Sports Hall as a badminton court. A Maps contributor's detailed description says the hall has five courts, is used for physical-education classes and school-team practice, and can be rented by outside users by the hour, day, week or month. Because that inventory and access explanation is not an owner rate card, confirm both before relying on it.

No current public coaching timetable is published. Compare the [racquet-sport directory](/category/racquet/) if the priority is a dedicated club with an itemised public product rather than a school facility.

## What it costs

No current school or hall tariff was found on 26 July 2026. Ask +66 63 225 5259 for the court-hour price and whether the amount is for the whole court or each player. Also ask about longer booking blocks, lighting, racket or shuttlecock hire, coaching, deposits and cancellation.

## Before you go

Maps lists 06:00-20:00 Monday-Saturday and Sunday closed. The listing warns that public-holiday hours can differ. Those are business periods, not proof that all five reported courts are available to the public throughout the day.

Call with the date, start time, player count and desired duration. Confirm visitor identification, campus entry, court number, payment method, footwear, shuttlecocks, rackets, changing facilities and whether a school event can displace the reservation.
