# Pattaya Park Water Park

Thai travel coverage describes the 345 Moo 12 resort attraction near Pratamnak as **สวนน้ำพัทยาปาร์ค**.

## What training is on offer

Pattaya Park Water Park is the water attraction inside the Pattaya Park Beach Resort complex. The operator's current water-park page describes giant water slides, a whirlpool and beachside swimming. Those are leisure facilities, not evidence of lap lanes, a coached swimming programme or a fitness class. Readers looking for structured pool use should compare the [Pattaya swimming directory](/category/swimming/) rather than treating every water attraction as a training venue.

The resort site and the exact Maps identity both resolve to Pattaya Park. This confirms the business and its water-park component; it does not establish that every photographed or historically advertised feature will operate on a particular day.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This venue may suit a family or leisure visitor who wants slides and recreational water time within one resort complex. It does not suit a swimmer who requires a measured lane, guaranteed uninterrupted distance, a coach or a published class. The operator calls it a water park, so the directory does not imply competitive-pool specifications.

Anyone with a height, mobility or medical constraint should obtain the attraction's current eligibility and supervision rules directly. The directory cannot determine whether a slide or water feature is appropriate for an individual.

## Before you go

The exact Maps listing showed **09:00-18:00 daily** at verification, but business hours do not guarantee that every feature operates for the entire window. Call on the intended date and confirm admission sales, last entry, slide operation, maintenance closures and weather policy.

Also ask what swimwear is accepted, whether flotation equipment is supplied or permitted, what adult supervision is required for children, and whether outside food, valuables storage or re-entry is allowed. Obtain cancellation or refund terms in writing if advance payment is requested.
