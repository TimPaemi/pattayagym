# Sriracha Arena Sport Club

Sriracha Arena publishes four indoor artificial-turf football pitches and six badminton courts. Its official Line account lists daily opening from 08:00 to midnight, parking, showers and equipment support.

## Booking

The operator publishes sport-specific contacts on its website:

- Football: +66 64 563 5665
- Badminton: +66 85 972 1888
- Academy and league enquiries: +66 86 532 2888

No current court price is published on the pages checked. Contact the relevant desk for the hourly rate, deposit, footwear rules and equipment inclusion.

The venue is in Thung Sukhla, serving the Laem Chabang–Sriracha area rather than central Pattaya.
