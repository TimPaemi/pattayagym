★ About this venue

Seafari Diving Center

Seafari is a current PADI 5 Star Instructor Development Center behind the Mike Shopping Mall area on Pattaya Second Road. PADI's live centre listing gives daily 08:00–19:00 hours, the 262 AOP 1/8 address, phone +66 81 762 4187 and four listed PADI instructors. Shop hours are not a boat timetable; the booked product controls the meeting and return plan.

What training is on offer

The operator site covers supervised first dives, recreational certification, certified-diver trips, technical training and professional development. PADI lists scuba, TecRec, snorkelling, conservation and Emergency First Response activities. The IDC label describes the centre's professional-training scope; it does not prove that every course or instructor is available every day.

PADI also lists air, Nitrox, Trimix and mixed-gas fills; equipment sales and rental; an onsite classroom; air conditioning; free Wi-Fi; a retail shop; a dive boat and parking. Equipment servicing is listed for cylinders, regulators, buoyancy-control devices and dive computers. The centre languages listed are English, Cantonese, Malay, Thai and Indonesian, but the chosen date still requires a language confirmation.

What it costs

Seafari's exact Discover Scuba Diving product page listed THB 4,500 per person for a one-day, two-dive introduction when checked on 2026-07-26. It is presented for people with no previous diving experience and is not a full certification course. The exact page is retained as priceSourceUrl.

Before paying, request the current written inclusion list. Ask specifically about equipment, boat charges, instructor supervision, food and drinks, transfers, photos, insurance, taxes and any compulsory medical or marine-park costs. The public product page establishes the headline price and two-dive format; this record does not add an inclusion the operator did not state clearly.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Seafari suits a first-time diver who wants a named one-day product, a traveller seeking a PADI course from a central booking base or an experienced diver looking for a centre with recreational, technical and professional scope. Compare it with the Pattaya dive-operator guide and the wider watersports directory.

It is a weaker fit for someone expecting the THB 4,500 experience to award a certification, needing a guaranteed distant-hotel pickup or wanting a complete trip total without contacting the shop. A listed centre language does not guarantee the instructor assigned to one date.

Getting there

The PADI and operator records place the centre behind the Mike Shopping Mall area on Pattaya Second Road. Use the exact Maps pin rather than assuming the mall's main entrance is the dive-centre entrance. A central shop can be convenient for paperwork or fitting, but it does not prove that the boat departs from the shop.

The Central Pattaya area guide compares other dive-shop booking bases and makes the same launch-point distinction. Use the booking confirmation for pickup, pier and return details.

Before you go

Send the exact product name, date, participant count, ages, certification status, recent-dive history, swimming confidence, equipment sizes and hotel location. Ask about the planned boat and sites, meeting point, pickup, expected return window, food, weather or cancellation policy and the applicable post-dive flying guidance.

Complete the operator's medical documentation honestly. A “yes” response can require qualified medical clearance; do not conceal a condition to preserve a booking. Certification courses may add knowledge, confined-water, open-water and swimming requirements that should not be transferred automatically to the Discover product.

What we could not verify

  • No space was confirmed for a particular date.
  • No universal pickup radius or fixed return time was published.
  • No claim is made about visibility, wildlife, boat condition or instructor quality.
  • Prices for other trips and courses are not copied into this record because the retained dated price is for the exact beginner product.

For non-scuba alternatives and the product distinctions, continue to the diving and watersports guide.
