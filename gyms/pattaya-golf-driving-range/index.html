★ About this venue

Pattaya Golf Driving Range

Pattaya Golf Driving Range is the active Third Road practice facility in North Pattaya. Its exact Maps identity and owner Facebook profile agree on the telephone and location. One current Pattaya Sports Club rate is published, but the ordinary public tray price, bay specification and equipment services remain contact-first.

What training is on offer

This is an outdoor golf driving range rather than a simulator or full course. The public sources confirm practice-range use but do not state the number of bays, covered-bay count, target distances, grass access, lighting specification or whether a short-game area is available.

Coaching and club hire were not published clearly enough to promise. A beginner can use a range for a lower-commitment practice session, but should not infer that an instructor or suitable rental club will be available on arrival. The Pattaya golf directory separates ranges, simulators and courses.

What it costs

Pattaya Sports Club’s exact member-discount page listed THB 55 for 55 balls when checked on 26 July 2026. The club states that the PSC membership card must be shown and that this benefit cannot be combined with another card. It is a conditional member rate, not the general public price.

No owner-published ordinary tray price, non-member package, multi-tray discount, club-rental tariff or coaching fee was found. A non-member should call for the full amount. A PSC member should show the valid card before ordering and confirm that the benefit is being honoured that day.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The range suits a golfer seeking ball practice in North Pattaya and a PSC member who can use the documented tray benefit. The current 07:00-22:00 Maps schedule supports both early and evening planning, subject to bay availability.

It is less suitable for someone who needs a guaranteed English-speaking coach, a published rental-club set or a climate-controlled simulator. The Pattaya course guide is the better route for a full round, while Golf Hub Pattaya is a different indoor product.

Getting there

Use the location-specific Maps result for the Third Road range and check plus code WVWV+HW. Several similarly named driving-range records exist around Pattaya, so the name alone is not enough. This is not Diana Driving Range.

The owner profile describes the Third Road location. No public source establishes a baht-bus route, parking arrangement, entrance landmark or fixed travel time.

Before you go

Call +66 84 161 5055 with the intended time and ask for the public tray price, ball count, bay availability, club rental, coaching and payment method. After dark, confirm that the full range and lighting remain in operation until the listed close.

The owner Facebook profile uses a generic “always open” label, while the exact Maps place publishes a specific daily 07:00-22:00 schedule. This record follows the detailed weekly listing and does not interpret the generic social label as 24-hour operation.

What we could not verify

  • No current public, non-member ball-tray price was published.
  • Bay count, covered bays, target distances, practice surfaces and lighting specification were not stated.
  • Club hire, coaching, left-handed equipment, pro-shop, food, locker and changing facilities remain unresolved.
  • Parking, reservation, payment and holiday-hour rules require direct confirmation.
