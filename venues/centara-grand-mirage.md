# Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya

Centara Grand Mirage is a Wong Amat resort built around the Lost World water park, with pools, a lazy river, waterfalls and slides. The current resort fact sheet also lists a fully equipped fitness centre and tennis courts.

The hotel's activities brochure includes tennis, beach games, yoga, Pilates and Muay Thai. These are resort programmes whose days and times can change; use the current hotel timetable rather than an older directory schedule.

## Access

The hotel presents these as resort facilities. No current public gym or water-park day-pass price was found, so non-residents should confirm access before travelling. The spa page currently lists sauna, steam room and hot and cold pools, with spa hours of 09:00–21:00.

Older unsupported details such as exact room counts, a climbing wall and fixed class times have been removed.
