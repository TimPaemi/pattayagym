# Silk Muay Thai

Silk Muay Thai is a residential camp off Thung Klom-Tan Man Road. The operator lists group sessions Monday-Saturday from 08:00-10:00 and approximately 15:00-18:00, with the wider site open until 19:00. Training includes cardio, bag work, time with a trainer, sparring and clinching; private sessions are arranged outside group periods.

The current published packages start at **THB 16,000 for one week**, **THB 34,000 for one month with one daily Muay Thai session**, and **THB 39,000 for one month with two daily sessions**, checked 25 July 2026. Package inclusions vary, so confirm the room, meal count and training frequency in writing before paying.

The onsite accommodation has private bathrooms, balconies, air conditioning, Wi-Fi and mini-fridges. Pool, sauna, spa and restaurant access are part of the residential offer. Drop-in and gym-only prices were not published on the pages checked.
