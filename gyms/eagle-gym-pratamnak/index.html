Location correction

The active listing now uses EAGLE GYM and places the venue on Soi Pattaya North 7, not at the former Pratamnak Soi 5 address. The phone remains +66 63 117 7766, and the listing links to a current Facebook page.

The published listing hours are 09:00–13:00 and 15:00–21:00 on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday through Saturday, with a 20:00 finish on Sunday. Wednesday was replaced by a Thai public-holiday closure during this check, so confirm Wednesday and holiday hours directly.
