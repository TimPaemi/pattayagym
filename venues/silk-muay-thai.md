# Silk Muay Thai

Silk Muay Thai is an East Pattaya residential camp rather than a simple walk-in pad gym. The operator presents it as a stay-and-train product with group sessions, private coaching and on-site rooms, which suits visitors who want accommodation and training under one booking. The limitation is that the public pages are much clearer on camp packages than on drop-in or gym-only access.

## What training is on offer

Silk's own pages describe scheduled group training Monday to Saturday, with the camp open from 08:00 to 19:00 and group work typically split into a morning block and an afternoon block. The operator's training description names cardio, bag work, technical rounds with trainers, sparring and clinching as the core format rather than a pure fitness-boxing class. Private training is also promoted as part of the offer, but the public pages do not publish a fixed menu of private-session lengths or coach-specific rates.

The venue is built around a residential setup. The operator lists private rooms, a restaurant, pool, sauna and spa access as part of the wider camp environment. That makes it a relevant option for readers comparing [Muay Thai camps with accommodation](/guides/muay-thai-camps-with-accommodation-pattaya/) or browsing the broader [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/), especially if an inland [East Pattaya base](/area/east-pattaya/) is acceptable.

## What it costs

Silk's current public package ladder on the main site starts at **฿16,000 for one week**, **฿34,000 for one month with one daily Muay Thai session**, and **฿39,000 for one month with two daily sessions** (operator website, checked 2026-07-27). The one-week summary says that starter package includes one daily Muay Thai session, one fundamentals class, one daily meal, spa/sauna/pool access and a private double or twin room. The monthly tiers are shown as residential packages rather than bare training fees.

No stable public drop-in tariff, week-without-room price or gym-only membership figure was published on the checked pages. If you do not want the accommodation bundle, ask directly for the price of one group session, one private session and one week of training without a room.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Silk suits a traveller who wants a camp structure and prefers one combined purchase for room, meals and training. Because the operator openly sells beginner-friendly package tiers and fundamentals content, it can work for a first longer stay better than a venue whose public face is built mainly around active fighters.

It is a weaker fit for someone who only wants a cheap one-off pad session, a central location or a transparent menu of single-session prices. For a shorter or simpler purchase, compare a training-only venue such as [Sudsakorn Muay Thai](/gyms/sudsakorn-muay-thai-gym/) or the broader [1 week versus 1 month Muay Thai guide](/guides/train-muay-thai-pattaya-1-week-1-month/). For a bigger multi-discipline residential comparison, [Kombat Group Thailand](/gyms/kombat-group-thailand/) is the clearer East-side alternative.

## Getting there

The camp lists Thung Klom-Tan Man 18 in Pattaya City, on the inland East Pattaya side rather than on the beach corridor. This is not a walkable add-on from Central Pattaya or Beach Road hotels. It makes more sense when the room is on site or when the visitor is already basing themselves in the Darkside / Nong Prue belt.

Because the camp product is residential, the useful transport question is not only how to reach the first session. It is also how far the site sits from your airport transfer, recovery meals and any off-site errands. Confirm the exact map pin and arrival instructions with the operator before travelling.

## Before you go

Ask for the exact package breakdown in writing. Silk's public pages are strong on headline camp pricing but lighter on operational detail such as check-in timing, equipment provision, training-language expectations and what changes if you miss a session. Also confirm whether the listed room is private or shared for your chosen tier and whether there are extra charges for card payment or later upgrades.

- No public private-session rate card by trainer or duration.
- No exact public statement of glove, wrap or pad-holding equipment terms.
- No public written cancellation or package-change policy on the checked pages.
