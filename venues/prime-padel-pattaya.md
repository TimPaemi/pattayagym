## Before you go

Check Playtomic before travelling. The live booking flow is the reliable place to confirm whether the slot you want is actually open. If you need coaching, message ahead rather than assuming a coach is available on demand just because coaching is part of the club identity.

Ask whether your chosen booking includes rackets and balls or whether those are separate add-ons. Covered courts reduce weather risk, but they do not remove the need to book at popular hours.
