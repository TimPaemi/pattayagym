★ About this venue

Monkey Badminton Court Pattaya

Monkey Badminton is a dedicated local court on Soi Sukhumvit 81 in East Pattaya. The current Thai badminton directory and live listing agree on the address, phone and 10:00–23:00 daily hours.

No current hourly rate is published. Contact the court to reserve a slot and ask whether racket hire, shuttlecocks, coaching or player-matching are available.

First visit

Confirm whether non-marking shoes are required and arrive before the booked hour. This is a neighbourhood badminton venue rather than a hotel or general fitness club.
