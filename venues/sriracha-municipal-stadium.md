# Sriracha Municipal Stadium

Thai Maps names the 243/70 Surasak 2 facility **สนามกีฬาเทศบาลเมืองศรีราชา**, matching its split 05:00-07:30 and 13:00-20:00 schedule.

Sriracha Municipality announced this stadium’s reopening on 4 September 2025 for public exercise, sport and public-benefit activities. The exact Maps place is at 243/70 Surasak 2 Road. The municipal notice gives two public exercise periods: 05:00-07:30 and 13:00-20:00 daily except public holidays.

## What training is on offer

The municipal source establishes public exercise use and separate permission arrangements for football and basketball areas. It does not advertise a coaching programme, athletics club, fitness class, swimming facility or regular league. The site is described as a municipal venue, not as a guaranteed casual team-sport session.

## What it costs

No current municipal fee table appears in the reopening notice checked on 2026-07-29. Ask the municipality whether the intended use needs a booking, deposit, identification or charge. Public exercise hours alone are not evidence that a team may reserve an area free of charge.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The stated public periods suit local exercise use. A football or basketball group needs to follow the distinct municipal permission route. The sources do not confirm private coaching, a drop-in football game, equipment hire, changing rooms, floodlights or a staffed desk at all public periods.

## Getting there

Use the listed Surasak 2 Road address and the exact Maps link. The record retains +66 38 327 888 as the municipality’s general contact; it is not presented as a dedicated stadium-booking line.

## Before you go

Treat the split schedule literally: the notice gives no public period from 07:30 to 13:00. Check public-holiday closures and event use before travelling, and obtain municipal permission before arranging organised sport.
