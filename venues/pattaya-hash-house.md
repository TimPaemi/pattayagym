# Pattaya Hash House Harriers

PH3 is a social hash for runners, joggers and walkers. Its current site says it has met every Monday since 1984. The group gathers outside Buffalo Bar on Third Road from 14:30, with the last bus to the week's trail site leaving at 15:00 and the first post-run circle at 16:00.

## Fees checked 25 July 2026

- men: ฿400
- women: ฿150
- children aged 16 or under: ฿50

The fee includes the organised run and the group's published transport/social arrangements. The trail location changes each week, so check the next-run page before leaving. Carry a phone and torch and follow the organiser's safety guidance; this is an informal trail event rather than a supported road race.
