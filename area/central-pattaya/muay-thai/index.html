Central Pattaya's five mapped Muay Thai records do not represent five equivalent walk-in classes. ISS Boxing and Muay Thai has the clearest central short-stay tariff. Petchrungruang, Venum Training Camp and Rage Fight Academy are South Pattaya or border-area camps that require a trip to their exact pins. MAX Muay Thai Stadium is a spectator venue, not a visitor-training gym. Start by deciding whether the purchase is coached training, uncoached gym access, a longer camp enquiry or a fight ticket; a single area label cannot make those products interchangeable. ISS publishes three separate products, checked on 27 July 2026. General gym and facility access costs THB 300 per day, THB 800 per week or THB 1,500 per month. Boxing and Muay Thai classes cost THB 1,000 per day, THB 4,000 per week or THB 8,000 per month. Its weight-cut and recovery room is separately priced at THB 200 per day, THB 600 per week or THB 1,000 per month. Gym access is listed Monday-Saturday 09:00-21:00, while the combat class block is Monday-Saturday 14:00-15:30. Do not compare the THB 300 gym pass with another camp's coached session. Ask whether beginner access and gloves or wraps are included on the intended date. Petchrungruang is a family camp training Thai fighters and visiting students. A locally verified listing checked on 29 July 2026 gives THB 5,000 for a 30-day product with one session a day, or THB 9,000 with two sessions a day. It lists Tuesday-Sunday periods of 09:00-11:00 and 15:00-18:00, but this is third-party evidence rather than an owner-posted timetable. Confirm visitor eligibility, the actual session start and inclusions with the gym before travelling. Venum is broader, advertising Muay Thai, boxing, MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and cross-training for beginners through experienced athletes, but it publishes no usable current tariff or class grid. Its official daily 07:00-19:00 hours conflict with Maps showing Monday-Saturday 09:00-19:00 and Sunday closed. A specific discipline and date therefore need direct confirmation. Rage also serves a mixed-discipline enquiry: its operator material supports Muay Thai, boxing, BJJ, MMA and functional training, plus three full-size rings, a mat zone, weights area, pool and accommodation. The current membership form names THB 1,500 as an agreement fee or refundable deposit but leaves the membership period selectable, so that amount is not a drop-in or package tariff. Ask for a written quote for the exact class, day, week, private or room-and-training product. Listed opening is Monday-Saturday 08:00-19:00, with individual class times unconfirmed. MAX belongs in a different decision. It publishes fight cards, a ticket route and seating plan but no routine visitor-training product. Its site said on 29 July 2026 that Pattaya tickets normally start at about THB 1,500 for standard or VIP/ringside seating; office hours are 13:00-21:00 daily, while card times remain event-specific. Check the intended date, seat section, all-in price and doors time rather than assuming a nightly show. For the simplest priced central class, compare ISS's named 14:00 block. For a longer traditional-camp enquiry, confirm Petchrungruang directly. Use Venum or Rage when several combat disciplines or facilities matter enough to justify cross-town confirmation. Use MAX only for a dated spectator plan. The beginners guide covers first-session questions, while the Muay Thai guide provides the wider Pattaya comparison.
