# Lek Thai Yoga

Lek Thai Yoga operates from Laguna Beach Resort 3 Maldives near Thepprasit Road. The current owner site offers therapeutic Thai yoga, massage, Yin yoga, Vipassana meditation, private sessions and teacher-training courses.

This is an appointment-led practice rather than a conventional drop-in studio timetable. Maps shows an 18:00 closing time, but arrange the service, date and meeting point directly before visiting the condominium complex.

The operator site publishes courses and products in US dollars, but no local walk-in tariff was clear enough to reproduce. Confirm the exact service, duration, currency, inclusions, cancellation terms and whether the session is private or shared.
