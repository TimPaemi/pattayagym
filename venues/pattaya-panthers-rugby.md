# Pattaya Panthers Rugby Football Club

Thailand's rugby federation writes the club as **พัทยาแพนเตอร์ รักบี้ฟุตบอลคลับ**, matching the team's published **19:15** Thursday training slot at Horseshoe Point.

## What training is on offer

Pattaya Panthers is a social rugby club rather than a bookable pitch or spectator attraction. Its current Facebook introduction says **all levels are welcome**, names both **contact and touch rugby**, and publishes training on **Thursday from 19:15 to 20:45**. The page locates current club activity at Horseshoe Point Resort.

The club's public description covers two formats. Contact rugby involves tackling and requires the organiser's placement and safety guidance. Touch rugby removes tackles but still needs a confirmed group, footwear and session plan. The official site describes **Pattaya Panties** as the women's touch branch, welcoming varied ages, abilities and experience levels with a focus on teamwork, fitness, skill and social participation.

## What it costs

No current first-hand membership fee, match fee, insurance charge or visitor drop-in price was published on the pages checked on 26 July 2026. A missing fee does not make the club unavailable. Ask for the complete cost and whether registration, insurance, kit or match travel creates a separate charge before joining contact play.

The Panthers' official site advertised the 2026 Chris Kays Memorial as a one-day event at Horseshoe Point on **Saturday 2 May 2026**. That date has passed, so it confirms recent operation rather than supplying a future event or entry price.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The all-levels invitation makes the Panthers a possible route for experienced players, returners and newcomers willing to contact the organiser and accept placement. The women's touch branch may suit someone specifically seeking a non-tackle team format, but its public page does not give a separate weekly time.

The club does not suit a visitor who wants to walk into an unsupervised contact drill or infer eligibility from the phrase “all levels.” Contact rugby needs an organiser's decision about age, experience, group and safety. A resort pin also does not guarantee that a youth, women's, mixed or adult session is running at a chosen time.

## Getting there

Current club material names **Horseshoe Point Resort, Pattaya**. Use the resort Maps pin as the general destination, then ask the Panthers for the exact pitch, gate and meeting point. A resort map pin does not identify a field entrance.

Use the Facebook page or email **PattayaPanthersRFC@gmail.com** before attending. The club also publishes the Instagram identity **pattaya_panthers_rfc**. Confirm transport home as well as arrival, because the training window ends at 20:45 outside the central beachfront grid.

## Before you go

Send the organiser your age, experience, preferred contact or touch format and whether you are visiting once or looking to join. Confirm that Thursday's 19:15-20:45 session is running, the expected arrival time, whether the group is adult, youth, women's or mixed, and whether a first-time player may join.

Ask about boots for the surface, any mouthguard requirement, water, changing, registration and insurance. The current Facebook schedule should be checked for the specific week rather than treated as a permanent fixture.
