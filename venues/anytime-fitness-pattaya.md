# Anytime Fitness Pattaya

Anytime Fitness currently has distinct Pattaya listings at Again Pattaya in Naklua and Bukis Point in South Pattaya. The phone above belongs to the Again Pattaya branch; contact the South Pattaya branch separately if that is where you plan to train.

## Price and first visit

The operator does not publish a location-specific tariff on the Pattaya club page. Ask about joining fees, access-key costs, minimum term, trial access and whether an overseas Anytime Fitness membership is accepted immediately. Both Maps listings show 24-hour access, while staffed hours and class schedules vary.
