# Pattaya Country Club

Its Thai Maps entity name is **พัทยาคันทรีคลับ**, tied to the 18-hole Highway 331 property.

Pattaya Country Club is an 18-hole course on Highway 331 at Khao Mai Kaeo. It suits golfers who want a published component tariff and can organise an inland trip around a confirmed tee time. The operator accepts advance requests rather than instant checkout, and its English and Thai pages disagree on the course closing time.

## What training is on offer

The operator publishes an 18-hole course layout and a separate driving range beside the course entrance. The range page describes more than 300 yards, daily operation until 20:00 and resident professionals available for instruction. It does not publish a lesson timetable, basket price or casual-use terms.

The [Pattaya golf directory](/category/golf/) separates full courses from ranges and simulators. A range visit and an 18-hole booking are different transactions: confirm whether a practice bay or professional lesson needs advance arrangement.

## What it costs

The club's exact rate page, checked 2026-07-26, lists an 18-hole green fee of ฿2,500 on weekdays or ฿3,000 at weekends. Nine holes cost ฿1,250 on weekdays or ฿1,500 at weekends. Caddie fees are ฿400 for 18 holes or ฿200 for nine, and carts are ฿600 for 18 holes or ฿350 for nine.

Rental charges on the same dated page are ฿1,200 for a full club set, ฿100 for an empty golf bag, ฿300 for shoes and ฿100 for an umbrella. Adding the published 18-hole green, caddie and cart components gives ฿3,500 on a weekday or ฿4,000 at a weekend. That arithmetic is not a new package and does not decide which components are optional. Ask for the payable total, rental availability, deposit, tip convention and any event or holiday variation.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The course suits a player who values price transparency and can wait for a confirmed reservation. It is less suitable for someone who needs instant online booking, a guaranteed late tee time or a published beginner lesson. The [golf-course guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) compares other Pattaya records by course format and fee evidence.

## Getting there

The address is 55 Moo 2 on Highway 331 in Khao Mai Kaeo, away from the central beach corridor. Use the exact live map rather than treating “Pattaya” as a central location. The operator describes the range as adjacent to the course entrance, but it does not publish a universal beach-hotel transfer, public-transport route, journey time or fare. The [East Pattaya area index](/area/east-pattaya/) gives wider inland context.

## Before you go

The online form is an advance request, not an instant reservation. The operator says it will respond within three days and publishes booking numbers +66 81 654 5654, +66 81 582 6073, +66 86 323 9713 and the main club lines. Send the date, player count, nine or 18 holes, preferred time, handedness and rental needs. Do not travel merely because the form was submitted.

The Thai rate-page text gives course hours of 06:00-18:00, matching the current exact Maps profile. English text on the same page says 06:00-20:00. This record uses the conservative 18:00 close; confirm the latest permitted start.
