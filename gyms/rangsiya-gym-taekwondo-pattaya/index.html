★ About this venue

Rangsiya Gym Taekwondo Pattaya

Rangsiya Gym is a taekwondo academy in Boonsampan, Nong Prue. The current operator page identifies the Pattaya school as being supervised by Kru June, Rangsiya Nisaisom, and describes her as the 2011 world champion, a former Thai national-team athlete and a 2012 Olympian. This record is specifically for the Pattaya branch. A separate Rangsiya identity in Sattahip publishes another address and phone number and should not be used to contact this school.

What training is on offer

The first-hand pages establish a taekwondo school rather than a general fitness gym. The operator page also shows current competitive activity: a 19 July post congratulated Pattaya athletes after the Chonburi Taekwondo Championship 2026. Current tournament charts separately list Rangsiya Gym competitors in several youth divisions. That evidence supports a competition pathway, but it does not show which ordinary class a new student should join.

The Maps profile currently publishes business periods of 15:00–20:00 Monday to Friday, 09:00–20:00 Saturday and 09:00–15:00 Sunday. These are opening periods, not an age-by-age lesson timetable. Families comparing other programmes can use the kids and youth directory or the East Pattaya area guide.

What it costs

No current Pattaya-branch tuition, trial, private-lesson or membership tariff was published on the checked first-hand pages as of 2026-07-26. The blank price field means the amount is unknown, not that the first class is free.

Ask for the complete first-month cost for the student's age and track. That should include any registration, uniform, belt, protective equipment, grading, insurance and competition charges, not only lesson tuition. If a package is quoted, request its number of sessions, validity period, make-up policy and refund terms in writing.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Rangsiya suits a family seeking taekwondo in the Boonsampan side of East Pattaya and willing to arrange the correct group directly. The current championship evidence may matter to a student interested in competition, while the named head-coach identity may appeal to families who want a clearly documented sporting background.

It is a weaker fit for a short-stay visitor who needs instant online booking, an English-language guarantee or a published drop-in price. Competition participation also does not prove that every student is expected to compete. Ask whether a recreational track exists and how contact or sparring is introduced.

Getting there

The exact Maps identity places the academy in Boonsampan, Pattaya, with Plus Code WWF5+GH and phone +66 96 936 5299. Use that live pin rather than searching only for “Rangsiya Gym,” which can surface the separate Sattahip branch. The Pattaya map can help compare the location with other verified records, but it does not establish a fixed taxi fare or travel time.

Before you go

Send the student's age, current belt, prior experience, goal, preferred days and teaching-language needs. Ask for the exact class start and finish, coach, group size, trial rule, guardian or observation policy and required arrival time. Confirm whether the student should bring a uniform or protective equipment and how injuries or additional needs should be disclosed.

Recheck holiday hours before travelling. The Maps schedule showed Thai public-holiday warnings during this review, so an ordinary weekly period should not be treated as a guarantee for a specific holiday date.

What we could not verify

  • No current public tuition, trial price or complete age-by-age timetable was available.
  • The opening periods do not prove a class begins at each listed opening time.
  • The checked sources do not guarantee English-language teaching, beginner placement or a recreational-only pathway.
  • Tournament entries establish current team activity, not the content, contact level or coach of a normal lesson.
