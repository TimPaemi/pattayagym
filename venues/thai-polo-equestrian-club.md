# Thai Polo & Equestrian Club

Thai Polo & Equestrian Club is a large working polo and equestrian complex in Pong. The operator publishes instruction, trial rides and club play alongside competition facilities, but it does not publish a visitor tariff or promise walk-in access. Treat the listed hours as a business window and arrange the exact activity before travelling.

## What training is on offer

The club describes a polo school with individual training, beginner chukkas after lessons, club chukkas and a weekend clinic. Its service page defines the weekend clinic as a two-hour morning lesson followed by four afternoon chukkas on each day. It also advertises a trial ride, but the public page does not state the ride length, rider prerequisites or whether a horse is always included.

The operator's facilities page describes a 250-hectare, or roughly 2,000-rai, property with three full-size polo fields, two practice fields, clubhouses, stable blocks, a 20-metre saltwater pool and an equine clinic. It also names grass dressage, show-jumping, eventing and cross-country facilities. Those are facility-level facts, not evidence that every discipline accepts casual visitors.

## What it costs

No current public price was found for a polo lesson, trial ride, weekend clinic, chukka, membership or event ticket on the operator pages checked on 26 July 2026. A missing tariff does not mean free access.

Ask the club for the total for the named product, whether horse use and equipment are included, the required experience level, cancellation terms and any spectator admission. Obtain the answer in writing before comparing it with another [Pattaya equestrian venue](/category/equestrian/).

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The published polo-school formats make the club relevant to someone deliberately arranging polo instruction or supervised play. The broad facility set may also fit an established rider entering a named competition or clinic after the organiser confirms eligibility.

It is a poor fit for a traveller expecting an unguided walk-in horse ride, a published low-cost attraction or automatic access to pools, fields and stables. Families seeking an ordinary bookable activity should not infer public admission from the size of the grounds or the event calendar.

## Getting there

The operator and Maps place the club at 111 Polo Drive, Moo 9, Pong, east of Pattaya. Maps shows the exact current identity as “THAI POLO & EQUESTRIAN CLUB” and lists phone +66 81 617 4744. This inland address belongs in the broad [East Pattaya area](/area/east-pattaya/), not the central beach corridor.

No operator source checked publishes a public shuttle, fixed transfer fare or reliable journey time. Use the exact Maps pin and confirm which gate or clubhouse the booking uses.

## Before you go

Maps lists Tuesday-Sunday 08:00-17:00 and Monday closed. The operator's current 2025/26 calendar separately names dated fixtures, including the Thai Polo Open final on 24 January 2026 and Pink Polo on 28 February 2026. Neither business hours nor a past fixture establishes access on a future date.

Confirm the activity, date, arrival point, horse allocation, rider experience, clothing, protective equipment and weather policy. Spectators should ask whether the chosen event is public and whether registration or admission is required.
