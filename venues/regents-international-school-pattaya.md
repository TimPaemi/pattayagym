# Regents International School Pattaya Sports Programme

Regents runs PE and competitive sport for its students, supported by school pools, a sports hall, athletics space and playing fields. The school also publishes specific holiday camps.

This is not verified as an independently bookable public venue. Families should use the school or a published camp registration channel; the directory should not imply that visitors can walk in to use the facilities.
