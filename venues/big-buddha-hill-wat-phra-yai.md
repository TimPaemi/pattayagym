# Wat Phra Yai — Big Buddha Hill Stair Climb

Wat Phra Yai is an active hilltop temple on Pratamnak, not a gym or outdoor fitness park. Its staircase can form one segment of a respectful walk, but it does not offer supervised training or a measured workout. The religious site comes first, and the official visitor page leaves access and admission questions unresolved.

## What training is on offer

No formal training is offered. The record exists because the approach includes a substantial staircase that walkers may encounter on a Pratamnak route. The Tourism Authority of Thailand's official page says the main Buddha image was built in 1977 and is approximately 10 metres wide and 18 metres high. It also describes guardian Buddha images associated with days of the week and views across Pattaya and the coast.

Those facts establish a cultural attraction, not a stair circuit, lap length, calorie count or exercise prescription. For conventional public exercise equipment, compare [Pratumnak Fitness Park](/gyms/pratumnak-fitness-park/) instead.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This listing suits a respectful walker already planning time on Pratamnak Hill who wants to understand what the staircase represents. It does not suit someone seeking weights, coaching, changing rooms, a measured stair workout or an accessible route documented in advance.

The [Pratamnak area index](/area/pratamnak/) separates public exercise, commercial gyms, hotel amenities and private clubs. Someone seeking organised outdoor activity should use the [running and cycling guide](/guides/running-cycling-clubs-pattaya/) rather than treating the temple as a sports venue.

## Before you go

The official Tourism Authority page lists daily visiting hours of 10:00-20:00. Check for local restrictions caused by worship, maintenance or an event. Dress for an active temple and follow posted rules about footwear, photography and sacred areas.

Walk without obstructing worshippers or other visitors. Do not run through groups, repeat drills on the landings, play loud audio, place equipment against religious objects or use the main platform as a training station. Anyone choosing stairs should make a conservative personal decision about heat, hydration, surface conditions and capability.
