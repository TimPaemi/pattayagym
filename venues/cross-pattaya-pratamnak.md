# Cross Pattaya Pratamnak — Cloud Club

## Facilities

The hotel's Cloud Club page confirms treadmills, exercise bicycles and a quiet yoga area. Towels, refreshments and snacks are supplied to users, and the hotel says private yoga or personal training can be arranged with 24 hours' notice.

The property also has a main saltwater pool and a separate shallow children's pool.

## Hours and access

- Cloud Club: 06:00–22:00
- Main and children's pools: 07:00–20:00

These are operator-published hours checked on 25 July 2026. The current site presents the facilities as hotel activities and does not publish a non-resident day pass. The earlier day-pass claim has been removed. Non-guests should ask the hotel about access before travelling.

No public gym or pool price is published in this record.
