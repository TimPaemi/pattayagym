East Pattaya's clubs result is a useful multi-sport contact page, not a running or cycling club calendar. It lists lawn bowls across several facilities, Huai Yai archery, cricket based around Horseshoe Point, and a Nong Prue municipal stadium. The category label should not turn any of those into a verified group ride, running club, fixed track session or public stadium booking. Pattaya Lawn Bowls Facilities is the most structured club-style entry: Pattaya Sports Club lists four facilities with indoor, covered, grass and artificial-rink options. Banchang is listed Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 10:00, while The Retreat has Wednesday, Friday and Sunday afternoon sessions; other times vary. Confirm the chosen venue, surface, visitor eligibility, bowls, fee and exact start rather than treating the times as one Pattaya-wide timetable. Pattaya Archery Club is a non-profit Huai Yai club with shooting Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 10:00–12:00. Its published beginner coaching is Tuesday and Saturday 10:00–12:00 or by arrangement, and the record carries annual membership pricing. Ask about membership eligibility, equipment, range rules, booking and supervision before attending; a shooting window is not a guarantee of an unsupervised drop-in lane. Pattaya Cricket Club is the correct contact for cricket rather than a generic East Pattaya activity. Current league evidence identifies Horseshoe Point as its home ground, but match and training times vary by fixture. Contact the club with the date, playing experience and whether you seek a match, training or social introduction; do not travel on the basis of a past fixture. Nong Prue Municipal Stadium has municipal facilities and current youth-football competition use, but public-use hours require confirmation with Nong Prue Municipality. It is not evidence of an open athletics track, running group, hire rate or cycling meeting point. Choose the specific club only after confirming its current session, or use the running and cycling guide for sport-led planning.
