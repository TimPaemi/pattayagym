# Eastern National Sports Center

The Eastern National Sports Center is a public regional complex on Chaiyapruek 2. Thailand's DASTA public-facility listing describes an indoor sports building and recreation space used for exercise and organised events, with daily 09:00-20:00 hours.

General admission is listed as **free**, checked 25 July 2026. That does not guarantee that every court, field or event is available without booking or a separate charge. Contact the centre about the sport, public time slot and equipment you need.

First-time visitors should also confirm the correct entrance, parking, changing facilities and any event-day restrictions.
