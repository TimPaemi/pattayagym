# Thai Wake Park Pattaya

Thai Wake Park Pattaya is a full-cable wakeboarding venue in Nong Pla Lai. The operator publishes lower-speed beginner periods as well as general riding, but its current numeric tariff is embedded in a booking system and could not be verified as stable page text. Select the rider, duration and equipment before comparing costs.

The Thai wake-park pages and local map index identify the 58 Soi 3 branch in Nong Pla Lai as **ไทยเวคพาร์ค พัทยา**.

## What training is on offer

The operator publishes full-cable riding and low-speed periods intended for beginners. Those periods run Monday-Friday 10:30-12:00 and Saturday-Sunday 09:00-10:30. A lower cable speed may help a first attempt, but it is not a guarantee of individual coaching or a risk-free session.

The price-conditions page says riding rates include a life jacket, basic board, service charge and VAT. A helmet is mandatory and available to rent. The public pages do not establish that an instructor is included with an ordinary riding ticket.

## What it costs

No dependable current numeric rate could be extracted from the operator's public page on 26 July 2026 without entering the live booking flow. No price is inferred from visitor reviews or older posts.

Use the official booking system to select the Pattaya park, date, rider, duration, lesson if needed and equipment upgrade. The operator says tickets are personal, cannot be shared and run continuously for the purchased duration without pausing. A ticket may be cancelled and refunded before riding starts; once started, it is non-refundable.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The published beginner-speed windows make the park relevant to a first-timer who is prepared to confirm instruction and equipment before booking. It also suits an experienced cable rider who understands the continuous ticket clock and can choose the required board and helmet.

It is less suitable for someone expecting a beach-based lesson, an automatically included coach or a published walk-up total. For other water activities, compare the [Pattaya watersports category](/category/watersports/) or browse the broader [East Pattaya area](/area/east-pattaya/).

## Getting there

The operator and Maps place the park at 58 Soi 3, Nong Pla Lai, Bang Lamung. Maps confirms the exact current identity “THAI WAKE PARK - Pattaya (TWP Pattaya)” and phone +66 87 997 9760. This is an inland cable park, not a central Pattaya beach launch.

No operator shuttle, fixed fare or reliable transfer time was published. Use the exact Maps pin and confirm the entrance if arranging a driver.

## Before you go

Published hours are Monday-Friday 10:30-18:30 and Saturday-Sunday 09:00-18:30. All riders must register, sign a waiver and provide a photo. A rider under 15 needs a parent or guardian present to sign, or the operator's specified notarised waiver arrangement.

Life jacket and helmet use are mandatory. Confirm whether the selected ticket includes coaching, which board is suitable, how equipment upgrades are charged and how weather or technical interruptions are handled.
