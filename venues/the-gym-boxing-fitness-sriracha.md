# The Gym Boxing & Fitness

The Gym Boxing & Fitness is a boxing-led exercise gym on the second floor of Atara Mall in Sriracha. Its active owner Facebook page and current Google Maps listing both show recent activity and daily opening from 10:00 to 22:00.

The venue promotes boxing and general fitness sessions for adults and children. It does not publish a reliable public price list or fixed class timetable, so contact the gym before travelling. The owner page and Maps listing currently show different phone numbers; both are retained above.

## First visit

Use the Srirachanakhon 3 entrance to Atara Mall and go to the second floor. Ask whether the first session is coached or open training, what gloves and wraps are supplied, and whether a child needs an age-specific booking.
