★ About this venue

First Serve Sports Club — not in Pattaya

What training is on offer

First Serve is an operating sports club, but its own site and current Maps identity place it in Pak Kret, Nonthaburi, not Pattaya. This record is retained as a location correction so the same name is not repeatedly mistaken for a Pattaya swimming option.

The operator's swimming pages advertise one-hour lessons for students aged three and older. Lessons can be private or small-group, and the site says teaching is available in Thai or English while warning that English instruction has limited times. The pool also publishes open-swim entry and lane booking. These are operator claims for the Nonthaburi club; they are not Pattaya recommendations.

What it costs

English-language swimming prices checked on 26 July 2026 were ฿1,200 for one student, ฿1,400 for two, ฿1,600 for three and ฿1,800 for four for a private trial lesson. Ten-class English packages were listed at ฿10,000, ฿12,000, ฿14,000 and ฿16,000 for groups of one through four respectively.

Open swimming was listed at ฿100 per hour for an adult and ฿60 per hour for a child under 12. The operator also published a ฿1,200 monthly daily-access option and a ฿1,000 monthly option limited to 18:00–21:00. Reconfirm every rate and validity rule before paying.

Who it suits — and who it does not

It may suit someone already based near Pak Kret who wants a one-hour lesson, a small private group or open pool time. English learners need to confirm that an English-speaking instructor is available for the exact slot.

It does not suit a traveller searching for swimming in Pattaya: Pak Kret is in Nonthaburi. Use the Pattaya swimming category or the Pattaya swimming-pools guide for local records. This venue must not be counted in Pattaya area comparisons.

Getting there

The official address is 25/477 Chaeng Wattana–Pak Kret 24, Si Chai Thong 19, Bang Talat, Pak Kret, Nonthaburi 11120. The current Maps listing resolves to First Serve Sports Club at that Nonthaburi location.

The operator publishes open-swim hours of 08:00–21:00 Monday–Friday and 07:00–21:00 Saturday–Sunday. Those pool hours do not establish lesson availability or an empty lane. Contact the club before making a cross-city journey.

Before you go

Call +66 2 101 3889 or +66 86 885 8799. State whether the request is for open swimming, lane booking, a trial or a ten-class course; for lessons, provide the swimmer's age, current level, preferred language and group size. Ask for the exact start time, instructor availability, package expiry, make-up policy, swimwear and cap rules, and whether a guardian must remain for a child.

What we could not verify

The checked pages did not establish a current day-by-day lesson timetable, which English slots remain available, whether open-swim admission guarantees a lane, or the refund and rescheduling terms. This record also provides no evidence of any Pattaya branch. The missing Pattaya connection is a confirmed location mismatch, not a missing-price problem.
