# Flight of the Gibbon Pattaya

## Closed

Flight of the Gibbon Pattaya formerly ran a zipline course at Khao Kheow. The Chonburi Attractions Association lists the business as closed, and the 26 July 2026 exact-name Maps check found no current venue or reopening signal.

The former phone, timetable, course statistics and package price have been removed because they are not current. Do not use this record to plan a visit.
