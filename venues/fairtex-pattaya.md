# Fairtex Training Center Pattaya

Fairtex Training Center Pattaya is at 179/185 Pattaya Nuea Road and publishes Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training. The operator lists hours of 07:30-20:00 Monday-Saturday, while individual programme times need confirmation. Its price page gives BJJ classes Monday-Friday 18:30-20:00 and Saturday open mat 10:00-12:00.

The Thai-language tourism reference uses **แฟร์เท็กซ์** for the gym at 179/185 Pattaya Nuea Road.

## What training is on offer

The operator names Muay Thai, private Muay Thai, private boxing and BJJ. It presents programmes for beginners through experienced fighters, and its packages page lists accommodation-and-training options. The published disciplines do not guarantee that a particular coach, class level or private slot is available on a chosen day.

## What it costs

Prices checked on 2026-07-29: Muay Thai is ฿800 for one session, ฿7,000 for ten and ฿16,500 for an unlimited month. Private Muay Thai is ฿1,500 and private boxing ฿2,000. BJJ is ฿300 single, ฿2,600 for ten or ฿4,000 monthly; BJJ plus one Muay Thai session daily is ฿19,500 monthly. Accommodation packages are separately priced.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The public beginner-to-experienced positioning and individual BJJ schedule give useful starting points for both disciplines. This does not confirm sparring policy, fitness requirements, children’s training, equipment hire, women-only sessions or medical clearance rules. Ask for the appropriate class before booking.
