# Pattaya City School 11 Swimming Pool

The Thai Maps identity **สระว่ายน้ำ โรงเรียนเมืองพัทยา 11 (มัธยมสาธิตพัทยา)** corresponds to the Nong Yai 6 school pool.

The pool at Pattaya City School 11 is a documented **50-metre, eight-lane** long-course competition venue. Race-programme sources provide those specifications, while the exact Maps search identifies the pool within the school in Nong Prue.

Thailand Aquatics' official competition page records the **18th Pattaya Open on 20-21 June 2026** at Pattaya City School 11 (Pattaya Demonstration Secondary School) and now provides event results. That is strong current evidence for sanctioned meet use. It is not evidence of an ordinary public lap-swimming timetable.

The record sits in the <a href="/category/swimming/">Pattaya swimming directory</a> because the pool itself is verified, not because public entry is verified. The <a href="/area/east-pattaya/">East Pattaya area page</a> gives location context for Nong Prue alternatives.

## Public access

Public lane access, day-pass prices, lessons, changing facilities and regular opening hours could not be verified from a current school or Pattaya City source. No phone number or public booking route was reliable enough to publish in this record. Competition results also do not show whether the pool is available between school activity and meets.

This is therefore an evidence-limited profile, most useful to confirmed event participants and spectators. Recreational swimmers should contact Pattaya City or the school before travelling and ask for the exact date, public session, lane availability, eligibility, fee and entrance. Do not assume that school hours, a past meet window or the live Maps pin creates walk-in pool access.

A swimmer who needs a documented visitor pass or ordinary lane hours should use the <a href="/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/">swimming-pools guide</a> to compare managed alternatives. School competition specifications and meet results answer a different question from recreational access.
