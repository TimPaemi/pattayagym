# Real Divers Pattaya

Real Divers is a PADI 5 Star IDC in Jomtien. PADI lists daily 08:00-19:30 shop hours and activities spanning recreational scuba, technical diving, freediving, snorkelling and first aid. The operator describes speedboat trips that normally leave in the morning, with a larger charter boat used when sea conditions require it.

## Price and first visit

The operator's 2026 anniversary offer lists its Discover Scuba Diving Gold package at THB 4,900, reduced from THB 5,900, for two coral-reef dives with photos and video. Check eligibility and availability directly, and ask what equipment, transfers, insurance and medical declarations are included.
