# Jetts Fitness Central Si Racha

Jetts Central Si Racha is **Room 226 on the second floor of Central Si Racha**, at 8/1 Sukhumvit Road. It is a separate branch from Jetts Little Walk in Pattaya Klang. The branch page publishes **24-hour access for members** and staffed hours of **06:00–22:00 Monday–Friday** and **08:00–20:00 on Saturday, Sunday and public holidays**.

That distinction matters for a short-stay visitor. The current visitor-pass flow allows Central Si Racha to be selected, but it limits visitor access to the staffed window. Do not interpret the 24-hour headline as permission to arrive for first registration or use a visitor pass after reception closes.

## Visitor-pass prices checked 26 July 2026

After selecting **Central Si Racha (Chonburi)** in Jetts Thailand's official visitor-pass page, the following branch products were displayed:

- **1 day: ฿500**
- **3 days: ฿1,000**
- **7 days: ฿1,500**
- **30 days: ฿2,500**

The operator says each pass covers the purchased Jetts home club and its classes, with access during staffed hours. The visitor page also says there is no entry fee or long-term contract and that identity can be established with an ID card or passport. These prices and terms describe the visitor product selected for Central Si Racha; they are not a general monthly-membership tariff and should not be copied to another branch without selecting that branch in the live flow.

## Member access is a different product

Jetts' membership page describes monthly, no-lock-in membership with all-club access, classes, gym equipment and 24/7 entry. It does not publish a stable Central Si Racha monthly amount on the pages checked. Ask the branch for the complete current membership quote, including any access-card charge, first payment, billing date, freeze or cancellation terms and when access to other clubs begins.

A visitor pass may be the clearer choice for a trip of up to 30 days because the price and duration are visible before purchase. A membership may suit a longer stay or someone who needs member-only 24-hour entry, but the two products should not be compared by price alone. Confirm whether a pass begins on purchase, first entry or a selected date and whether its days are consecutive.

## First visit

Use **+66 80 281 3660** for the branch or **+66 2 255 8042** as the second number published on the official club page. Arrive while staff are present with the same identity document used for registration. Before paying, check the class timetable for the intended dates and ask whether booking is required, because “all classes” does not guarantee a place in a full session.

Also confirm the mall entrance available at the intended hour, parking validation, locker and towel arrangements, footwear rules and any induction requirement. For very early or late training, establish that the selected product is a 24-hour membership rather than a visitor pass. The mall address and branch phone are supported by both the Jetts club page and the current Maps listing.
