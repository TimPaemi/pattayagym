# Pattaya Monkey Hash House Harriers

Pattaya Monkey H3 is active. The operator says its runs take about two and a half hours, take place every second odd-numbered month and are usually held on the third Saturday. The trail and meeting point change, so the next-run page is the source to use.

The group explicitly describes itself as **men only** and says entrants should be able to walk at least 8 km over moderately difficult terrain in two hours. This is not a beginner sightseeing walk.

## Current run fee

Run 125 on 25 July 2026 had a **฿1,300** signup fee. The operator said this included a shirt memento, transport, beer or soft drinks, circle food and the post-run venue. Registration had closed by verification time. That fee applies to this run only; check the next event rather than assuming it will stay the same.
