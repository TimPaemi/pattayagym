# Chee Chan Golf Resort

Chee Chan Golf Resort is a Na Jomtien golf record with a current dated public rate card, which makes it easier to price honestly than many Pattaya-area courses. It is an 18-hole David Dale design beside Chee Chan Mountain, but the green fee is only part of the total because the resort's 2026 public card also states separate compulsory caddie-and-cart charges.

## What training is on offer

The resort's own golf pages describe **an 18-hole championship course** designed by **David Dale of Golfplan**. The about page also says the site occupies **580 rai (229 acres)** and sits beside the Buddha image on Chee Chan Mountain, which the resort says can be seen from every hole.

This is not just a tee-sheet product. The site navigation and facilities pages also support a **driving range**, **golf academy**, **restaurant**, **pro shop** and **locker rooms**. The facilities page says the locker rooms include spacious changing facilities, showers and a grooming area. Those are normal golf-club support features, but they matter because many course pages in Pattaya publish a scenic image without stating the basic pre- and post-round setup.

Use the wider [golf category](/category/golf/) for course comparison and the [best golf courses guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) for a decision shortlist that distinguishes championship rounds from easier resort-day products.

## What it costs

The resort's public rate card valid **1 April 2026 to 30 September 2026**, checked again on **2026-07-27**, lists:

- **THB 4,500** for **weekday 18 holes**
- **THB 2,250** for **weekday 9 holes**
- **THB 5,500** for **weekend or public-holiday 18 holes**
- **THB 2,750** for **weekend or public-holiday 9 holes**

The same official card states that these green fees **exclude** cart and caddie:

- **THB 1,300** excluded cart-and-caddie fee for **18 holes**
- **THB 700** excluded cart-and-caddie fee for **9 holes**

That means the documented required totals start higher than the headline green fee. A weekday 18-hole round begins from **THB 5,800** before any club rental, food or tip. A weekend or public-holiday 18-hole round begins from **THB 6,800** on the same basis.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Chee Chan suits a player who wants a destination course with a transparent public card and is comfortable booking a proper golf round rather than a casual range visit only. It also suits travellers who want a premium south-of-Pattaya golf day and need the cost separated into green fee plus compulsory course charges.

It is a weaker fit for someone seeking a cheap driving-range session, a central Pattaya sport block or a same-day walk-up activity that can be priced in one line. This is a planned round, not a quick Beach Road booking. The [Sattahip and Na Jomtien area guide](/area/sattahip/) is the relevant location filter when comparing it with farther-south golf and resort records.

## Before you go

Reserve a tee time directly with the club and ask whether the current date still falls inside the public-rate-card period or a different promotion. Confirm whether rental clubs, dress-code enforcement, academy bookings, range use and practice access are priced separately from the round.

Also check whether the player wants 9 or 18 holes before asking for a quote. The official card prices both, and the excluded cart-and-caddie amount changes with the selected round length.
