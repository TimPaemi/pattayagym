★ About this venue

Balance Yoga Studio Pattaya

Balance Yoga Studio Pattaya is a small Pratamnak studio inside a condominium rather than a street-front commercial gym. Its current site ties the venue to yoga, stretching, sound healing and nail-board products, which is useful if that mixed wellness menu is exactly what you want. The limitation is schedule certainty: the public timetable page was not current enough to use as a live weekly calendar.

What training is on offer

The current Balance site presents a broader programme than a simple drop-in yoga room. The main homepage names styles and sessions including Power Vinyasa, kundalini, yin yoga, stretching, body balance, sound healing and a children’s class. That makes the venue a genuine yoga record, but also one where not every priced item is a standard yoga class.

If your goal is an ordinary yoga session, separate that from the studio’s sound-healing and nail-board products. They are sold on the same price page but they are not interchangeable class types. For a broader decision between city yoga options, compare the Pratamnak area page and the wider yoga guides rather than assuming every wellness product here is a drop-in yoga lesson.

What it costs

The current price page listed a one-time group yoga class at ฿500, a sadhu nails board session at ฿1,500, a handmade nail-board session at ฿1,000 and a group sound-healing session at ฿1,000 (operator price page, checked 2026-07-27). Those are direct studio prices, but they describe different services and should not be treated as a single “yoga range”.

The practical question is therefore not only “how much is Balance?” but “which product am I booking?” If you want yoga, confirm the exact class and date. If you want sound healing or nail-board work, ask whether it is group or individual, how long it lasts and what preparation is required.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Balance suits a traveller staying around Cosy Beach or Pratamnak who wants a smaller studio setting and is open to a mixed yoga-and-wellness menu. It also suits someone comfortable booking directly and confirming the exact class rather than relying on a fully current public timetable.

It is less suitable for a visitor who wants a large commercial fitness club, a street-level walk-in class with posted daily reception hours or a fully current online schedule they can trust without messaging. If you need a simpler current drop-in comparison, another Pattaya yoga venue with a clearer timetable may be easier.

Getting there

The studio is inside Cosy Beach View Condominium on the 14th floor at 400/398 Moo 12, Kasetsin 9, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi. That matters because this is not a ground-floor gym frontage you can treat like a mall unit. The record keeps both published contacts: +66 82 186 1937 and +66 62 082 5824. The first number is presented for Thai and English communication, while the second is presented for Russian and English.

For planning, treat this as a Pratamnak building-access venue. Save the exact map and allow time to find the correct entrance and floor.

Before you go

Book before travelling. The checked public schedule page still displayed a February 2026 timetable on 2026-07-27, so it should be treated as a format example rather than a live weekly promise. Ask which class is actually running on your date, what language support is available, what to bring and whether mats or props are provided.

Because the studio is inside a condominium, also ask about reception, elevator access, late arrival and whether the session starts exactly on time. Those small building details matter more here than at a roadside studio.

What we could not verify

  • No dependable current week-by-week timetable was visible on the checked public schedule page as of 2026-07-27.
  • No public page on the checked site stated the exact duration of every class and wellness product.
  • No public page on the checked site gave a full equipment or mat-provision policy.
  • The checked public pages did not show a complete cancellation or no-show policy.
