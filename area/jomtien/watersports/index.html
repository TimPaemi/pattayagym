Jomtien's watersports page spans scuba, wind sports, crewed sailing, marina racing and contact-first powered or paddle activities. It also retains Mermaids Dive Center as a permanent-closure warning. Choose the product before the location: a Jomtien dive shop, Blue Lagoon kite school and Ocean Marina charter do not share a departure point, timetable or eligibility model. Jomtien Dive Center publishes the clearest broad dive tariff. Prices checked 27 July 2026 include Pattaya fun diving at THB 2,800, Samaesan at THB 3,200, Discover Scuba Diving at THB 3,900, snorkelling at THB 1,495 and PADI Open Water at THB 15,900. Equipment rental is THB 300 per day, a computer THB 500 and a third dive THB 1,000. The shop is open 08:00-20:00, while normal trips are listed 08:00-15:30; neither window alone proves the meeting or boat-departure point. Real Divers Pattaya offers another Jomtien beginner ladder. Its 26 July price page lists Discover Scuba at THB 3,300 for one dive plus snorkelling, THB 3,900 for two dives with photos, THB 4,500 with local pickup, and a promotional THB 4,900 Gold package with two dives, photos, video, equipment and meals. Its Open Water promotion is THB 14,500. Compare the exact inclusion rather than the package name, and complete the medical and certification checks honestly. For wind sports, Clubloongchat publishes one-hour lessons at THB 1,700, SUP or surf boards at THB 300, beginner windsurf sets at THB 500 and sailboats at THB 1,200. A first kitesurf or sailboat rental requires an instructor for the first hour at an additional THB 400. KBA Kiteboarding Asia Pattaya lists a one-hour kitesurf trial at THB 2,200, six-hour beginner course at THB 11,000, twelve-hour course at THB 20,000 and efoil introduction from THB 2,000 for 30 minutes. Both Na Jomtien schools are weather-dependent; confirm branch availability, equipment, supervision and rebooking terms. At Ocean Marina, SailBreeze lists an eight-hour shared cruise at THB 3,250 adult and THB 1,625 for ages 3-12, with under-threes free. Its half-day page lists THB 2,500 excluding VAT but conflicts between four and 4.5 hours. Ocean Marina Jomtien separately publishes a THB 7,500 Platu regatta entry fee and 2026 race dates; that is competition entry, not marina admission, a sailing lesson or casual boat hire. Pattaya Water Sports Club lists flyboard, wakeboard, SUP, jet ski, kayak and other activities from a Na Jomtien base, but no stable activity tariff or eligibility table. Request the activity, duration, launch point, equipment, guide, taxes, deposit and insurance in writing. Use the diving and watersports guide for the wider comparison, and do not follow old Mermaids directions.
