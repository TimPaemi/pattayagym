# Bangkok Hospital Pattaya Rehabilitation Center

This is a clinical rehabilitation service rather than a conventional gym. The hospital describes assessment and rehabilitation for musculoskeletal, orthopaedic and neurological conditions, including recovery after surgery or injury.

## Appointment and cost

No public fee table was available. Contact the Rehabilitation Center directly for appointment requirements, referral or imaging needs, therapist availability and an estimate for the proposed treatment. The centre is on the third floor of Building A and lists daily hours from 08:00 to 20:00.
