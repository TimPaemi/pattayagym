# Bira Circuit

Bira Circuit is a 2.41-kilometre road-racing track in Pong, north-east of central Pattaya. It offers shared motorcycle and car sessions plus whole-circuit hire. Access depends on the event calendar or an accepted reservation; it is not a turn-up-and-drive attraction.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

The live official track-hire page lists:

- shared motorcycle session, 09:00–12:00: ฿1,800
- shared car session, 13:00–17:00: ฿3,500
- full-circuit weekday hire: ฿130,000
- full-circuit weekend hire: ฿150,000

Confirm eligibility, vehicle inspection, required safety equipment and whether a session is running before travelling. The old page's unsupported claims about FIA status, pit capacity, spectator capacity and historic events have been removed.
