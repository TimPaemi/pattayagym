★ About this venue

Khao Chi Chan (Buddha Mountain)

Khao Chi Chan is a cultural sightseeing and light-walking stop near Na Jomtien, not a training venue. The Tourism Authority of Thailand lists daily 06:00-18:00 opening and asks visitors to dress respectfully and stay clear of the Buddha image and cliff. The Maps result identifies the cliff-carved attraction and distinguishes it from the nearby Wat Khao Chi Chan result.

The Thai Department of Mineral Resources page calls this 109-metre cliff landmark เขาชีจรรย์.

What training is on offer

No organised sport or training is documented here. The usable activity is a quiet walk around the landscaped grounds beneath the cliff-carved Buddha image. That makes it a cultural stop rather than a gym, running route, fitness park or recreational facility. This record intentionally does not describe it as a place for workouts or group exercise.

What it costs

No current official admission price, donation policy, guided-tour price, parking fee or other visitor charge was found in the Tourism Authority and Maps sources checked on 26 July 2026. Do not infer that entry is free from the lack of a quoted price. Confirm any current admission or parking arrangement directly with an official visitor source before travel.

Who it suits — and who it does not

It suits visitors seeking a respectful cultural stop and light walking in the Na Jomtien / Sattahip area. It does not suit someone looking for a training session, a managed sports facility, a swimming pool, shade for exercise or a documented public fitness programme. Visitors who cannot follow the stated respectful-dress and safe-distance guidance should choose another activity.

Getting there

The listed location is Khao Chi Chan, Na Jomtien, Sattahip District, Chonburi 20250. Use the exact Maps result for the Buddha Mountain attraction, not the nearby Wat Khao Chi Chan result. The sources checked do not provide a dependable public-transport fare, walking route, parking capacity or accessibility detail.

Before you go

Plan within the published 06:00-18:00 window and dress respectfully. Bring sun protection if needed, but do not treat that as evidence of a venue service. Confirm any current admission, parking, event or weather-related access information through an official source before travelling.

What we could not verify

No official current admission price, public phone number, parking policy, guided-tour arrangement, accessibility detail, visitor-facility inventory, public transport instruction or fitness use was published in the sources checked on 26 July 2026.
