★ About this venue

Pattaya Park Water Park

Thai travel coverage describes the 345 Moo 12 resort attraction near Pratamnak as สวนน้ำพัทยาปาร์ค.

What training is on offer

Pattaya Park Water Park is the water attraction inside the Pattaya Park Beach Resort complex. The operator's current water-park page describes giant water slides, a whirlpool and beachside swimming. Those are leisure facilities, not evidence of lap lanes, a coached swimming programme or a fitness class. Readers looking for structured pool use should compare the Pattaya swimming directory rather than treating every water attraction as a training venue.

The resort site and the exact Maps identity both resolve to Pattaya Park. This confirms the business and its water-park component; it does not establish that every photographed or historically advertised feature will operate on a particular day.

What it costs

No stable public admission tariff was visible on the operator pages checked on 27 July 2026. A missing price is not evidence that the water park is closed, and this record does not replace it with an amount from a reseller, review or old photograph.

Contact Pattaya Park on +66 38 251 201 and request the dated adult and child totals. Ask whether the quote covers the water park only or a combination with another part of the resort, how the child band is defined, and whether lockers, towels, tubes, deck seating or any deposit cost extra. The family-sport guide explains why a named admission product and its supervision rules matter more than an undated headline price.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This venue may suit a family or leisure visitor who wants slides and recreational water time within one resort complex. It does not suit a swimmer who requires a measured lane, guaranteed uninterrupted distance, a coach or a published class. The operator calls it a water park, so the directory does not imply competitive-pool specifications.

Anyone with a height, mobility or medical constraint should obtain the attraction's current eligibility and supervision rules directly. The directory cannot determine whether a slide or water feature is appropriate for an individual.

Getting there

The current listing places Pattaya Park at 345 Moo 12, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, on the Pratamnak and Jomtien side of Pattaya. Use the exact water-park or resort pin, not a generic neighbourhood destination. The Pratamnak area page gives broader location context without promising a route, travel time or fare.

The checked operator page does not publish a public-transport instruction, parking entitlement or a separate street entrance for water-park visitors. Ask which gate, ticket counter and parking arrangement apply before setting out.

Before you go

The exact Maps listing showed 09:00-18:00 daily at verification, but business hours do not guarantee that every feature operates for the entire window. Call on the intended date and confirm admission sales, last entry, slide operation, maintenance closures and weather policy.

Also ask what swimwear is accepted, whether flotation equipment is supplied or permitted, what adult supervision is required for children, and whether outside food, valuables storage or re-entry is allowed. Obtain cancellation or refund terms in writing if advance payment is requested.

What we could not verify

The operator did not expose a current public tariff, child age or height definition, last-entry time, ride-by-ride schedule, locker or towel terms, accessibility arrangements, lifeguard deployment or weather-refund policy. No claim is made here about lap dimensions, coaching or specific features beyond the slides, whirlpool and beachside water activity named by the operator.
