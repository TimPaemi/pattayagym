# Laem Chabang International Country Club

Laem Chabang is a 27-hole golf club in Si Racha. The operator describes three nine-hole loops and lists both daytime and night-golf charges.

## Visitor charges checked 25 July 2026

- 18-hole green fee: **฿3,500 weekday** or **฿4,000 weekend**
- caddie fee: **฿450**
- compulsory buggy: **฿980**
- full club set: **฿1,300**
- shoes: **฿400**

The required green fee, caddie and buggy total **฿4,930 on a weekday** or **฿5,430 at a weekend**, before any caddie tip. The club says VAT is included and rates may change. Advance booking is required; confirm the available tee-time window and whether night golf is operating on your date.
