★ About this venue

Ashtanga Yoga Pattaya

A Thai directory describes the business as สถาบันสอนอัษฎางค์โยคะ and lists the studio phone as 086 079 1731, while the current record keeps its practice name in English.

Ashtanga Yoga Pattaya is a small practice-focused studio in Building A at Royal Hill Condotel on Thappraya Road. Its operator describes Mysore-style learning and publishes direct Thai- and English-language phone contacts. The key limitation is deliberate: the studio now tells visitors to request the current schedule and prices.

What training is on offer

The practice page describes Mysore style as individual instruction within a shared room. Students learn a sequence gradually and progress at their own pace rather than following every movement in a synchronised class. That format can accommodate different experience levels, but it requires the teacher to place each student appropriately.

An undated image on the schedule page shows self-practice, private sessions by arrangement, Ashtanga Elements and Yin or restorative sessions. Because the operator’s contact page explicitly says to ask for the current schedule and prices, those image slots are not reproduced here as a dependable present-week timetable.

What it costs

No price is presented as current in this record. The schedule page displays an undated image with baht amounts, while the operator’s contact page instructs visitors in capital letters to contact the studio for the current schedule and prices. That direct warning takes precedence over attaching a fresh check date to an old image.

The former THB figures and priceAsOf metadata have therefore been removed. Ask for the drop-in price, private-session price, pass validity, expiry, payment method, missed-class rule and refund terms in one reply. A blank price means unresolved, not free.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This studio suits someone specifically seeking Ashtanga or Mysore-style practice and willing to contact the teacher before arrival. The shared-room, individual-progression description can appeal to a practitioner who does not want a generic fitness-yoga flow. The Pattaya yoga guide explains how this differs from retreats and hotel activity classes.

It is less suitable for a visitor who needs instant online booking, a reliably dated weekly timetable or a current public price before messaging. Browse the yoga directory for alternatives, while remembering that several Pattaya yoga records are also appointment-led.

Getting there

The operator contact page places the studio at Royal Hill Condotel, Building A, on Thappraya Road. The current Maps identity gives the same complex and lists +66 86 079 1731. Use the exact building and map pin rather than only the broader Royal Hill name.

The Pratamnak area index provides location context. The public pages do not publish a visitor-parking process, reception procedure, baht-bus route or fixed transfer time.

Before you go

Call +66 86 079 1731 for Thai or +66 82 210 2446 for English. Send the intended date, prior Ashtanga experience and whether you want Mysore practice, an elements class, Yin/restorative work or a private session. Ask which class is running and how early to arrive.

Confirm mat provision, clothing, cash or QR acceptance, instructor language, changing facilities and any condominium access instructions. The live Maps profile shows day-specific business hours, but those should not override the studio’s own request to confirm the current class schedule.

What we could not verify

  • No dated current price card or timetable was published.
  • The current relationship between Maps business hours and actual class start times is unresolved.
  • Class capacity, mat provision, teaching language by session and private-session duration were not stated.
  • Parking, changing facilities, booking deadline, cancellation and refund terms require direct confirmation.
