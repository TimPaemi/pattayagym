★ About this venue

Pattaya Water Sports Club

A Thai-language Pattaya hotel brochure lists Pattaya Water Sports Club as พัทยา วอเตอร์ สปอร์ต คลับ and notes 09:00-17:00 hours at 73/95.

What training is on offer

The operator site lists flyboard, wakeboard, stand-up paddleboard, jet ski, kayak, stand-up jet ski, Red Shark water bike and banana-boat activities. It also advertises a yachting service connected with Ocean Marina. These labels establish the operator's activity range; they do not show that every option runs every day or that every product is a lesson.

The live Maps identity categorises the business as a watersports centre at Soi Na Jomtien 22 and links the same operator domain. It is separate from Clubloongchat: the address, phone and published activity mix differ. Compare other water-based records in the watersports category and use the diving and watersports guide to separate a powered ride, paddle activity, yacht enquiry and certified dive product.

What it costs

No stable current activity tariff was published on the operator-published pages checked on 26 July 2026. The missing price does not make the venue a skip: its current Maps and operator identities confirm that it exists.

Request a written quote that names the activity, duration, participant and passenger count, instructor or guide, equipment, transfer or launch arrangement, taxes and deposit. Do not carry a price from one powered or paddle product to another.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The club may suit a visitor prepared to book a specific powered-water or paddle activity and confirm the operating conditions. A mixed group should ask whether passengers and non-riders are accepted and how each person is charged.

It does not suit someone who needs a published price, guaranteed same-day walk-in slot or independently verified beginner lesson before contact. The operator's activity list alone does not establish minimum skill, swimming ability, age, licence or medical requirements. A yacht enquiry is also a different product from an activity launched at the beach base.

Getting there

The exact Maps listing gives 73/95 Moo 4, Soi Na Jomtien 22, Na Chom Thian, Sattahip, Chonburi 20250, the phone +66 81 781 9196 and the official website. It lists 08:30–17:00 every day. Those are business hours, not a guarantee that weather, equipment and staff allow every activity throughout the window.

The Na Jomtien location is south of central Pattaya. Confirm whether the meeting point is the listed base, Ocean Marina or another launch location, and ask whether transport is included. The Sattahip and Na Jomtien area page provides the broader corridor context.

Before you go

Contact the club by phone, Facebook or Line @pwsc with the exact date, participant ages, experience, swimming ability where relevant, group size and desired activity. Ask for start and finish times, weather cancellation and refund rules, safety briefing, instructor or guide role, lifejacket and helmet provision, passenger limits and rescue arrangements.

For powered equipment, ask what identification, licence, deposit and damage terms apply. Confirm clothing, footwear, valuables storage, shower or changing provision, and whether food or water is included. Recheck conditions on the day and follow the operator's go/no-go decision.

What we could not verify

We could not verify a current rate card, activity-by-activity schedule, lesson syllabus, staff qualifications, language guarantee, age or licence table, deposit amount, insurance wording, cancellation policy or exact Ocean Marina arrangement. The operator and base are current; those product and eligibility details require direct confirmation.
