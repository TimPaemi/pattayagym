★ About this venue

EasyKart Pattaya Thepprasit

EasyKart Pattaya Thepprasit is a late-opening outdoor kart venue on Thepprasit Road with separate products for children, first-time adult drivers and faster sessions on the advanced circuit. It suits travellers who want a clearly published kart menu rather than a quote-by-message activity. Its limitation is that the operator does not publish weather rules, waiting times or a full safety-equipment list on the checked pages.

The Thai operator page labels the 168/24 Thepprasit circuit on Thepprasit Road as อีซี่คาร์ท พัทยา (เทพประสิทธิ).

What training is on offer

The operator describes four kart products on the current EasyKart Pattaya Thepprasit page: a kids kart, a regular kart, a fast kart and a two-seat kart. The same page says the beginner track is 350 metres and places the kids and regular products on that easier circuit, while the fast kart is reserved for the advanced track.

The eligibility rules are part of the product, not a footnote. EasyKart lists the kids kart for ages 7-13 above 125 cm, the regular kart for ages 12+ above 135 cm, the fast kart for ages 15+ above 150 cm and the two-seat kart for one adult driver with a passenger above 110 cm. That makes this one of the clearer family-facing records in the adventure directory.

What it costs

The operator's current rate card on the checked Thepprasit page lists kids kart at ฿499 for one race (operator page checked 2026-07-27). Regular kart is listed at ฿699 for one race or ฿1,999 for three races plus a free T-shirt (operator page checked 2026-07-27). Fast kart is listed at ฿1,499 for two races or ฿1,999 for three races plus a free T-shirt (operator page checked 2026-07-27). The two-seat kart is listed at ฿699 for one race or ฿1,999 for three races plus a free T-shirt (operator page checked 2026-07-27).

Those are kart-session prices, not a promise of an empty track or private use. The operator page does not publish a separate helmet fee, insurance add-on, locker fee or spectator package on the checked pages. If your plan depends on a child riding, confirm the height rule before travelling rather than assuming a near-threshold passenger will be admitted.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This venue best suits families with children who meet the posted height rules, first-timers who want a beginner track, and mixed groups where not everyone wants the fastest kart. The split between kids, regular and fast products is more useful than a one-price adventure listing because you can match the booking to the rider.

It is not the right pick for someone seeking coaching, competition preparation or a guaranteed low-noise beginner environment. The operator pages do not publish private instruction, dedicated training sessions or quiet-family-only periods. If the trip priority is a broader compare-all-activities shortlist, start with the adventure category and then narrow by access model.

Getting there

EasyKart is on Thepprasit Road at 168/24 Moo 12 in Nong Prue, with the operator's contact page and the live Maps pin both pointing to the same Thepprasit location. That places it in the south side of the Pattaya urban belt rather than deep East Pattaya or Sattahip. The record belongs with other central-south activity listings because the area matcher treats South Pattaya and Thepprasit references as part of the broader central filter.

The checked operator contact page publishes the same phone number as the venue file: +66 33 042 455. Use the exact pin in Maps rather than a generic EasyKart city search because Pattaya has more than one EasyKart location in operator marketing.

Before you go

Book the exact kart category you want, not just "go karting in Pattaya". The operator separates eligibility by age and height, and those rules are the first thing to confirm if a child or teenager is involved. Ask about current waiting time, rain handling and whether your selected product is beginner-track or advanced-track before making a dedicated trip.

What we could not verify

  • The checked pages do not publish weather-cancellation or rain-delay rules.
  • No separate published fee for helmets, lockers or other gear was visible on the checked pages.
  • The exact advanced-track length was not repeated here because the operator material around track lengths is inconsistent across EasyKart pages.
  • No published waiting-time guidance or last-race cutoff was visible on the checked pages as of 2026-07-27.
