# Rusich Club Pattaya

Rusich is not a football academy. Its current programme covers wrestling disciplines, Muay Thai and mixed fighting, grappling, hand-to-hand fighting, MMA and functional training for age-specific child and adult groups.

## Price and first visit

The operator lists THB 350 per class for its named combat classes, checked 25 July 2026. Contact the club for the current timetable, age group, trial rules and any uniform or protective-equipment cost.
