## Before you go

Maps publishes **09:00-18:00 daily**, but holiday or operational hours can change. Call first and ask for the date, arrival time, activity, total price, required identification, minimum age, clothing or footwear rule, and whether advance payment is required. Also ask what eye and hearing protection the range supplies.

On arrival, wait for the range officer's briefing. Do not handle equipment, ammunition or targets until instructed, and disclose any language or accessibility need before the session is accepted.
