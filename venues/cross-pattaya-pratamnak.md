# Cross Pattaya Pratamnak — Cloud Club

Cross Pattaya Pratamnak's Cloud Club is best understood as a hotel wellness amenity, not a normal public gym. The hotel's own pages publish useful facility and hour details, but they do not currently publish a non-resident access product, so this record is valuable mainly as an honest hotel-facility reference rather than a guaranteed day-pass recommendation.

## What training is on offer

The operator presents **Cloud Club** as the property's fitness and wellness centre. The current public hotel copy says the space is suitable for activities "from treadmill and bicycle workouts to a quiet space for yoga." It also says **private yoga lessons or a personal trainer can be arranged with 24 hours' notice**.

That language supports a real fitness room and wellness area, but it does not establish a commercial class timetable or a public membership model. This matters when comparing it with an independent gym. A hotel may honestly publish treadmills, bicycles and yoga space while still offering them only as guest amenities or by private arrangement.

The hotel's broader facility pages also support a **saltwater pool** and a **children's pool**. Those are useful leisure or recovery features, but they should not be turned into a lap-training claim or a public family-pool ticket unless the hotel publishes that separate access product.

If your actual goal is a hotel pool day rather than a room or stay package, compare named public offers in the [swimming category](/category/swimming/) and the [swimming pools guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/). If you need a normal independent weights session, use the [fitness category](/category/fitness/) instead.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

## Before you go

The current public hotel information says **Cloud Club is open 06:00-22:00** and the pools **07:00-20:00**. It also says towels, refreshments and snacks are provided in Cloud Club. Those are hotel-published amenity details, not a guarantee that a non-resident can buy access on arrival.

Before turning up, ask whether the date is guest-only, whether outside visitors are accepted, whether advance reservation is required for the gym or yoga, and whether children may use the pool without an overnight booking.
