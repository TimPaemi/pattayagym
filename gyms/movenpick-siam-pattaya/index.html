★ About this venue

Mövenpick Siam Hotel Na Jomtien Pattaya

Thai Maps calls the Na Jomtien hotel at Sukhumvit kilometre 156.5 โรงแรมเมอเวนพิค สยาม นาจอมเทียน พัทยา, consistent with the recorded 24-hour gym and 07:00-20:00 pool.

Mövenpick Siam is a Na Jomtien resort with a 24-hour fitness centre, seafront leisure pool, spa and children’s club. The operator presents these as hotel facilities rather than a public gym product. It best suits registered guests; no current non-resident fitness pass is published.

What training is on offer

The resort’s facilities page confirms a fitness centre that remains available around the clock. It does not publish an equipment inventory, floor area, coached-class calendar or staffed-gym schedule. The same page lists a seafront pool open daily from 07:00 to 20:00.

For family activity, Little Bird Kid’s Club serves ages two to twelve. Its current page describes cultural activities, eco crafts, cooking and a library, with some parent-and-child options. The resort also advertises kayaking and stand-up paddleboarding for in-house guests. These are resort activities, not substitutes for a coached gym class or a reserved swimming lane.

What it costs

No separate fitness-centre day pass, pool ticket or non-resident gym tariff was published on the operator pages checked on 26 July 2026. A blank directory price means public access is unresolved, not free.

The kids-club page says its sessions are free for in-house guests, but that statement should not be extended to outside visitors or to every resort activity. Ask the hotel whether the intended room or booking package includes the exact facility, whether watersports require a reservation, and whether equipment, deposits or cancellation charges apply.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits a hotel guest who wants fitness access without planning around a commercial gym’s staffed hours. It also suits a family comparing a gym, pool and scheduled children’s activities in one property. The family-friendly Pattaya guide compares this model with swim schools and separately ticketed attractions.

It does not suit a non-resident who needs a confirmed public gym day pass or a swimmer who requires lane dimensions and reserved training periods. For standalone options, browse the fitness directory before choosing a resort around an assumed public facility.

Getting there

The hotel is at 55 Moo 2 on Sukhumvit Road, kilometre 156.5, in Na Jomtien. The exact Maps identity, hotel site and current telephone agree on the property. Use the hotel pin and entrance rather than treating “Pattaya” in the name as a central Beach Road location.

The Sattahip and Na Jomtien index provides corridor context. No universal journey time, fare or public-transport route is quoted because those depend on the starting point and current traffic.

Before you go

Little Bird Kid’s Club currently publishes 09:00-19:00 Sunday to Friday and 09:00-21:00 Saturday. It states a ten-guest session capacity, a 30-minute session followed by ten minutes for sanitising, and parent supervision for children under six. Confirm the child’s age group, available session and supervision rule with the resort.

Guests should ask where gym access is activated, whether a room key is sufficient, where towels and lockers are collected, and which activities need advance booking. Non-residents should call +66 33 078 888 before travelling.

What we could not verify

  • No public fitness, pool or combined day-pass tariff was published.
  • The operator does not list the gym’s machines, free-weight range, floor area or staffed hours.
  • No lap-lane dimensions or coached-swimming timetable was found.
  • Locker, towel, changing-room, watersport-deposit and non-resident eligibility terms require direct confirmation.
