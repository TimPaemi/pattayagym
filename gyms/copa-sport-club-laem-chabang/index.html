★ About this venue

Copa Sport Club Laem Chabang

Copa Sport Club Laem Chabang is a local booking-led sports venue, not a club with a published online rate card. The owner Facebook identity and Maps listing checked on 25 July 2026 support badminton courts, artificial-turf football pitches and parking at the Bang Lamung address. Call +66 81 589 4191 before travelling, especially if your plan depends on a particular court, pitch or late slot.

What training is on offer

The public listing identifies two activity types: badminton and artificial-turf football. It is therefore useful for a group arranging court or pitch time, rather than for someone seeking a structured coaching academy with a published programme. The available sources do not confirm coaching, leagues, a gym floor, equipment rental, changing rooms or a fixed number of courts or pitches. Treat the operator's answer as the current source for those practical details.

What it costs

No venue-owned badminton-court rate, football-pitch rate, deposit, cancellation rule or equipment fee was found on the sources checked on 25 July 2026. This page deliberately does not turn an absent rate card into an estimated price. Ask which sport, date, time and duration you want, then confirm the quoted total and any deposit before booking.

Who it suits — and who it does not

It suits players who can contact a local venue directly and are flexible enough to confirm availability. The distinct badminton and football offer can make it practical for groups whose activity is already decided. It does not suit someone who needs a public online booking flow, a documented walk-in tariff, confirmed coaching, or published rules about footwear and equipment before leaving home.

Getting there

The address in the record is 90/2 Moo 8, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. Enter that address or use the Maps link for navigation, then ask the club to confirm the entrance and parking arrangement. Parking is mentioned in the current business description, but the sources do not state its capacity, cost or whether it is reserved for bookings.

Before you go

The Maps listing showed Saturday hours of 09:00–23:00 when checked. That single-day listing is not a full weekly schedule. Call with the sport you want to play and ask about the day's opening hours, availability, deposit, cancellation terms and whether you must bring your own rackets, shuttlecocks, boots or ball.

What we could not verify

No current tariff, complete weekly timetable, court or pitch count, booking method, equipment policy, coaching menu, changing-room information or detailed parking terms was publicly available in the owner and Maps sources checked on 25 July 2026.
