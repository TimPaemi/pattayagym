# Sityodtong Muay Thai Camp

Sityodtong is the current Bang Lamung home of the camp established by Kru Yodtong Senanan in 1960. The operator says it trains beginners, intermediate and advanced students, including adults and children, through private lessons, pad work and small groups.

The current Maps schedule is Monday-Saturday 08:00-10:00 and 15:00-17:30, with Sunday closed. The official site publishes the same address and current phone number and also advertises a four-week fight-camp option with housing.

The training-price page was live but its values were not readable in accessible text during this check. Contact the camp for current session, private-training and residential costs before booking.
