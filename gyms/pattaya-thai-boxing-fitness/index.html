★ About this venue

Soi Seven MuayThai

Soi Seven MuayThai is the current operator identity linked from the live Jomtien Thai Boxing Gym Maps place on Jomtien Soi 7. A dated operator video on 29 June 2026 and the shared Facebook link support current activity. The public evidence confirms a Muay Thai gym, but not a dependable visitor price or class timetable.

What training is on offer

The current Maps category identifies a Muay Thai gym, and the linked operator page now uses the name Soi Seven MuayThai. The same page published a training video on 29 June 2026. Those sources support active Muay Thai practice at the Soi 7 identity, but they do not publish a complete menu of group classes, private work, pad rounds, bag work or sparring.

The operating periods should not be read as class start times. For a broader shortlist with published and contact-first options, use the Pattaya Muay Thai directory.

What it costs

No current operator rate card or exact price post was available on 26 July 2026. A visitor comment on Maps mentions ฿500, but it is not an operator tariff and is therefore not recorded as the price. Ask the Facebook page for the current single-session, private-session, weekly and monthly amounts, and request the answer in writing with the date.

Also ask whether the fee includes coaching, pad work or equipment. A blank price field means the current operator-published tariff is unknown, not that training is free.

Who it suits — and who it does not

This is a possible fit for someone staying around Jomtien Soi 7 who wants a local Muay Thai contact and is willing to confirm the exact session before travelling. The current activity signal is stronger than the old legacy record, and the live Maps place links directly to the operator identity.

It is a weaker choice for a traveller who needs a published beginner lesson, fixed English-language instruction, equipment hire or an itemised package before contact. The Muay Thai beginners guide gives more context, while FIGHT EVO360 is another Jomtien-area combat record with a broader documented programme.

Getting there

The live Maps place is on Jomtien Soi 7 and uses Plus Code VVWC+P9 for the exact pin. Its displayed address is otherwise only Pattaya City, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, so use the linked place rather than guessing a street number.

The gym belongs in the Jomtien area directory. No operator statement about parking, baht-bus access, fares or walking time was found.

Before you go

Maps lists 09:00-18:00 Monday-Saturday and Sunday closed. Those are business hours, not a promise that coached training begins at 09:00 or remains available continuously. Message the operator with the date, experience level and preferred time.

Ask whether the session is group or private, its duration, the current fee, teaching language and whether gloves and hand wraps are supplied or available for hire. Confirm beginner eligibility and sparring expectations before joining.

What we could not verify

  • No current operator price post or rate card was found.
  • No class-by-class weekly timetable is published.
  • No public phone number is shown on the current Maps place.
  • Session length, trainer language, equipment hire, changing facilities and accommodation are not confirmed.
