# Pattaya Monkey Hash House Harriers

Pattaya Monkey Hash House Harriers is an active men-only hash group rather than a fixed running venue. The operator's own site says the run normally takes about two and a half hours, happens every second odd-numbered month and usually lands on the third Saturday. That makes it useful for a visiting runner who wants a social trail challenge, but not for someone who needs a weekly club at one permanent location.

## What training is on offer

The main site describes the format as an A-to-B-to-C style trail with at least one water stop, a duration of about 2.5 hours and terrain that requires participants to be able to walk at least 8 km over moderately difficult ground in two hours. The same site's virgin policy says first-timers are expected to complete the full trail. That places the group closer to a social endurance challenge than to a casual sightseeing jog or an urban run club.

Because Monkey Hash is event-based, the exact route changes. If your main goal is repeatable weekly running, use the broader [running and cycling clubs guide](/guides/running-cycling-clubs-pattaya/) instead.

## What it costs

The most recent event page still visible during the 2026-07-27 check was Run 125 on Saturday 25 July 2026. That page listed a **฿1,300** signup fee for that run. The operator said the fee included a shirt memento, transport, beer or soft drinks, circle food and the post-run venue. That is useful evidence of the current product format, but it should be treated as an event-specific price, not a permanent year-round standard.

Because the next-run page can change from event to event, always check the current live run notice before paying. For comparison, this is a very different product from the Monday [Pattaya Hash House](/gyms/pattaya-hash-house/) run, which has a current recurring fee structure and regular Buffalo Bar pickup rhythm.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Monkey Hash suits experienced or at least robust recreational runners who enjoy social hash culture, can handle changing terrain and are comfortable with an event that moves around the wider Pattaya region.

It does not suit beginners who want a short urban jog, women looking for a mixed-gender club, or anyone who needs a predictable weekly training base with fixed meeting logistics. The operator's own language is clear that this is men only and expects participants to be capable on moderately difficult terrain. If you need a more general running-club overview, go to the [running and cycling guide](/guides/running-cycling-clubs-pattaya/) rather than forcing this product into a generic fitness comparison.

## Getting there

There is no one permanent venue. The operator says the meeting point and trail change by run, and the next-run page is the working source. That means the route has to be planned from the exact event page rather than from an old recommendation or a generic map result.

Do not navigate to a stale Monkey Hash pin and assume that is the current start. Open the live next-run page and confirm the event logistics before leaving your hotel.

## Before you go

Read the current run notice all the way through. Check whether registration is still open, what transport is being arranged and what shoes, water, cash and clothing are expected for that specific run.
