# MAVINN Muay Thai Pattaya

MAVINN is a fight gym on Thepprasit Road offering Muay Thai, boxing, MMA and strength and conditioning. Its all-access passes also cover open-gym use, the pool, sauna, ice bath and recovery area. Current Google Maps activity and a May 2026 ONE Championship report independently confirm that the Pattaya camp is operating.

## Prices checked 24 July 2026

- first session for a first-time visitor: free
- single morning or afternoon session: ฿700
- all-access day: ฿1,000
- one week, six training days: ฿4,500
- one month, 24 training days: ฿16,000
- three months, 72 training days: ฿42,000

The operator says passes are non-refundable and begin on the first training day. Training runs six days a week with Sunday closure; use the booking link on the official page for current class times.

## First visit

The camp suits travellers who want several combat disciplines and recovery in one place. Confirm what equipment is included and whether a chosen session is beginner-appropriate. The official site gives Thepprasit Road rather than a numbered street address, so use the linked Maps listing for the entrance.
