# Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya Pool

Hard Rock Hotel describes a 2,000-square-metre freeform leisure pool with an artificial beach, jacuzzi, children's area, slides and lifeguards. This is a recreation pool rather than a dedicated lap-training venue.

## Current non-guest price

The hotel's pool page, checked on 25 July 2026, lists:

- adult day access: **฿500**, including one cocktail
- child under 12: **฿400**, including one juice
- inflatable hire: **฿200 per day**

Non-guest access is listed from 09:00 to 19:00; the pool itself operates from 07:00 to 21:00. Confirm availability with the hotel before travelling, particularly on busy or event dates.
