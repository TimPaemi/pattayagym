# Manhattan Pattaya Fitness Centre

Manhattan Pattaya's official facilities page places the gym in the ground-floor swimming-pool area and lists daily hours of 08:00 to 21:00. It says a reservation is required.

## Access and first visit

The checked hotel pages present the facility as a guest amenity and do not publish a non-resident day pass or gym fee. The former Pratamnak location and unsupported public-access claims have been removed. Hotel guests should reserve through reception; anyone else should confirm eligibility before travelling.
