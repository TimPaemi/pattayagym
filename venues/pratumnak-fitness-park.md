# Pratumnak Fitness Park

The Thai fitness guide groups this location under **เขาพระตำหนักและสวนสาธารณะออกกำลังกาย**.

Pratumnak Fitness Park is the exercise area at Chaloem Phrakiat Park on Pratumnak Road. Municipal records support a walking and running surface, a multipurpose court and 12 outdoor exercise stations. It suits self-directed outdoor training; it does not provide the published staffing, equipment inventory or weather protection of a commercial gym.

## What training is on offer

Pattaya City's park description gives the site an area of about 14.5 rai. It records approximately 2,476 square metres of asphalt-concrete walking and running surface, a 1,190-square-metre multipurpose court and 12 exercise stations. The same municipal record notes lighting beside the walking and running route and within the park.

A local update published on 10 March 2026 identifies the site as Pattaya Fitness Park, also called Chaloem Phrakiat Park, and describes it as a place for running, jogging and walking with exercise apparatus along the route. That supports cardio and general outdoor exercise, but not the much broader free-weight and machine inventory previously claimed on this page.

## What it costs

No current municipal tariff is published in the city material, live Maps listing or March 2026 local update checked on 26 July 2026. The site is described as a public park, but this page does not turn that description into an unsupported promise about free equipment, classes or services.

There is no published membership, day pass, coaching fee or equipment-hire charge. There is also no staffed tariff desk listed online. If a temporary organised activity is operating when you arrive, check its terms separately rather than assuming it is part of the park.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The park suits walkers, runners and people comfortable building a session around fixed outdoor exercise stations. It is also a useful no-membership alternative for someone comparing options in the [Pattaya fitness directory](/category/fitness/), provided they do not require a specific machine or coached class.

It is not the right substitute for a gym if you need air conditioning, a verified barbell setup, controlled loads, lockers, showers or staff supervision. The [cheapest Pattaya gyms guide](/guides/cheapest-gyms-pattaya/) covers commercial alternatives where price and equipment can be compared more directly.

## Getting there

The March 2026 local update places the park on Pratumnak Road just before the entrance to Buddha Hill. Pattaya City's material identifies the wider site as Chaloem Phrakiat Park on the lower part of Pattaya Hill.

No current source publishes a baht-bus route, parking arrangement or accessible-entrance description for the exercise area. Use the live map pin and compare the wider [Pratamnak area page](/area/pratamnak/) before setting out.

## Before you go

Bring drinking water and clothing suitable for outdoor conditions. Inspect each station before loading it: the municipal source records the number of stations, not their current mechanical condition or the weight available on any apparatus.

There is no published booking requirement for ordinary park use and no online class timetable. Lighting is recorded by the city, but a formal opening and closing schedule is not, so confirm the current park status before planning a very early or late session.

## What we could not verify

- No formal daily opening schedule or current municipal tariff is published.
- The present condition and exact exercise type of each of the 12 stations are not documented.
- Toilets, drinking water, lockers, showers and staffed assistance are not confirmed.
- Parking, accessible access and public-transport details are not published.
