# Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya

Thai Maps identifies the 277 Moo 5 Naklua coastal resort and water-park property as **เซ็นทารา แกรนด์ มิราจ บีช รีสอร์ท พัทยา**.

Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya is a North Pattaya hotel-and-water-park product, not a public sports club. The current resort pages and fact sheet support a large Lost World water park, fitness facilities and scheduled activities, which makes it relevant for family swimming and hotel-based recreation. The limitation is access: the checked public pages do not publish a dependable non-resident gym or water-park pass.

## What training is on offer

The current Centara site describes the property as a Lost World-themed beachfront resort with an outdoor water park. Its July 2025 fact sheet lists an outdoor water park with a lazy river, giant water slide, Volcano Island and Misty Forest. The same fact sheet confirms the Naklua address, hotel contact and the resort identity. This is a swimming-and-leisure listing first, not a lane-swimming or coached-aquatics record.

The broader hotel pages and the current activities brochure also support a fitness centre and a rotating activity programme that includes tennis, yoga, Pilates, Muay Thai and beach-side family activities. Those are resort activities whose timing can change with the house schedule, season and occupancy. For readers browsing [swimming records](/category/swimming/) or comparing [family-friendly sport in Pattaya](/guides/family-friendly-pattaya/), Centara is best understood as a resort recreation base rather than an ordinary public pool.

## What it costs

No dependable public non-resident day-pass, gym-pass or water-park ticket table was found on the checked official pages on 2026-07-27. The current resort site sells hotel stays and packages, including Lost World-themed offers, but that is different from publishing a local-use pool or gym tariff.

That means you should not assume a public day-use option exists just because the amenities are marketed online. Before travelling from another hotel, ask Centara whether there is a current outside-guest product for the pool, water park, fitness centre or tennis courts, and whether the quoted amount includes towels, lockers or food credit.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Centara suits travellers who are already considering a resort stay and want swimming, family water play and some optional fitness or activity programming inside the same property.

It is a weak fit for someone who wants a simple lap-swim product, a transparent day-pass gym or a serious coached Muay Thai or yoga purchase. For a clearer public swim or training transaction, compare a dedicated venue in the [swimming category](/category/swimming/) or use the [swimming pools guide](/guides/swimming-pools-pattaya/). If the real priority is an ordinary gym session, the [hotel gym guide](/guides/hotel-gym-pattaya/) and commercial [fitness listings](/category/fitness/) are more direct.

## Getting there

The resort lists 277 Moo 5, Naklua, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150 on the Wong Amat / North Pattaya side of the city. That places it away from Jomtien and East Pattaya training clusters. For a staying guest, that is just a hotel location. For a non-guest, it means you should confirm access before making the journey because the public pages do not show a guaranteed day-use product.

Ask the hotel for the correct arrival procedure for the exact product you want.

## Before you go

Treat Centara like a resort programme, not a normal sports-club timetable. Ask whether the activity you want is guest-only, whether children and adults use separate sessions, whether weather changes affect beach or pool programming, and whether the current day requires prior reservation. If the plan involves tennis or a coached class, confirm the precise time rather than relying on a brochure category.

If you are booking for fitness rather than for a room, be specific: ask whether you want pool-only use, water-park access, a fitness-centre visit, tennis or a spa-and-wet-area product. Those are separate purchases even when they sit on one resort property.
