# Amazing Thailand Pattaya Marathon

Amazing Thailand Pattaya Marathon is a dated annual road-running event, not a year-round running club or training base. The official 2026 site confirms that the next race weekend is 26-27 September 2026, centred on Terminal 21 Pattaya. It suits a runner planning a Pattaya trip around one organised race weekend, but it is not the right record for someone looking for weekly coached sessions.

The Thai event listing uses **พัทยามาราธอน** for the 2026 Pattaya race weekend.

## What training is on offer

The official event site describes Pattaya Marathon 2026 as the 33rd edition of the race and a World Athletics International Standard Road Race. It publishes five event categories: marathon 42.195 km, half marathon 21.1 km, 10 km, 4.5 km family run and 4.5 km wheelchair. The same site says Saturday 26 September 2026 carries the 10 km and 4.5 km races, while Sunday 27 September 2026 carries the marathon and half marathon.

The official race-venue section names Terminal 21 Pattaya on North Pattaya Road as the start-finish area, bib-collection point and expo base. The event pages also publish current course maps, cut-off times and schedule windows. As checked on 2026-07-29, the marathon start time was listed as 03:30, the half marathon as 05:10, the 10 km as 05:45 and the 4.5 km as 06:30.

This is a race-weekend product rather than an ongoing coached programme. The official site does not present weekly training sessions, a club membership or local pace-group timetable under the Pattaya Marathon identity.

## What it costs

The 2026 official fee table is now live. On the operator's event page, checked 2026-07-29, the early-price registration window for 5-6 August 2026 listed **฿800 for the marathon** and **฿750 for the half marathon**. For 7-9 August 2026, the same page listed **฿500 for the 10 km** and **free entry for the 4.5 km family run and 4.5 km wheelchair race** while quota lasts.

The same official page then lists standard pricing from later in the registration period: **฿950 for the marathon** and **฿900 for the half marathon** for registrations from 7 August to 12 September 2026, plus **฿600 for the 10 km** from 10 August to 12 September 2026. The page also says these prices exclude a **฿50 system fee per entry**.

Those are race-entry fees, not accommodation, transport or recovery costs. Check separately for bib-collection requirements, payment method, refund policy and whether group registration changes anything for your entry type.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits a runner choosing between race categories and deciding whether to anchor a Pattaya stay around one organised weekend. The course maps and start times make it useful for marathon, half-marathon and 10 km runners who need a dated, official planning reference.

It is a weak fit for someone who wants routine training, coached track work or a local social-running community outside race week. For ordinary year-round options, use the [clubs category](/category/clubs/), the [running and cycling guide](/guides/running-cycling-clubs-pattaya/) or general Pattaya venue searches rather than treating a once-a-year race as a standing club.

## Getting there

The official event page places the race venue at Terminal 21 Pattaya on Pattaya Nuea Road. That makes exact access dependent on the chosen race day and where you are staying.

If you are travelling in for the event, use the official race-venue map and the exact category page rather than assuming every distance starts and finishes in the same lane layout. Terminal 21 is the useful landmark for bib collection and the expo as well as the race start.

## Before you go

The official page says registration runs from 5 August 2026 to 12 September 2026, with separate early-price windows and category quotas. Check the event schedule for bib-collection windows before booking a late arrival: the current page lists bib and souvenir pickup beginning on Thursday 24 September 2026 at Terminal 21 Pattaya.

Bring the exact registration confirmation and review the current rules, cut-off time and start-time page for your chosen distance. If you are booking accommodation around the race, use the official road-route and venue pages first, then decide whether you want North Pattaya convenience or another area such as [Jomtien](/area/jomtien/).
