# Pattaya Country Club

Pattaya Country Club is on Highway 331 at Khao Mai Kaeo. The club publishes daily operating hours of 06:00-18:00 and accepts advance tee-time requests online.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

- 18-hole green fee: ฿2,500 weekday or ฿3,000 weekend
- caddie fee: ฿400
- golf cart: ฿600
- full club set: ฿1,200
- shoes: ฿300

The required green, caddie and cart total is therefore ฿3,500 on weekdays or ฿4,000 at weekends before tips and optional rental. Confirm the tee time and current cart policy when the club responds to the booking request.
