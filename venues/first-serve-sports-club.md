# First Serve Sports Club — not in Pattaya

First Serve's own site gives its address as 25/477 Chaeng Wattana–Pak Kret 24, Si Chai Thong 19, Bang Talat, Pak Kret, Nonthaburi 11120. The current Maps listing confirms that Nonthaburi location and active operation. It is therefore not a Pattaya-area venue.
