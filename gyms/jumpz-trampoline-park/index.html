★ About this venue

JUMPZ Harbor Pattaya

JUMPZ Harbor Pattaya is a permanently closed trampoline park formerly located on the sixth floor of Harbor Pattaya. Keep this record as a closure warning, not a venue recommendation. HarborLand still operates other Pattaya-branded facilities, so the exact branch and playground name matter when checking current options.

Closed

The exact live Google Maps listing remained marked permanently closed on 26 July 2026. HarborLand Group's current location list names JumpZ at Megabangna, Seacon Bangkae, ICONSIAM, Future Park Rangsit and One Bangkok, but not at Harbor Pattaya.

The operator does list a HarborLand playground at Terminal 21 Pattaya. That is a different venue and does not establish that JUMPZ Harbor Pattaya has reopened. Families should use the kids and youth sport directory or the Central Pattaya area page to find current alternatives.

The former hours, phone and entrance prices have been removed. Do not travel to Harbor Pattaya expecting this trampoline park to be operating.

What we could not verify

  • The operator does not publish the closure date or reason.
  • No owner announcement of a Pattaya relocation or JUMPZ replacement was found.
  • Former admission prices and play rules are obsolete and intentionally omitted.
