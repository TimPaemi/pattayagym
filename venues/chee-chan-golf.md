# Chee Chan Golf Resort

Chee Chan is an 18-hole David Dale course in Na Jomtien, laid out beside Khao Chi Chan. Its facilities include a grass driving range, locker rooms, pro shop, restaurant and golf academy.

## Public rates checked 25 July 2026

The resort's current public-rate card is valid from 1 April to 30 September 2026:

- weekday 18 holes: ฿4,500
- weekday 9 holes: ฿2,250
- weekend or public holiday 18 holes: ฿5,500
- weekend or public holiday 9 holes: ฿2,750
- caddie and cart, 18 holes: ฿1,300
- caddie and cart, 9 holes: ฿700

The green-fee prices exclude the compulsory caddie and cart charge. Reserve a tee time and confirm any dress code, club rental and promotion conditions directly with the resort.
