# Jomtien Dive Center

Jomtien Dive Center runs local Pattaya and Samae San boat trips, fun dives, snorkelling and PADI training. Its live Maps listing places the shop at 97/3 Moo 12 on Jomtien Beach Road; the operator also publishes a mobile contact number.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

- Pattaya fun-diving day: from **฿2,800**
- Samae San fun-diving day: **฿3,200**
- six dives over two days: **฿7,900**
- ten dives over five days: **฿12,000**
- snorkelling day: **฿1,495**
- PADI Open Water course: from **฿15,900**
- PADI Open Water course plus one fun-diving day: from **฿17,995**

The operator gives a normal trip window of roughly 08:00 to 15:30, while Maps lists the shop daily from 08:00 to 20:00. The fun-dive page says equipment other than a dive computer, lunch, refreshments and pickup within five kilometres are included. Confirm the required certification, equipment sizes, medical requirements and departure point before paying.
