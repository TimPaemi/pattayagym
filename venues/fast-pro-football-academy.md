# FAST PRO Football Academy Pattaya

FAST PRO Football Academy Pattaya is a two-site youth football programme for children aged 4-15, with training at Horseshoe Point near Mabprachan and at IP Soccer Club near Thepprasit. The official site now publishes weekday training windows again and still offers a free-trial booking route, but it does not show a current fee table.

## What training is on offer

The academy's current site describes structured football training for children aged 4-15, split into two broad groups rather than one mixed open session. It presents **age 11-15** as advanced training with strength and match-performance work, and **age 4-10** as technical development using the **FUNino** coaching methodology. The same site says players train in small groups and that the work focuses on technique, confidence, game understanding and real match experience.

FAST PRO also publishes the coaching standard more clearly than many youth-football records in Pattaya. Its homepage says the sessions are led by **UEFA A and AFC C licensed coaches**, while the longer coaching section names **Zarko Djalovic** as a UEFA-licensed European coach with Thai professional-club experience. That gives parents a clearer evidence trail than a generic "academy coach" claim.

The two current venue pages add different practical details. The Horseshoe Point page says that location uses **high-quality natural grass football fields** and is aimed at families around Mabprachan Lake in East Pattaya. The IP Soccer Club page keeps the Jomtien-side identity live for players closer to Sukhumvit and Thepprasit.

## What it costs

No current public tuition, registration or term-fee table was published on the checked academy pages as of 2026-07-27. The useful verified commercial fact is that the site still offers a **free trial training** booking route, so parents can ask for the exact current product before committing.

Because no dated price page is available, ask for the full amount for the child's exact plan rather than a vague "monthly football" quote. Confirm whether the academy charges by month, by sessions per week or by term; whether the fee differs between Horseshoe Point and IP Soccer Club; and whether kit, tournament entries or extra coaching cost more.

Also ask whether the free trial includes only one session, whether a parent must stay on site, and whether the player should bring boots, shin guards and water from the first visit. A blank directory price here means the academy does not publish a stable tariff, not that the programme is closed.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

FAST PRO suits families who want a children-only football pathway with a clearer technical-development structure than a casual kickabout. It is especially relevant for households split between **East Pattaya / Mabprachan** and **Jomtien / Thepprasit**, because the academy actively publishes both training sites rather than treating one field as a one-off overflow pitch.

It also suits beginners. The official FAQ says beginners are welcome and that players are divided by age and skill level. That is more helpful for a new family than a record that only shows a generic training photo and a phone number.

It is not the right fit for an adult player, a casual one-off tourist session or a parent who needs a published fixed budget before making contact. It is also a weak choice if the child needs a clearly published goalkeeper programme, strength-only plan or school-holiday camp product; the checked pages do not establish those details.

## Getting there

The academy currently publishes two different Pattaya training locations. **Horseshoe Point** is presented as the East Pattaya base near **Mabprachan Lake**. **IP Soccer Club** is the Jomtien-side base, with the published address **64/124 Soi Thepprasit 5/1, Pattaya 20150** and site wording that places it near **Sukhumvit Road and Thepprasit Night Market**.

Use the exact pin for the intended field rather than searching only for FAST PRO by name. The academy is one programme across two pitches, so a driver still needs the correct site for that day's session.

## Before you go

The current homepage now publishes the ordinary weekly training windows: **Horseshoe Point Tuesday-Friday 17:00-18:30** and **IP Soccer Club Monday, Thursday and Friday 17:30-19:00**. The site also says players train three to four times per week and that weekend friendly games or tournaments may be part of the rhythm.

Before travelling, message or call **+66 80 595 6949** with the child's age, experience and preferred location. Ask which group the player fits, whether the published time applies that week, whether a free trial is still available on the chosen date, and whether the field session is on grass or another surface that day.
