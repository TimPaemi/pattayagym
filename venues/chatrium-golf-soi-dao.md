# Chatrium Golf Resort Soi Dao Chanthaburi

Chatrium Golf Resort Soi Dao Chanthaburi is an 18-hole resort course currently operating in partial form during a 2026 enhancement programme. It suits golfers willing to travel well beyond Pattaya for a resort stay-and-play trip rather than a same-corridor city round. Its main limitation is obvious: until 1 November 2026 the resort is only operating the back nine.

## What training is on offer

The resort's main site describes an 18-hole championship course at the foot of Soi Dao Mountain. The current short-term reality is different: the same operator page says the course entered a phased enhancement period in March 2026, with holes 10-18 open from 25 July to 30 October 2026 while holes 1-9 remain under renovation.

That means the current golf product is effectively a nine-hole operating loop inside an 18-hole resort identity. A golfer choosing between this and other [golf records](/category/golf/) should therefore treat it as a limited-operation venue until the full course returns on the operator's stated 1 November 2026 date.

## What it costs

The current published prices on the checked offer pages are stay-and-play products, not standalone public green-fee tables. The nine-hole stay-and-play offer starts from ฿2,850 per night and includes breakfast, one nine-hole green fee, caddie fee and golf cart (operator offer page checked 2026-07-27). The 18-hole stay-and-play offer starts from ฿3,700 per night and, during the current works period, uses the open nine twice rather than the full original routing (operator offer page checked 2026-07-27).

Those are hotel-and-golf packages. They are useful if you are deliberately planning an overnight golf stay, but they should not be presented as if they were a normal walk-in golf tariff. The checked pages did not expose a separate current standalone local-player green fee, caddie ladder, replay fee or non-resident day package outside the accommodation offers.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits golfers who actively want a Chanthaburi resort stay and are comfortable with a partial-course period. It also suits travellers who prefer to bundle room, breakfast, caddie and cart into one current product instead of arranging a separate resort and golf round.

It does not suit someone who wants a standard current full-18 Pattaya-area round or a quick city-side tee time. Until the resort reopens the whole course, this is a deliberate destination choice rather than an interchangeable entry in a golf ranking.

If you are choosing between current Pattaya golf and a resort detour, start with the [best golf courses guide](/guides/best-golf-courses-pattaya/) and then decide whether the accommodation element is the actual value driver.

## Getting there

The resort's published address is 224 Moo 2, Tubsai, Pong Nam Ron, Chanthaburi 22140. That is outside Pattaya's normal urban sport zones and should be planned as a separate Chanthaburi trip rather than as a central, Jomtien or Darkside round. The operator's listed phone is +66 39 460 960.

Because this course is materially farther out than the usual Pattaya golf cluster, confirm the exact arrival window and current routing before travelling. The resort identity is clear; the day shape is what needs planning.

## Before you go

Ask one direct question first: which holes are operating on your date? The operator pages are clear about the current enhancement phase, but a traveller still needs written confirmation of the actual routing and whether the booked product is nine holes once or nine holes twice.

Also confirm whether your quote is the resort stay-and-play package or a different golf arrangement. If you are travelling from Pattaya rather than staying overnight, make sure the resort confirms that your chosen product fits a same-day arrival plan.
