# Pattaya Jomtien Beach Volleyball Courts

Beach Volleyball Thailand's current Pattaya entry describes three floodlit courts near the Sea Rescue station where doubles and faster six-a-side games normally begin around 16:00. Use is free, with donations welcomed for equipment. The exact live Maps result is named in Thai as Sanam Volleyball Dongtan and lists the court area as open 24 hours; that does not guarantee an organised game at every hour.

This is an informal outdoor playing group, not a staffed booking venue. Wind, rain, tournaments and player turnout can change the session. Bring water, sun protection and suitable beach clothing; arrive before the usual start time and ask the players how games are being organised that day.
