Sattahip / Far South's adventure page currently has one documented operator: Tarzan Adventure Pattaya at 108/14 Moo 6, Na Chom Thian. It is a booked zipline-and-ATV activity, not a menu of interchangeable outdoor providers, so the narrow result needs to be read as a coverage limit. A traveller based in Na Jomtien, Bang Saray or Sattahip can use this page to identify one current local contact, but should not infer that the district has a public climbing park, a walk-in kart circuit, a skydive centre or several equivalent jungle courses. The exact map pin and a confirmed booking matter more than the broad Sattahip label. Tarzan's current operator channels explicitly promote zipline and ATV activities. That is enough to distinguish it from a sightseeing stop or a generic attraction listing, but it does not establish a course count, line length, platform height, ATV route, activity duration, group size or difficulty grade. Those omissions affect the decision: a visitor seeking a short first-time zipline, a long technical course, a guided ATV outing or an activity suitable for mixed ages needs the operator to name the exact product before comparing it with a different Pattaya-area option. The directory's adventure guide can help frame that wider search, while this page remains specific about the one operator it can document. There is no stable, current operator-published price table for either Tarzan's zipline or ATV offer. That means this one-record page cannot honestly publish a local cheap-versus-premium spread. Do not treat a map-reseller amount as the operator's final rate: it may contain transport, a commission, a different activity duration or conditions that do not match the intended date. Call +66 92 235 4800 and request a written quote naming the activity, participant count, total price and date. Ask separately whether a guide, protective equipment, transfer, insurance, photos, meal, tax or deposit is included. A usable quote should also say what happens if rain, wind or site conditions interrupt the activity. The listed daily window is 09:00–17:30, but it is not a published timetable of starts or last admission. Reserve before travelling rather than arriving near the end of the day and assuming a full activity remains available. The record does not publish a universal hotel pickup, parking instruction, public-transport route, language option or accessible route. Ask for the meeting point in writing, especially when travelling from the Pattaya hotel corridor; Na Chom Thian covers a large southern area and the geographic label does not substitute for an operator instruction. Eligibility is the other decision point. No current operator-published material found for this listing gives age, height, weight, health, guardian or prior-experience rules. Families and mixed groups should disclose those details to the operator and ask about footwear, clothing, helmets, other protective equipment and supervision before paying. Choose Tarzan when a confirmed, contact-first zipline or ATV booking in the southern corridor matches the day. Look beyond Sattahip when a visible tariff, a specified course format, guaranteed transfer or documented participation rules are essential; this page identifies one verified option, not an all-purpose adventure marketplace.
