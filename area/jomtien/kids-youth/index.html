Jomtien's listed kids' sport coverage is two youth-football programmes with different site models, age bands and price visibility, not a general menu of children's activities. FAST PRO Football Academy runs at IP Soccer Club near Thepprasit and at Horseshoe Point near Mabprachan; AF Academy Pattaya publishes an IP Soccer Club timetable but treats its Naklua address as company contact information, with training locations varying. A family should therefore book a named group and confirm the actual field, not navigate to a broad Jomtien label or assume both programmes meet at the same place every day. FAST PRO is the clearer fit for ages 4–15 when the family can choose between its two published routes. Its site describes ages 4–10 as technical development using FUNino and ages 11–15 as advanced work with strength and match-performance elements. It says beginners are welcome, groups are divided by age and ability, and the programme is led by UEFA A and AFC C licensed coaches. Its ordinary windows are Horseshoe Point Tuesday–Friday 17:00–18:30 and IP Soccer Club Monday, Thursday and Friday 17:30–19:00. The Jomtien-side IP address is 64/124 Soi Thepprasit 5/1, near Sukhumvit and Thepprasit Night Market. A free-trial booking route is published, but no current tuition, registration, sibling-discount or term-fee table is public. Ask whether the chosen field, age group, trial, boots, shin guards, weather plan and any tournament cost are included before committing. AF Academy spans a younger-to-older age range of 3–17 and provides the more concrete short-pack prices. Its published IP Soccer Club pattern puts Preparatory and Junior groups at 17:00–18:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, followed by Intermediate and Senior at 18:00–19:00. The operator's age bands are Preparatory 3–6, Junior 7–9, Intermediate 10–14 and Senior 14+. Its checked English price page lists a free first trial, a ฿600 single 60-minute session with no rescheduling, eight 60-minute sessions for ฿3,000, and twelve for ฿3,600. The eight-session description includes friendly matches and tournaments and permits up to two reschedules; that inclusion and limit are not stated for every package. Ask for product validity, holiday handling, missed-session, uniform, registration and tournament terms in writing. The key access difference is field certainty. FAST PRO names both its IP Soccer Club and Horseshoe Point bases, so parents can select the right location for the day. AF expressly needs a live pin for the booked group: its company address is not evidence of a permanent pitch, and its public information does not give every group a city-wide timetable. Neither listing establishes a coach-to-player ratio, guaranteed English instruction, a goalkeeper programme, parent-stay rule, transport, insurance or a rain-cancellation remedy. Choose FAST PRO for the age 4–15 two-site pathway, its published weekday windows and a trial after pricing is confirmed. Choose AF when its age band, IP Soccer Club slot and visible short-pack pricing fit the child. Look elsewhere when a fixed school-holiday camp, a named permanent Jomtien ground, a complete seasonal calendar or another sport is required.
