# Battle & Conquer Gym

Battle & Conquer combines twice-daily Muay Thai group training with a general weights gym, sauna and cold plunge in Pratamnak. The live Google Maps listing supplies the full street address, replacing the old description that placed it near Pattaya Beach.

## Prices checked 25 July 2026

Muay Thai packages are ฿2,500 for one week, ฿7,000 for one month, ฿18,000 for three months and ฿35,000 for six months. These include group training, the boxing room, weights gym, sauna and cold plunge.

Gym-only access is ฿200 for one day, ฿600 for one week, ฿1,300 for one month and ฿12,000 for one year. A single group Muay Thai session is ฿500. Private 90-minute training is ฿1,200 for one session, ฿4,300 for five or ฿7,500 for ten.

## First visit

Group training runs 08:00–10:00 and 16:00–18:00 every day. Contact the gym before arrival if you need gloves, wraps or a private trainer. The earlier claim that this was Pattaya's only fully air-conditioned Muay Thai gym has been removed because the current sources do not support it.
