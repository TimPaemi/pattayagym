If you only read one thing: the MMA category is a two-record shortlist, not a complete timetable of interchangeable MMA classes. As checked on 26 July 2026, it contains Rambaa Somdet M16 in Nong Prue and BOXING CLUB Mixfight Pattaya in Khao Makok. Both are active mixed-discipline schools, but their public evidence is stronger for overall venue activity and broad disciplines than for a class-by-class MMA schedule. Contact the school before travelling if cage work, grappling rounds or fight preparation is the reason for the visit.

How the two records differ

Rambaa is the East Pattaya option. Its live listing publishes two training periods Monday to Saturday, 07:00–09:00 and 15:00–18:30, with Sunday closed. The record ties the gym to Muay Thai and MMA, and independent fight databases document Rambaa Somdet's Shooto and MMA history. That background does not prove which discipline is taught in every listed period, whether a beginner can join, or whether private coaching is available on a particular day.

Mixfight is farther south in Khao Makok and advertises a broader school programme. A March 2026 owner update names Muay Thai, boxing, MMA, jiu-jitsu, yoga, fitness, children's groups and fight preparation. Its live hours are 08:00–21:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; 08:00–19:00 on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday; and Sunday closed. Independent event records place combat-sports events, including MMA bouts, at the venue. Again, an event record confirms relevant activity, not a standing visitor class at every open hour.

Price is an unresolved comparison

No current operator-published group, private, weekly or monthly tariff was found for either record during the checks completed on 25 July 2026. The empty price fields mean the rate is unknown, not free or necessarily inexpensive. Ask for the price of the exact discipline and format, whether registration or equipment is extra, how long the session runs and whether payment covers open gym, a group class or one-to-one coaching.

Who this category suits

The two records suit a reader willing to contact a school and verify the session before travelling. Rambaa is the more direct location fit for an East Pattaya base and publishes split morning and afternoon periods. Mixfight publishes longer operating windows and a wider list of disciplines, which may suit a family or training group with different interests. Neither record is yet strong enough for a price-led recommendation, a guaranteed English-language class or a claim that a travelling white belt can simply join.

Before choosing, send the operator your experience level, desired discipline, preferred date and whether you need group or private training. Ask about the class start time, sparring or rolling rules, gloves and protective equipment, gi or no-gi requirements, coaching language and current fee. For the broader distinction between striking, BJJ and MMA records in the city, use the BJJ and MMA guide.

The East-side residential camps also matter even when they sit outside the two-record MMA shortlist. Kombat Group Thailand currently publishes dedicated MMA residential packages from ฿14,900 for one Bronze week or ฿23,900 for one Standard week, checked 27 July 2026, while Silk Muay Thai remains a Muay-Thai-led stay-and-train option rather than an MMA booking. That is the practical boundary for this category: a venue can support a mixed-combat buyer without becoming a transparent local MMA drop-in on the evidence now available.

03 Common questions

MMA in Pattaya — FAQ.

Is there an MMA gym in Pattaya?

Yes. Pattaya has a dedicated mixed martial arts camp, listed in this category, training striking, grappling and full MMA. Many of the city Muay Thai camps and the BJJ academy also cover parts of the MMA skill set.

Can beginners train MMA in Pattaya?

Yes. The MMA camp welcomes beginners as well as experienced and competitive fighters, with coaching scaled to your level. You do not need a martial-arts background to start. Contact the camp about beginner classes and what to bring.

How does MMA training in Pattaya work for visitors?

Like the Muay Thai camps, the MMA camp caters to training tourists with drop-in sessions and longer packages. If you want to combine MMA with Muay Thai or BJJ, the camps and academy are all within the Pattaya area; see also the Muay Thai and BJJ categories.
