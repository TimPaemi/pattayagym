# Lumpinee Boxing Stadium

Lumpinee is an active Bangkok fight venue, not a Pattaya training gym. It belongs in the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/) because Pattaya visitors do use it as a stadium day trip, but the useful decision is whether you want a spectator card with fixed seating and date-specific start times rather than a coached session.

## What training is on offer

The operator pages checked on 27 July 2026 support a live event programme rather than a public training product. The main stadium page lists three current event types: Friday ONE Lumpinee cards from 18:30 to 23:30, Saturday Lumpinee Super Champ cards from 18:30 to 21:00, and selected Saturday-morning ONE Fight Night events starting at 08:00 or 09:00 depending on the date. The calendar page then names the actual upcoming cards, including KAT PRO World Series on 27 July 2026 at 18:00, ONE Lumpinee on 31 July 2026 from 18:30 to 23:30, Lumpinee Super Champ on 1 August 2026 from 18:30 to 21:00 and ONE Fight Night on 15 August 2026 at 09:00.

That is useful if you are planning a Bangkok fight night around a Pattaya stay, but it does not establish visitor pad work, a class timetable, private coaching or open-gym access. Anyone who wants to train should go back to a current Pattaya camp record such as [ISS Boxing and Muay Thai](/gyms/wko-muay-thai/) or use the [best Muay Thai gyms guide](/guides/best-muay-thai-pattaya/).

## What it costs

The stadium homepage publishes a dated seating ladder rather than one flat ticket. Checked 2026-07-27, Friday ONE Lumpinee tickets were listed at ฿1,000, ฿2,500, ฿3,500 and ฿5,000. Saturday Lumpinee Super Champ tickets were listed at ฿1,000, ฿1,500 and ฿2,000. Saturday-morning ONE Fight Night tickets were listed at ฿1,200, ฿2,400, ฿3,750 and ฿4,500. Those are spectator prices from the operator page, not training fees.

Before paying, match the ticket tier to the exact event date because the calendar varies. Also confirm whether the operator is issuing digital tickets, what time gates open for that card, and whether any special morning event changes the travel plan from Pattaya.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record suits someone who is already in Pattaya but wants a specific Bangkok Muay Thai card with current operator pricing and a confirmed calendar. It is also useful for readers comparing the spectator side of the sport with Pattaya's day-to-day training options.

It does not suit anyone searching for a Pattaya class, a beginner session or a place to buy gloves and join pads that afternoon. For that, use the [train Muay Thai for 1 week or 1 month guide](/guides/train-muay-thai-pattaya-1-week-1-month/) or go straight to Pattaya gym records in the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/).

## Getting there

The stadium's own address is 6 Ramintra Road, Anusawari, Bang Khen, Bangkok 10220. That places it well outside Pattaya and makes it a planned Bangkok trip rather than a same-neighbourhood add-on. Use the exact [Maps pin](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lumpinee+Boxing+Stadium+Ramintra) and the event page together because the useful arrival time depends on the named card, not on a generic assumption about Saturday nights.

## Before you go

Check the event calendar again on the day of travel. The operator publishes several different card types and not every Saturday uses the same start time. If you are choosing between a fight card and actual training during a Pattaya stay, compare this record with Pattaya camp pages first so you do not accidentally swap a spectator night for a training day.
