# Lumpinee Boxing Stadium

Lumpinee is in Bang Khen, Bangkok, so attending from Pattaya requires a dedicated trip and enough buffer for Bangkok traffic. The current stadium programme lists ONE Lumpinee on Friday from 18:30 to 23:30 and Lumpinee Super Champ on Saturday from 18:30 to 21:00, with doors for the Saturday card at 17:30. Additional ONE Fight Night dates can run early on selected Saturdays.
