★ About this venue

Elite Gym & Fitness Pattaya

Elite is a 1,600-square-metre air-conditioned gym at 392/62 Sukhumvit Road. Its operator separates ordinary gym membership, functional training, personal training and standalone fitness classes. That distinction corrects the former record, which quoted the higher functional-training price as if it were the ordinary day pass.

What training is on offer

The operator publishes general gym access, functional training, personal training and a class page naming Bodycombat, Bodyfit, Elite Balance, Elite Dance, yoga and Zumba. The rate page says ordinary gym memberships include daily classes except functional-training classes. Functional products, by contrast, include gym membership and access to all classes.

That inclusion difference matters more than the headline price. For other access models, compare the fitness directory or the gym day-pass guide.

What it costs

Operator prices checked 27 July 2026:

  • ordinary gym: THB 300 day, THB 1,200 week, THB 1,800 for ten visits within three months, THB 2,200 month, THB 5,400 three months, THB 9,900 six months, or THB 18,600 year;
  • functional training: THB 400 once, THB 2,500 for ten visits within three months, or THB 3,000 month;
  • personal training: THB 2,000 for one hour or THB 18,000 for ten; and
  • standalone fitness class: THB 300 once or THB 1,800 for ten.

The operator’s one-month functional card incorrectly repeats “use 10 times under 3 month” beside the monthly label. Confirm whether that product is unlimited for one month and when validity starts.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Elite suits a short-stay visitor who wants a published ordinary day pass, and a longer-stay member who values air conditioning, broad hours and optional coached products. Someone wanting functional training should compare the higher product rather than assume it is included in the THB 300 pass.

It is a weaker fit for a visitor who needs a specific class at any arbitrary time: the daily venue hours do not replace the class timetable. The East Pattaya page provides location context for other inland choices.

Getting there

The operator lists Sukhumvit 392/62 and phone +66 98 191 9421. Maps identifies the same business at 392/62 and currently shows a 22:00 close. The operator location page places it between Soi 42 and Soi 44, opposite Soi 65.

Use the exact Maps pin on Sukhumvit Road. No checked operator page published a baht-bus instruction, fixed fare or guaranteed parking space.

Before you go

Published hours are 06:30-22:00 daily. Ask whether a first visit requires identification, a deposit, app registration or advance class booking. For a class, confirm the date, exact start, instructor and whether the ordinary membership or a separate class product covers it.

Bring training clothes and suitable shoes, and ask about towels, lockers, changing facilities and drinking water. For personal or functional training, state the goal and experience level and obtain the session duration, trainer assignment, expiry and cancellation terms in writing.

What we could not verify

  • The live class-by-class weekly timetable was not readable on the checked class page.
  • Towel, locker, access-card, joining-fee and equipment-hire terms were not published on the rate page.
  • The functional-training one-month validity wording is internally inconsistent and needs confirmation.
  • Holiday hours, class capacity, booking cut-offs and refund rules were not established.
