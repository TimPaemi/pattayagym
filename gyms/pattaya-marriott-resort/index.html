★ About this venue

Pattaya Marriott Resort and Spa

Pattaya Marriott Resort and Spa is an operating Na Jomtien hotel. Marriott's current experiences page lists a 24-hour fitness centre, a family pool, a children's pool and an adult or infinity pool. Asset World Corporation, the owner, also describes three pools and a fitness centre.

The hotel does not publish a non-resident gym or pool day pass on the sources checked. Treat the facilities as hotel-guest amenities unless the resort confirms otherwise. The former day-pass tag and unsupported pool timetable have been removed.

No standalone sports-facility tariff is quoted because room prices are date-dependent and are not evidence of public gym access.
