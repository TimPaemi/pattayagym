★ About this venue

Human Strong Gym Pattaya

Human Strong Gym Pattaya is an open-plan Pratamnak gym with a documented mix of weights, resistance machines, cardio equipment and combat coaching. The operator Facebook page and Maps listing support the venue identity and Pratamnak Soi 4 location. Listed hours are 07:00-22:00 Monday to Friday and 08:00-22:00 Saturday and Sunday, but the public sources do not provide a current fee table or a class timetable.

What training is on offer

The operator describes free weights, resistance machines, treadmills, exercise bikes, personal training, Muay Thai and boxing. That is a useful combination for someone wanting normal gym training with the possibility of combat coaching. It does not prove that a Muay Thai or boxing class runs at a particular time, or that coaching is included in ordinary entry. Ask whether combat training is a scheduled group session or a private appointment, and ask which equipment or coaching is available on the day.

What it costs

No current first-hand day, week, month, coaching or combat-training price was visible in the sources checked on 25 July 2026. This record does not substitute an assumed local gym price for a published rate. Ask about casual entry, membership, personal training, Muay Thai and boxing separately, and confirm the total before attending.

Who it suits — and who it does not

The supported equipment and coaching mix makes Human Strong relevant to someone who wants strength and cardio training in the same place as potential boxing or Muay Thai instruction. It may suit a regular gym user who can check the coaching arrangement in advance. It does not suit a visitor who needs a confirmed public day pass, a published fight-class timetable, a trainer roster, or a detailed equipment list before leaving home.

Getting there

The listed address is Pratamnak Soi 4, Pattaya, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. Use the Maps link to navigate and confirm the exact entrance with the operator. The checked sources do not publish a public phone number, transport fare, nearby landmark instruction or parking policy, so those details are not claimed here.

Before you go

Confirm the day's opening hours, visitor access and payment method. If combat coaching is the reason for the trip, ask whether it is offered that day, whether it needs an appointment and whether it is priced separately. The published weekday and weekend hours identify an opening window, not a promise of a particular coached session.

What we could not verify

No current tariff, public phone number, class-by-class timetable, trainer names, coaching format, equipment inventory beyond the published categories, locker terms, parking policy or visitor-access rule was available from the operator and Maps sources checked on 25 July 2026.
