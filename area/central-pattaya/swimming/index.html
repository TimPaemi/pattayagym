Central Pattaya's swimming page has one verified option, and it is a hotel leisure-pool day rather than a lap-swim cluster: Hard Rock Hotel Pattaya Pool at 429 Moo 9 on Pattaya Beach Road. The operator describes a 2,000-square-metre freeform pool with a sand beach, jacuzzi, underwater speakers, children's zone and slides; that leisure inventory does not establish a measured lane or competition layout. That narrow coverage is useful to state plainly. A visitor staying around Beach Road, Pattaya Klang or the central shopping belt can use a published non-resident pool product without assuming hotel-guest status, but the listing does not prove that Central Pattaya has a measured public training pool, lane programme or coached swim session. Choose it as a recreational pool day, then use the full swimming directory when lane training, instruction or another access model is the priority. The operator publishes an explicit price for this retained record: non-resident entry is THB 500 net for an adult, including one selected cocktail, and THB 400 net for a child under 12, including one fruit punch. The same official pool page lists tubes from THB 200 net per day. That supports a product-specific price comparison but not a claim that it is the clearest or cheapest local pool offer. These are dated, named inclusions rather than a vague hotel day-pass claim, so the practical starting price is visible before calling. They are not a general food-and-drink credit, and the published material does not give a current cabana price even though it says a cabana can accommodate up to four people or one family. Ask whether a tube or cabana is available for the chosen date instead of treating it as included in the entry figure. Access timing is another distinction worth separating from the hotel timetable. The pool operates daily from 07:00 to 21:00, while the published non-guest window is 09:00 to 19:00. A non-resident should plan around the latter, call +66 38 428 755 before travelling and ask which hotel entrance handles pool-day check-in. The published product does not establish advance-reservation rules, capacity limits, parking validation, towel or locker inclusion, changing-facility terms, or entry to the gym and spa. A guest who wants any of those should get a direct answer in writing rather than relying on the pool description. Hard Rock's stated pool features explain who it suits: it describes a 2,000-square-metre freeform leisure pool with a sand beach, jacuzzi, underwater speakers, guitar-shaped deck, children's zone, slides and a cocktail pool. That is a family or leisure choice close to Central Pattaya's hotel corridor, not evidence of competition dimensions. The property does not publish a lane length, lane timetable, coached programme or reserved lap space, so a swimmer working to a structured set should not infer those from the pool's size. The child ticket and children's zone make the day product easier to assess for families, but the operator does not publish every age, supervision or capacity rule. The child tariff is stated for children under 12, but the checked page does not explain proof-of-age requirements or the price applied once a child is 12. A family booking near that boundary should confirm the charged category, adult-to-child supervision requirement and whether slides or other pool features use separate age or height rules. For a Central Pattaya stay, the decision is therefore straightforward: pay the documented hotel-pool rate when a centrally located leisure day and its stated drink inclusion are what you want; call first to confirm entry conditions and extras; and travel to another area only after deciding that documented lap space, instruction or a different pool type matters more than this convenient Beach Road access. One verified listing is a coverage limit, not a promise that every swimming need can be met here.
