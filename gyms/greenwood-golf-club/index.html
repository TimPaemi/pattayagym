★ About this venue

Greenwood Golf Club

Greenwood Golf Club is a north-of-Pattaya golf day rather than a casual city practice stop. Its official pages clearly confirm the current course identity, the 27-hole format and the Ban Bueng address, but they are weaker on a dependable public green-fee number than some other regional clubs. This is therefore a contact-first course record, not a price-led one.

What training is on offer

The official Greenwood course page confirms 27 total holes arranged as three championship courses, labelled A, B and C. The same page describes them as links-style, British-style and American-style nines and credits the course design to Peter W. Thomson, with operation since 1993.

For a golfer choosing between formats, that matters more than a generic “18 holes available” claim. A 27-hole property can assign different nines on different dates, and the experience changes with the loop combination. Greenwood’s official site also links to online booking and separate pages for restaurant, locker-room and pro-shop facilities, so it presents as a full-course operation rather than a practice-only venue.

The public pages do not publish beginner coaching, academy structure or a range-only starter product in extractable text. The verified offer here is the full-course golf product, with supporting club facilities around it.

What it costs

Greenwood’s official site has a public green-fee page, but as checked on 2026-07-27 that page did not expose a dependable extractable current numeric tariff in the accessible source text. It does say that prices may vary by day and season. That means the blank public price in this record is an evidence gap, not a claim that the club is low-cost or free.

Before booking, call the club and ask for the exact date-specific total: green fee, caddie fee, buggy or cart rule, rental clubs, shoes, taxes, card surcharge, deposit and cancellation terms. Because this is a regional golf day, also ask which nine-hole combination is in play for the selected booking and whether any maintenance affects the chosen loop.

The checked official pages show two close but conflicting Bangkok phone numbers: 02-0264694 on the home, course and green-fee pages, and 02-0266494 on the contact page. Use the contact page first and confirm the active reservation number when you speak to the club.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Greenwood suits a golfer who wants a 27-hole regional course and is comfortable contacting the club for a written total instead of relying on a clean public rate card. It is stronger for someone comparing course format and route than for someone trying to sort Pattaya golf strictly by headline green fee.

It is a weaker fit for a visitor who only wants a quick city practice session or refuses enquiry-led pricing. For a broader regional comparison, use the best golf courses guide and the golf category. Burapha Golf Club is another larger-format northern comparison, while Pattaya Country Club is a better record if you want a current public total already broken into components.

Getting there

The official contact page lists 88/8 Moo 6, Tambon Klong-Kiew, Ban-Bung, Chonburi 20220. That is outside Pattaya proper, so route planning is part of the decision. Do not attach a universal Pattaya transfer time to this record; the actual journey depends on your hotel, tee time and traffic.

If you are booking a driver, agree waiting time, tolls, golf-bag capacity and the return plan before departure. If you are self-driving, ask the club which entrance, bag-drop point and parking area are in use for the booked tee time.

Before you go

The checked official course and contact pages list daily hours of 06:00-18:00. Treat those as club operating hours, not proof that every tee time or loop is available throughout that whole window. Confirm the first tee, last practical booking slot and the exact loop allocation for the chosen date.

Send the number of players, requested date, preferred start time, handicap or tee preference and rental needs in one enquiry. If the club answers by phone only, ask them to repeat any total or booking condition in writing.

What we could not verify

  • No dependable extractable public green-fee figure was found on the checked official pages.
  • No public caddie fee, buggy fee or rental-club tariff was verified.
  • No public schedule showing which nine-hole combinations run on which dates was found.
  • No beginner lesson or academy tariff was verified from the checked pages.
