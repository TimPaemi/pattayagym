Naklua's golf page is a two-range practice comparison, not a course tee-time list. Pattaya Golf Driving Range is the active Third Road facility, while Diana Driving Range is at Diana Garden Resort on North Pattaya Road. Both are contact-first ball-practice options; neither record confirms a full 18-hole round, a simulator, a named academy or a fixed online reservation. The exact map pin matters because the range names and North Pattaya labels are easy to confuse. Pattaya Golf Driving Range has the clearer conditional price evidence. Pattaya Sports Club's member-discount page listed THB 55 for 55 balls on 26 July 2026, requiring a valid PSC card and not combinable with another card. This is a member benefit, not the ordinary public tray price. Maps lists daily 07:00–22:00, while the owner Facebook profile has a generic always-open label; use the detailed Maps hours and confirm lighting, bay availability and the non-member price by phone at +66 84 161 5055. The sources do not establish bay count, covered bays, target distances, club hire, coaching or a short-game area. Diana offers an earlier stated window, daily 06:00–22:00, and a current Maps identity at 209/112 North Pattaya Road, with phone +66 92 451 5626. Its location at the Diana Garden Resort property does not turn it into a resort golf package. No operator-published basket price, ball count, bay rate, rental-club fee or coaching tariff was found. Ask the range to separate balls, bay, lighting, club hire, instruction and any deposit before travelling. Do not promote customer-comment prices into an operator rate, and do not confuse North Pattaya Road with Soi Diana in central Pattaya. For a Naklua stay, choose Third Road when the documented PSC member rate is relevant or its later 22:00 close fits the plan; choose Diana when its 06:00 opening and North Pattaya Road location are more useful. Both require a direct check for the details that decide a practice visit: ball count, open bay, equipment, payment, weather and last basket sale. Travel elsewhere when a full round, measured launch-monitor data, a confirmed lesson or a published rental set is essential. The Pattaya golf-course guide is the better comparison for booked courses; this page only documents two local ball-practice options.
