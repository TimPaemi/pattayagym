# Phoenix Gold Golf & Country Club

Thai Maps names the property **ฟินิกซ์ โกล์ด กอล์ฟ แอนด์ คันทรี คลับ**.

Phoenix Gold is a 27-hole club in Huai Yai, arranged as the Mountain, Ocean and Lakes nines. It suits golfers who value a choice of loops, documented practice facilities and pre-bookable rental sets. It does not publish a stable current green-fee table, so the exact playing transaction remains contact-first.

## What training is on offer

The operator says the course opened in 1993 with an original design by Denis Griffiths and documents 2014-15 upgrades by Pirapon Namatra and GolfEast. Mountain is described as three-dimensional with views towards Khao Chi Chan, Ocean as tree-lined with elevation changes and Gulf views, and Lakes as the shortest loop with position and lay-up decisions. Those descriptions do not guarantee the pair of nines assigned on a future date.

The official facilities page lists a driving range open daily 06:00-18:00 with 12 bays using mats and natural turf, plus a chipping green and putting green. It does not publish a current lesson, basket or practice-only tariff. The [Pattaya golf directory](/category/golf/) helps distinguish courses, ranges and other golf products.

## What it costs

The operator's price list, checked 29 July 2026, lists an 18-hole green fee of **THB 3,000 on weekdays** and **THB 3,500 on weekends**, plus **THB 400** for a caddie and **THB 700** for a cart. The published standard stack is therefore THB 4,100 weekday or THB 4,600 weekend before any tip. The same page lists a 27-hole green fee of THB 4,100 weekday or THB 4,900 weekend, with THB 600 caddie and THB 1,050 cart charges.

Ask reservations for one written total covering the green fee, caddie, cart, rental clubs, shoes, locker or required charges, taxes, deposit and payment surcharge. Request the cancellation, weather and no-show terms too. Do not use an agent, reseller, old review or city-wide estimate as the venue's current rate.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Phoenix Gold suits a golfer who wants a 27-hole choice, a documented range and the ability to request equipment before arrival. It is less suitable for someone who needs instant online pricing, a guaranteed loop or a public beginner lesson. ## Before you go

The current exact Maps identity and operator site support daily 06:00-18:00 operation. The pro shop is listed 05:30-18:00. These are facility windows, not a last tee time; ask how early the group must check in and which nine combination is expected.

The facilities page lists Titleist and TaylorMade rental sets with separate left- and right-handed stock and advises pre-booking an appropriate set. It also lists locker rooms, showers and shoe cleaning. The club says carts are optional for groups of up to four, with one player per cart, and mandatory for groups of five. Reconfirm group-size permission, cart and caddie allocation, dress code and rental availability.
