# Fitness 7 Pattaya

Fitness 7 Pattaya is a true general-purpose 24-hour gym, not a Muay Thai camp or a hotel fitness room. The official Pattaya branch page is clear on the big picture: a large mall-based facility with weights, cardio, functional space and a boxing ring, open around the clock. Its most useful public strength is that the operator also publishes a current branch-wide rate card instead of hiding all pricing behind chat.

## What training is on offer

The Pattaya branch page describes more than 2,000 square metres of training space on the first floor of The Avenue Pattaya. The official amenities language covers cardio machines, weight machines, free weights, a boxing ring and a functional-training area, and the branch page says free classes are provided at this location. The broader About Us page also says Fitness 7 provides staff help, boxing gear, kettlebells, mats, rollers and other miscellaneous equipment. The boxing ring and gear support striking practice, but the public evidence is stronger for general fitness access than for a named combat timetable.

## What it costs

Fitness 7's current main site publishes a branch-wide rate card checked on 2026-07-27: **฿350 for 1 day**, **฿1,600 for 1 month**, **฿4,500 for 3 months**, **฿8,400 for 6 months** and **฿14,400 for 12 months**. The site presents those as access products with locker rooms, showers, free consultation and free class access at selected branches. Because the Pattaya branch page explicitly says free classes are provided there, the branch appears to fall inside that selected-branch wording.

Before paying, ask whether the one-day pass is a full 24-hour entry or a limited visit window, whether any joining fee applies at Pattaya, and whether class access needs a booking or member check-in.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Fitness 7 suits travellers or residents who want central Pattaya access, late hours and a conventional mixed-equipment gym rather than a specialist sport venue. It is especially relevant for readers comparing [24-hour gyms in Pattaya](/guides/24-hour-gyms-pattaya/) or wanting a central option alongside the broader [fitness category](/category/fitness/).

It is a weaker fit for someone who wants a serious coached Muay Thai programme, a resort pool day or a quieter residential training environment. If the real need is structured combat training, compare a dedicated venue such as [ISS Boxing and Muay Thai](/gyms/wko-muay-thai/) or the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/). If the goal is an East-side local gym rather than a central mall branch, [East Pattaya listings](/area/east-pattaya/) will be more relevant.

## Getting there

The branch sits on the first floor of The Avenue Pattaya, room A100, on Pattaya Second Road in Central Pattaya. That makes it one of the easier large gyms for someone already staying in the central-south corridor. Because it is in a mall complex, use the exact branch entry and current mall access instructions rather than assuming every entrance stays open the same way overnight.

## Before you go

Call or message ahead if the class matters to you. The public branch page says classes are included, but it does not publish the class timetable on the checked page. Also ask whether your chosen pass includes shower use and whether a towel is provided or rented.

If you plan to use the boxing ring, confirm whether ring time is structured, free-use or class-led. A ring on the floor is not the same as a guaranteed coached boxing session.
