# Pattana Sports Resort

Pattana is a sports resort in Khaokhunsong, Si Racha, north of Pattaya. The current operator site confirms a 27-hole golf course, sports facilities, accommodation and a 50-metre swimming pool.

## Current golf offer checked 25 July 2026

The operator's July early-bird offer is ฿1,650 for 18 holes including green fee, caddie and cart on weekday tee times before 07:30. It is valid from 1-31 July 2026 and is limited to Thai nationals and foreign residents in Thailand.

That promotion is not a general tourist rate. Visitors who do not meet the conditions should ask the golf desk for the applicable green fee, caddie fee, cart rule and tee-time availability.
