★ About this venue

Sanctuary of Truth Museum

The Sanctuary of Truth is an operating cultural museum in Naklua, not a gym, sports club or training venue. The OpenStreetMap way 223698118 records the Thai name ปราสาทสัจธรรม, which helps Thai-speaking visitors match the attraction identity. This legacy directory record remains visible with a non-sport label so older activity references do not become an accidental training recommendation. The useful decision is between its published day and night visits.

What training is on offer

No sport training is offered in the verified visitor product. The operator describes guided museum visits, language-specific tours and accessibility support. Optional visitor activities do not turn the attraction into a riding school, combat gym or organised sports facility.

The operator publishes frequent English tours and scheduled Thai, Chinese, Russian, Arabic and other language tours. A listed tour language and time should be reconfirmed for the chosen date.

What it costs

The operator's visit and ticket pages, checked on 26 July 2026, publish:

  • Day visit: ฿500 for an adult or ฿250 for a child 110-140 centimetres tall.
  • Night visit: ฿700 for an adult or ฿350 for a child 110-140 centimetres tall.
  • Children below 110 centimetres: free.
  • Parking: free.

The online product is date-specific and described as non-refundable. Day tickets are sold 08:00-17:00 with tours from 08:20 to 18:00. Night tickets are sold 17:30-19:30, with the night tour from 18:20 to 20:30. Verify the selected product rather than comparing only the lowest adult amount.

Who it suits — and who it does not

It suits a visitor intentionally booking a guided cultural attraction and choosing between daylight and evening presentation. The operator publishes wheelchairs and chairs, a wooden lift, a prayer room and a shuttle for senior visitors and people with disabilities, although individual needs should still be discussed before purchase.

It does not suit anyone seeking exercise instruction, riding, a gym, a sports club or a general attraction pass with refundable flexibility. For actual sport, use the Naklua area page or browse all Pattaya sports categories.

Getting there

The official directions and Maps place the museum at 206/2 Soi Naklua 12, in Naklua, North Pattaya. The directory address also retains the operator's 206/2 Moo 5, Pattaya-Naklua Road form. Maps confirms the current museum identity; the operator's general contact number is +66 38 110 653.

Use the exact pin and Soi Naklua 12 approach. No fixed transfer time or fare is quoted because the starting point and traffic are not fixed.

Before you go

The operator requires a helmet inside the wooden structure. Dress modestly; shawls or sarongs can be borrowed against a ฿200 deposit. The visit page prohibits drones, food and drink, pets and smoking in restricted areas.

Check the date, day-versus-night product, participant heights, language tour and last ticket time. Accessibility equipment and shuttle support should be requested directly if essential.

What we could not verify

  • Tour-language capacity and availability were not guaranteed for every date.
  • Optional activity prices and operating times were not reproduced because they are not sports training and may vary.
  • Queue length, tour-group size and total visit duration were not published as guarantees.
  • The public pages do not establish that every route inside the structure is suitable for every mobility requirement.
