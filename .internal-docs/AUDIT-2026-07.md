## 2 · S1 — act now

### S1-1 · A prohibited sister domain ships in author `sameAs`, and the hard gate falsely passes

**What and where.** `scripts/lib/timpaemi-author.js:56-61` says sister sites do not belong in `sameAs` and then inserts `https://pattaya-authority.com/`. The resulting reference appears twice per affected page, including `404.html:42-43` and the live homepage. `scripts/check-no-network-links.js:49-53` only detects a sister domain when it occurs inside `href=`, despite `AGENTS.md:9` explicitly requiring zero references “including JSON-LD, `sameAs`”.

**Count.** 344/355 local HTML files; 688 HTML occurrences; two occurrences on the live homepage. The gate nevertheless returned `OK — 355 pages, 357 followed timpaemi.com credits, 0 sister-site links`.

**Command.** C18, C19 and C20 in §9.

**Why.** The local network rule is unambiguous, and Google’s spam policies were expanded to generative-AI responses on 15 May 2026; spam reports have been eligible to trigger manual actions since 14 April 2026. An undisclosed cross-property entity mesh is therefore an avoidable trust and competitor-report surface. The fact that the links are JSON-LD rather than visible anchors does not satisfy the zero-reference rule.

**Smallest fix.** Remove the sister URL from `NETWORK_SAMEAS`, rebuild through the one authoritative chain, and change the gate to search sister-domain strings in all shippable text—not only `href=`. Keep the single followed `timpaemi.com` publisher credit.

### S1-2 · Operational, research and private material is publicly served

**What and where.** `_headers:118` adds `noindex,nofollow` to `.internal-docs/*`, but Cloudflare still serves exact file paths. `_redirects:58-62` blocks root rulebooks but does not block `.internal-docs/`, `research/`, or `private/`. Live checks returned 200 for `.internal-docs/AUDIT_REPORT.md`, `research/BACKLOG.md`, and `private/README.md`.

**Count.** `.internal-docs`: 71 tracked files, 10,127,269 bytes on disk; `research`: 69 tracked files, 392,446 bytes; `private`: two tracked files, 251,132 bytes. The three tested representative live URLs returned 200. `.internal-docs` has a live `X-Robots-Tag`; the tested `research` and `private` files do not.

**Command.** C21 and C22.

**Why.** This is a current information-disclosure defect. `noindex` is not access control, and the threat cost is higher now that Google changed spam-report handling on 14 April 2026. The severity is **my judgement, unevidenced** as a security classification; no dated search standard assigns a severity to publishing internal project files.

**Smallest fix.** Publish from an explicit output allowlist or block those three path trees at the deployment layer. This is index/access-affecting and therefore also appears in §6.

### S1-3 · Six non-operating or qualified source statuses are dropped from `data.js`

**What and where.** Six venue Markdown records carry a status that is absent from their `data.js` object: `fight-evo360` (`schedule-unconfirmed`, `venues/fight-evo360.md:12`; `data.js:48`), `crossfit-pattaya` (`former-crossfit-affiliate`, `venues/crossfit-pattaya.md:14`; `data.js:52`), `first-serve-sports-club` (`not-in-pattaya`, `venues/first-serve-sports-club.md:13`; `data.js:109`), `pattaya-triathlon` (`unverified`, `venues/pattaya-triathlon.md:12`; `data.js:191`), `pattaya-boxing-world` (`unverified`, `venues/pattaya-boxing-world.md:13`; `data.js:193`), and `pattaya-running-routes` (`informational`, `venues/pattaya-running-routes.md:12`; `data.js:196`).

**Count.** Six mismatches; all six generated pages are indexable and carry `FAQPage`. Five mention the qualification somewhere in visible prose; `fight-evo360` has no generated status warning, even though its body discusses timetable uncertainty.

**Command.** C23 and C24.

**Why.** The audit brief classifies a front-matter/data status mismatch as S1 because the generator treats the record as operating. Google’s structured-data policy also requires markup to match visible content, and Google’s trust questions require claims to be supportable; spam-report handling changed 14 April 2026.

**Smallest fix.** Make status a single sourced field consumed by `data.js`, visible status UI, schema eligibility, FAQs, category cards and sitemap rules. Preserve historical/informational records only when the qualification is explicit everywhere they are surfaced.

### S1-4 · `dateModified` is the venue verification/action date, not the page modification date

**What and where.** `build-v2.js:1172` passes `modified: g.verified`. Every one of the 215 venue pages therefore has `dateModified` equal to its verification date; 52 also equal `priceAsOf`. This is separate from sitemap `lastmod`, whose cached HTML hashes currently agree with generated-file Git dates.

**Count.** 215/215 venue `dateModified` values equal `verified`; 52/215 equal `priceAsOf`; zero future schema dates. Across all pages, 355 `dateModified` properties were found and `favorites/index.html` was the only page without one.

**Command.** C25 and C26.

**Why.** Google says dates must describe publication or update of the page, “not the date of the action described on the page”; the audit’s 2026 baseline specifically identifies checked-on and price dates leaking into `dateModified` as a direct hit. Manufacturing freshness can cause Google to disregard date signals and undermines trust.

**Smallest fix.** Source `dateModified` from a content-hash/Git-backed substantive page-change date. Keep `verified` and `priceAsOf` visibly labelled as their own facts.

### E · Technical/performance

#### S2-E1 · Versioned assets revalidate after four hours

The corpus contains 2,402 versioned asset references—average 6.8 per page, range 5–10. ### H · Accessibility

#### S2-H1 · The primary boundary color is only 1.22:1 against the canvas

`styles.css:76` defines `--line: #dfe3d4` against `--bg: #f7f8f3`; computed contrast is 1.22:1. This color is used as the sole visible boundary on inputs, selects, buttons and cards. Text pairs checked were at least 4.61:1.

**Why.** WCAG 2.2 is the current standard and AA non-text contrast requires 3:1 for visual information needed to identify controls. Exact applicability depends on each component, but controls using this border as their only boundary fail.
