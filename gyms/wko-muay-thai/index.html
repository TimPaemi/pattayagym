ISS Boxing and Muay Thai

The current operator identity is ISS Boxing and Muay Thai. The former WKO wording has been removed from the venue name because the live site, social page and Maps listing now use ISS.

Prices checked 25 July 2026

  • gym and facilities: ฿300 per day, ฿800 per week or ฿1,500 per month
  • boxing and Muay Thai classes: ฿1,000 per day, ฿4,000 per week or ฿8,000 per month
  • recovery and weight-cut room: ฿200 per day, ฿600 per week or ฿1,000 per month

The operator schedules boxing and Muay Thai classes Monday-Saturday from 14:00 to 15:30 and lists general gym access from 09:00 to 21:00. Google Maps shows slightly different closing times, so confirm holiday and same-day access before travelling.
