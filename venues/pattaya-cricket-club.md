# Pattaya Cricket Club

Pattaya Cricket Club remains active. Its site says the club was formed in January 2010 and describes a first XI, an over-45 side and junior development, with women welcome. Current Bangkok Cricket League records list the Pattaya team and Horseshoe Point as its home ground for the 2025/26 competition.

This is a member and match programme, not a walk-in cricket attraction. Consult the club and league fixtures before travelling; match dates and grounds can change. The operator's current contact page provides an email form but no public phone, so the former phone has been removed.

No current membership or session tariff was published on the first-hand sources checked.
