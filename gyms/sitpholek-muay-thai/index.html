★ About this venue

Sitpholek Institute of Muay Thai Techniques

The 93/123 Moo 11 camp appears in Thai Maps as ค่ายมวย ศิษย์พ่อเหล็กและยิม.

Sitpholek is an active Muay Thai gym at 93/123 Moo 11 on Khao Talo Soi 8. The current Maps identity matches the operator’s Facebook page and the phone published by Frank Sitpholek. Its public website is old, however, so historical prices and a Sunday-rest statement are not presented as a current tariff or timetable.

What training is on offer

The operator site identifies the business as the Sitpholek Institute of Muaythai Techniques and explicitly welcomes professionals and starters. Maps categorises the current identity as a Muay Thai gym. Those sources support Muay Thai instruction for different experience levels, but they do not establish a current daily class format, trainer roster or fight-preparation pathway.

The record therefore does not restore older MMA, Lethwei, accommodation, title or promotion claims. Someone comparing live camp evidence can use the Muay Thai directory and the East Pattaya area page.

What it costs

No current operator-published tariff was found on 27 July 2026. The old website displays day, week and month figures alongside an obsolete exchange-rate statement and a description of nearby accommodation; without a publication date or newer confirmation, those amounts are historical evidence only and are not repeated as current prices.

Ask for the price of one group session, one-to-one pad work, one week and one month. Request the validity period, number of sessions, equipment inclusion, payment method and cancellation rule. A missing rate is not a reason to exclude an operating gym and does not mean training is free.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Sitpholek may suit a beginner or experienced trainee staying near Khao Talo who is comfortable arranging the exact session by phone or Facebook. Its confirmed local identity and direct operator number make a contact-first booking possible.

It is a weaker fit for someone who will only choose from a live online timetable and complete current rate card. Travellers building a longer stay should compare the Muay Thai training-holiday guide, because this record does not verify a residential package.

Getting there

Maps gives 93/123 M11, Khao Talo Soi 8, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150, phone +66 89 934 5001 and Plus Code WW32+VQ. This is inland East Pattaya rather than Beach Road or central Pattaya. No operator source checked published a baht-bus route, fixed fare, parking instruction or travel time.

Use the exact Maps pin and ask the gym to confirm the entrance. Do not navigate from the broad “Pattaya” label alone.

Before you go

Maps lists 08:00-18:00 daily, but the older operator page says training was every day except Sunday. That conflict is why the record does not convert business hours into a class timetable. Contact the gym for the intended date and ask for the exact start, duration and coach.

State experience level and whether the goal is a first lesson, fitness, technical work or fight preparation. Confirm gloves, wraps, shin guards, sparring expectations, shower or changing access, teaching language and accepted payment. The beginner Muay Thai guide gives a first-session checklist.

What we could not verify

  • No current day, week, month or private-training tariff was published.
  • No current class-by-class timetable, Sunday policy or holiday schedule was established.
  • Current trainer roster, languages, equipment hire and sparring policy were not published.
  • Accommodation, MMA, Lethwei and fight-promotion claims from the former record were not supported by the current checked sources.
