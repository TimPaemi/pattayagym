# Pattaya Lawn Bowls Facilities

Pattaya Sports Club's current lawn-bowls directory lists four separate places rather than the three claimed in the previous record. This is a multi-venue entry, so check the location and contact before travelling.

## Current facilities and prices

- **Banchang Lawn Bowls Club** lists six covered artificial rinks. The published day rate is ฿250 including equipment and facility use, with open roll-ups at 10:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
- **Coco Sports Indoor Lawn Bowling** lists four indoor international-size rinks. Monday and Wednesday open play is ฿300 including all-day play, equipment, buffet and facility use.
- **Paradise Lawn Bowls Pattaya** lists eight outdoor floodlit artificial rinks. Advance booking is requested; no stable public price was found.
- **The Retreat** lists two greens with five grass and five artificial rinks. Public access is listed at ฿300 per day including equipment and facilities, with afternoon roll-ups on Wednesday, Friday and Sunday.

Those prices were checked on 25 July 2026 against the Pattaya Sports Club source linked above. Phone details differ by facility and are published on that page.

## First visit

Choose Banchang or Coco if weather protection matters. Paradise requires advance contact, while The Retreat publishes both grass and artificial play. Ask whether the day's session is a roll-up, competition or private booking and whether bowls in your size are available.
