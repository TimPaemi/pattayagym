# Siam Bayshore Pattaya Tennis Courts

Siam Bayshore publishes six tennis courts open to the public daily from 07:00 to 18:00. In-house guests receive one complimentary daytime hour.

## Price and first visit

The hotel lists THB 350 per court-hour, THB 150 per hour for a racket and THB 100 per hour for four balls, checked 25 July 2026. The operator also publishes rain-adjustment terms. Contact the front desk for a reservation and confirm whether lighting or evening play is available.
