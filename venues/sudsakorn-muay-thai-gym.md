# Sudsakorn Muay Thai Gym

Sudsakorn Muay Thai Gym is a straightforward East Pattaya camp with a usable public rate card and two daily training windows Monday to Saturday. The operator site supports the gym's Muay Thai focus and beginner-friendly messaging, while the live Maps identity confirms the phone, address area and Sunday closure. It is one of the clearer contact-and-price records in the [Muay Thai category](/category/muay-thai/).

## What training is on offer

A Thai activity listing names the 999/1 Pattaya venue **ค่ายมวยสุดสาคร** for visitors comparing local Muay Thai camps.

The operator presents Sudsakorn as a Muay Thai gym rather than a hotel package or multi-sport compound. Current public information supports two coached training periods, **08:00-11:00** and **16:00-19:00**, from Monday to Saturday. The site also pitches the gym to people learning boxing skills, improving fitness and losing weight, which is useful for beginners or ordinary travellers rather than only active fighters.

What the checked public pages do not publish is a class-by-class breakdown inside those windows. There is no dependable public split for beginner group, fighter group, children, private training or sparring-only slots. The useful reading is that the camp has two daily training blocks, not that every visitor can arrive at any minute inside three hours and receive the same product.

If you are comparing it against more residential products, use the [East Pattaya area page](/area/east-pattaya/) and the [Muay Thai beginners guide](/guides/muay-thai-pattaya-beginners/) to keep training-only and accommodation-based offers separate.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Sudsakorn suits someone who wants a traditional Muay Thai base with a published training rhythm and dated rates before making contact. It is especially practical for a visitor comparing one-session, two-session, weekly and monthly training-only commitments.

It does not suit someone who wants a documented room package, a luxury recovery setup or a public promise about trainer language, private coaching format or family accommodation. Those buyers should compare the [stay-and-train camp guide](/guides/muay-thai-camps-with-accommodation-pattaya/) or a venue with explicit residential pricing instead of treating every Muay Thai gym as the same product.

## Before you go

Send the gym the exact date, whether you want one session or two, and whether you are new to Muay Thai. The operator publishes the broad training windows and rates, but not the finer points that affect a first session.

Ask what equipment you need to bring, whether gloves or wraps can be borrowed or bought on site, whether private training is available, and whether the quoted weekly or monthly plan must run on consecutive days. Also confirm whether Sunday is always fully closed or only closed for ordinary training.
