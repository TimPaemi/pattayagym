★ About this venue

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse

Columbia Pictures Aquaverse is the current operating identity for the Na Jomtien park formerly branded as Cartoon Network Amazone. It is a themed water-and-attractions day out rather than a training pool, but it still belongs in the swimming side of the directory because the public product is built around slides, pools and paid water access. The honest limitation is that the checked official pages did not show one stable static general-admission price.

What training is on offer

The operator's current About Us page describes Aquaverse as a Sony Pictures-branded water and movie theme park near Pattaya. The current site presents eight themed zones rather than the legacy Cartoon Network branding. Public-facing attractions include water slides, wave-pool-style activity, children's water play and optional land attractions rather than coached swim instruction or lane swimming.

That distinction matters when comparing it with a public swim session or a hotel pool. Aquaverse is a family leisure product designed around rides, themed environments and bookable attractions. If the real goal is swimming practice or regular exercise, the swimming category and the family-friendly guide are better decision tools than a pure water-park listing.

What it costs

The operator's live official pages checked on 2026-07-27 confirmed the park identity, location, contact details and stated daily opening window of 10:00-18:00, but they did not expose one dependable static public ticket amount suitable for quoting here. Admission appears to run through a live booking flow and package offers that can vary by date or promotion.

That means you should not reuse old Cartoon Network Amazone ticket prices or a reseller number as if it were the current operator tariff. Before paying, ask or check the official booking flow for the exact date, adult-versus-child pricing, resident-versus-tourist conditions if any, transport inclusions and whether specific land attractions, surfing products or premium experiences cost extra.

If a seller quotes a park ticket, ask whether it covers only entry or also transfers, food credit, locker use or access to add-on activities. This is the kind of attraction where the smallest headline number can hide a materially different day product.

Who it suits — and who it does not

Aquaverse suits families, groups and short-stay visitors who want a structured themed water-park day and are comfortable treating it as an attraction purchase. It can also suit travellers in Na Jomtien or Sattahip who want one planned day rather than a repeated training routine.

It does not suit someone looking for a lap pool, regular coached swimming, a cheap local swim or a gym-and-pool membership. For those buyers, a dedicated swimming-pools guide or another swimming listing will be more useful. If the real plan is a broader activity day rather than a water-focused one, compare the adventure guide instead of assuming every attraction with water belongs in the same purchase.

Getting there

The operator lists 888 Moo 8, Na Jomtien, Sattahip, Chonburi 20250, which places the park south of central Pattaya in the Na Jomtien belt. The official site also advertises contact and support pages for planning the journey. This is not a walk-up central-beach activity for most travellers staying in North or Central Pattaya.

Because the park runs as a ticketed attraction, the practical route question is not only the map pin. It is also where ticketed guests check in, whether transport is bundled and what time you need to arrive for your chosen booking window.

Before you go

Use the current official booking flow for the exact day you want. Confirm park opening hours, current ticket type, age or height restrictions for any key ride, whether swimwear rules apply across all water zones and whether lockers, towels or transport are extra. If you are travelling with children, check the operator's current guest-services page for family and support information before purchase.

This record uses the former name only to stop stale recommendations from sending people to an outdated brand. If someone still tells you to book Cartoon Network Amazone, translate that advice into the current Columbia Pictures Aquaverse booking flow before paying anything.

What we could not verify

  • No stable static public general-admission price on the checked official pages.
  • No dependable all-day price list for optional land attractions or premium add-ons.
  • No public lane-swimming or coached-swim product, which appears not to be the venue's purpose.
  • No single official transfer fare suitable for quoting as a standard amount.
