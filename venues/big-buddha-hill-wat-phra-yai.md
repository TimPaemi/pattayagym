# Wat Phra Yai — Big Buddha Hill Stair Climb

Wat Phra Yai is an active hilltop temple, not a gym. It remains in this directory only as a practical stair section for walkers already using Pratamnak Hill. The current Maps listing and recent visitor updates confirm the temple and its prominent staircase.

No exact step count, workout prescription, operating hours or admission price is stated here because a current first-hand source did not publish those details. Dress and behave for an active religious site, avoid blocking worshippers, and do not treat the temple platform as a training circuit.
