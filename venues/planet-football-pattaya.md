# Planet Football Pattaya

Planet Football is a youth football operator with two East Pattaya venues. The directory pin and address above refer to **Planet Football Stadium at 55/15 Moo 2 on Siam Country Club Road**, the operator's headquarters. Its second venue, FC Planet Stadium, is on Soi 10 off Phon Prapha Nimit 34 Road. The current Maps results distinguish the two pins, so check which stadium is named in the selected programme before travelling.

The operator says its coaches hold UEFA or AFC qualifications and that sessions are separated by age and ability. This is a structured coaching programme rather than an unsupervised public football field. Its pathway runs from beginner open sessions to development training and an invite-only academy.

## Open sessions and prices checked 26 July 2026

Three Saturday open-session pages publish the same **09:00–10:30** period at the Planet Football sports hall and a fee of **฿400 per player**:

- **Mini Kicks** is for boys and girls aged 4–6 and focuses on basic football movement, coordination and confidence. The operator says parents may be encouraged to join activities.
- **Kiddy Kicks** is for ages 7–9 and adds ball mastery, game knowledge, communication and teamwork.
- **Soccer School** is for ages 10–13 and covers technical work, positions, finishing, phases of play and tactics over a stated 12–15-week cycle.

The operator says children should register as full members after their first session. Registered players must wear the Planet Football Soccer School kit with long socks and shin pads. The Mini Kicks page specifies trainers or futsal shoes for the indoor sports hall; the older groups' pages say football boots or similar. Ask the coach which footwear is correct for the actual surface and age group. A towel and water bottle are also recommended.

## Development and academy pathway

The Development Programme is for players seeking more training and a route towards team football. It has two age bands: 7–10 and 11–15. The current page lists Saturday and Sunday sessions at Planet Football Stadium, with the 11–15 group from 13:30–15:00 and the 7–10 group from 15:30–17:00. A combined 7–15 session is listed at FC Planet Stadium on Monday from 17:30–19:00.

The published Development Programme tuition is **฿2,500 per calendar month**, checked 26 July 2026. A separate **฿2,500 yearly membership** includes three Development Programme kits and a training ball. Confirm whether both payments are due when joining, the first billing date, absence policy and whether match or tournament costs are extra.

FC Planet Academy is not an open-session product. The operator describes it as invite-only, normally reached through Soccer School or the Development Programme, with three weekday training sessions between 17:00 and 19:00 and weekly competition. Families seeking an academy place should contact the head coach about a trial rather than arriving at a scheduled session.

## Facilities and booking

Planet Football Stadium has a 7-a-side pitch, an enclosed 5-a-side pitch and a multi-sports hall marked for futsal and basketball. The operator lists scoreboards, lighting, changing rooms, showers, spectator areas and an air-conditioned clubhouse bar. FC Planet Stadium has a full-size artificial-turf pitch configurable for 7-, 9- or 11-a-side play. The facility page says metal studs are not allowed.

Use **+66 38 248 212** for the main venue and **+66 95 995 7693** for facility bookings. Before paying, provide the child's age, experience and preferred date; confirm the stadium pin, coach, session status, first-session procedure, kit charge, footwear, shin pads and the total cost for the chosen pathway.
