# Hilton Pattaya Fitness Centre

Hilton's current hotel listing confirms a fitness centre, spa and outdoor infinity pool on the 16th floor. The hotel is at 333/101 Moo 9 above Central Pattaya on Beach Road.

## Access and first visit

Hilton does not publish a current public gym day pass, non-resident access policy or fitness-centre timetable on the checked pages. The former claims of 24-hour access and an arrangeable outside day pass have therefore been removed. Hotel guests should confirm access at reception; non-residents should call before travelling.
