# Better Bodies Gym

Better Bodies is a weight-training gym at Somphong Condotel in Ban Amphur, Na Jomtien. The operator publishes unusually clear access prices and describes staff guidance for several training goals. It is a neighbourhood gym rather than a resort package or class-only studio, and it does not publish a weekly group-class timetable.

## What training is on offer

The operator describes a practical resistance-training floor for general fitness, weight loss or gain, injury prevention and contest preparation. Its services page says a trainer can show newcomers how to use equipment and offers personal training and contest preparation on request. It also describes member tracking that can cover weight, body-fat percentage, programme and diet, heart rate and blood pressure.

That is an operator account of coaching support, not medical treatment or a published rehabilitation service. Training help is listed in Thai, English and Dutch. Free weights and resistance machines are documented at category level, but the site does not publish a machine-by-machine inventory, rack count or class timetable. Compare other access models in the [Pattaya fitness category](/category/fitness/).

## What it costs

The operator's services page published these rates when rechecked on **26 July 2026**:

- one day: **THB 250**
- one week: **THB 400**
- one month: **THB 1,000**
- three months: **THB 2,500**
- six months: **THB 4,500**

These are access periods, not scheduled class packages. The same page says nutritional advice, a body-fat check and progress checks are free for members and describes personal training as free during the first month. Confirm that the offer applies to the selected membership and ask whether any service, tax or payment condition is separate. Free drinking water and Wi-Fi are also listed.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The published menu makes Better Bodies easiest to compare for a one-day trial or a one-to-six-month local routine. It is a weaker fit for an early-morning workout, Sunday session or visitor seeking a scheduled group class: opening begins at 10:00, Sunday is closed and no class timetable was published. Someone choosing between this gym and a resort facility should compare the exact training floor and staff support, not hotel amenities that are not part of this membership.

The multilingual help may suit a new lifter who wants equipment orientation, subject to staff availability. It does not suit someone needing 24-hour access, a guaranteed rehabilitation clinician or a class at a fixed time. The [digital-nomad fitness guide](/guides/pattaya-digital-nomad-fitness/) helps longer-stay visitors compare location and membership length.

## Getting there

Opening is listed Monday to Saturday from **10:00 to 20:00**, with Sunday closed. The exact Maps identity remains active and links back to the operator site. The operator and Maps formats do not agree on every postcode and phone detail: the website publishes Somphong Condotel unit 99/15 on Sukhumvit Soi 26 and lists **0910 428 879** for Thai or English and **0848 832 967** for English, while Maps displays **084 883 2967**. Use the exact pin and confirm the entrance rather than relying on the postcode alone.

The directory groups the location with the southern corridor; the [Sattahip and Na Jomtien area page](/area/sattahip/) supplies broad context, not a fixed route, fare or parking entitlement.

## Before you go

For a first visit, state whether you want a one-day trial, unsupervised weight training, a personal-training appointment or contest preparation. Ask when a trainer will be present, whether a first-month personal-training offer applies to the chosen membership, what changing and locker arrangements are available, and which payment methods are accepted. The published THB 250 day price makes a trial possible without inventing a commitment, but it does not guarantee a trainer at every point in the ten-hour opening window.

## What we could not verify

- The checked pages did not publish a full equipment inventory, group-class schedule or trainer roster by hour.
- Locker, shower, towel, parking, accessibility and payment-method details were not established.
- The operator and Maps phone formats differ; confirm the preferred number and exact entrance before travelling.
