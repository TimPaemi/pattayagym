# Pattaya Sports Club Association

Pattaya Sports Club Association operates from 3/197 Pattaya 3rd Road. Its current site covers golf services, badminton, cricket, lawn bowls, pickleball, padel, tennis and table tennis, plus member discounts. It is an association office and information hub, not a single multi-sport facility with its own courts.

## Current membership

As checked on 25 July 2026, the operator publishes:

- One year: **฿600 per year**, plus a **฿400 signup fee**
- Six years: **฿3,000**, plus a **฿400 signup fee**

The club lists 095 706 2646 and 038 415 424. Maps currently shows the office open Monday-Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 and closed at weekends.

The former tennis, pickleball, badminton and table-tennis facility claims have been corrected: those sports appear in the association's network and information pages, not as proof of on-site courts.
