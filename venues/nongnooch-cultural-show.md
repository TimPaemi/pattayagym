# Nong Nooch Thai Cultural Show

This is a spectator attraction rather than a place to train. Nong Nooch's current event page lists four daily Thai cultural performances at 10:30, 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30, with martial-arts sequences forming part of the staged programme. The garden itself is open daily from 08:00 to 18:00.

The operator did not expose a stable general-admission price on the pages checked on 25 July 2026. Buy through the official site or at the venue and check whether a selected ticket includes the cultural and elephant shows.
