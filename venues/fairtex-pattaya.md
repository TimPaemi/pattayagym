# Fairtex Training Center Pattaya

## Training

Fairtex publishes Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu programmes for beginners through experienced fighters. The centre is open six days a week from 07:30 to 20:00; exact class times should be confirmed for the programme you want.
