Pratamnak's Muay Thai page is a comparison between two nearby, contactable gyms with different levels of price clarity, not a list of interchangeable tourist camps. Battle & Conquer Gym at 359/119 Moo 12 publishes both group blocks and a dated price list, while Eagle Gym Pratamnak on Soi 5 publishes group blocks and current activity but not a reliable public tariff. Both are hill-side options for a Pratamnak stay; neither record establishes accommodation, a residential camp package, a guaranteed coach assignment or a drop-in place simply because the building is open. Battle & Conquer is the clearer option when the budget has to be known before travelling. Its operator lists group Muay Thai from 08:00–10:00 and 16:00–18:00, with general facility hours of 07:00–22:00 daily. The current price list names a ฿500 group session; Muay Thai packages are ฿2,500 for one week, ฿7,000 for one month, ฿18,000 for three months and ฿35,000 for six months. Those packages include the listed gym, sauna and cold plunge facilities. A private 90-minute session is listed at ฿1,200, with five- and ten-session options, while gym-only entry is separately priced. That is a useful choice for someone comparing a short training block with general fitness access, but it does not say which trainer takes a particular session, whether sparring is offered, whether gloves are supplied, or whether a specific group class has space. Eagle's published group windows are Monday to Saturday 09:00–11:00 and 16:00–18:00. Its active Facebook and Instagram identity supports Muay Thai alongside boxing and judo, so a visitor should ask which discipline is actually scheduled within the chosen block rather than reading the window as a Muay Thai-only timetable. The gym advertises private one-to-one tutoring separately. Its public channels refer to pricing updates, yet do not expose a dependable current price ladder for group class, private tuition or longer membership. Ask +66 92 268 9613 for the exact date, discipline, group or private format, total charge, coach, gear and whether Sunday training is available; do not turn an older social image or a different Pattaya gym's rate into Eagle's price. For practical arrival, use each venue's own mapped address rather than treating “Pratamnak” as one walkable training strip. Battle & Conquer's published address is 359/119 Moo 12, while Eagle is identified as Pratamnak Soi 5. Confirm the entrance, parking or scooter access, payment method, changing facilities and the required arrival time directly. Published opening hours are not a booking confirmation, particularly for a private session or a visitor who needs beginner instruction. Choose Battle & Conquer when a dated visible rate, twice-daily Muay Thai blocks and access to weights and recovery facilities matter most. Choose Eagle when the smaller multi-discipline gym and its stated class periods fit, after it confirms the current fee and session. Look beyond Pratamnak when accommodation, a fixed weekly camp plan, a named coach, a women's programme, kids' provision or a guaranteed sparring schedule is essential.
