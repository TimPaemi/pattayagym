Camps with accommodation.

By TimPaemi · 8 min read · Updated 2026-07-27 · How we rank →

Accommodation is not one standard camp product. Compare the room, training load, meals, facility access, taxes and change terms that each operator actually publishes.

Pattaya · 215 venues hand-checked · Compare gyms

If you only read one thing: “accommodation included” is not one standard product. Fairtex publishes room-and-training packages, Kombat publishes several bundles with materially different room and facility access, Rage documents on-site rooms without a public package price, and Sityodtong requires a direct housing quote. Compare the written inclusions, not the camp label.

This guide uses operator pages checked on 26 July 2026. It does not convert a hotel gym, a nearby guesthouse or an unpriced room into a verified residential Muay Thai package. For the underlying venue evidence, open each linked record; for training-only alternatives, use the Muay Thai directory.

The amounts above are not interchangeable. Fairtex prices a set number of sessions with a room. Kombat's Standard and Deluxe products include a wider bundle, while Bronze is deliberately more basic. Rage and Sityodtong need direct quotes. The Battle route is training-only and should never be described as an accommodation package.

Fairtex: the clearest published package ladder

Fairtex Training Center Pattaya publishes short, medium and monthly room-and-training products. Its checked rate page listed a six-night package with ten training sessions at ฿16,000 for one person or ฿23,000 for two people sharing. A 15-night package with 20 sessions was ฿35,000 for one or ฿49,000 for two sharing. The one-month product was ฿46,500 for one or ฿63,000 for two sharing.

The operator describes a deluxe room, training access and use of the gym and pool in its package material. Do not infer meals, airport transfer, laundry, private tuition or an unlimited number of coached sessions unless the written quote names them. The occupancy column also matters: a two-person amount is not the single-person rate multiplied by two, and “sharing” should be checked against the exact room configuration.

Fairtex also publishes training-only prices: ฿800 for one session, ฿7,000 for ten and ฿16,500 for one month, with a private session at ฿1,500. Those figures create a useful control. If a traveller already has suitable accommodation, compare the training-only total with the residential package rather than assuming the package is automatically cheaper.

Kombat Group: read the tier before reading the headline price

Kombat Group Thailand publishes several residential tiers. For Muay Thai or boxing, the checked one-week Standard price was ฿20,900 and Deluxe was ฿24,900. The operator lists training, gym use, three meals per day including Sunday, an air-conditioned en-suite room, pool access, a T-shirt, a 30-minute private session, body analysis, juice and a massage among the stated inclusions for those packages.

The Bronze one-week Muay Thai or boxing product was ฿12,900; two weeks was ฿22,900 and four weeks ฿39,900. Bronze includes training, gym access, three Thai meals per day and a single fan room with a Thai-style bathroom. The operator explicitly excludes the pool and resort facilities from that tier. A traveller comparing ฿12,900 with ฿20,900 is therefore comparing different accommodation and facility products, not merely two discounts for the same stay.

Kombat's rate page says 7% VAT and a 3% card charge are excluded, as are airfare and taxi costs. Add those stated charges before comparing the complete amount. Confirm the exact discipline, arrival date, room, meal arrangement, pool eligibility, private-session scheduling and cancellation terms in the final invoice.

Rage: verified rooms, quote-first package

Rage Fight Academy documents on-site rooms with air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a refrigerator, bathroom, tea and coffee facilities, a kitchenette, bedding and housekeeping. Its camp page also documents a pool alongside Muay Thai and boxing areas, three full-size rings, a BJJ/MMA mat zone and a functional-fitness and weight room.

That evidence supports a genuine train-and-stay route, but the operator page checked on 26 July did not publish a dependable room-and-training tariff or a class-by-class timetable. Request a written quote that separates room nights, the exact discipline and number of sessions, gym or pool access, housekeeping, deposits, taxes and any meals. An equipment list and a room description do not establish coaching frequency.

Rage is relevant when mixed combat facilities and on-site accommodation matter more than a public package ladder. It is a weaker choice for someone who will not contact the operator before fixing a budget.

Sityodtong: training-led housing requires confirmation

Sityodtong Muay Thai is an established camp whose current schedule material explicitly separates beginner, intermediate and advanced participation. The camp also describes a four-week fight-camp route with housing. No current residential tariff or complete inclusion list was published on the pages checked for this guide.

This is a contact-first path for someone choosing the training environment before room amenities. Ask whether the quote covers a private or shared room, air conditioning, bathroom, utilities, cleaning, meals and the exact number of group or individual sessions. Also ask whether housing is on the same property and which address is used at check-in. Do not apply an old package price or assume a fighter-oriented route is the only option for a beginner.

The DIY route: published training plus separately verified housing

A residential camp reduces coordination. A DIY stay gives more control over room location, length and the option to change gyms. Battle & Conquer publishes group training at ฿500 for one session, ฿2,500 for one week and ฿7,000 for one month. Those are training prices, not room prices. The traveller must obtain a separate accommodation quote and map the route between the two exact pins.

A DIY comparison should include the room's taxes and deposit, training, daily transport, laundry, food and any separate fitness or recovery access. It should also preserve cancellation flexibility: a non-refundable room and a non-refundable training block can create two separate liabilities. The beginner Muay Thai guide compares current session products before a new student commits to a long package.

Do not convert “near a gym” into “camp accommodation.” The room provider and gym should each confirm their own product. Do not use an estimated neighbourhood rent as if it were an operator tariff.

How to compare the written quote

  • Training: name the discipline, group or private format, sessions included, normal weekly closure and validity period.
  • Room: confirm private or shared occupancy, air conditioning or fan, bathroom, dates, housekeeping and utilities.
  • Food: record the number of meals, which days are covered and whether dietary requirements were accepted in writing.
  • Facilities: ask whether the quoted tier includes the pool, general gym, recovery areas and access on rest days.
  • Total: add VAT, card fees, deposits, transfers and equipment that the operator explicitly excludes.
  • Changes: obtain cancellation, date-change, injury and early-departure terms before paying.

The best package is the one whose documented training load, room and total match the traveller's plan. A lower headline price can be correct and still be the wrong comparison when it removes air conditioning, pool access or another required inclusion.

Booking sequence

  1. Choose training frequency and discipline before choosing the room tier.
  2. Request a dated, itemised quote from the operator contact linked in the venue record.
  3. Match names, dates, occupancy, taxes and every inclusion to the invoice.
  4. Check the exact pin and arrival instructions before buying separate transport.
  5. Keep the cancellation and change terms with the payment record.

For broader location context, compare the Naklua area around Fairtex with the East Pattaya area used by several inland camp routes. An area page is not a travel-time guarantee; use exact pins.

Questions people ask

Which Pattaya Muay Thai camp has the cheapest verified accommodation package?

Among the current numeric packages compared here, Kombat's one-week Bronze Muay Thai or boxing tier is ฿12,900. It is a fan-room product with a Thai-style bathroom and excludes the pool and resort facilities, so it should not be compared as equivalent to Kombat Standard, Deluxe or Fairtex room products.

Does every stay-and-train package include meals?

No. Kombat publishes meal inclusions for the compared tiers. Fairtex's checked package evidence supports room, training, gym and pool use, but this guide does not infer meals. Rage and Sityodtong require a written inclusion list. Confirm the exact quote rather than relying on the phrase “all-inclusive.”

Should a first-timer book a month immediately?

A longer booking increases commitment to one room, location and coaching format. Compare a short published product or contact the camp about extension terms before locking in a month. The right choice depends on cancellation flexibility, training fit and the complete written total, not a universal trip length.
