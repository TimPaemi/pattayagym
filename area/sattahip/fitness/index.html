Sattahip's fitness listings are three very different contact-first options spread between Sattahip town and Bang Saray, rather than a single resort-strip gym market. The Best Fitness Sattahip is the broadest documented gym offer, with equipment, personal training, yoga and dance; WeSquare is a Bang Saray neighbourhood fitness listing with limited public operator detail; and Chan Pilates is a Pilates studio. The area label spans enough distance that a visitor should choose the discipline and exact pin before comparing a listed opening window or trying to arrange a casual visit. The Best Fitness is the record to contact when a conventional gym plus coached or class-based options is the aim. Its active Facebook and Instagram profiles, Maps identity and phone support the Sattahip listing at 65/16, while the published offer names fitness equipment, personal training, yoga and dance. Maps showed Saturday 07:00–21:00 at the last check, but no complete weekly schedule or class grid is documented. The public material also does not establish a day pass, membership price, trainer fee, trial rule, equipment inventory or whether yoga and dance are included with ordinary access. Call +66 65 823 7903 with the intended day and ask about opening time, visitor entry, the particular class, clothing and payment instead of reading one Saturday window as a confirmed visit. WeSquare is more useful as a local Bang Saray lead than as a described facility. Its active Maps record gives 88/259 Moo 9, Bang Saray, +66 96 531 9363, and hours of Monday–Friday 07:00–21:00 and Saturday–Sunday 07:00–20:00. That schedule is better defined than the public description of what is inside: no operator website, current social channel, class programme, trainer service, facility list, tariff or visitor policy was found. A cited local property report confirms it as a nearby gym amenity, not as a rate card or an evidence base for a pool, sauna, coaching or a particular machine. Ask directly whether a visitor can train, the casual price, payment conditions, staffed hours, changing facilities and entrance before travelling. Chan Pilates should be compared as an appointment-led specialist studio, not as a substitute for an unspecified gym floor. The current Facebook, Linktree and Maps trail supports its Bang Saray Beach Road identity, +66 96 997 4552 and daily 08:00–20:00 listed hours. The supported discipline is Pilates, but those hours are not a published class timetable or an automatic walk-in promise. No stable owner rate card, instructor list, apparatus inventory, private-versus-group menu, language information, experience requirement or cancellation policy is public. Contact the studio for the exact format, appointment time, total, package conditions, grip-sock or clothing expectations, and the entrance and parking arrangement. Choose The Best Fitness after confirming a gym or scheduled class near Sattahip town. Choose WeSquare when the Bang Saray location and a directly confirmed general-fitness visit matter more than published detail. Choose Chan Pilates when a booked Pilates session is the goal. Look elsewhere when a visible fee table, complete group timetable, verified equipment list, hotel day-pass product, guaranteed English instruction or a fixed drop-in entitlement is essential.
