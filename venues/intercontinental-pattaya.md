# InterContinental Pattaya Resort

Thai Maps shows the 437 Phra Tamnak Road resort property as **อินเตอร์คอนติเนนตัล พัทยา รีสอร์ท**, with three pools recorded for 07:00-20:00.

InterContinental Pattaya belongs in this directory as a hotel fitness and recovery record, not a confirmed public gym. It suits a registered guest considering a workout, leisure pools and spa facilities in one resort. The operator does not publish a non-resident sport pass or current fitness-centre timetable.

## What training is on offer

The current IHG amenities page names a fitness centre, three outdoor lagoon-style pools, a beach and Amburaya Spa. It identifies the pools as Spirituality Lagoon, Splash Lagoon and Serenity Pool. Splash Lagoon is the shallow option intended for younger guests and children. Poolside sun beds, service, towels and bottled water are also listed.

These are resort leisure pools, not a documented lap-training facility. The operator does not state lane lengths, lane availability or a dedicated exercise-swimming period. The fitness-centre icon confirms that a room exists; it does not establish its equipment inventory, personal training, classes or locker arrangements.

Amburaya Spa is described as having six private treatment suites for individuals or couples. The amenities page lists hydrotherapy, sauna and steam bathing plus treatment categories including body scrubs, wraps, facials, foot baths, reflexology and therapy baths.

This record suits a hotel guest who values a combined fitness, pool and spa setting. It may suit a non-guest only after the resort gives a written access confirmation.
