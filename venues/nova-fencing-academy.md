# Nova Fencing Academy

Nova Fencing Academy is a dedicated Pattaya fencing venue at 195/14-195/15 Moo 8 in Nong Prue, with published weekday and weekend opening hours rather than a generic sports-school listing.

## What training is on offer

The academy says its coaching is available to children, teenagers and adults. Its own introduction describes structured fencing training with professional coaching and safety standards, while a July 2026 Pattaya News report identifies programmes for beginners through experienced fencers. This is a specific fencing option, not a martial-arts class using foam equipment as a side activity.

Ask which weapon is being taught in the selected group, whether a beginner starts with a trial lesson, and whether the academy supplies mask, jacket, glove and weapon for first sessions. The published material supports coaching for several ages; it does not promise that every age or level trains at every opening hour.

## What it costs

No dated public price table was accessible in the checked operator and independent sources. Contact the academy for the trial, group, private and equipment terms before travelling. Ask whether fees are per class, term or membership, and whether competition entries or protective equipment are additional.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Nova suits a child, family or adult looking for a specialist Olympic-sport introduction in Pattaya. It is less suitable for someone who requires an instant public rate card or a drop-in combat-fitness workout without a booking conversation.

## Getting there

The operator and local report give 195/14-195/15 Moo 8, Nong Prue, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. Use the exact map pin and call +66 95 812 7889 if the entrance is unclear.

## Before you go

State the student's age, prior fencing experience, preferred day and whether equipment is needed. The academy lists 10:00-20:00 Monday to Friday and 10:00-19:00 at weekends; confirm the actual class start rather than treating those as a timetable.
