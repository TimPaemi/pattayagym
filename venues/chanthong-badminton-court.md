# Chanthong Badminton Court

The Chanthong Pattaya lists a badminton court among its hotel facilities. The operator's site gives the hotel address at **390/24 Moo 10, Pattaya Sai 3 Road** and the phone **+66 97 189 7783**. A separate current Maps record identifies Chanthong Badminton Court at the same Third Road property and lists **10:00-23:30 daily**.

The hotel also advertises billiards, an outdoor swimming pool and common areas. Those other amenities do not prove that a badminton booking includes pool access or any wider hotel-facility pass.

## Booking and first visit

The operator does not publish a court tariff, court count, surface specification, booking method, equipment-hire menu or clear non-resident access policy. The listed hours therefore confirm an operating window, not that an outside player can arrive without a reservation.

Phone before travelling and state the date, start time, player count and requested duration. Ask whether outside players can book, whether hotel guests receive priority, whether the quote is per court or per person and whether rackets or shuttlecocks are included. Confirm footwear, changing facilities, parking, cancellation terms and the Third Road entrance. A blank price field means that no stable current first-hand amount was found, not that the court is free.
