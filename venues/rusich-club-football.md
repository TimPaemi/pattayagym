# Rusich Club Pattaya

Rusich is an East Pattaya combat and functional-training club, not a football academy despite its legacy record ID. It suits children, adults and families able to match a specific discipline, age band and timetable. The operator publishes several prices and schedules, but one membership should not be assumed to cover every programme.

## What training is on offer

The current operator site lists combined judo, sambo and jiu-jitsu wrestling; MMA; hand-to-hand fighting; Muay Thai and mixed fighting; grappling; and functional training with CrossFit elements. It says beginners and experienced participants are accepted, subject to the appropriate group.

Exact class pages checked 2026-07-26 list adult wrestling from age 11 on Tuesday and Thursday, 19:00-20:30; adult MMA from age 16 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 10:00-11:30; and MMA with hand-to-hand fighting from age 11 on those days, 19:00-20:30. Children's wrestling ages 4-10 runs Tuesday and Thursday, 17:30-18:30; children's hand-to-hand fighting uses Monday, Wednesday and Friday at the same time. Grappling from age 10 is Saturday, 10:00-11:30.

## What it costs

The named combat-class pages checked 2026-07-26 list ฿350 for one class. Adult MMA and adult MMA/hand-to-hand products list a free trial and ฿3,000 for one month of three weekly sessions. Adult wrestling lists a free trial and ฿2,500 for one month of two weekly evening sessions.

Children's wrestling lists a free trial and ฿2,500 for one month; children's hand-to-hand fighting lists a free trial and ฿3,000 for one month. The grappling page lists ฿1,200 for one month and does not display a free-trial line. Several pages advertise 10% off two memberships and 15% off three, also checked 2026-07-26. Confirm qualifying products and whether registration, uniform or protective equipment costs extra.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Rusich suits a family or training group whose participants can use different age-specific programmes at one club. It is less suitable for an unplanned walk-in or someone seeking one all-discipline membership: the value differs between two-session wrestling, three-session MMA and Saturday grappling.

The [kids and youth directory](/category/kids-youth/) compares other age-banded programmes. Adults focused on grappling should also use the [BJJ and MMA guide](/guides/bjj-mma-pattaya/) to distinguish BJJ, wrestling, grappling and mixed sessions.

## Getting there

The club publishes 46/65 Moo 11, Pattaya 20150, phone and WhatsApp +66 62 002 6726 and `info@rusich.club`. Use the exact map and ask the club to confirm the entrance. “East Pattaya” covers a broad inland area; the [East Pattaya index](/area/east-pattaya/) does not establish a fixed travel time, fare or public-transport route.

## Before you go

Send the participant's age, experience, preferred discipline and date. Ask which named coach and language apply. The public site is available in English and Russian, but that does not prove the coaching language of every class.

Confirm what clothing is acceptable for a trial, whether a gi is required for wrestling or jiu-jitsu content and which protective equipment is needed for striking. For a child, ask whether a guardian may observe and how collection is handled. Reconfirm the date because a published weekly timetable does not guarantee that a holiday or event leaves the class unchanged.
