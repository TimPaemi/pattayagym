# Yoga Pattaya Studio

Yoga Pattaya Studio is a group and private yoga studio on Thepprasit Soi 12. The operator publishes a broad class menu, multilingual teaching and current pass prices. Most classes accept drop-ins, but the timetable changes and the selected teacher and language should be confirmed before arrival.

## What training is on offer

The operator lists Ashtanga, Mysore, Hatha, Hatha Vinyasa, Vinyasa Flow, stretching, Fitness Yoga and Sri Sri classes, with levels from beginner to advanced. It publishes English, Russian and Thai as teaching languages, but that does not mean every session uses all three.

The FAQ says most classes are open level and available as drop-ins. Pregnancy yoga requires advance booking. Mats can be borrowed without charge; the studio also lists three bathrooms and two changing rooms with lockers.

## What it costs

Prices below were published by the operator and checked on 26 July 2026:

- Drop-in: ฿500 for a standard one-hour class or ฿600 for Ashtanga.
- Three classes valid two weeks: ฿1,200 standard or ฿1,500 Ashtanga.
- Eight classes: ฿3,100 standard or ฿3,500 Ashtanga; 12 classes: ฿4,000 standard or ฿4,800 Ashtanga.
- Ten mixed classes: ฿4,000; 15 any classes valid two months: ฿6,000.
- Two weeks: ฿2,900 for one-hour classes or ฿3,500 unlimited any classes.
- One month: ฿4,500 unlimited standard, ฿5,000 Ashtanga or ฿5,800 all classes.
- Three months all classes: ฿13,000.
- Private class: ฿1,500 for one person or ฿1,800 for two.

The price page says purchases are non-refundable and non-transferable. Confirm the validity period for the exact pass before payment; the page describes most cards as valid 30 days but separately gives longer validity for some products.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

The open-level policy, drop-ins, free mat borrowing and explicit language information make this a workable choice for a visitor who can match a current class to their level and preferred teaching language. It also suits someone wanting Ashtanga-specific options rather than a generic hotel class.

It is less suitable for anyone who needs a fixed weekly timetable far in advance, a guaranteed single-language session or a card-payment option. The FAQ says the studio does not accept credit cards. For a wider comparison, use the [Pattaya yoga category](/category/yoga/) or the [Jomtien-area directory](/area/jomtien/).

## Getting there

The operator publishes the address as 315/322 Thepprasit Soi 12, Nong Prue. Maps confirms the live studio identity on Soi Thepprasit 12 and lists +66 82 246 5366. Use the exact pin because Thepprasit is a long road and the studio is inside a numbered soi.

No fixed transfer time, fare or parking arrangement was published on the checked pages.

## Before you go

Read the newest schedule post rather than treating Maps business hours as class times. The operator asks students to arrive 10-15 minutes early. Confirm class style, level, language, teacher, remaining space and payment method. Tell the teacher before class if leaving early; pregnancy yoga needs pre-booking.
