# Greenwood Golf Club

Greenwood is a 27-hole golf club in Khlong Kiew, Ban Bueng. Its official site describes three nine-hole loops and credits the design to Peter W Thomson. The site lists daily opening from 06:00 to 18:00, and a Pattaya Sports Club report documents play here in July 2026.

## Price and first visit

The club publishes a green-fee page but notes that rates can vary; a reliable current numeric tariff was not available in extractable text during this check. Use **+66 2 026 6494**, which is the number currently shown on the club's contact page and live Maps listing. A separate green-fee page still prints +66 2 026 4694, so the record follows the two matching current sources. Phone for the visitor green fee, caddie fee, buggy policy and available loop combination for your date. The course is outside Pattaya proper, so include road travel time when choosing a tee time.
