# Bangkok Hospital Pattaya Rehabilitation Center

Thai Maps names the parent hospital **โรงพยาบาลกรุงเทพพัทยา**; this directory record specifically covers its Building A, 3rd-floor rehabilitation centre only here.

## What training is on offer

Bangkok Hospital Pattaya's Rehabilitation Center is a **clinical hospital department, not a gym, recovery lounge or self-directed exercise facility**. The official page says it handles post-orthopaedic, musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation. It describes physician assessment and treatment by physical and occupational therapists, with programmes adapted to the patient's needs.

The centre lists an evaluation room, treatment room, traction room, chest-therapy room, child-development stimulation room, activities-of-daily-living room and exercise zone. Published capabilities include heat and cold modalities, whirlpool, laser and electrical therapy, traction, partial-body-weight support, shock-wave therapy, paediatric occupational therapy and acupuncture. These are department capabilities, not promises that a named treatment is appropriate for an individual.

## What it costs

No stable public tariff for an ordinary rehabilitation assessment or treatment course was found on the official page checked on 26 July 2026. Separate time-limited performance-testing packages shown by the hospital are named testing products and should not be treated as the price of physiotherapy. Request a written estimate for the assessed plan, including professional, procedure, equipment and hospital charges.

If using insurance, confirm direct billing, pre-authorisation, exclusions and the documents the insurer requires before the appointment. International patients should also ask which languages are available for the booked consultation and whether an interpreter must be arranged.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

This record may suit a patient seeking a hospital-based assessment or a clinician-directed rehabilitation pathway. The official page names physicians, physical therapists, occupational therapists, nurses and assistants as part of the team.

It does not suit someone seeking an ordinary workout, sports massage menu or unsupervised machine access. The directory cannot diagnose an injury or determine a treatment. For an emergency, use the hospital's appropriate emergency pathway rather than relying on this department's daytime listing. The [clubs category](/category/clubs/) retains the record for discoverability, not to reclassify clinical care as a club.

## Getting there

The hospital places the centre on the **third floor of Building A**, at **301 Moo 6, Sukhumvit Road Km 143, Naklua**. It publishes centre hours of **08:00–20:00 every day**. The wider hospital's 24-hour Maps operation does not make the Rehabilitation Center a round-the-clock walk-in service. Reconfirm the building, entrance and appointment time before arrival; the [Naklua area page](/area/naklua/) provides broader location context.

## Before you go

Call the centre directly on **+66 38 259 986** or use **info.pattaya@bangkokhospital.com**. Describe whether the request concerns a recent injury, post-treatment recovery, neurological rehabilitation, paediatric occupational therapy or another need. Ask whether the first booking should be with a physician or therapist and whether to bring a referral, imaging, operation note, medication list or previous plan.

Request the proposed appointment type, expected duration, written estimate, language arrangement and insurance documents before travelling.

## What we could not verify

The official page did not publish an ordinary assessment tariff, session-by-session treatment price, expected course length, language roster or a rule for walk-in care. The directory can verify the department, location, hours, contacts and published capabilities; it cannot select a treatment or predict an outcome.
